# DVT location

- Proximal lower extremity
    - Pelvic / iliac / inferior vena cava (IVC)
    - Femoral / popliteal
- Distal lower extremity
    - Peroneal
    - Anterior tibial
    - Posterior tibial
    - Muscular (soleus and gastrocnemius)
- Upper limb / chest
    - Brachiocephalic
    - Subclavian
    - Axillary
    - Internal jugular
    - Brachial
    - Superior vena cava (SVC)

# Proximal lower extremity: treatment

- No contraindication to anticoagulation
    - Anticoagulation
    - Consider catheter-directed therapy in appropriate candidates
        - Pharmacomechanical thrombolysis
        - Mechanical thrombectomy
    - Consider compression therapy for symptom control
        - Only if the patient tolerates therapeutic anticoagulation
- Contraindication to anticoagulation
    - IVC filter
        - Retrievable filter preferred
    - Then ask whether the contraindication persists or is likely to recur
        - Contraindication resolved and not likely to recur
            - Anticoagulation
            - Filter removal
        - Contraindication persists or is likely to recur
            - Re-evaluate regularly for change in status

# Distal lower extremity: treatment

- No contraindication to anticoagulation
    - Anticoagulation
- Contraindication to anticoagulation
    - Follow-up with serial US
        - Progression to proximal vein, treat on the pelvic / iliac / IVC and femoral / popliteal pathway above
        - Local progression, but not to proximal deep vein, re-evaluate for risk / benefit of anticoagulation
        - No progression, continue to follow as clinically indicated

# Upper limb / chest: treatment

- No contraindication to anticoagulation
    - Anticoagulation
    - Consider catheter-directed therapy in appropriate candidates
        - Pharmacomechanical thrombolysis
        - Mechanical thrombectomy
- Contraindication to anticoagulation
    - Follow until the contraindication is resolved or the DVT progresses

# Next

- Anticoagulation started, follow as clinically indicated
- IVC filter placed, re-check whether the contraindication persists or is likely to recur, and remove the filter once it does not
- Contraindication persists, re-evaluate regularly for change in status
- Distal DVT on serial US that progresses to a proximal vein, switch to the proximal lower extremity pathway on this page
- Distal DVT with local progression only, re-evaluate for risk / benefit of anticoagulation
- Upper limb / chest DVT with a contraindication, follow until it is resolved or the DVT progresses
