# Anticoagulation

- Therapeutic Anticoagulation for VTE (VTE-D)
- Check Contraindications to Therapeutic Anticoagulation (VTE-E)
- If chemotherapy-induced thrombocytopenia, see Management of Anticoagulation for VTE in Patients with Chemotherapy-Induced Thrombocytopenia (VTE-F)
- If extension of VTE or new VTE while on recommended anticoagulation therapy, see Progression or New Thrombosis on Therapeutic Anticoagulation (VTE-G)

# Catheter-directed thrombolysis or thrombectomy

- Choice of regimen should be made based on institutional expertise/preferences, in conjunction with interventional radiology or vascular surgery colleagues (VTE-H)
- Appropriate candidates may include
    - Patients at risk of limb loss (eg, phlegmasia cerulea dolens)
    - Patients with central thrombus propagation despite anticoagulation
    - Patients with moderate to severely symptomatic proximal DVT
- Candidates with high bleeding risk or contraindication to fibrinolytic may be candidates for percutaneous mechanical thrombectomy
- Check Contraindications to Thrombolysis and Indications for Thrombolysis (VTE-I)
- Providers can consult with interventional radiology or vascular surgery colleagues to determine the appropriate use at their institutions of
    - Mechanical embolectomy
    - Suction embolectomy
    - US-facilitated catheter-directed thrombolysis devices

# Graduated compression stockings

- GCS did not reduce the incidence of post-thrombotic syndrome (PTS) in a double-blind randomized trial (Kahn SR, et al. Lancet 2014;383:880-888)

# IVC filter

- Recommend IVC filter removal, if tolerating anticoagulation
