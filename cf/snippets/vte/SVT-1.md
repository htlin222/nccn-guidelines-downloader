# Source

- NCCN Venous Thromboembolic Disease v1.2026, SVT-1, p15
- Applies to clinical suspicion of acute superficial vein thrombosis (SVT)
    - Pain, erythema, and tenderness involving a superficial vein in the extremity
- Anticoagulation dosing: Therapeutic Anticoagulation for VTE (VTE-D)
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Workup/imaging

- Comprehensive medical H&P
- CBC with platelet count
- PT, aPTT
- Liver and kidney function tests
- Consider venous ultrasound (US) based on clinical judgment

# Catheter-related SVT

- If peripheral catheter is related and catheter no longer indicated, remove catheter
    - For SVT associated with a peripherally inserted central catheter (PICC) line, catheter removal may not be necessary
        - Especially if the patient is treated with anticoagulation and/or symptoms resolve

# Upper extremity SVT (median, basilic, and/or cephalic veins)

- Use symptomatic treatment and monitor for progression
    - Symptomatic treatment includes warm compresses
    - Nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs)
    - Elevation
- If progression symptomatically or on imaging, prophylactic dose anticoagulation is recommended
    - Rivaroxaban 10 mg PO daily and fondaparinux 2.5 mg SC daily have been shown to be effective in some studies that included a limited number of patients with cancer
    - Therapeutic dosing may be used at the clinician's discretion (VTE-D)
- Consider therapeutic dose anticoagulation (VTE-D) if the clot is in close proximity to the deep venous system
    - Close proximity is defined as within approximately 3 cm

# Lower extremity SVT (great and small saphenous veins)

- Prophylactic dose anticoagulation for at least 6 weeks if:
    - SVT >5 cm in length
    - SVT extends above knee
    - Prophylactic dosing (VTE-D): rivaroxaban 10 mg PO daily, or fondaparinux 2.5 mg SC daily
    - If SVT is within 3 cm from the saphenofemoral junction, treat with therapeutic dose anticoagulation instead (VTE-D)
- Therapeutic dose anticoagulation (VTE-D) for at least 3 months if SVT is within 3 cm of the saphenofemoral junction
- Consider repeat US in 7-10 days if SVT <5 cm in length or below knee
    - If repeat US shows progression, consider prophylactic or therapeutic anticoagulation as appropriate (VTE-D)
