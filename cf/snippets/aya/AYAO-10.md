# Evaluation

- Insurance availability and security
    - Employer-provided
    - Parent's insurance
    - Medicaid
    - Health insurance marketplace
- Assessment of risk for losing insurance
    - Loss of employment
    - Age out of parent's insurance
- Assessment of financial toxicity
- Risk for financial loss or bankruptcy
- Child care
- Transportation
- Accommodation if traveling to receive treatment
- Stability of housing and basic household socioeconomic needs

# Supportive care services and interventions

- Link qualified AYA patients to Medicaid, Social Security, and/or disability insurance
    - Provide information regarding medication assistance programs for patients with limited or no insurance
    - Provide information regarding hospital pharmacy vouchers or low-cost medication programs
    - Direct AYA patients to legal resources/advocates for assistance with understanding health insurance coverage and transition to independent health insurance coverage
- Educate AYA patients about benefits for which they may qualify
    - Short- or long-term disability
    - State disability benefits
    - Public assistance
- Provide information on obtaining financial assistance for fertility-based services
    - Local and institutional grants may be available
- Provide school support, education services, and work reintegration for patients
    - Consider educational scholarship resources or tuition assistance programs (eg, The Ruth Cheatham Foundation)
- Refer for career counseling and/or education support as indicated
- Encourage discussion with guidance counselors and educators about impact of cancer care on education
- Refer to mental health experts, social workers, or financial counselors as needed to assess the psychosocial impact of financial toxicity
    - Loss of employment
    - Withdrawing from school
    - Not being able to socialize with friends due to decreased income
- Refer to transportation assistance programs
    - Van ride programs
    - Voucher programs
- Provide AYAs with a list of recommended and reliable online sources and financial support programs to access information related to their cancer
    - See Online Resources for AYA Patients and Survivors
- Integrate financial assistance into AYA survivorship care plans
- Consider need for long-term follow-up care for monitoring and treatment of late effects long after completion of treatment
