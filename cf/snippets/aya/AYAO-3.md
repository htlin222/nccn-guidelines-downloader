# Communication and education preferences and needs

- Speak with the patient individually to solicit questions/concerns without a caregiver/partner present, so the patient can speak freely
- Establish standards of communication with caregivers/partners
- Provide cancer information and education at an age- and developmentally appropriate level
    - See Online Resources for AYA Patients and Survivors

# Fertility and sexual health/identity

- Discuss risks of impaired fertility due to cancer and its therapy
- Discuss fertility preservation options
- Consider referral to a fertility preservation/reproductive health program (AYAO-5)
- Discuss contraception prior to initiating therapy
    - Consult with OB/GYN for patients with ovaries/uterus
    - Consult the CDC Summary Chart of U.S. Medical Eligibility Criteria to assist with the safety and efficacy of selection of appropriate contraception for individuals with childbearing potential
    - Strongly encourage condom use
- Monitor for pregnancy while on therapy, in accordance with institutional requirements
- Assess gender identity, pronouns, sexual activity, and concerns

# Psychosocial assessment

- Individual (AYAO-7 and AYAO-8)
- Relationships (AYAO-9)
- Socioeconomic issues (AYAO-10)

# Family history and genetic risk

- Take a complete family history
- Refer for subsequent evaluation by a genetic counselor for risk assessment and counseling as needed
    - Approximately 8%–9% of all patients with cancer will have a germline mutation in a cancer-predisposing gene
- Risk factors for breast cancer
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Genetic/Familial High-Risk Assessment: Breast, Ovarian, Pancreatic, and Prostate
    - Chest irradiation
- Risk factors for colon cancer
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Genetic/Familial High-Risk Assessment: Colorectal, Endometrial, Esophageal, and Gastric
- Risk factors for sarcomas
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Soft Tissue Sarcoma
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Pediatric Soft Tissue Sarcoma
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Bone Cancer
- Risk factors for multiple endocrine neoplasia (MEN)
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Neuroendocrine and Adrenal Tumors
- Risk factors for pediatric diffuse high-grade gliomas
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Pediatric Central Nervous System Cancers
