# Referral

- Strongly consider a referral for treatment at a cancer center with expertise in comprehensive care of AYAs
    - Particularly for pediatric cancer types
    - The center should have access to clinical trials for AYAs
    - Such centers provide a multidisciplinary approach, a team of providers with expertise in AYA cancer treatment, with access to clinical trials for the AYA population
    - Such centers manage specific issues including
        - Mental health
        - Fertility counseling
        - Genetic counseling
        - Education and career development
        - Employment management with disease demands
        - Survivorship
        - Wellness promotion
