# Behavioral factors

- Ongoing assessment, as patterns may change throughout care or related to treatment and symptom management
    - Adherence to therapy
    - Tobacco, vaping, alcohol, cannabis, or other substance use
    - Assess for access to firearms or other dangerous objects in the home
    - Sexual behavior/risks/concerns
    - Assess nutritional requirement and potential deficits based on age
    - Exercise needs, hobbies, and recreational activities
    - Sleep patterns
- Use of both integrative therapies and complementary and alternative medicine (CAM)

# Adherence to therapy and safety plans

- Educate about the expectations of treatment and explain the patient's responsibility to adhere to therapy
- Refer to health or pediatric psychologist for adherence intervention if needed
    - Include a list of barriers and facilitators
- Provide education and/or guidance about all medications and their dose, purpose, and adverse effects
    - Prior to the start of treatment
    - Every time there is a change in treatment
- Simplify and modify dosing schedule, when medically possible, to fit into the AYA's lifestyle and normal activities
- Consider the use of technology (eg, smartphone applications)
    - See Select Principles of Supportive Care Services/Interventions: Technology Use (AYAO-D)
- Provide access to systematic and standardized symptom management for side effects related to cancer treatment
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Supportive Care

# Substance use

- Provide education on risks and benefits of substance use and smoking during treatment
- Provide education and/or guidance on cannabinoid use
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Antiemesis
- Refer patients with signs, symptoms, or a history of a substance use disorder to a risk reduction or substance use counseling program
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Smoking Cessation

# Sexual health

- Provide education about the impact of treatment on sexual health and safe sexual practices, including oral, anal, and vaginal sex
    - In light of risk of infection
    - Risk for bleeding
    - Prevention of pregnancy
    - Sexually transmitted infections

# Diet and exercise

- Provide education about potential diet/nutritional changes associated with cancer treatment and possible interventions
- Refer to a registered dietitian-certified specialist in oncology (RD-CSO)
- Provide education on physical conditioning and related health benefits during and following cancer treatment
    - Stout NL, et al. Cancer 2020;126:2750-2758
- Refer to a rehabilitation specialist (ie, physiatrist, physical therapist, occupational therapist) to address physical impairments and initiate physical activity interventions
- Medical evaluation and clearance by a physician (such as an oncologist or a physiatrist) is recommended prior to initiating exercise in patients in whom exercise modifications or precautions might be needed
- Refer patients to the American Cancer Society Guideline for Diet and Physical Activity for Cancer Prevention
- Refer patients to the U.S. Preventive Services Task Force Healthy Diet and Physical Activity for Cardiovascular Disease Prevention in Adults With Cardiovascular Risk Factors: Behavioral Counseling Interventions

# Sleep

- Refer patients with sleep disturbance to supportive medicine or behavioral health for interventions
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Supportive Care
    - Overview of sleep disorders, screening, evaluation, treatment, and sleep hygiene, see NCCN Guidelines for Survivorship
    - Sleep/wake disturbances including insomnia or sedation, see NCCN Guidelines for Palliative Care

# Integrative therapies and CAM

- Assess patient's use of or desire for integrative therapies and interventions
- Refer to integrative oncologist when patient is interested in evidence-based integrative therapies to manage side effects of treatment and overall well-being
    - https://integrativeonc.org/practice-guidelines
- Monitor use of dietary supplements that might impact effectiveness of treatment or treatment medications
    - https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/cam/hp/dietary-interactions-pdq
