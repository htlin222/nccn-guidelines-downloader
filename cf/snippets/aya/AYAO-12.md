# Any radiation therapy (RT)

- Recommend a dental exam and cleaning every 6 mo for patients who received RT
- Recommend annual skin exams as performed by a dermatologist

# Total body irradiation (TBI)

- Thyroid disease screening
- Cardiovascular risk factor screening (dyslipidemia)
- Ocular screening (cataracts)
- Screening for secondary malignant neoplasms (SMNs)
- Impaired glucose metabolism screening
- Gonadal dysfunction screening

# Cranial or craniospinal radiation

- Neuroendocrine axis screening
- Neuropsychological evaluation
- Ocular screening
- Gonadal dysfunction screening
- Audiologic evaluation
    - For cranial radiation doses ≥30 Gy
- Colorectal cancer screening
    - For lumbar, sacral, or whole spine

# Chest radiation

- Screening for cardiomyopathy
- Screening for valvular heart disease
- Cardiovascular risk assessment and screening
- Pulmonary function screening
- See NCCN Guidelines for Breast Cancer Screening and Diagnosis
    - There is an increased risk of breast cancer in individuals assigned male at birth who receive chest radiation (de Vries S, et al. Blood 2023;142:806-811)

# Neck radiation

- Thyroid disease screening
    - Clement SC, et al. Cancer Treat Rev 2018;63:28-39

# Abdominal or pelvic radiation

- Colorectal cancer screening
- Assessment for bowel dysfunction
- Assessment of gonadal and sexual function/dyspareunia
- Screening for kidney or bladder disease
- Screening for impaired glucose metabolism (for abdominal radiation)
- Screening for dyslipidemia (for abdominal radiation)
- Consider screening for cardiac disease (cardiomyopathy and vascular disease) if RT to upper abdomen
- Consider high-risk obstetrics referral if pregnant

# Limb radiation

- Physical therapy to address post-radiation fibrosis-related deficits
- Consider carotid artery screening
- Orthopedic screening to assess fragility fractures
- Avascular necrosis
