# Source

- NCCN Adolescent and Young Adult (AYA) Oncology v1.2027, AYAO-5, p14
- Applies to AYAs with cancer at any level of risk for impaired fertility or sexual dysfunction due to cancer treatments
    - Regardless of gender identity, sexual orientation, or financial status
    - Addressing fertility and sexual health and function should be an essential part of their care
    - Supporting references: Meacham LR, et al. J Adolesc Young Adult Oncol 2020;9:662-666; Mulder RL, et al. Lancet Oncol 2021;22:e68-e80; Mulder RL, et al. Lancet Oncol 2021;22:e57-e67; Mulder R, et al. Lancet Oncol 2021;22:e45-e56; Su HI, et al. J Clin Oncol 2025;43:1488-1515
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Risk assessment and discussion

- Perform an assessment of the risk for gonadotoxicity and impaired fertility due to the cancer and its treatment
- Discuss that assessment with the patient
- Discuss options for fertility preservation with the patient
- Do all of the above:
    - As soon as possible prior to the start of therapy
    - Throughout the course of treatment
        - Supporting reference: Green DM, et al. Pediatr Blood Cancer 2014;61:53-67
    - In survivorship
- Assess for routine reproductive/gynecologic health care when transitioning from pediatric to adult care

# Referral

- Initiate referral for a reproductive health specialist as soon as possible
- And/or provide resources for off-site/remote sperm banking as soon as possible
- Do this for all patients who are interested in or ambivalent about pursuing fertility preservation

# Emotional and psychosocial impact

- Consider the emotional impact of conversations surrounding fertility preservation
    - Especially for the younger AYA patient
    - Especially for the sexual gender minority (SGM) patient
- See Comprehensive Psychosocial/Behavioral Considerations
    - Individual (AYAO-7 and AYAO-8)
    - Relationships (AYAO-9)
    - Socioeconomic Issues (AYAO-10)

# Financial

- Assist AYA patients in identifying financial resources for fertility preservation
