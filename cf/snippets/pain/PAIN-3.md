# Selecting the analgesic regimen

- Optimize pain management therapies to improve function and meet patient's goals of care
- Select the most appropriate analgesic regimen based on
    - Pain diagnosis (PAIN-K)
    - Comorbid conditions
    - Safety
    - Potential drug interactions
    - Estimated trajectory of pain
    - Medication availability
    - Expense/financial toxicity
- Analgesic regimen may include
    - Opioids (PAIN-G)
    - Acetaminophen
    - Nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) (PAIN-E)
    - Adjuvant analgesics (PAIN-F)
- If pain is continuous, consider regularly scheduled analgesics

# Alongside the analgesic regimen

- Anticipate and treat analgesic adverse effects, including opioid-induced constipation (PAIN-H)
- Provide psychosocial/spiritual support (PAIN-C)
- Provide patient and family/caregiver education (PAIN-I)
- Optimize non-pharmacologic interventions (PAIN-D)
- Optimize interventional strategies (PAIN-M)
- Optimize multidisciplinary care (PAIN-L)
- Consider consultation
    - Radiation oncology
    - Pain medicine
    - Palliative care

# Reevaluation

- Reevaluate pain at each contact and as needed
- Meet patient-specific goals for
    - Comfort
    - Function
    - Safety
