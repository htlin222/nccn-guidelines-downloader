# Assessment

- ___ (age) yo, metastatic penile cancer
- Systemic therapy given ___ (systemic_therapy)
- Molecular testing ___ (mgpt_status)
- Response on imaging of chest/abdomen/pelvis ___ (response)

# Systemic chemotherapy

- Systemic chemotherapy
    - Principles of Systemic Therapy (PN-D)
- Discuss palliative care and therapies for local control
- Consider molecular testing in a Clinical Laboratory Improvement Amendments (CLIA)-approved laboratory
    - Include multigene panel testing (MGPT)
    - MGPT would identify rare and actionable mutations and fusions

# Response assessment

- Imaging of chest/abdomen/pelvis
    - May include CT, MRI, PET/CT, and/or chest x-ray
    - When appropriate, imaging should be done with contrast unless contraindicated
    - Principles of Imaging (PN-E)

# Complete/partial response or stable

- Consolidation surgery, select patients
    - Bilateral superficial and deep ILND
    - Unilateral/bilateral PLND

# No response or disease progression

- Consider subsequent-line systemic therapy
    - Principles of Systemic Therapy (PN-D)
- or Consider RT for local control
    - Principles of Radiotherapy (PN-C)
- and/or Clinical trial
- and/or Best supportive care
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Palliative Care
