# Primary lesion, initial treatment was topical or local therapy

- Clinical examination
    - Includes examination of the penis and inguinal region
    - If an abnormal clinical examination, patient affected by obesity, or prior inguinal surgery, then consider one of the following of the inguinal region
        - Ultrasound
        - CT with contrast
        - MRI with contrast
- Schedule
    - Years 1 to 2: every 3 months
    - Then Years 3 to 5: every 6 months
    - Then Years 5 to 10: every 12 months

# Primary lesion, initial treatment was partial or radical penectomy

- Clinical examination
    - Includes examination of the penis and inguinal region
    - If an abnormal clinical examination, patient affected by obesity, or prior inguinal surgery, then consider one of the following of the inguinal region
        - Ultrasound
        - CT with contrast
        - MRI with contrast
- Schedule
    - Years 1 to 2: every 6 months
    - Then Years 3 to 10: every 12 months

# Lymph nodes, pNX

- Applies to patients on active surveillance of clinically negative nodes and at low risk for inguinal metastases
- Clinical examination
    - Includes examination of the penis and inguinal region
    - If an abnormal clinical examination, patient affected by obesity, or prior inguinal surgery, then consider one of the following of the inguinal region
        - Ultrasound
        - CT with contrast
        - MRI with contrast
- Schedule
    - Years 1 to 2: every 6 months
    - Then Years 3 to 4: every 12 months

# Lymph nodes, pN0 or N1

- Clinical examination
    - Includes examination of the penis and inguinal region
    - If an abnormal clinical examination, patient affected by obesity, or prior inguinal surgery, then consider one of the following of the inguinal region
        - Ultrasound
        - CT with contrast
        - MRI with contrast
- CT abdomen/pelvis
- Chest radiograph
- Schedule
    - Years 1 to 2: every 6 months
    - Then Years 3 to 4: every 12 months

# Lymph nodes, pN2 or N3

- Clinical examination
    - Includes examination of the penis and inguinal region
    - If an abnormal clinical examination, patient affected by obesity, or prior inguinal surgery, then consider one of the following of the inguinal region
        - Ultrasound
        - CT with contrast
        - MRI with contrast
- CT abdomen/pelvis
- Chest CT
- Schedule
    - Year 1: every 3 months
    - Then Years 2 to 4: every 6 months
