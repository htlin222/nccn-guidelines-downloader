# Source

- NCCN Penile Cancer v2.2026, PN-2, p6
- Applies once a pathologic diagnosis has been made, to choose primary treatment
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- ___ (age) yo
- Pathologic diagnosis ___ (pt_stage)
- Inguinal lymph nodes on exam ___ (inguinal_nodes)

# Primary treatment, pT1a

- Choose one of the following
- Wide local excision
    - Principles of Penile Organ-Sparing Approaches (PN-A)
- Partial penectomy
    - Principles of Surgery (PN-B)
    - Principles of Radiotherapy (PN-C)
- Glansectomy in select cases
    - Principles of Penile Organ-Sparing Approaches (PN-A)
- Mohs surgery in select cases (category 2B)
    - Principles of Penile Organ-Sparing Approaches (PN-A)
- Laser therapy (category 2B)
    - Principles of Penile Organ-Sparing Approaches (PN-A)
- RT (category 2B)
    - Principles of Radiotherapy (PN-C)

# Primary treatment, pT1b

- Choose one of the following
- Wide local excision
    - Principles of Penile Organ-Sparing Approaches (PN-A)
- Partial penectomy
    - Principles of Surgery (PN-B)
    - Principles of Radiotherapy (PN-C)
- Total penectomy
    - Principles of Surgery (PN-B)
    - Principles of Radiotherapy (PN-C)
- RT (category 2B)
    - Principles of Radiotherapy (PN-C)
- Chemo/RT (category 3)
    - Principles of Radiotherapy (PN-C)
    - Principles of Systemic Therapy (PN-D)

# Primary treatment, T2 or greater

- Choose one of the following
- Partial penectomy
    - Principles of Surgery (PN-B)
    - Principles of Radiotherapy (PN-C)
- Total penectomy
    - Principles of Surgery (PN-B)
    - Principles of Radiotherapy (PN-C)
- RT (category 2B)
    - Principles of Radiotherapy (PN-C)
- Chemo/RT (category 3)
    - Principles of Radiotherapy (PN-C)
    - Principles of Systemic Therapy (PN-D)
