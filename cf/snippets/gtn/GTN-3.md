# Source

- NCCN Gestational Trophoblastic Neoplasia v2.2026, GTN-3, p12
- Applies to persistent GTN after initial therapy
- Consider consultation with a clinician or center with expertise in management of gestational trophoblastic diseases
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- hCG ___ (hcg)
- Response to initial therapy ___ (response)
- Single agent used in first-line therapy ___ (firstline_agent)
- WHO score ___ (who_score)

# Good response to initial therapy followed by hCG level plateau or re-elevation (hCG <1000)

- Change to single agent not used in first-line therapy
    - Methotrexate
    - Dactinomycin
    - See Systemic Therapy for GTN (GTN-D) for specific recommendations
- and Repeat workup to check for metastasis
- and Recalculate WHO score based on imaging
- and Consider hysterectomy with salpingectomy
- Monitoring during treatment
    - hCG assay every 2 weeks, at the start of each treatment cycle
    - Principles of Gynecologic Survivorship (GTN-E)
- Response assessment
    - Normal hCG levels
        - Continue systemic therapy for 2–3 treatment cycles past normalization
    - hCG level plateaus (<10% change) for 2 treatment cycles, or hCG level rises (>10% change) for 1 treatment cycle
        - Repeat workup to check for metastasis
        - WHO rescoring
        - and Transition to combination therapy: EMA-CO
        - See Systemic Therapy for GTN (GTN-D) for specific recommendations

# Good response to initial therapy followed by rapid rise in hCG level (hCG >=1000), or poor response to initial therapy

- Change from single-agent systemic therapy to combination EMA-CO
    - See Systemic Therapy for GTN (GTN-D) for specific recommendations
- and Repeat workup to check for metastasis
- and Recalculate WHO score based on imaging
- and Consider hysterectomy with salpingectomy
- Monitoring during treatment
    - hCG assay every 2 weeks, at the start of each treatment cycle
    - Principles of Gynecologic Survivorship (GTN-E)
- Response assessment
    - Normal hCG levels
        - Continue systemic therapy for 2–3 treatment cycles past normalization
    - hCG level plateaus (<10% change) for 2 treatment cycles, or hCG level rises (>10% change) for 1 treatment cycle
        - Administer platinum-based regimens
        - Consider surgical resection if feasible
        - See Systemic Therapy for GTN (GTN-D) for specific recommendations

# Imaging after treatment

- Post-treatment imaging is not routinely recommended for follow-up after hCG normalization in patients with post-molar GTN or choriocarcinoma, where hCG is a reliable tumor marker
- It may be necessary to follow patients with brain metastases with MRI brain with contrast for 2–3 years
- See Principles of Imaging (GTN-B)
