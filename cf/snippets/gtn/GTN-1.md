# Workup, unless previously done

- H&P
    - If visible lesions are seen in lower genital tract, do NOT biopsy, due to risk of hemorrhage
- Repeat CBC with differential and platelet count
- Repeat liver function tests
- Repeat renal function tests
- Repeat thyroid function tests
- Repeat chemistry profile
- Imaging, see Principles of Imaging (GTN-B)
- hCG assay
    - If hCG is elevated with no evidence of disease on imaging, consider the possibility of LH crossover or phantom hCG
    - Consult with laboratory medicine/pathology to test for phantom hCG, with serial dilution study or comparison of serum to urine hCG
    - If hCG is elevated, although at a low level that is stable over time, and false positive hCG ruled out, quiescent GTN is possible
        - Close follow-up, rather than immediate treatment, may be considered
- Determine FIGO stage and prognostic score
    - See FIGO Staging System for GTN (ST-1)
    - See Prognostic Scoring Index for GTN (ST-2)
- Histopathologic assessment, see Principles of Pathology (GTN-A)
- Assess for distress
    - Refer to the NCCN Distress Thermometer and Problem List, which includes social determinants of health
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Distress Management (DIS-A)

# Next

- Consider consultation with a clinician or center with expertise in management of gestational trophoblastic diseases
- Low-risk GTN, prognostic score <7, go to GTN-2
- High-risk GTN, prognostic score >=7, or Stage IV, go to GTN-4
- Intermediate trophoblastic tumor, go to GTN-5
    - PSTT
    - ETT
    - Prognostic scoring is not valid for intermediate tumors
