# Primary treatment

- Single-agent systemic therapy options
    - Methotrexate
    - Dactinomycin
    - See Systemic Therapy for GTN (GTN-D) for specific recommendations
    - Regimens are continued until 2–3 full cycles past normalization of the hCG
- or Hysterectomy with salpingectomy
    - Preferred when fertility preservation is not desired
    - Leave ovaries in situ, even in presence of theca lutein cysts
    - See Principles of Surgical Management (GTN-C)
    - Consider hysterectomy alone without further systemic therapy for patients without choriocarcinoma and a WHO score of <5
- or Second curettage for localized disease in the uterus
    - See Principles of Surgical Management (GTN-C)

# Monitoring during treatment

- hCG assay every 2 weeks, at the start of each treatment cycle

# Response assessment

- Good response to initial therapy
    - Normal hCG level
- Poor response to initial therapy
    - hCG level plateaus (<10% change) for 3 treatment cycles
    - or hCG level rises (>10% change) for 2 treatment cycles

# Follow-up/surveillance after normal hCG level

- See Principles of Gynecologic Survivorship (GTN-E)
- Continue systemic therapy for 2–3 treatment cycles past hCG normalization
- hCG assay every month for 12 months
- Contraception, oral contraceptives preferred
    - Oral contraceptives are preferred over IUDs because they suppress endogenous LH/FSH, which may interfere with hCG measurement at low levels

# Next

- Normal hCG level followed by rapid rise in hCG level (>10% change), go to Response assessment (GTN-3)
