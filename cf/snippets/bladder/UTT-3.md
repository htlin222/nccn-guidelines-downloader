# Workup

- Imaging of upper tract collecting system
    - Principles of Imaging for Bladder/Urothelial Cancer (BL-A)
- Cytology
- Cystoscopy
- Tissue diagnosis
    - Ureteroscopy and biopsy
    - or percutaneous biopsy
    - and/or selective washings
- Renal function tests
- Nuclear medicine renal scan (optional)
- Chest x-ray or CT
- CBC, chemistry profile
- Bone scan, if clinical suspicion or symptoms of bone metastases
    - Principles of Imaging for Bladder/Urothelial Cancer (BL-A)
- Consider germline (constitutional) MGPT and genetic counselor referral
    - If younger age at presentation
    - and/or personal or family history of Lynch syndrome-related cancers
    - See Criteria for the Evaluation of Lynch Syndrome Based on Personal or Family History of Cancer in the NCCN Guidelines for Genetic/Familial High-Risk Assessment: Colorectal, Endometrial, and Gastric

# Primary treatment, upper ureter

- Nephroureterectomy with cuff of bladder
    - Regional lymphadenectomy if high grade (BL-B)
    - With or without perioperative intravesical chemotherapy, Principles of Instillation Therapy (BL-F)
    - Consider neoadjuvant chemotherapy in selected patients, Principles of Systemic Therapy (BL-G 1 of 8)
- or Endoscopic resection, for low grade (BL-B)

# Primary treatment, mid ureter

- Endoscopic resection, for low grade (BL-B)
- or Nephroureterectomy with cuff of bladder
    - With or without perioperative intravesical chemotherapy, Principles of Instillation Therapy (BL-F)
    - For high-grade disease (BL-B), add regional lymphadenectomy and consider neoadjuvant chemotherapy in selected patients
- or Excision and ureteroureterostomy/ileal ureter, in highly selected patients
    - With or without perioperative intravesical chemotherapy, Principles of Instillation Therapy (BL-F)

# Primary treatment, distal ureter

- Distal ureterectomy
    - Regional lymphadenectomy if high grade (BL-B)
    - Reimplantation of ureter, preferred if clinically feasible
    - With or without perioperative intravesical chemotherapy, Principles of Instillation Therapy (BL-F)
    - Consider neoadjuvant chemotherapy in selected patients, Principles of Systemic Therapy (BL-G 1 of 8)
- or Endoscopic resection, for low grade (BL-B)
- or Nephroureterectomy with cuff of bladder
    - Regional lymphadenectomy if high grade (BL-B)
    - With or without perioperative intravesical chemotherapy, Principles of Instillation Therapy (BL-F)
    - Consider neoadjuvant chemotherapy in selected patients, Principles of Systemic Therapy (BL-G 1 of 8)

# Endoscopic resection and mitomycin ureteral gel

- Complete or near complete endoscopic resection or ablation is recommended prior to mitomycin ureteral gel application
- Mitomycin ureteral gel is most suitably indicated for a tumor that is
    - Residual
    - Low-grade
    - Low-volume (5-15 mm)
    - Solitary
    - In the upper urinary tract
    - In a patient who is not a candidate for, or not seeking, nephroureterectomy as a definitive treatment
- Mitomycin for pyelocaliceal application may be administered via ureteral catheter or a nephrostomy tube

# Primary treatment, metastatic

- Systemic therapy
    - Principles of Systemic Therapy (BL-G 3 of 8, BL-G 4 of 8, and BL-G 5 of 8)
