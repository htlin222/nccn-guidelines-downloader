# Initial evaluation

- History and physical (H&P)
- Consider germline (constitutional) multigene panel testing (MGPT) and genetic counselor referral
    - If younger age at presentation
    - And/or if personal or family history of Lynch syndrome-related cancers
    - See Criteria for the Evaluation of Lynch Syndrome Based on Personal or Family History of Cancer in the NCCN Guidelines for Genetic/Familial High-Risk Assessment: Colorectal, Endometrial, and Gastric
- Office cystoscopy
- Consider cytology
- Abdomen/pelvis imaging
    - Must include imaging of the upper urinary tract collecting system
    - Do it before transurethral resection of bladder tumor (TURBT)
    - Principles of Imaging for Bladder/Urothelial Cancer (BL-A)
- Screen and actively promote smoking cessation
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Smoking Cessation

# Primary evaluation and surgical treatment

- Examination under anesthesia (EUA), bimanual
- TURBT
    - Principles of Surgical Management (BL-B)
- Single-dose intravesical chemotherapy within 24 hours of TURBT
    - Gemcitabine (category 1, preferred) or Mitomycin (category 1)
    - Immediate intravesical chemotherapy reduces the recurrence rate by 35% for selected patients
    - Most efficacious in patients with low-grade, low-volume Ta urothelial cancer
    - Do not use post-TURBT intravesical chemotherapy if concern for bladder perforation
    - See Principles of Instillation Therapy (BL-F)
- Imaging of upper tract collecting system, if not previously done
    - Principles of Imaging for Bladder/Urothelial Cancer (BL-A)

# Presumptive clinical stage

- Principles of Pathology Management (BL-C)
- Bladder Cancer: Non-Urothelial and Urothelial with Subtype Histology (BL-D)
- The modifier "c" refers to clinical staging based on bimanual EUA, endoscopic surgery (biopsy or transurethral resection [TUR]), and imaging studies
- The modifier "p" refers to pathologic staging based on cystectomy and lymph node dissection

# Additional staging workup for muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC)

- Complete blood count (CBC)
- Chemistry profile, including alkaline phosphatase
- Chest imaging
    - Principles of Imaging for Bladder/Urothelial Cancer (BL-A)
- Bone imaging, if clinical suspicion or symptoms of bone metastases
    - Principles of Imaging for Bladder/Urothelial Cancer (BL-A)
