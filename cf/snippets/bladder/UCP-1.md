# Workup

- Digital rectal examination (DRE)
- Cystoscopy
    - Including bladder biopsy
- TUR biopsies of prostate
    - To include stroma
- Prostate-specific antigen (PSA)
- Needle biopsy, if DRE is abnormal
    - In selected patients
- Imaging of upper tract collecting system
    - Principles of Imaging for Bladder/Urothelial Cancer (BL-A)

# Mucosal prostatic urethra

- Primary treatment: TURP and BCG
    - Principles of Surgical Management (BL-B)
- Follow-up imaging
    - Principles of Imaging for Bladder/Urothelial Cancer (BL-A)
- Local recurrence: cystoprostatectomy, with or without urethrectomy

# Ductal + acini

- Additional workup: chest x-ray or CT, with or without abdomen/pelvis CT
    - Principles of Imaging for Bladder/Urothelial Cancer (BL-A)
- Primary treatment: cystoprostatectomy, with or without urethrectomy
    - or TURP and BCG
    - Principles of Surgical Management (BL-B)
- Follow-up imaging
    - Principles of Imaging for Bladder/Urothelial Cancer (BL-A)
- Local recurrence: cystoprostatectomy, with or without urethrectomy

# Stromal invasion

- Additional workup: chest x-ray or CT, and abdomen/pelvis CT
    - Principles of Imaging for Bladder/Urothelial Cancer (BL-A)
- Primary treatment: cystoprostatectomy, with or without urethrectomy, plus neoadjuvant chemotherapy
    - Principles of Surgical Management (BL-B)
    - Principles of Systemic Therapy (BL-G 1 of 8)
- Consider adjuvant chemotherapy, if neoadjuvant not given
    - Principles of Systemic Therapy (BL-G 1 of 8)

# Metastatic

- Systemic therapy
    - See Principles of Systemic Therapy (BL-G 3 of 8, BL-G 4 of 8, and BL-G 5 of 8)
