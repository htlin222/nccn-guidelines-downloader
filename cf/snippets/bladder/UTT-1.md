# Workup

- Imaging of upper tract collecting system
    - Principles of Imaging for Bladder/Urothelial Cancer (BL-A)
- Cytology
- Cystoscopy
- Ureteroscopy and biopsy
    - or percutaneous biopsy
    - and/or selective washings
- Renal function tests
    - If comparative indication to nephron-sparing approach, see UTT-2
- Chest x-ray or CT
- CBC
- Chemistry profile
- Nuclear medicine renal scan (optional)
- Bone scan, if clinical suspicion or symptoms of bone metastases
    - Principles of Imaging for Bladder/Urothelial Cancer (BL-A)
- Consider germline (constitutional) MGPT and genetic counselor referral
    - If younger age at presentation
    - and/or personal or family history of Lynch syndrome-related cancers
    - See Criteria for the Evaluation of Lynch Syndrome Based on Personal or Family History of Cancer in the NCCN Guidelines for Genetic/Familial High-Risk Assessment: Colorectal, Endometrial, and Gastric

# Next

- Primary treatment follows Principles of Surgical Management (BL-B)
- Non-metastatic, go to UTT-2
- Metastatic, systemic therapy
    - See Principles of Systemic Therapy (BL-G 3 of 8, BL-G 4 of 8, and BL-G 5 of 8)
- Adjuvant treatment and follow-up (UTT-4)
