- Applies to metastatic disease, stage IVB (Any T, Any N, M1b)
- Imaging throughout follows Principles of Imaging for Bladder/Urothelial Cancer (BL-A)
- All recommendations are category 2A unless otherwise indicated

    - Metastatic, stage IVB (Any T, Any N, M1b)
    - The modifier "c" refers to clinical staging based on bimanual EUA, endoscopic surgery (biopsy or TUR), and imaging studies
    - The modifier "p" refers to pathologic staging based on cystectomy and lymph node dissection
- Clinical suspicion or symptoms of bone metastases: ___ (bone_symptoms)
- Estimated GFR ___ (gfr)

# Additional workup

- Bone scan or MRI, if clinical suspicion or symptoms of bone metastases
- Chest CT
- Consider central nervous system (CNS) imaging
    - Per Principles of Imaging for Bladder/Urothelial Cancer (BL-A)
- Estimate GFR to assess eligibility for cisplatin
    - For patients with borderline GFR, consider timed urine collection, which may more accurately determine eligibility for cisplatin
- Consider biopsy if technically feasible
- Molecular testing
    - Per Principles of Alternative Risk Classifiers and Biomarkers (BL-I)

# Primary treatment

- Systemic therapy
    - Principles of Systemic Therapy (BL-G 3 of 8)
    - See also Principles of Systemic Therapy (BL-G 4 of 8 and BL-G 5 of 8)
- And/or palliative RT
    - Per Principles of Radiation Management of Invasive Disease (BL-H)
