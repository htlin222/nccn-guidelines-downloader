# Follow-up

- Muscle-invasive or selected metastatic disease treated with curative intent
    - Follow-up (BL-E)
    - Imaging per Principles of Imaging for Bladder/Urothelial Cancer (BL-A)

# Treatment of muscle-invasive local recurrence or persistent disease, preserved bladder

- Cystectomy
    - Per Principles of Surgical Management (BL-B)
    - Follow-Up (BL-E)
- Or chemoradiotherapy, only if no prior RT
    - Per Principles of Systemic Therapy (BL-G 6 of 8)
    - Per Principles of Radiation Management of Invasive Disease (BL-H)
- Or systemic therapy
    - Per Principles of Systemic Therapy (BL-G 3 of 8)
    - See also Principles of Systemic Therapy (BL-G 4 of 8 and BL-G 5 of 8)
- Or palliative TURBT and best supportive care
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Palliative Care

# Treatment of recurrent or persistent CIS, Ta, or T1, preserved bladder

- Consider intravesical therapy
    - Per Principles of Instillation Therapy (BL-F)
- Or cystectomy
    - Per Principles of Surgical Management (BL-B)
    - Follow-Up (BL-E)
- Or TURBT
- If no response
    - Cystectomy, per Principles of Surgical Management (BL-B), Follow-Up (BL-E)
    - If not a cystectomy candidate, consider concurrent chemoradiotherapy per Principles of Systemic Therapy (BL-G 6 of 8), only if no prior RT
    - If not a cystectomy candidate, consider change in intravesical agent
    - If not a cystectomy candidate, consider a clinical trial
