# Stage I primary treatment

- Radiation therapy (RT), preferred
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy (VAG-C)
    - Intracavitary brachytherapy alone
        - Only in select favorable cases with small lesions <2 cm and limited thickness (5 mm or less)
    - External beam RT (EBRT) + intracavitary brachytherapy
        - Use interstitial brachytherapy if >0.5 cm residual thickness at brachytherapy
        - With or without concurrent platinum-containing chemotherapy
            - May not be suitable for all patients
            - Use with caution in patients who are older, frail, and/or have multiple comorbidities
- or Surgical resection
    - With or without lymph node assessment
    - Considered only for select cases
    - Principles of Surgery (VAG-E)

# Stage II-IVA primary treatment

- Chemoradiation + brachytherapy, preferred
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy (VAG-C)
    - Chemoradiation may not be suitable for all patients
        - Use with caution in patients who are older, frail, and/or have multiple comorbidities
    - Concurrent chemotherapy has been shown in many series to improve outcomes, and is often used in stage II-IV disease
    - Concurrent platinum-containing chemotherapy with EBRT uses cisplatin as a single agent
        - Carboplatin if cisplatin intolerant
        - Principles of Systemic Therapy (VAG-D)
- or EBRT + brachytherapy
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy (VAG-C)
