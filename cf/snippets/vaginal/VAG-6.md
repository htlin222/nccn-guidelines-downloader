# Source

- NCCN Vaginal Cancer v2.2026, VAG-6, p12
- Applies to stage IVB disease, or recurrent disease with distant metastases
- Treatment branches on whether the distant disease is limited or disseminated
- All recommendations are category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Limited disease

- Systemic therapy (VAG-D)
    - Consider additional testing, see Principles of Pathology (VAG-A 2 of 2)
- Consider local treatment of the primary
    - Chemoradiation (VAG-C), per Systemic Therapy for Vaginal Cancer (VAG-D)
    - With or without brachytherapy (VAG-C)
- Consider local treatment of metastases
    - Surgery for select patients, per Principles of Surgery (VAG-E)
    - or Individualized EBRT (VAG-C)
    - or Local ablative therapies
        - eg, radiofrequency ablation
        - eg, cryoablation
        - eg, stereotactic body RT (SBRT)
- Best supportive care (NCCN Guidelines for Palliative Care)

# Disseminated disease

- Systemic therapy (VAG-D)
    - Consider additional testing, see Principles of Pathology (VAG-A 2 of 2)
    - With or without palliative RT
- And best supportive care (NCCN Guidelines for Palliative Care)
