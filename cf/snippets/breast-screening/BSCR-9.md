# Non-spontaneous or multi-duct discharge

- Spontaneous multi-duct discharge that is milky may be due to pregnancy, and if not, should be evaluated for galactorrhea
- Greenish, coffee-colored, yellow, brown/black multi-duct discharge is nearly always non-neoplastic, and reassurance can be provided
- However, if the discharge is bothersome to the patient, referral to a breast specialist may be considered
- Age <40 y
    - Observation
    - Educate to stop compression of the breast and report any spontaneous discharge
- Age ≥40 y
    - Screening mammogram with tomosynthesis if not done in the past year (BSCR-18)
        - Tomosynthesis can decrease call-back rates and improve cancer detection compared with 2D mammography alone
    - Educate to stop compression of the breast and report any spontaneous discharge

# Spontaneous, unilateral, single duct, clear or bloody, persistent and reproducible on examination

- Age <30 y
    - Ultrasound
    - With or without diagnostic mammogram with tomosynthesis
- Age ≥30 y
    - Diagnostic mammogram with tomosynthesis
    - Ultrasound

# Follow-up after imaging, BI-RADS category 1–3

- Assessment Category Definitions (BSCR-C)
- If the BI-RADS category 3 finding is unrelated to the nipple discharge, manage the mammographic finding by BSCR-18
- Refer for surgical consultation, or
- MRI with and without contrast
- MRI BI-RADS category 1–3
    - 6-mo follow-up physical examination, with or without imaging, for 1–2 y
        - Instruct the patient to monitor for and report any changes
        - Imaging modality would depend on original imaging
        - Probably benign findings are typically monitored at 6, 12, and 24 months
    - Stable or resolves
        - Screening (BSCR-1)
        - Continue regular screening with age-appropriate imaging modality
    - Suspicious imaging abnormality or progression
        - Core needle biopsy (BSCR-15)
        - Surgical excision (BSCR-16)
- MRI BI-RADS category 4–5
    - Core needle biopsy (BSCR-15)
    - Surgical excision (BSCR-16)
    - Benign
        - Duct excision, based on clinical suspicion and patient preference
    - Malignant
        - See NCCN Guidelines for Breast Cancer

# Follow-up after imaging, BI-RADS category 4–5

- Assessment Category Definitions (BSCR-C)
- Core needle biopsy (BSCR-15)
- Surgical excision (BSCR-16) if not amenable to core needle biopsy
- Benign
    - Consider surgical consultation for duct excision, based on clinical suspicion and patient preference
- Malignant
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Breast Cancer
