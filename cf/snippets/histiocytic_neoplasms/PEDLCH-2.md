# Source

- Applies to unifocal LCH and to single-system LCH, by presentation site
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

- Sentinel lymph node changes are not another system involved
    - Enlargement alone is not another system involved
    - Hypermetabolic activity on FDG-PET imaging alone is not another system involved
    - Counts as another system only if histologically confirmed by lymph node biopsy
        - CD1a+ histiocytes
        - Characteristic immuno-architecture
- Critical organ involvement checked
    - Liver
    - Spleen
    - Bone marrow
    - CNS
    - Primary GI tract
- Smoking and cannabis use: ___ (smoking_cannabis)

# Isolated bone involvement, unifocal LCH

- Observation, if asymptomatic
- Limited curettage, with or without intralesional steroid infiltration
    - Triamcinolone injection or equivalent corticosteroid
- Bisphosphonate (HIST-D)
- Indomethacin
- Systemic therapy for CNS-risk lesions and special sites (HIST-D)
- For single site bone lesions with symptomatic extra-skeletal component, RT or systemic therapy may be warranted
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy (HIST-G)

# Skin disease, including multifocal skin LCH

- Observation
    - For small areas with no inflammatory changes
- Topical middle-to-high potency steroids
    - For larger areas, or for lesions with inflammatory changes
- Intralesional steroid infiltration
    - Triamcinolone injection or equivalent corticosteroid
    - For nodular lesions
- Low-dose oral methotrexate, with or without leucovorin rescue
- Psoralen plus UVA
- Narrowband UVB
- Surgical excision

# Single system involving risk organ or pulmonary LCH

- Systemic therapy (HIST-D)
- Provide resources for cessation of smoking, if applicable
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Smoking Cessation
- Provide resources for cessation of cannabis use, if applicable

# Other single-system LCH not involving critical organs

- Applies to lymph nodes and soft tissue
- Critical organs are liver, spleen, bone marrow, CNS, primary GI tract
- Observation
- Local therapy
    - Surgical excision
    - RT
        - Principles of Radiation Therapy (HIST-G)
- Systemic therapy in symptomatic cases (HIST-D)
