# Asymptomatic

- Asymptomatic, and no impending organ dysfunction, and no critical organ involvement
    - Observation

# Symptomatic, impending organ dysfunction, or critical organ involvement

- Multisystem and/or CNS involvement
    - Systemic therapy (HIST-D)
    - For neurodegenerative LCH, clinical findings may not be accompanied by imaging changes
    - Evaluate and monitor cognitive symptoms carefully
    - Consider early treatment if cognitive decline is evident
- Multifocal bone disease
    - Bisphosphonate (HIST-D)
    - RT, see Principles of Radiation Therapy (HIST-G)
    - Systemic therapy (HIST-D)
- Single-system pulmonary LCH
    - Smoking cessation
        - Provide resources for cessation of smoking, see NCCN Guidelines for Smoking Cessation
        - Provide resources for cessation of cannabis use
    - Observation
    - Prednisone
    - Systemic therapy (HIST-D)
    - Lung transplant for severe or refractory cases
