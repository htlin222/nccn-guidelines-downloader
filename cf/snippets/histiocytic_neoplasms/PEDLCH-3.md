# Source

- NCCN Histiocytic Neoplasms v2.2026, PEDLCH-3, p19
- Applies to pediatric Langerhans cell histiocytosis with any of these presentations
    - Multisystem LCH
    - Unifocal LCH that progresses on local therapy
    - Unifocal LCH involving critical organs (CNS, liver, spleen, bone marrow)
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Multifocal single-system LCH, asymptomatic

- All three must hold
    - Asymptomatic
    - NO impending organ dysfunction
    - NO critical organ involvement
- Observation
- or Systemic therapy (select patients) (HIST-D)

# Multifocal single-system LCH, symptomatic or with organ dysfunction

- Any of these
    - Symptomatic
    - Impending organ dysfunction
    - Critical organ involvement
- Multifocal bone LCH
    - Bisphosphonate (HIST-D)
    - Limited curettage, with or without intralesional steroid infiltration
        - Triamcinolone injection or equivalent corticosteroid
    - Indomethacin
    - Systemic therapy (HIST-D)

# Multisystem LCH, with or without CNS involvement

- Systemic therapy (HIST-D)
