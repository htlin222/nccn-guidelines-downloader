# Unifocal RDD

- Asymptomatic
    - Observation
- Symptomatic, potentially resectable
    - Surgical resection
- Symptomatic, unresectable (bulky/site of disease)
    - RT
        - Principles of Radiation Therapy (HIST-G)
    - Systemic therapy (HIST-D)

# Multifocal RDD

- Asymptomatic
    - Observation
- Symptomatic
    - Systemic therapy (HIST-D)
    - Palliative RT for symptomatic disease not responsive to systemic therapy
        - Principles of Radiation Therapy (HIST-G)

# Follow-up

- Principles of Supportive Care (HIST-E)
- Treatment response assessment
    - Imaging of involved sites
        - FDG-PET/CT scan (preferred)
        - or contrast-enhanced CT/MRI
    - After 2-3 cycles of systemic therapy and at completion
    - After completion of surgical resection
    - After RT
        - Principles of Radiation Therapy (HIST-G)
- Surveillance of patients on observation or in remission
    - H&P and labs at least annually or more frequently as clinically indicated
    - FDG-PET/CT scan no more than every 6 months after starting therapy until stabilization of the disease
    - Organ-specific imaging (contrast-enhanced CT/MRI)
        - No more than every 6 months until disease stabilization
        - Then every 6-12 months
    - For patients who are symptomatic with a single-site bone lesion, imaging surveillance can potentially end after year 1, with continued tracking of symptoms

# Next

- Relapsed/refractory disease
    - Systemic therapy (HIST-D)
    - If duration of response >1 year, consider same regimen
    - Otherwise use a regimen not used for first-line therapy
