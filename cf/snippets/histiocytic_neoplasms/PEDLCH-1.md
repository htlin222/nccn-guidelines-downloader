# Essential

- H&P with attention to common sites of involvement
    - Common sites: bone, bone marrow, skin, GI system, lymph node, liver, spleen, oral mucosa, lung, CNS
    - Characteristic Features of Histiocytic Neoplasms (HIST-B)
    - Principles of Diagnostic Evaluation (HIST-A)
- Skeletal radiographic survey to detect skeletal lesions
- Laboratory evaluation
    - Principles of Diagnostic Evaluation (HIST-A)
    - If unexplained cytopenia or anemia, consider bone marrow biopsy, to assess for involvement with LCH, hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis, or other concurrent myeloid neoplasms
- Tissue biopsy (PEDLCH-1A)
    - Principles of Pathology (HIST-C)

# Useful under certain circumstances, based on symptoms or organ involvement

- Whole-body FDG-PET/CT or FDG-PET/MRI (vertex to toes), can be used if available
    - If a concern is present for multisystem LCH (MS-LCH) with risk organ involvement
    - If disease reactivation is suspected
- Contrast-enhanced CT/MRI for assessment of other organ system involvement
    - Low-dose CT (LDCT) of the chest for suspected pulmonary and thymus LCH
    - US for suspected liver/spleen LCH
    - US for suspected lymph node involvement based on abnormal physical examination findings
- Contrast-enhanced brain MRI (white matter, pituitary, and sella turcica), to rule out pituitary gland involvement and neurodegenerative CNS LCH
    - New onset isolated AVP deficiency, previously known as diabetes insipidus
    - CNS-risk bone lesions (mastoid and temporal bones; bones forming orbit)
    - MS-LCH with high-risk organ involvement
    - MRI of the spine is optional, and should be conducted if neurological symptoms are present or other imaging shows vertebral bone lesion

# Referrals and confirmatory testing

- Refer to other subspecialties, based on the clinical pattern
    - Dental
    - Dermatology
    - Endocrinology
    - Ear, nose, and throat (ENT)
    - Neurology
    - Neuropsychology
    - Neurosurgery
    - Ophthalmology
    - Orthopedics
    - Psychosocial support
    - Rehabilitation services
- If necessary for diagnosis confirmation, other testing may include
    - PFTs
    - Echocardiogram
    - ERCP
    - Electrical auditory brainstem response (E-ABR)
