# Confirm this is the right branch

- HIV-1(-)/HHV8(-) idiopathic MCD, nonsevere, stay on this page
- HHV8(+) MCD, go to CD-4B
    - All patients with HIV infection should be on combination antiretroviral therapy (cART)
    - For principles of concurrent HIV management and supportive care, see the NCCN Guidelines for Cancer in People with HIV
- Fulminant/severe, go to CD-5
    - Severe disease is defined as any of the following
    - ECOG performance status of at least 2
    - Stage IV kidney dysfunction, that is estimated glomerular filtration rate under 30 mL/min/1.73 m2, or creatinine over 3 mg/dL
    - Anasarca and/or ascites, pleural effusion, or pericardial effusion
    - Hemoglobin 8 g/dL or lower
    - Pulmonary involvement/interstitial pneumonitis with dyspnea

# First-line therapy

- Suggested treatment regimens: first-line therapy (CD-C 1 of 3)
- For the management of complications associated with anti-CD20 mAb therapy, see Supportive Care for B-Cell Lymphomas in the NCCN Guidelines for B-Cell Lymphomas

# Response assessment

- Assess response using the imaging modalities performed during workup
    - C/A/P CT with contrast, or
    - PET/CT

# Next

- Response, then continue on therapy as follows
    - If siltuximab, continue until progression
    - If rituximab-based time-limited therapy, observe
- Relapsed disease after a response, go to alternate first-line therapy (CD-C 1 of 3) before moving onto treatment for relapsed/refractory or progressive disease
    - Encourage biopsy to rule out transformation to DLBCL, or concomitant development of other malignancies or opportunistic infections
    - Rituximab with or without prednisone may be repeated without limit if progression is 6 months or more after completion of rituximab
- No response, go to alternate first-line therapy (CD-C 1 of 3) before moving onto treatment for relapsed/refractory or progressive disease
    - Encourage biopsy to rule out transformation to DLBCL, or concomitant development of other malignancies or opportunistic infections
    - Response assessed with the imaging modalities performed during workup
- After alternate first-line therapy, relapsed/refractory or progressive disease, go to CD-6
