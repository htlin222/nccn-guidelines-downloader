# Assessment

- ___ (age) yo, multicentric Castleman disease, relapsed/refractory or progressive
- HHV8 ___ (hhv8)
- HHV8(+): criteria for active disease present
- Prior therapy already given ___ (prior_regimens)
- Encourage biopsy, to rule out
    - transformation to DLBCL
    - concomitant development of other malignancies
    - opportunistic infections
- Fulminant/severe disease, defined as any of
    - ECOG performance status ___ (ecog), severe if 2 or higher
    - Stage IV kidney dysfunction
        - estimated glomerular filtration rate ___ (egfr) mL/min/1.73 m2, severe if under 30
        - creatinine ___ (creatinine) mg/dL, severe if over 3 mg/dL
    - Anasarca and/or ascites
    - Pleural effusion
    - Pericardial effusion
    - Hemoglobin ___ (hgb) g/dL, severe if 8 g/dL or lower
    - Pulmonary involvement/interstitial pneumonitis with dyspnea
- Organ failure present or absent

# Kaposi sarcoma precautions

- Occult KS is prevalent in HIV/HHV8+ MCD and is likely to flare after rituximab or prednisone
- Consider baseline imaging and direct visualization, to screen for pulmonary and/or GI involvement
- Monitor closely for KS progression
- Continue KS-directed therapy for ~3 months post last rituximab, to minimize risk of KS flare, given long action of rituximab
- See NCCN Guidelines for Kaposi Sarcoma

# Second-line therapy

- HHV8(+) with criteria for active disease present and no organ failure
    - Suggested Treatment Regimens: Second-Line Therapy (CD-C 2 of 3)
- Fulminant/severe [HHV8(+) or HHV8(-)] disease with no organ failure
    - Suggested Treatment Regimens: Second-Line Therapy (CD-C 2 of 3)
- Fulminant/severe [HHV8(+) or HHV8(-)] disease with organ failure
    - Suggested Treatment Regimens: Second-Line Therapy (CD-C 2 of 3)
- Choosing the regimen
    - Single-agent therapy is preferred for asymptomatic patients with no organ failure
    - Combination therapy is preferred for patients with fulminant disease and organ failure

# Response assessment

- Use the imaging modalities performed during workup
    - C/A/P CT with contrast, or
    - PET/CT

# Next

- Response
    - Observation, or
    - Maintenance valganciclovir if HHV8(+)
- No response
    - Alternate second-line combination therapy with or without rituximab (CD-C 2 of 3) not previously given, before moving onto subsequent therapy
    - Encourage biopsy, to rule out transformation to DLBCL, concomitant other malignancies, or opportunistic infections
- Relapsed/refractory disease
    - Consider alternative single-agent or combination therapy
    - Suggested Treatment Regimens: Alternative Regimens for Subsequent Therapy (CD-C 2 of 3)
    - Encourage biopsy, to rule out transformation to DLBCL, concomitant other malignancies, or opportunistic infections
