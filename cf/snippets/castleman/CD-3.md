# First-line therapy, surgically resectable

- Complete resection
    - Observation
    - Recurrence, go to relapsed disease below
- Incomplete resection
    - Asymptomatic, observation
    - Symptomatic, see surgically unresectable below

# First-line therapy, surgically unresectable

- Involved-site RT (ISRT)
    - The optimal dose of ISRT in unresectable UCD is not established
    - Doses as high as 40 Gy in 1.5-2 Gy fractions have been used
    - Lower doses may be considered depending upon clinical circumstances (eg, proximity to critical structures, treatment intent)
    - Advanced radiation techniques (eg, intensity-modulated RT [IMRT], image-guided RT [IGRT], protons) are recommended to limit dose to surrounding normal tissues
    - Normal tissue dose constraints are in the Principles of Radiation Therapy section of the NCCN Guidelines for Hodgkin Lymphoma
    - Patients with non-bulky disease may be observed after ISRT
- or Suggested Treatment Regimens: First-line Therapy (CD-C 1 of 3)
- or Consider embolization
- or Observation in asymptomatic patients

# Relapsed disease

- Surgically resectable
    - Complete resection
        - Observation
        - Recurrence, go to relapsed/refractory disease below
    - Incomplete resection
        - Alternate first-line therapy not previously given (CD-C 1 of 3)
- Surgically unresectable
    - Alternate first-line therapy not previously given (CD-C 1 of 3)

# Relapsed/refractory disease

- Encourage biopsy to rule out transformation to DLBCL, concomitant development of other malignancies, or opportunistic infections
- Consider surgery
- or ISRT, dose and technique as above
    - Patients with non-bulky disease may be observed after ISRT
- or embolization if amenable
- or Suggested Treatment Regimens: Second-line and Subsequent Therapy (CD-C 2 of 3)
