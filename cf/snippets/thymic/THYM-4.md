# Source

- NCCN Thymomas and Thymic Carcinomas v2.2026, THYM-4, p9
- Applies to recurrent, advanced, or metastatic disease
- All patients should be treated by a multidisciplinary team with experience in the management of thymomas and thymic carcinomas
- All recommendations are category 2A unless otherwise indicated

- Resectability ___ (resectability)
    - Determination of resectability should be made by a thoracic surgeon, with primary focus on thoracic oncology and in multidisciplinary consultation as needed
    - Resectability is defined as complete (R0) resection
- Thymoma: evaluated clinically for signs of myasthenia gravis and other paraneoplastic syndromes ___ (paraneoplastic)
    - Appropriate workup and treatment if present

# Surveillance

- Chest CT with contrast
    - MRI is an appropriate alternative to CT in certain clinical situations
- FDG-PET/CT as clinically indicated
    - Includes skull-base to mid-thigh
- Thymic carcinoma: chest CT with contrast every 3–6 mo for 2 y, then annually until year 5
- Thymoma: chest CT with contrast every 6 mo for 2 y, then annually until year 10
- The duration for surveillance has not been established

# Locally advanced disease

- Unresectable
    - Concurrent chemoradiation, see Principles of Radiation Therapy (THYM-B) and Principles of Systemic Therapy (THYM-C)
- Potentially resectable
    - Systemic therapy (THYM-C)

# Reassessment after initial therapy

- Resectable
    - Surgery, see Principles of Surgical Resection (THYM-A)
- Unresectable
    - Consider systemic therapy (THYM-C) or RT (THYM-B)
    - RT (THYM-B) ± chemotherapy (THYM-C)

# Solitary metastasis or ipsilateral pleural metastasis

- Resectable
    - Surgical resection of primary tumor and isolated metastases (THYM-A)
    - Consider postoperative RT (THYM-B)
- Medically inoperable or unresectable
    - Consider local therapy, which can include image-guided thermal ablation or RT (THYM-B)
    - or systemic therapy (THYM-C)
    - or consider observation

# Evidence of extrathoracic metastases

- Systemic therapy (THYM-C)
