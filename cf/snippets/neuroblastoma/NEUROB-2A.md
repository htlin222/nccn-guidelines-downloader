# Factors required for risk assignment

- Pathology reviewed by a pathologist with expertise in classification of neuroblastoma
- INRG stage assigned using the INRG Staging Criteria (ST-1)
- DI
    - If information about DI is missing, assign risk based on other available factors
    - Ploidy may be inferred by next-generation sequencing (NGS)

# When biopsy cannot be done first

- Applies to infants in whom safety considerations preclude biopsy
    - Coagulopathy
    - Impending organ failure
    - Especially those with hepatomegaly leading to respiratory compromise
- Symptomatic patients should start intermediate-risk treatment emergently prior to biopsy
    - See Hsu LL, et al. Med Pediatr Oncol 1996;27:521-528 for symptoms
    - A total score ≥2 should lead to emergent therapy
- Once symptoms have resolved following initial therapy, patients should undergo biopsy
    - Risk assignment should be revised based on available data

# Practice variation outside North America

- Cooperative groups outside North America may treat these patients as intermediate risk
- Cooperative groups outside North America may treat all patients with INRG Stage M disease ≥12 months as high risk
