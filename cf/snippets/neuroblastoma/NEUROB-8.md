# Induction therapy

- Initial cytoreductive chemotherapy
    - Chemotherapy Induction Regimens (NEUROB-D 7 of 13)
    - Principles of Systemic Therapy (NEUROB-D)
- Stem cell harvest
- Resection of primary tumor and locoregional disease
    - Principles of Surgery (NEUROB-E)
    - Anatomic imaging of primary site prior to surgery
    - Subtotal resection if gross total will jeopardize vital organs, major nerves, and/or major vessels
