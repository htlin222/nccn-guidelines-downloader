# Group assignment

- Special high-risk groups include
    - Stage L2, and
        - Age 18 months or older, and
        - MYCN non-amplified, and
        - UH
    - Stage M, and
        - Age 12 to <18 months, and
        - MYCN non-amplified, and
        - Any of the following unfavorable features
            - UH
            - Diploid DNA content
            - Presence of SCAs
- Common high-risk groups include all other categories not included as part of special high-risk groups

# Consolidation therapy, common high-risk group

- Tandem high-dose chemotherapy with autologous stem cell rescue (category 1)
    - Principles of Systemic Therapy (NEUROB-D)
    - Non-myeloablative approaches have been evaluated in single-institution studies and may be appropriate in select patients
    - Single transplant is currently standard in Europe
- RT to primary site and sites of residual metastatic disease detected at end of induction
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy (NEUROB-H)

# Consolidation therapy, special high-risk group

- Single high-dose chemotherapy with autologous stem cell rescue
    - Principles of Systemic Therapy (NEUROB-D)
    - Non-myeloablative approaches have been evaluated in single-institution studies and may be appropriate in select patients
    - Single transplant is currently standard in Europe
- RT to primary site and sites of residual metastatic disease detected at end of induction
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy (NEUROB-H)
