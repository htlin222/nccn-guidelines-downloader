# Stage L1

- Obtain MIBG scan to confirm localized disease
    - No MIBG scan required if the patient is <6 months of age with adrenal tumor, unless signs and symptoms are suggestive of metastatic disease
- Infants <6 months with isolated adrenal masses <5 cm in diameter
    - Observation
        - Use ultrasound (US) for surveillance when clinically indicated and appropriate
- Other
    - Surgical resection
        - Obtain cross-sectional imaging to delineate new baseline disease status at least 1 month postoperatively, then transition to US for surveillance if possible
        - If resection is incomplete and MYCN amplification is detected, see Risk Classification (NEUROB-2)

# Stage MS

- Asymptomatic with favorable biology
    - Observation
        - Use ultrasound (US) for surveillance when clinically indicated and appropriate
        - Disease Surveillance/Follow-up (NEUROB-F)

# After resection

- New baseline imaging no sooner than 1 month after surgery
    - Disease Surveillance/Follow-up (NEUROB-F)
