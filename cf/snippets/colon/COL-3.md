# Source

- NCCN Colon Cancer v2.2026, COL-3, p12
- Applies to pMMR/MSS colon cancer appropriate for resection (nonmetastatic)
- Imaging for primary and adjuvant treatment decisions: Principles of Imaging (COL-A)
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- ___ (age) yo, colon cancer at ___ (site)
- Mismatch repair / microsatellite status ___ (mmr_status), confirmed pMMR/MSS
- ___ (ctnm)
- Nonmetastatic and appropriate for resection
    - For tools to aid in optimal assessment and care of older adults with cancer, see the NCCN Guidelines for Older Adult Oncology
- Counseled for family history and considered for risk assessment
    - For suspected LS, FAP, and attenuated FAP, see the NCCN Guidelines for Genetic/Familial High-Risk Assessment: Colorectal, Endometrial, and Gastric

# Findings and primary treatment

- Resectable, nonobstructing
    - Colectomy with en bloc removal of regional lymph nodes
        - Principles of Surgery and Locoregional Therapies (COL-C)
- Resectable, obstructing
    - One-stage colectomy with en bloc removal of regional lymph nodes (COL-C)
    - or Resection with diversion
    - or Diversion
    - or Stent (in selected cases)
- Clinical T4b or bulky nodal disease
    - Consider neoadjuvant therapy
        - FOLFOX or CAPEOX
        - or Systemic therapy (COL-D) and consider radiation therapy (RT) with or without infusional fluorouracil or capecitabine prior to surgery
            - Principles of Radiation and Chemoradiation Therapy (COL-E)
    - Then colectomy with en bloc removal of regional lymph nodes (COL-C)
- Locally unresectable or medically inoperable
    - Systemic therapy (COL-D)
    - Re-evaluate for conversion to resectable disease
        - Principles of Imaging (COL-A)
        - Principles of Surgery and Locoregional Therapies (COL-C)
    - Surgery with or without intraoperative RT (IORT)
        - Principles of Radiation and Chemoradiation Therapy (COL-E)

# Adjuvant treatment

- Up to 6 months of perioperative treatment in total
- Imaging per Principles of Imaging (COL-A)
- FOLFOX (preferred)
- or CAPEOX (preferred)
- or Capecitabine
- or Fluorouracil/leucovorin
