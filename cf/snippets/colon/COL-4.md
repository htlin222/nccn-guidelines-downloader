# High-risk factors for recurrence in stage II disease

- Historical high-risk factors, exclusive of those cancers that are MSI-H
    - Poorly differentiated/undifferentiated histology
    - Lymphatic/vascular invasion
    - Bowel obstruction
    - <12 lymph nodes examined
    - Perineural invasion (PNI)
    - Localized perforation
    - Close, indeterminate, or positive margins
    - High-tier tumor budding
- In patients with high-risk, stage II disease, there are no data that correlate risk features and selection of chemotherapy
- There are insufficient data to recommend the use of multigene assay panels to determine adjuvant therapy

# Adjuvant treatment by pathologic stage

- Tis; T1, N0, M0; T2, N0, M0
    - Observation
- T3, N0, M0, no high-risk features
    - Observation (preferred)
    - or consider capecitabine (6 mo)
    - or fluorouracil/leucovorin (6 mo)
- T3, N0, M0 at high risk for systemic recurrence, or T4, N0, M0
    - Capecitabine (6 mo)
    - or fluorouracil/leucovorin (6 mo)
    - or FOLFOX (6 mo)
    - or CAPEOX (3 mo)
    - or Observation
    - Consider RT for T4 with penetration to a fixed structure (COL-E)
    - A survival benefit has not been demonstrated for the addition of oxaliplatin to fluorouracil/leucovorin in stage II colon cancer
- T1–3, N1 (low-risk stage III)
    - Preferred: CAPEOX (3 mo)
    - Preferred: FOLFOX (3–6 mo)
    - Other options include capecitabine (6 mo)
    - Other options include fluorouracil/leucovorin (6 mo)
- T4, N1–2; T Any, N2 (high-risk stage III)
    - Preferred: CAPEOX (3–6 mo)
    - Preferred: FOLFOX (6 mo)
    - Other options include capecitabine (6 mo)
    - Other options include fluorouracil/leucovorin (6 mo)
    - Consider RT for T4 with penetration to a fixed structure (COL-E)

# Aspirin for somatic PI3K pathway alterations

- For stage II and III, if somatic PI3K pathway alterations are present
    - Start aspirin 100–162 mg PO daily, unless contraindicated
    - For 3 years, starting after recovery from surgery
    - Aspirin should not be initiated until after recovery from surgery
    - For patients receiving adjuvant therapy and aspirin, aspirin can be given concurrently with adjuvant chemotherapy
- Somatic PI3K pathway alterations include
    - Mutations in PIK3CA exon 9 and 20
    - Other PIK3CA, PIK3R1, and PTEN mutations
    - Deep deletions of PTEN

# ctDNA

- ctDNA is a prognostic marker, but not predictive
- There is currently insufficient evidence to recommend routine use of ctDNA assays outside of a clinical trial
- De-escalation of care and treatment decision-making are not recommended based on ctDNA results
- Participation in clinical trials is encouraged
