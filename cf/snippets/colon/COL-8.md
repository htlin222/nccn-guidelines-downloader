# Stage I surveillance

- Colonoscopy at 1 year after surgery
    - If advanced adenoma, repeat in 1 year
    - If no advanced adenoma, repeat in 3 years, then every 5 years
        - Advanced adenoma means villous polyp, polyp >1 cm, or high-grade dysplasia
        - Interval per Kahi CJ, et al. Gastroenterology 2016;150:758-768

# Stage II and III surveillance

- History and physical examination every 3–6 months for 2 years, then every 6 months for a total of 5 years
- CEA every 3–6 months for 2 years, then every 6 months for a total of 5 years
    - Only if the patient is a potential candidate for further intervention
- C/A/P CT every 6–12 months from date of surgery for a total of 5 years
    - Category 2B for a frequency of less than 12 months
- Colonoscopy in 1 year after surgery
    - Except if no complete preoperative colonoscopy, then colonoscopy in 3–6 months
    - If advanced adenoma, repeat in 1 year
    - If no advanced adenoma, repeat in 3 years, then every 5 years
        - Advanced adenoma means villous polyp, polyp >1 cm, or high-grade dysplasia
        - Interval per Kahi CJ, et al. Gastroenterology 2016;150:758-768
- FDG-PET/CT is not indicated
- ctDNA is not recommended for surveillance
- Principles of Survivorship (COL-H)

# Stage IV surveillance

- History and physical examination every 3–6 months for 2 years, then every 6 months for a total of 5 years
- CEA every 3–6 months for 2 years, then every 6 months for a total of 5 years
    - Only if the patient is a potential candidate for further intervention
- C/A/P CT every 3–6 months for 2 years, then every 6–12 months for a total of 5 years
    - Category 2B for a frequency of less than 6 months
- Colonoscopy in 1 year after surgery
    - Except if no complete preoperative colonoscopy, then colonoscopy in 3–6 months
    - If managed nonoperatively with a complete clinical response, initiate colonoscopy surveillance from first documentation of complete response
    - If advanced adenoma, repeat in 1 year
    - If no advanced adenoma, repeat in 3 years, then every 5 years
        - Advanced adenoma means villous polyp, polyp >1 cm, or high-grade dysplasia
        - Interval per Kahi CJ, et al. Gastroenterology 2016;150:758-768
- FDG-PET/CT is not indicated
- Principles of Survivorship (COL-H)
