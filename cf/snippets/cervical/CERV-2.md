- NCCN Cervical Cancer v2.2026, CERV-2, p11
- Applies to clinical stage IA1 through select IB2 when fertility sparing is intended
- Clinical stage assessed per Principles of Imaging (CERV-B)
- There are no data to support a fertility-sparing approach in small neuroendocrine tumors or gastric type adenocarcinoma

# Stage IA1, no lymphovascular space invasion (LVSI)

- Cone biopsy with negative margins
    - Cold knife conization (CKC) is the preferred method of diagnostic excision
    - Loop electrosurgical excision procedure (LEEP) is acceptable, provided adequate margins and proper orientation are obtained
    - Endocervical curettage (ECC) above the excision should be added, except in pregnancy
    - Negative margins means negative for invasive disease or histologic high-grade squamous intraepithelial lesion (HSIL) at margins
    - Preferably a non-fragmented specimen with at least 1-mm negative margins
    - If positive margins, repeat cone biopsy or perform trachelectomy

# Stage IA1 with LVSI

- Cone biopsy with negative margins
    - Cold knife conization (CKC) preferred, LEEP acceptable if adequate margins and proper orientation are obtained
    - Endocervical curettage (ECC) above the excision should be added, except in pregnancy
    - Negative margins means negative for invasive disease or histologic HSIL at margins
    - Preferably a non-fragmented specimen with at least 1-mm negative margins
    - If positive margins, repeat cone biopsy or perform trachelectomy
- Plus sentinel lymph node (SLN) mapping or pelvic lymphadenectomy
    - Principles of Evaluation and Surgical Staging (CERV-C)

# Stage IA2-IB1, conservative surgery criteria

- Based on cone biopsy, and all of the following criteria must be met
    - No LVSI (preferred)
    - Negative cone margins for cancer
    - Squamous cell (any grade) or usual type adenocarcinoma (grade 1 or 2 only) (preferred)
    - Tumor size 2 cm or less
    - Depth of invasion 10 mm or less on LEEP/cone
    - Negative imaging for locoregional disease (MRI recommended)
- If all criteria met, cone biopsy with negative margins
    - Cold knife conization (CKC) preferred, LEEP acceptable if adequate margins and proper orientation are obtained
    - Endocervical curettage (ECC) above the excision should be added, except in pregnancy
    - Negative margins means negative for invasive disease or histologic HSIL at margins
- Plus SLN mapping or pelvic lymphadenectomy
    - Principles of Evaluation and Surgical Staging (CERV-C)

# Stage IB1 not meeting conservative surgery criteria, or select IB2

- Radical trachelectomy
    - Fertility-sparing surgery for stage IB has been most validated for tumors <2 cm
    - For stage IB2 lesions 2-4 cm, abdominal approach is favored
    - Small cell neuroendocrine histology and gastric type adenocarcinoma are not considered suitable tumors for this procedure
- Plus SLN mapping or pelvic lymphadenectomy
    - For SLN mapping, the best detection rates and mapping results are in tumors <2 cm
    - Principles of Evaluation and Surgical Staging (CERV-C)
- With or without para-aortic lymphadenectomy (CERV-C)

# After completion of childbearing

- Total hysterectomy after completion of childbearing is at the patient's and surgeon's discretion
- Strongly advised in patients with continued abnormal pap smears or chronic persistent human papillomavirus (HPV) infection
