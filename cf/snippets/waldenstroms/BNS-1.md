# Essential workup

- Contrast-enhanced brain and entire spine MRI
- Lumbar puncture for cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) analysis
    - Cytology
    - Multiparameter flow cytometry
    - PCR for IgH gene rearrangement
    - ASO-PCR for MYD88 L265P

# Workup useful in certain circumstances

- Biopsy of affected tissue
- Concurrent bone marrow aspiration and biopsy
    - IgH gene rearrangement
    - MYD88 L265P testing by ASO-PCR

# Diagnosis

- Definitive diagnosis
    - Presence of clonal B cells in CSF or tissue biopsy, with similar profile to systemic disease
    - With or without leptomeningeal enhancement or masses in MRI
- Probable diagnosis
    - Abnormal MRI findings without evidence of clonal B cells in CSF or tissue biopsy

# Management by symptom status

- Asymptomatic
    - Observation
- Symptomatic
    - Rituximab can be added to these regimens if systemic control is needed
    - Preferred
        - Ibrutinib
        - Zanubrutinib
    - Other recommended
        - Bendamustine
        - Cytarabine
        - Fludarabine
        - Pirtobrutinib
    - Useful in certain circumstances
        - Intrathecal methotrexate
        - Methotrexate
        - Radiotherapy
