# Essential

- Review of all slides, with at least one paraffin block representative of the tumor, by a hematopathologist with expertise in the diagnosis of PTCLs
    - Rebiopsy if consult material is nondiagnostic
- Biopsy adequacy
    - Excisional or incisional biopsy is preferred over core needle biopsy
    - FNA biopsy alone is insufficient for the initial diagnosis of lymphoma
    - Core needle biopsy is an appropriate alternative to excisional or incisional biopsy under certain circumstances
    - When a lymph node is not easily accessible for excisional or incisional biopsy, a combination of core needle biopsy and FNA biopsy in conjunction with appropriate ancillary techniques may be sufficient for diagnosis
- Adequate immunophenotyping to establish diagnosis
    - For differential diagnosis of NK/T-cell neoplasms, use of immunophenotyping and genetic testing is in TCLYM-E
    - IHC panel may include CD20, CD3, CD10, BCL6, Ki-67, CD5, CD30, CD2, CD4, CD8, CD7, CD56, CD21, CD23, TCR beta, TCR delta, PD1/CD279, ALK, TP63
        - If nodal T-cell lymphoma of T-follicular helper (TFH) cell origin is suspected, the IHC panel should include a minimum of two or ideally three TFH cell markers from CD10, BCL6, PD1/CD279, CXCL13, ICOS
    - With or without flow cytometry panel, which may include kappa/lambda, CD45, CD3, CD5, CD19, CD10, CD20, CD30, CD4, CD8, CD7, CD2, TCR alpha/beta, TCR gamma/delta, TRBC1
- Epstein-Barr virus-encoded RNA in situ hybridization (EBER-ISH)

# Useful in certain circumstances

- Molecular analysis to detect clonal TRB and TRG rearrangements, or other assessment of clonality
    - Clonal TRB and TRG gene rearrangements alone are not sufficient for diagnosis, as these can also be seen in patients with non-malignant conditions
    - Interpret results in the context of overall presentation (TCLYM-A)
- Consider cytogenetics, by fluorescence in situ hybridization (FISH) or conventional chromosome analysis
    - DUSP22 rearrangement, if anaplastic large cell lymphoma (ALCL), ALK negative (TCLYM-A)
    - TP63 rearrangement, if IHC is positive for TP63
- Consider FISH or multigene panel testing (MGPT) to detect JAK2 and TYK2 rearrangements (TCLYM-A)
- Consider MGPT to support the diagnosis of TFH subtypes (TCLYM-A)
- Additional IHC panel to characterize subtypes of PTCL, including cytotoxic granule proteins (TIA-1, granzyme B, perforin)
- Assessment of human T-cell lymphotropic virus (HTLV)-1/2 by serology or other methods is encouraged, as results can impact therapy
    - See map for prevalence of HTLV-1/2 by geographic region
    - HTLV-1/2 has been described in patients in non-endemic areas
