# Mature T-cell and NK-cell neoplasms

- WHO4R: T-cell prolymphocytic leukemia
    - ICC: T-cell prolymphocytic leukemia
    - WHO5: T-prolymphocytic leukaemia
- WHO4R: T-cell large granular lymphocytic leukemia
    - ICC: T-cell large granular lymphocytic leukemia
    - WHO5: T-large granular lymphocytic leukaemia
- WHO4R: Chronic lymphoproliferative disorder of NK-cells
    - Provisional entity in WHO4R
    - ICC: Chronic lymphoproliferative disorder of NK cells
    - WHO5: NK-large granular lymphocytic leukaemia
- WHO4R: Aggressive NK-cell leukemia
    - ICC: Aggressive NK cell leukemia
    - WHO5: Aggressive NK-cell leukaemia
- WHO4R: Adult T-cell leukemia/lymphoma
    - ICC: Adult T-cell leukemia/lymphoma
    - WHO5: Adult T-cell leukaemia/lymphoma

# EBV-positive NK-cell and T-cell lymphomas, the WHO5 family

- ICC: Primary nodal EBV-positive T-cell/NK-cell lymphoma
    - Provisional entity in ICC
    - WHO4R: not previously included
    - WHO5: EBV-positive nodal T- and NK-cell lymphoma
- WHO4R: Extranodal NK/T-cell lymphoma, nasal type
    - ICC: Extranodal NK/T-cell lymphoma, nasal type
    - WHO5: Extranodal NK/T-cell lymphoma

# Intestinal T-cell and NK-cell lymphoid proliferations and lymphomas, the WHO5 family

- WHO4R: Enteropathy-associated T-cell lymphoma
    - ICC: Enteropathy-associated T-cell lymphoma
        - ICC also lists type II refractory celiac disease here
    - WHO5: Enteropathy-associated T-cell lymphoma
- WHO4R: Monomorphic epitheliotropic intestinal T-cell lymphoma
    - Provisional entity in WHO4R
    - ICC: Monomorphic epitheliotropic intestinal T-cell lymphoma
    - WHO5: Monomorphic epitheliotropic intestinal T-cell lymphoma
- WHO4R: Intestinal T-cell lymphoma, NOS
    - ICC: Intestinal T-cell lymphoma, NOS
    - WHO5: Intestinal T-cell lymphoma, NOS
- WHO4R: Indolent T-cell lymphoproliferative disorder of the GI tract
    - Provisional entity in WHO4R
    - ICC: Indolent clonal T-cell lymphoproliferative disorder of the GI tract
    - WHO5: Indolent T-cell lymphoma of the gastrointestinal tract
- ICC: Indolent NK-cell lymphoproliferative disorder of the gastrointestinal tract
    - WHO4R: not previously included
    - WHO5: Indolent NK-cell lymphoproliferative disorder of the gastrointestinal tract
