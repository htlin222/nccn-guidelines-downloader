# Clinical pretreatment evaluation, if not previously done

- Biomarker testing (NSCL-19)
- Brain MRI with and without contrast
    - If MRI is not possible, CT of head with contrast
- FDG-PET/CT scan
    - Performed skull base to mid-thigh
    - Positive FDG-PET/CT scan findings for mediastinal nodal or distant disease need pathologic or other radiologic confirmation
    - If FDG-PET/CT scan is positive in the mediastinum, lymph node status needs pathologic confirmation
- Pathologic confirmation of metastatic lesion, if possible

# Extent of metastatic disease

- Solitary or limited metastases confirmed
    - Includes selected patients with stage M1c and a limited number and volume of metastatic lesions amenable to definitive local therapy
    - Clinical trials have included up to 3 to 5 metastatic sites
    - PS 0-2, definitive local therapy below
    - PS 3-4, advanced/metastatic disease (NSCL-19)
- Multiple lesions, advanced/metastatic disease (NSCL-19)

# Initial treatment, solitary or limited metastases, PS 0-2

- Brain, see NCCN Guidelines for Central Nervous System Cancers
    - Stereotactic radiosurgery (SRS) alone, or
    - Surgical resection, if symptomatic or warranted for diagnosis, followed by SRS or whole brain RT (WBRT)
- Other site
