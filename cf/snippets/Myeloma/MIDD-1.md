# Clinical findings, MIDD subtypes

- Light chain deposition disease (LCDD) ~80%
    - Of which 80%–90% are Kappa LC
- Heavy chain deposition disease (HCDD) ~10%
- Light and heavy chain deposition disease (LHCDD) ~10%

# Workup

- Tests outlined on MYEL-1 to identify the plasma cell abnormality, if not previously done
- Evaluate for kidney or other organ damage
    - For evaluating kidney damage, see MGRS-1
    - For evaluating other organ damage, see NCCN Guidelines for Systemic Light Chain Amyloidosis

# Additional evaluation as clinically indicated

- PCR assay for MYD88 L265P
- Excisional lymph node biopsy, if other B-cell lymphomas are suspected
- Peripheral blood flow cytometry for diagnosis of CLL/SLL or LPL
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia/Small Lymphocytic Lymphoma
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Waldenström Macroglobulinemia/Lymphoplasmacytic Lymphoma

# Treatment

- Treat if evidence of organ damage by underlying pathogenic clone

# Transplant considerations

- Autologous HCT consolidation preferable in eligible patients (by clone subtype)
- If patient has deep and sustained clonal response after induction therapy, with or without autologous HCT consolidation
    - Consider solid organ transplant to treat the affected organ/s in eligible patients
- Candidacy for solid organ transplant is acceptable without consolidative autologous HCT if patients attained deep and durable response
