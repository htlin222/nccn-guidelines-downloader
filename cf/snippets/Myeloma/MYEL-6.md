# Source

- NCCN Multiple Myeloma v5.2026, MYEL-6, p14
- Applies to multiple myeloma (symptomatic) with relapse or progressive disease
- Also covers refractory disease with lack of treatment options
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- ___ (age) yo, multiple myeloma (symptomatic)
- Current disease state ___ (disease_status)
    - Assessed against Response Criteria for Multiple Myeloma (MYEL-E)
- Previously treated with ___ (prior_therapy)
- CNS disease ___ (cns_disease)
    - For MM with CNS disease, see CNSM-1
- HCT candidacy ___ (hct_candidacy)
- Patient's preferences and goals of care ___ (goals_of_care)
    - Discuss the patient's preferences and goals of care

# Consider referral

- Chimeric antigen receptor (CAR) T-cell therapy specialist
- Palliative care specialist and supportive care for symptom management
    - Supportive Care Treatment for Multiple Myeloma (MYEL-H)
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Palliative Care and NCCN Guidelines for Supportive Care

# Treatment for relapse or progressive disease

- Clinical trial, if eligible
- Therapy for previously treated myeloma
    - Myeloma Therapy (MYEL-G)
    - See MYEL-F for considerations for myeloma therapy and for specific populations (eg, Black/African American individuals)
    - Follow up with the tests listed on MYEL-4 under Follow-up/Surveillance
- Autologous HCT
    - Assess for HCT candidacy
    - Follow up with the tests listed on MYEL-4 under Follow-up/Surveillance
- Allogeneic HCT
    - Assess for HCT candidacy
    - Donor lymphocyte infusion can be considered in patients with disease relapse after allogeneic HCT
    - Follow up with the tests listed on MYEL-4 under Follow-up/Surveillance

# Refractory disease and lack of treatment options

- Continue palliative care
- Consider re-evaluation of goals of care
- Consider hospice initiation
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Supportive Care and NCCN Guidelines for Palliative Care

# Next

- Relapse or progressive disease, choose one of the treatment options above
- Refractory disease and lack of treatment options, continue palliative care and consider hospice initiation
- MM with CNS disease, see CNSM-1
- After treatment, follow up with the tests listed on MYEL-4 under Follow-up/Surveillance
