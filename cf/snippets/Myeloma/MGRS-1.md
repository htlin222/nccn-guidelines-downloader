# Source

- NCCN Multiple Myeloma v5.2026, MGRS-1, p40
- Applies when monoclonal gammopathy of renal significance (MGRS) is suspected
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- Monoclonal gammopathy of renal significance (MGRS) suspected
- M protein in serum and/or urine ___ (m_protein)
- eGFR ___ (egfr) mL/min
- eGFR decline per year ___ (egfr_decline)
- Acute kidney injury (AKI) stage ___ (aki_stage)
- 24-h urine total protein ___ (urine_protein)
- Albumin:creatinine ___ (acr) mg/mmol
- Fanconi syndrome present
- Evidence of light chain proteinuria

# Initial workup: evaluate for kidney disease

- Kidney function: established GFR (eGFR)
- Urinalysis
- 24-h urine for total protein, UPEP, and UIFE
- Metabolic panel

# Renal biopsy decision

- Renal biopsy recommended if any of:
    - Acute kidney injury (AKI) stage 3
    - eGFR <60 mL/min and >2 mL/min per year decline
    - Proteinuria (>1 g/day), albumin:creatinine >30 mg/mmol
    - Fanconi syndrome
- Consider renal biopsy if any of:
    - AKI stage 1 or 2
    - eGFR <60 mL/min and <2 mL/min per year decline
    - Albumin:creatinine 3–30 mg/mmol and glomerular filtration rate (GFR) <60 mL/min
    - Evidence of light chain proteinuria
- Defer renal biopsy if all of:
    - Stable eGFR
    - Normal urinalysis
    - No evidence of light chain proteinuria

# Additional workup to confirm diagnosis of MGRS

- Light microscopy
- Immunofluorescence staining
    - For IgG subclasses, IgA and IgM
    - For kappa and lambda
    - M protein detected in serum and/or urine must match the one found in the renal biopsy
- Electron microscopy
- FDG-PET/CT, low-dose CT, or whole-body MRI without contrast, as clinically indicated
- Bone marrow biopsy if suspected to have MM or Waldenström macroglobulinemia/lymphoplasmacytic lymphoma (WM/LPL)

# Additional workup as clinically indicated

- FISH panel for myeloma
- Polymerase chain reaction (PCR) assay for MYD88 L265P
- Excisional lymph node biopsy, if other B-cell lymphomas are suspected
- Peripheral blood flow cytometry for diagnosis of chronic lymphocytic leukemia
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia/Small Lymphocytic Lymphoma
- Evaluate for light chain amyloidosis
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Systemic Light Chain Amyloidosis
