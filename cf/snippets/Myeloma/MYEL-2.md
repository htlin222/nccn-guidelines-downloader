# Source

- NCCN Multiple Myeloma v5.2026, MYEL-2, p10
- Applies to solitary plasmacytoma, or solitary plasmacytoma with minimal marrow involvement
- All criteria must be present for the diagnosis; for diagnostic criteria see Definitions of Myeloma and Related Plasma-Cell Disorders (MYEL-A)
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- Plasmacytoma site ___ (site)
- ___ (plasmacytoma_type) plasmacytoma
- Bone marrow plasma cells (BMPCs) ___ (bmpc_pct) %
    - BMPCs 10% or more is regarded as active (symptomatic) MM, and systemic therapy should be considered
- Consider FISH testing on the plasmacytoma biopsy if clonal cells are absent or inadequate in the marrow
- Baseline imaging technique used at diagnosis ___ (baseline_imaging)
    - Principles of Imaging (MYEL-C)
    - Whole-body FDG-PET/CT is the first choice for initial and continued evaluation of solitary extraosseous plasmacytoma
    - Whole-body MRI is the first choice for initial evaluation of solitary osseous plasmacytoma
        - PET/CT if MRI is not available
        - If whole-body MRI is not available, consider MRI of the spine and pelvis, whole-body FDG-PET/CT, or low-dose whole-body CT
- Structurally unstable lesion
- Neurologic compromise due to mass effect
- High risk of progression

# Primary treatment

- RT
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy (MYEL-D)
- With or without surgery
    - Consider surgery if structurally unstable, or if there is neurologic compromise due to mass effect
    - Systemic therapy may be indicated in patients with high risk of progression, based on the clinical context
- Or consider clinical trial

# Follow-up/surveillance

- Follow-up interval every 3–6 months
    - Reassess after at least 3 months following radiation, as the assessment of response with imaging may not be accurate if the scans are performed sooner
    - Patients with soft tissue and head/neck plasmacytoma could be followed less frequently as clinically indicated, after the initial 3 month follow-up
- CBC, differential, and platelet count
- Serum chemistry for creatinine and corrected calcium
- Serum quantitative immunoglobulins, SPEP, with SIFE
- Serum FLC assay
- 24-hour urine for total protein and UPEP with UIFE, as needed
- Bone marrow aspirate and biopsy as indicated
- All plasmacytomas should be imaged yearly, for at least 5 years
    - Preferably with the same technique used at diagnosis
    - Principles of Imaging (MYEL-C)
- See NCCN Guidelines for Survivorship
