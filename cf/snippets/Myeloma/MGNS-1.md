- Applies to suspected IgM MGNS (monoclonal gammopathy of neurological significance)
- All recommendations are category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Initial workup

- Rule out other causes of neuropathy
    - Diabetes
    - Cobalamin deficiency
    - Thyroid dysfunction
    - Lyme disease
    - HIV infection
    - Syphilis
    - Autoimmune disease
    - Cryoglobulinemia
- Evaluate for the following as appropriate
    - Light chain amyloidosis, see NCCN Guidelines for Systemic Light Chain Amyloidosis
    - WM, see NCCN Guidelines for WM/LPL
    - POEMS (POEMS-1)
- Anti-MAG antibodies
    - In patients presenting with suspected disease related to peripheral/axonal neuropathy, rule out amyloidosis in patients presenting with nephrotic syndrome or unexplained cardiac problems
- Ganglioside antibody panel
- Nerve conduction study (NCS)/electromyogram (EMG)
    - In patients presenting with suspected disease related to peripheral/axonal neuropathy, rule out amyloidosis in patients presenting with nephrotic syndrome or unexplained cardiac problems
- Neurology consult
- MYD88 L265P allele-specific PCR (AS-PCR) testing of bone marrow
    - MYD88 wild-type occurs in <10% of patients and should not be used to exclude diagnosis of WM if other criteria are met
- Chest/abdomen/pelvis CT with contrast when possible

# Useful in certain circumstances

- Sural nerve biopsy
- CXCR4 gene mutation testing

# Clinical findings

- High suspicion
    - Sensory predominant
    - Length dependent
    - Slow progression (years)
    - Bilateral and symmetrical
    - Antibodies present
    - Demyelination by EMG/NCS
- Low suspicion
    - Motor/pain predominant
    - Non-length dependent
    - Rapid progression (weeks to months)
    - Unilateral/asymmetrical
    - Antibodies not present
    - No demyelination by EMG/NCS
- Intermediate suspicion is neither high nor low suspicion

# Next

- High suspicion, see NCCN Guidelines for Waldenström Macroglobulinemia/Lymphoplasmacytic Lymphoma
- Intermediate suspicion and affecting activities of daily living (ADLs), see NCCN Guidelines for Waldenström Macroglobulinemia/Lymphoplasmacytic Lymphoma
- Low suspicion, observation
- Intermediate or high suspicion and not affecting ADLs, observation
