# Primary treatment

- Initiate myeloma therapy
    - Myeloma Therapy: MYEL-G
    - See MYEL-F for considerations for myeloma therapy and for specific populations (eg, Black/African American individuals)
- Initiate bone-targeting treatment
    - Supportive Care Treatment for Multiple Myeloma: MYEL-I
- Supportive care treatment as indicated for symptom management
    - Also see NCCN Guidelines for Supportive Care
    - Management of Renal Disease in Multiple Myeloma: MYEL-L
    - Supportive Care Treatment for Multiple Myeloma: MYEL-I

# Transplant assessment

- Assess for candidacy for transplant after starting therapy
- Reassess for transplant as performance status improves
- Refer to hematopoietic cell transplant (HCT) center
- Harvest hematopoietic stem cells
    - Consider harvesting for 2 transplants if appropriate
- Autologous transplantation: category 1 evidence supports proceeding directly after primary therapy to high-dose therapy and HCT
    - Collecting stem cells and delaying HCT is also an option
- Renal dysfunction is not a contraindication to transplant
- Advanced age is not a contraindication to transplant

# Follow-up/surveillance

- Laboratory assessments appropriate for monitoring treatment toxicities may include
    - Complete blood count (CBC) with differential
    - Metabolic panel
- Serum quantitative immunoglobulins
- SPEP
- SIFE
    - Needed only if protein electrophoresis is negative during follow-up
- 24-hour urine for total protein, as clinically indicated
- UPEP, as clinically indicated
- UIFE, as clinically indicated
    - Needed only if protein electrophoresis is negative during follow-up
- Serum FLC assay
- Imaging annually or as clinically indicated, ideally with the same technique used at diagnosis
    - Whole-body MRI without contrast
    - Low-dose CT
    - FDG-PET/CT
    - Principles of Imaging: MYEL-C
- Bone marrow aspirate and biopsy at relapse, with FISH as clinically indicated
- Consider MRD testing as indicated for prognostication, after shared decision with patient
- See NCCN Guidelines for Survivorship
