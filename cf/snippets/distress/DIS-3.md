# Screening

- Brief screening for distress (DIS-A)
    - Screening tool
    - Problem list
    - The NCCN Problem List and the NCCN Distress Thermometer Screening Tool may be modified to fit the needs of the local population
    - See Discussion (MS-8) for information about other validated screening tools

# Evaluation, for moderate to severe distress or score of 4 or higher on screening tool (DIS-A)

- Clinical assessment by the primary oncology team
    - May include clinical interviews
    - May include validated scales/screeners for anxiety and depression
    - By oncologist, nurse, advanced practice professional, or social worker
- Assess for increased risk, see Psychosocial Distress Patient Characteristics (DIS-B)
    - Periods of vulnerability
    - Risk factors for distress
- Assess concerns, see Problem List (DIS-A)
    - Physical
        - Consider referral for palliative care management, see NCCN Guidelines for Palliative Care and NCCN Guidelines for Adult Cancer Pain
    - Emotional, including anxiety and depression
    - Social
    - Practical
    - Family
    - Spiritual/religious
- Unrelieved physical symptoms, treat as per disease-specific or supportive care guidelines
    - Consider referral for palliative care management, see NCCN Guidelines for Palliative Care and NCCN Guidelines for Adult Cancer Pain

# Treatment by referral

- Use of an evidence-based model of care, such as the Collaborative Care Model, is encouraged to coordinate the delivery of patient-centered psychosocial interventions
- Mental health professional, see Psychological/Psychiatric Treatment Guidelines (DIS-5)
    - Licensed mental health providers including psychiatrists, psychologists, advanced practice clinicians, clinical social workers, mental health counselors, and marriage and family therapists
- Social work and counseling services, see Social Work and Counseling Services (DIS-22)
- Chaplaincy care, see Chaplaincy Care (DIS-24)
- Follow-up and communication with primary oncology team, primary care physician, and family/caregivers

# Next

- Clinical evidence of moderate to severe distress, or score of 4 or higher on screening tool (DIS-A), go to evaluation, then to treatment by referral
- Clinical evidence of mild distress, or score of less than 4 on screening tool (DIS-A), manage with primary oncology team plus resources available, go to Expected Distress Symptoms (DIS-4)
    - If necessary, refer as under treatment by referral (DIS-5, DIS-22, DIS-24)
