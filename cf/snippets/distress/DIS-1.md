# Why the term distress was chosen

- Is more acceptable and less stigmatizing than other terms
    - Less stigmatizing than psychiatric
    - Less stigmatizing than psychosocial
    - Less stigmatizing than emotional
- Sounds normal and less embarrassing
- Can be defined and measured by self-report

# Definition of distress in cancer

- A multifactorial unpleasant experience
- Nature of the experience
    - Psychological
        - Cognitive
        - Behavioral
        - Emotional
    - Social
    - Spiritual
    - Physical
- May interfere with one's ability to cope effectively with
    - Cancer
    - Its physical symptoms
    - Its treatment

# Distress extends along a continuum

- Common normal feelings
    - Vulnerability
    - Sadness
    - Fears
- Problems that can become disabling
    - Depression
    - Anxiety
    - Panic
    - Social isolation
    - Existential and spiritual crisis
