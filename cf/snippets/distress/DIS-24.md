# Assessment

- Spiritual/religious beliefs and practices: ___ (beliefs)
- Spiritual/existential concerns identified: ___ (spiritual_concerns)
- Ritual needs: ___ (ritual_needs)
- Specific faith community, spiritual director, or pastoral psychotherapist already involved: ___ (faith_community)

# Chaplaincy assessment

- Interpersonal conflict regarding spiritual/religious beliefs and practices
- Concerns with lack of meaning/purpose
- Struggles with morality/values
- Doubts about beliefs
- Perception of being attacked by evil
- Dealing with issues of forgiveness
- Concerns about relationship with the sacred
- Concerns about dying/death and/or afterlife
- Grief/loss
- Feelings of worthlessness or being a burden
- Feelings of guilt/shame/blame
- Loneliness
- Conflict between religious beliefs and recommended treatment
- Ritual needs
- Instruments cited for this assessment
    - The Religious and Spiritual Struggles Scale, development and initial validation (Exline JJ, Pargament KI, Grubbs JB, Yali AM. Psycholog Relig Spiritual 2014;6:208-222)
    - The Existential Concerns Questionnaire (ECQ), development and initial validation of a new existential anxiety scale in a nonclinical and clinical sample (van Bruggen V, Ten Klooster P, Westerhof G, et al. J Clin Psychol 2017;73:1692-1703)

# Interventions based on assessment

- Follow the Spiritual Health Victoria spiritual care standards (https://spiritualhealth.org.au/standards)
- Spiritual/existential support/care
- Spiritual/existential counseling
- Spiritual/existential education, including resources
- Spiritual/existential ritual
- Meditation and/or prayer
- Referral to spiritual/existential community resources
    - Specific faith community
    - Spiritual director
    - Pastoral psychotherapist
- Referral to other health care professional
    - Palliative care
    - Mental health professional
