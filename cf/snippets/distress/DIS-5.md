# Source

- NCCN Distress Management v2.2026, DIS-5, p12
- Applies to a patient referred by the oncology team to the mental health team
    - Mental health team means licensed mental health providers, including psychiatrists, psychologists, advanced practice clinicians, clinical social workers, mental health counselors, and marriage and family therapists
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Evaluation for

- Distress
- Behavioral changes
- Relationship concerns
- Psychological/psychiatric disorder
- Psychiatric history/medications
- Substance use disorder
- Medical causes
    - Refer to primary oncology team
- Pain and symptom control
    - NCCN Guidelines for Adult Cancer Pain
    - NCCN Guidelines for Palliative Care
- Fatigue
    - NCCN Guidelines for Cancer-Related Fatigue
- Sleep/wake disturbances
    - NCCN Guidelines for Palliative Care: Sleep/Wake Disturbances (PAL-22)
    - NCCN Guidelines for Survivorship: Sleep Disorders (SSD-1)
- Body image
- Sexual health
    - NCCN Guidelines for Survivorship: Sexual Health (SSH-1)
- Impaired cognitive function
    - NCCN Guidelines for Survivorship: Cognitive Function (SCF-1)
- Safety
- Suicide risk
    - Formal assessment should be conducted as per The Joint Commission's Suicide Prevention Recommendations
    - In the United States, 988 (text, call, or web) is the crisis and suicide helpline
- Existential distress/demoralization

# Follow-up

- Follow-up and communication with primary oncology team
- Follow-up and communication with primary care physician
- Follow-up and communication with family/caregivers
