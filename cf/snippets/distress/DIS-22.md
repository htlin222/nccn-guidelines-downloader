# Source

- NCCN Distress Management v2.2026, DIS-22, p29
- Applies to patients referred by the oncology team to social work, counseling services, case management, or patient navigation
- Social work and counseling services include mental health care as described in the psychological/psychiatric treatment guidelines (DIS-5)
- All recommendations are category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- Patient/family assessment done
- Referred by the oncology team to: ___ (referral_service)
- Category of concern: ___ (category_of_concern)
- Type of concern: ___ (type_of_concern)
- If psychosocial concerns, severity: ___ (psychosocial_severity)
- Family and caregiver availability: ___ (family_caregiver_availability)

# Category

- Practical concerns
- Psychosocial concerns
    - Mild
    - Severe or moderate

# Type of concern

- Illness-related problems
- Basic needs
    - Housing
    - Food
    - Financial/insurance concerns
    - Assistance with activities of daily living (ADLs)
    - Transportation
- Employment/school/career concerns
- Cultural/language issues
- Family and caregiver availability
- Safety

# Social work and counseling interventions

- These services include mental health care as described in the psychological/psychiatric treatment guidelines (DIS-5)
- Mild
    - Patient/family education
    - Education/support group sessions
    - Resource lists
- Severe or moderate
    - Patient and family counseling/psychotherapy
    - Community resource mobilization/linkage
    - Teaching problem-solving
    - Advocacy and patient/family education
