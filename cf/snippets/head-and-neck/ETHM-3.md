# Source

- NCCN Head and Neck Cancers v2.2026, ETHM-3, p64
- Applies to ethmoid sinus tumors diagnosed after incomplete resection (eg, polypectomy)
- For a minor salivary gland tumor, see the salivary gland algorithm for management after resection, NCCN Guidelines for Salivary Gland Tumors (SALI-1)
- For SNUC with neuroendocrine features, small cell, high-grade olfactory esthesioneuroblastoma, or SNEC histologies, systemic therapy should be a part of the overall treatment, in both primary and adjuvant treatment
    - Consider a clinical trial
    - Consider referral to a major medical center that specializes in these diseases
    - Principles of Systemic Therapy for Non-Nasopharyngeal Cancers (SYST-A)
- All recommendations are category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Primary treatment, gross residual disease

- Surgery (preferred), if feasible
    - Principles of Surgery (SURG-A)
    - N+ neck disease is uncommon in ethmoid cancers, but, if present, requires neck dissection and appropriate risk-based adjuvant therapy
- or RT
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy (ETHM-A)
    - For minor salivary gland tumors, see SALI-A
    - Primary RT is an option for minimal residual squamous cell carcinoma
- or Concurrent systemic therapy/RT
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy (ETHM-A)
    - Principles of Systemic Therapy for Non-Nasopharyngeal Cancers (SYST-A)

# Adjuvant treatment after surgery, gross residual disease

- RT (ETHM-A)
- or Consider systemic therapy/RT (category 2B), if adverse pathologic features
    - Positive margins
    - Close margins, ie, tumors adjacent to the cribriform plate and/or medial wall of the orbit
    - Unfavorable histology, ie, adenoid cystic, high-grade tumors
    - Intracranial and/or intraorbital extension
    - Cribriform plate location
    - Medial wall of orbit location
    - Perineural invasion
    - Lymphovascular space invasion

# Primary treatment, no residual disease on physical exam, imaging, and/or endoscopy

- RT
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy (ETHM-A)
    - For minor salivary gland tumors, see SALI-A
- or Surgery, if feasible
    - Principles of Surgery (SURG-A)
    - N+ neck disease is uncommon in ethmoid cancers, but, if present, requires neck dissection and appropriate risk-based adjuvant therapy

# Adjuvant treatment after surgery, no residual disease

- RT (ETHM-A)
- or Observe, for T1 only (category 2B), if all pathologic features are favorable
    - Negative margins
    - Favorable histology, including low grade
    - Not located along the cribriform plate or medial wall of the orbit
    - No perineural invasion, and lymphovascular space invasion
- or Consider systemic therapy/RT (category 2B), if adverse pathologic features
    - Positive margins
    - Close margins, ie, tumors adjacent to the cribriform plate and/or medial wall of the orbit
    - Unfavorable histology, ie, adenoid cystic, high-grade tumors
    - Intracranial and/or intraorbital extension
    - Cribriform plate location
    - Medial wall of orbit location
    - Perineural invasion
    - Lymphovascular space invasion
