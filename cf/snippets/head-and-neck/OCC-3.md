# Histology treated on this page

- Poorly differentiated or nonkeratinizing squamous cell
- NOS
- Anaplastic (not thyroid)
- Squamous cell carcinoma
- Treatment for nasopharyngeal (NASO-2) and p16-positive oropharyngeal cancers (ORPHPV-3 and ORPHPV-4) guides management of EBV-positive and HPV-positive occult primary tumors

# Definitive treatment, N1

- Neck dissection, per Principles of Surgery (SURG-A)
    - Then OCC-4
- or RT (category 2B), per Principles of Radiation Therapy (OCC-A)
    - Then Post Systemic Therapy/RT or RT Neck Evaluation (FOLL-A, 2 of 2)

# Definitive treatment, N2-3

- Concurrent systemic therapy/RT (category 2B)
    - Radiation per Principles of Radiation Therapy (OCC-A)
    - Systemic therapy per Principles of Systemic Therapy for Non-Nasopharyngeal Cancers (SYST-A)
- or Induction chemotherapy (category 3), followed by systemic therapy/RT
    - Chemotherapy per Principles of Systemic Therapy for Non-Nasopharyngeal Cancers (SYST-A)
    - See Discussion on induction chemotherapy
    - Radiation per Principles of Radiation Therapy (OCC-A)
- or RT, per Principles of Radiation Therapy (OCC-A)
- Each of the above is followed by Post Systemic Therapy/RT or RT Neck Evaluation (FOLL-A, 2 of 2)

# Next

- After neck dissection, go to OCC-4
- After Post Systemic Therapy/RT or RT Neck Evaluation (FOLL-A, 2 of 2)
    - Neck dissection, per Principles of Surgery (SURG-A), then OCC-4
    - Recurrent or persistent disease (ADV-3)
