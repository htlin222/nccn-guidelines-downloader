# Source

- NCCN Head and Neck Cancers v2.2026, Occult Primary, OCC-3, p78
- Applies to definitive treatment of an occult primary, once the neck node histology and N stage are known
- Definitive treatment on this page covers poorly differentiated or nonkeratinizing squamous cell, NOS, anaplastic (not thyroid), and squamous cell carcinoma
- For definitive treatment, use treatment for nasopharyngeal (NASO-2) and p16-positive oropharyngeal cancers (ORPHPV-3 and ORPHPV-4) to guide management of EBV-positive and HPV-positive occult primary tumors
- All recommendations are category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- ___ (age) yo, occult primary
- Neck node histology ___ (node_histology)
    - Poorly differentiated or nonkeratinizing squamous cell
    - Or NOS
    - Or anaplastic (not thyroid)
    - Or squamous cell carcinoma
- Neck node stage ___ (n_stage)

# Definitive treatment, N1

- Neck dissection
    - Principles of Surgery (SURG-A)
    - Then go to OCC-4
- Or RT (category 2B)
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy (OCC-A)
    - Then Post Systemic Therapy/RT or RT Neck Evaluation (FOLL-A, 2 of 2)
        - Neck dissection, Principles of Surgery (SURG-A), then go to OCC-4
        - Recurrent or persistent disease, go to ADV-3

# Definitive treatment, N2-3

- Concurrent systemic therapy/RT (category 2B)
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy (OCC-A)
    - Principles of Systemic Therapy for Non-Nasopharyngeal Cancers (SYST-A)
- Or induction chemotherapy (category 3) followed by systemic therapy/RT
    - Principles of Systemic Therapy for Non-Nasopharyngeal Cancers (SYST-A)
    - See Discussion on induction chemotherapy
    - Followed by systemic therapy/RT, Principles of Radiation Therapy (OCC-A) and Principles of Systemic Therapy for Non-Nasopharyngeal Cancers (SYST-A)
- Or RT
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy (OCC-A)
- Then Post Systemic Therapy/RT or RT Neck Evaluation (FOLL-A, 2 of 2)
    - Recurrent or persistent disease, go to ADV-3
