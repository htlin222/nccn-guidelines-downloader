# Source

- NCCN Head and Neck Cancers v2.2026, GLOT-1, p44
- Applies to cancer of the glottic larynx, at clinical staging and workup
- Complete workup may not be indicated for Tis or T1, but H&P examination and biopsy are required
- Direct laryngoscopy under anesthesia is generally recommended for all cases
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Workup

- H&P including a complete head and neck exam
    - Mirror and/or fiberoptic examination as clinically indicated
    - Document and quantify tobacco use history (pack years smoked)
    - Document alcohol use, and counsel
    - Advise all patients who currently smoke to quit smoking
    - Advise those who formerly smoked to remain abstinent from smoking
    - For additional cessation support, refer to the Smoking Cessation and Treatment Resources in the NCCN Guidelines for Smoking Cessation
    - Screen for distress, using the NCCN Distress Thermometer and Problem List, which includes social determinants of health (DIS-A)
- Biopsy of primary site or FNA of the neck
    - Image-guided (US or CT) needle biopsy of cystic neck nodes may offer better diagnostic yield than FNA by palpation alone for initial diagnosis in this setting
    - For unresectable or metastatic disease where there is a plan for systemic therapy, a core biopsy would allow for biomarker testing
- CT with contrast and thin angled cuts, or cuts <1.25 mm through larynx, and/or MRI with and without contrast of primary and neck (IMG-A)
- EUA with endoscopy
- Multidisciplinary consultation as clinically indicated

# As clinically indicated

- Chest CT, with or without contrast (IMG-A)
- Consider FDG-PET/CT (IMG-A)
- Preanesthesia studies
- Pulmonary function evaluation for conservation surgery candidates
- Consider videostrobe for select patients
- Dental evaluation (DENT-A)
- Nutrition, speech, and swallowing evaluation/therapy (NUTR-A)
- Audiogram
- Smoking cessation counseling
- Fertility/reproductive counseling, see fertility and reproductive endocrine considerations in the NCCN Guidelines for Adolescent and Young Adult (AYA) Oncology
- Screening for hepatitis B
- PD-L1 testing by IHC (CPS)
