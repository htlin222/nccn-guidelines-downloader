# Workup

- History and physical, including a complete head and neck exam
    - Mirror examination as clinically indicated
    - Document and quantify tobacco use history, in pack years smoked
    - Document alcohol use, and counsel
    - Advise all patients who currently smoke to quit smoking
    - Advise those who formerly smoked to remain abstinent from smoking
    - For additional cessation support, refer to the Smoking Cessation and Treatment Resources in the NCCN Guidelines for Smoking Cessation
    - Screen for distress, using the NCCN Distress Thermometer and Problem List, which includes social determinants of health (DIS-A)
- Nasopharyngeal fiberoptic examination
- Biopsy of primary site, or FNA of the neck
    - For cystic neck nodes, image-guided needle biopsy (US or CT) may offer better diagnostic yield than FNA by palpation alone for initial diagnosis
    - For unresectable or metastatic disease where there is a plan for systemic therapy, a core biopsy would allow for biomarker testing
- MRI with and without contrast of skull base to clavicle
    - With or without CT of skull base/neck with contrast, to evaluate skull base erosion
- Imaging for distant metastases (IMG-A)
    - FDG-PET/CT and/or chest CT with contrast
    - Bone scan if PET/CT not done
- Epstein-Barr virus (EBV)/DNA testing
    - For nonkeratinizing or undifferentiated histology, consider testing for EBV in tumor tissue and blood
    - Detection in pathologic specimens: ISH for EBV-encoded RNA (EBER), or IHC staining for latent membrane protein (LMP)
    - EBV DNA load in serum or plasma may be quantified by PCR targeting genomic sequences such as BamHI-W, Epstein-Barr virus nuclear antigen (EBNA), or LMP
    - These tests vary in their sensitivity
    - The EBV DNA load may reflect prognosis and change in response to therapy
- Consider HPV testing, may inform etiology
- Multidisciplinary consultation as clinically indicated

# As clinically indicated

- Dental/prosthodontic evaluation (DENT-A)
- Nutrition, speech, and swallowing evaluations/therapy (NUTR-A)
- Audiogram
- Consider ophthalmologic and endocrine evaluation
- Smoking cessation counseling
- Fertility/reproductive counseling, see fertility and reproductive endocrine considerations in the NCCN Guidelines for Adolescent and Young Adult (AYA) Oncology
- Screening for hepatitis B
