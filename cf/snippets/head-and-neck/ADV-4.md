# Source

- NCCN Head and Neck Cancers v2.2026, ADV-4, p73
- Applies to recurrent or persistent disease with distant metastases
- Treatment branch is performance status
- Clinical trial preferred
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- ___ (age) yo
- Recurrent or persistent disease with distant metastases
    - Consider MGPT for biomarker identification
- Extent of disease ___ (extent)
- Sites of distant metastases ___ (met_sites)
    - Distant metastases only, consider palliative RT as clinically indicated (eg, bone metastases) (RAD-A)
    - If locoregional recurrent or persistent disease, consider locoregional treatment based on disease extent and symptoms (ADV-3)
- PS ___ (performance_status)

# Treatment, PS 0-1

- Clinical trial preferred
- Combination systemic therapy
    - See Principles of Systemic Therapy for Non-Nasopharyngeal Cancers (SYST-A) or Systemic Therapy for Nasopharyngeal Cancers (NASO-B)
- or Single-agent systemic therapy (SYST-A, or NASO-B)
- or For selected patients with limited metastases
    - Surgery (SURG-A)
    - or RT (ADV-A)
    - or Concurrent systemic therapy/RT (ADV-A; SYST-A, or NASO-B)
- or Best supportive care

# Treatment, PS 2-3

- Clinical trial preferred
- Single-agent systemic therapy (SYST-A, or NASO-B)
- or Best supportive care, with or without
    - Palliative RT (ADV-A)
    - or Palliative surgery

# Treatment, PS 4

- Clinical trial preferred
- Best supportive care, with or without palliative RT (ADV-A)

# Persistent disease or progression

- After treatment for PS 0-1
    - Systemic therapy (SYST-A, or NASO-B), clinical trial preferred
    - or Palliative RT (ADV-A)
    - or Best supportive care
- After treatment for PS 2-3
    - Best supportive care
    - or Alternate single-agent systemic therapy (SYST-A, or NASO-B)
    - or Palliative RT (ADV-A)

# Next

- Persistent disease or progression on treatment, re-treat per the persistent disease or progression options on this page
