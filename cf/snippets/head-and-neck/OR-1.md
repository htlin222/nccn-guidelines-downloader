# Source

- NCCN Head and Neck Cancers v2.2026, OR-1, p12
- Cancer of the Oral Cavity (Including Mucosal Lip)
- Applies to buccal mucosa, floor of mouth, oral tongue, alveolar ridge, retromolar trigone, hard palate
- Cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma of the vermilion lip is not included in this guideline, see NCCN Guidelines for Squamous Cell Skin Cancer
- All recommendations category 2A unless stated otherwise

# Workup

- History and physical (H&P), including a complete head and neck exam
    - Mirror and fiberoptic examination as clinically indicated
    - Document and quantify tobacco use history in pack years smoked
    - Document alcohol use, and counsel on it
    - Advise all patients who currently smoke to quit smoking
    - Advise those who formerly smoked to remain abstinent from smoking
    - For additional cessation support, refer to the Smoking Cessation and Treatment Resources in the NCCN Guidelines for Smoking Cessation
    - Screen for distress, using the NCCN Distress Thermometer and Problem List, which includes social determinants of health (DIS-A)
- Biopsy
    - Image-guided (ultrasound or CT) needle biopsy of cystic neck nodes may offer better diagnostic yield than fine-needle aspiration (FNA) by palpation alone for initial diagnosis in this setting
    - For unresectable or metastatic disease where there is a plan for systemic therapy, a core biopsy would allow for biomarker testing
- Multidisciplinary consultation as clinically indicated
- Genetic testing in the context of early age of onset or family history

# Workup as clinically indicated

- Chest CT, with or without contrast (IMG-A)
- CT with contrast and/or MRI with and without contrast of primary and neck
- Consider FDG-PET/CT (IMG-A)
- Examination under anesthesia (EUA) with endoscopy
- Preanesthesia studies
- Dental/prosthodontic evaluation (DENT-A)
    - Including Panorex or dental CT without contrast (IMG-A)
- Nutrition, speech, and swallowing evaluation/therapy (NUTR-A)
- Smoking cessation counseling
- Fertility/reproductive counseling
    - See fertility and reproductive endocrine considerations in the NCCN Guidelines for Adolescent and Young Adult (AYA) Oncology
- Screening for hepatitis B
- PD-L1 testing by immunohistochemistry (IHC), combined positive score (CPS)
