# Treatment of primary and neck

- Endoscopic or open partial laryngectomy + neck dissection, see Principles of Surgery (SURG-A)
- or Definitive RT (SUPRA-A)

# Adverse pathologic features (footnote j)

- Extranodal extension
- Positive margins
- Close margins
- pT3 or pT4 primary
- pN2 or pN3 nodal disease
- Perineural invasion
- Vascular invasion
- Lymphatic invasion
- See Discussion

# Adjuvant treatment by pathology stage

- Node negative (T1-T2,N0)
    - No adjuvant treatment on this page; go to follow-up (FOLL-A, 1 of 2)
- Node negative (T3-T4a,N0)
    - See Adjuvant Treatment (SUPRA-3) and (SUPRA-8)
- One positive node without other adverse pathologic features
    - Consider RT (SUPRA-A)
- Positive node; other adverse pathologic features
    - RT (SUPRA-A)
    - or Consider systemic therapy/RT (SUPRA-A, SYST-A)
- Positive node; positive margin
    - Re-resection, if feasible in highly selected patients
    - or RT (SUPRA-A)
    - or Consider systemic therapy/RT (SUPRA-A, SYST-A)
- Extranodal extension
    - Systemic therapy/RT (SUPRA-A, SYST-A) (category 1)
    - or RT (SUPRA-A) (category 2B, for select patients)
