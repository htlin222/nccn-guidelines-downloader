# Treatment of primary and neck, oligometastatic disease

- Induction chemotherapy, if PS 0–1, followed by RT to primary and regional nodes and to oligometastases as indicated
    - Systemic therapy regimen per Systemic Therapy for Nasopharyngeal Cancers (NASO-B)
    - RT per Principles of Radiation Therapy (NASO-A)
- or Cisplatin/RT
- or Maintenance capecitabine
- or Concurrent cisplatin + RT, if PS 0–1
    - RT per Principles of Radiation Therapy (NASO-A)
- or Systemic therapy, if PS 0–2
    - Regimen per Systemic Therapy for Nasopharyngeal Cancers (NASO-B)

# Treatment of primary and neck, widely metastatic disease

- Widely metastatic and good performance status (PS) 0–2
    - Systemic therapy, per Systemic Therapy for Nasopharyngeal Cancers (NASO-B)
    - If CR or near CR, consider definitive RT to primary and regional nodes (preferred) and to oligometastases as indicated
        - RT per Principles of Radiation Therapy (NASO-A)
        - Evidence for RT to primary and regional nodes: You R, et al. JAMA Oncol 2020;6:1345-1352
    - or Continued systemic therapy, per Systemic Therapy for Nasopharyngeal Cancers (NASO-B)
- Widely metastatic and poor PS 3–4
    - Best supportive care
