# T3,N0

- Primary treatment, one of
    - Resection of primary (SURG-A)
    - Clinical trial
- Adjuvant treatment after resection
    - Strongly consider postoperative RT to primary site (MM-A)
    - With or without systemic therapy (category 2B)
        - Data supporting adjuvant systemic therapy in mucosal melanoma are far fewer than for cutaneous melanoma
        - Options may include nivolumab (category 2B)
        - Options may include cisplatin/temozolomide (category 2B)
        - Nivolumab and hyaluronidase-nvhy subcutaneous injection may be substituted for IV nivolumab
        - Nivolumab and hyaluronidase-nvhy has different dosing and administration instructions compared to IV nivolumab

# T4a,N0

- Primary treatment, one of
    - Resection (SURG-A)
    - Clinical trial
- Adjuvant treatment after resection
    - Postoperative RT to primary site (MM-A)
    - With or without systemic therapy (category 2B)
        - Data supporting adjuvant systemic therapy in mucosal melanoma are far fewer than for cutaneous melanoma
        - Options may include nivolumab (category 2B)
        - Options may include cisplatin/temozolomide (category 2B)
        - Nivolumab and hyaluronidase-nvhy subcutaneous injection may be substituted for IV nivolumab
        - Nivolumab and hyaluronidase-nvhy has different dosing and administration instructions compared to IV nivolumab

# T3-T4a,N1

- Primary treatment, one of
    - Resection plus neck dissection of the positive neck (SURG-A)
    - Clinical trial
- Adjuvant treatment after resection
    - Postoperative RT to primary site and neck (MM-A)
    - With or without systemic therapy (category 2B)
        - Data supporting adjuvant systemic therapy in mucosal melanoma are far fewer than for cutaneous melanoma
        - Options may include nivolumab (category 2B)
        - Options may include cisplatin/temozolomide (category 2B)
        - Nivolumab and hyaluronidase-nvhy subcutaneous injection may be substituted for IV nivolumab
        - Nivolumab and hyaluronidase-nvhy has different dosing and administration instructions compared to IV nivolumab

# T4b,N0

- Primary treatment, one of
    - Clinical trial (preferred)
    - Primary RT (MM-A)
    - Systemic therapy
        - See Systemic Therapy for Metastatic or Unresectable Disease (MELSYS-1) in the NCCN Guidelines for Melanoma: Cutaneous

# T4b,N1

- Primary treatment, one of
    - Clinical trial (preferred)
    - Best supportive care
    - Primary RT (MM-A)
    - Systemic therapy
        - See Systemic Therapy for Metastatic or Unresectable Disease (MELSYS-1) in the NCCN Guidelines for Melanoma: Cutaneous
