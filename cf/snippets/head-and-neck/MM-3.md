# Primary treatment

- T3,N0
    - Resection, per Principles of Surgery (SURG-A)
- T3,N1 or T4a,N0-1
    - Clinical trial (preferred)
    - or Resection with or without neck dissection, per Principles of Surgery (SURG-A)
- T4b,N0
    - Clinical trial (preferred)
    - or Primary RT, per Principles of Radiation Therapy (MM-A), and/or systemic therapy
        - For systemic therapy, see Systemic Therapy for Metastatic or Unresectable Disease (MELSYS-1) in the NCCN Guidelines for Melanoma: Cutaneous
- T4b,N1
    - Clinical trial (preferred)
    - or Best supportive care
    - or Primary RT, per Principles of Radiation Therapy (MM-A)
    - or Systemic therapy
        - See Systemic Therapy for Metastatic or Unresectable Disease (MELSYS-1) in the NCCN Guidelines for Melanoma: Cutaneous

# Adjuvant treatment after resection

- After resection for T3,N0
    - Strongly consider postoperative RT, per Principles of Radiation Therapy (MM-A)
    - With or without systemic therapy (category 2B)
        - Adjuvant systemic therapy may be used for mucosal melanoma, but data to support its use are far fewer than for cutaneous melanoma
        - Options may include nivolumab (category 2B) or cisplatin/temozolomide (category 2B)
        - Nivolumab and hyaluronidase-nvhy subcutaneous injection may be substituted for IV nivolumab
        - Nivolumab and hyaluronidase-nvhy has different dosing and administration instructions compared to IV nivolumab
- After resection with or without neck dissection for T3,N1 or T4a,N0-1
    - Postoperative RT, per Principles of Radiation Therapy (MM-A)
    - With or without systemic therapy (category 2B)
        - Adjuvant systemic therapy may be used for mucosal melanoma, but data to support its use are far fewer than for cutaneous melanoma
        - Options may include nivolumab (category 2B) or cisplatin/temozolomide (category 2B)
        - Nivolumab and hyaluronidase-nvhy subcutaneous injection may be substituted for IV nivolumab
        - Nivolumab and hyaluronidase-nvhy has different dosing and administration instructions compared to IV nivolumab
