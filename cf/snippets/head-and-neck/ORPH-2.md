# Treatment of primary and neck

- Resection of primary and ipsilateral or bilateral neck dissection
    - Principles of Surgery (SURG-A)
    - Tumors in the base of tongue, posterior pharyngeal wall, and soft palate require consideration of bilateral neck treatment, as do tumors of the tonsil invading the tongue base
    - For pN0-N1 and no poor pathologic risk features, single-modality treatment should be considered whenever possible
    - For T1-T2 primary tumors near midline and resected to adequate margins and with no adverse pathologic features, a staged contralateral neck dissection can be performed in order to avoid RT
    - Lateral tumors pN0-N1 resected with favorable pathologic features can be observed
    - If PD-L1 positive (CPS ≥ 1): neoadjuvant pembrolizumab, except for N0 disease
        - Principles of Systemic Therapy for Non-Nasopharyngeal Cancers (SYST-A)
- or Definitive RT
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy (ORPH-A)
- or For T1-2,N1 only: concurrent systemic therapy/RT (category 2B)
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy (ORPH-A)
    - Principles of Systemic Therapy for Non-Nasopharyngeal Cancers (SYST-A)

# Adjuvant treatment after resection

- If neoadjuvant pembrolizumab received
    - RT + pembrolizumab, with cisplatin if extranodal extension and/or positive margin, followed by adjuvant pembrolizumab
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy (ORPH-A)
    - Principles of Systemic Therapy for Non-Nasopharyngeal Cancers (SYST-A)
- pN0 and no adverse pathologic features
    - No adjuvant treatment; go to follow-up (FOLL-A, 1 of 2)
- pN1 without other adverse pathologic features
    - Consider RT (ORPH-A)
- Adverse pathologic features
    - Defined as extranodal extension, positive margins, close margins, pT3 or pT4 primary, pN2 or pN3 nodal disease, nodal disease in levels IV or V, perineural invasion, vascular invasion, and lymphatic invasion (Discussion)
    - Extranodal extension with or without positive margin
        - Systemic therapy/RT (ORPH-A, SYST-A)
    - Positive margin
        - Re-resection, if feasible
        - or RT (ORPH-A)
        - or Systemic therapy/RT (ORPH-A, SYST-A)
    - Other risk features
        - RT (ORPH-A)
        - or Consider systemic therapy/RT (ORPH-A, SYST-A)
