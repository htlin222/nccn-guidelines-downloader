# Source

- NCCN Head and Neck Cancers v2.2026, SUPRA-7, p58
- Cancer of the Supraglottic Larynx
- Applies to response assessment after induction chemotherapy
    - Induction chemotherapy per Principles of Systemic Therapy for Non-Nasopharyngeal Cancers (SYST-A)
- Radiation therapy throughout this page: Principles of Radiation Therapy (SUPRA-A)
- Surgery throughout this page: Principles of Surgery (SURG-A)
- All recommendations are category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Response: CR at primary site

- Definitive RT (category 1), per Principles of Radiation Therapy (SUPRA-A)
- Then Post Systemic Therapy/RT or RT Neck Evaluation (FOLL-A, 2 of 2)

# Response: PR at primary site

- RT (category 1) (SUPRA-A)
- or Systemic therapy/RT (category 2B) (SUPRA-A, SYST-A)
- Then Post Systemic Therapy/RT or RT Neck Evaluation (FOLL-A, 2 of 2)

# Response: less than PR at primary site

- Surgery, per Principles of Surgery (SURG-A)
    - Then adjuvant treatment, below

# Unresectable primary or nodal disease

- Treatment of very advanced head and neck cancer, go to ADV-1

# Adjuvant treatment after surgery

- No adverse pathologic features
    - RT (SUPRA-A)
- Adverse pathologic features, defined as extranodal extension, positive margins, close margins, pT3 or pT4 primary, pN2 or pN3 nodal disease, perineural invasion, vascular invasion, and lymphatic invasion (Discussion)
    - Extranodal extension and/or positive margin
        - Systemic therapy/RT (category 1) (SUPRA-A, SYST-A)
    - Other risk features
        - RT (SUPRA-A)
        - or Consider systemic therapy/RT (SUPRA-A, SYST-A)

# Next

- After definitive RT, RT, or systemic therapy/RT, Post Systemic Therapy/RT or RT Neck Evaluation (FOLL-A, 2 of 2)
- Follow-up, go to FOLL-A (1 of 2)
- Unresectable primary or nodal disease, go to ADV-1
- Recurrent or persistent disease, go to ADV-3
