# Source

- NCCN Head and Neck Cancers v2.2026, MAXI-2, p67
- Applies to maxillary sinus tumors staged T1-2, N0, for primary and adjuvant treatment
- For SNUC with neuroendocrine features, small cell, high-grade olfactory esthesioneuroblastoma, or SNEC histologies, systemic therapy should be a part of the overall treatment, in both primary and adjuvant treatment
    - Consider a clinical trial
    - Consider referral to a major medical center that specializes in these diseases
    - Principles of Systemic Therapy for Non-Nasopharyngeal Cancers (SYST-A)
- All recommendations are category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Primary treatment, T1-2, N0, all histologies except adenoid cystic

- Resection
    - Principles of Surgery (SURG-A)
    - See the salivary gland algorithm for management after resection, see Salivary Gland Tumors (SALI-1)

# Adjuvant treatment, all histologies except adenoid cystic

- Margin negative
    - Follow-up (FOLL-A, 1 of 2)
- Perineural, vascular, or lymphatic invasion
    - Consider RT
        - Principles of Radiation Therapy (MAXI-A)
    - or Consider systemic therapy/RT (category 2B)
        - Principles of Radiation Therapy (MAXI-A)
        - Principles of Systemic Therapy for Non-Nasopharyngeal Cancers (SYST-A)
- Close or positive margin
    - RT
        - Principles of Radiation Therapy (MAXI-A)
    - or Consider systemic therapy/RT (category 2B)
        - Principles of Radiation Therapy (MAXI-A)
        - Principles of Systemic Therapy for Non-Nasopharyngeal Cancers (SYST-A)

# Primary treatment, T1-2, N0, adenoid cystic

- Resection
    - Principles of Surgery (SURG-A)
    - See the salivary gland algorithm for management after resection, see Salivary Gland Tumors (SALI-1)

# Adjuvant treatment, adenoid cystic

- Margin negative
    - Consider RT
        - Principles of Radiation Therapy (MAXI-A)
- Positive margin
    - Re-resection if feasible
        - See the salivary gland algorithm for management after resection, see Salivary Gland Tumors (SALI-1)
    - RT (preferred)
        - Principles of Systemic Therapy for Non-Nasopharyngeal Cancers (SYST-A)
        - For adenoid cystic tumors and minor salivary gland tumors, see SALI-A
    - or Consider observation, for margin negative, no perineural spread
