- NCCN Head and Neck Cancers v2.2026, NASO-2, p37
- Applies to cancer of the nasopharynx, clinical staging M0, treatment of primary and neck
- The recommendations are based on clinical trial data for those with EBV-associated nasopharynx cancer (Discussion)
- All recommendations are category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# T1, N0, M0

- Definitive RT
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy (NASO-A)

# T2, N0, M0

- Definitive RT
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy (NASO-A)
- With or without concurrent systemic therapy, if high-risk features
    - High-risk features include bulky tumor volume and high serum EBV DNA copy number
    - Systemic Therapy for Nasopharyngeal Cancers (NASO-B)

# T0 (EBV+)-2, N1, M0 or T3, N0, M0

- Concurrent systemic therapy/RT
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy (NASO-A)
    - Systemic Therapy for Nasopharyngeal Cancers (NASO-B)
- Consider induction or adjuvant chemotherapy, if high-risk features
    - High-risk features include bulky tumor volume and high serum EBV DNA copy number
    - Systemic Therapy for Nasopharyngeal Cancers (NASO-B)

# T3, N1-3, M0 or T4, N0-3, M0 or T0 (EBV+)-2, N2-3, M0

- Clinical trials (preferred)
- or Induction chemotherapy followed by systemic therapy/RT (preferred) (category 1)
    - See Discussion on induction chemotherapy
    - Systemic Therapy for Nasopharyngeal Cancers (NASO-B)
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy (NASO-A)
- or Concurrent systemic therapy/RT followed by adjuvant chemotherapy
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy (NASO-A)
    - Systemic Therapy for Nasopharyngeal Cancers (NASO-B)
- or Concurrent systemic therapy/RT (category 2B)
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy (NASO-A)
    - Systemic Therapy for Nasopharyngeal Cancers (NASO-B)
