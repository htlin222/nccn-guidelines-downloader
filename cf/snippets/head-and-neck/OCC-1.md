# Presentation

- H&P
    - Document and quantify tobacco use history, in pack years smoked
    - Document alcohol use, and counsel
    - Advise all patients who currently smoke to quit smoking
    - Advise those who formerly smoked to remain abstinent from smoking
    - For additional cessation support, refer to the Smoking Cessation and Treatment Resources in the NCCN Guidelines for Smoking Cessation
    - Screen for distress, using the NCCN Distress Thermometer and Problem List, which includes social determinants of health (DIS-A)
- Complete head and neck exam
    - Attention to skin
    - Palpation of the oropharynx
    - Mirror and fiberoptic examination as clinically indicated, to examine nasopharynx, oropharynx, hypopharynx, and larynx
- FNA
    - Repeat FNA, core, or open biopsy may be necessary for uncertain or nondiagnostic histologies
    - Patient should be prepared for neck dissection at time of open biopsy, if indicated

# Pathology on FNA

- Squamous cell carcinoma, adenocarcinoma, and anaplastic/undifferentiated epithelial tumors, go to the workup below
    - Determined with appropriate immunohistochemical stains
- Lymphoma, see NCCN Guidelines for B-Cell Lymphomas and NCCN Guidelines for T-Cell Lymphomas
- Thyroid, see NCCN Guidelines for Thyroid Carcinoma
- Melanoma, workup and treatment per NCCN Guidelines for Melanoma: Cutaneous
    - Skin exam, note regressing lesions
    - Workup for Mucosal Melanoma (MM-1)
    - Primary Therapy for Mucosal Melanoma (MM-4)

# Workup of squamous cell carcinoma, adenocarcinoma, and anaplastic/undifferentiated epithelial tumors

- CT with contrast, or MRI with and without contrast, of skull base through thoracic inlet (IMG-A)
- FDG-PET/CT as indicated, before EUA (IMG-A)
- Chest CT with contrast, if PET/CT not done (IMG-A)
- HPV, EBV testing for squamous cell or undifferentiated histology
    - p16-positive unknown primary disease should only be considered HPV-positive with HPV-specific testing
    - Whether HPV or EBV positive status may help to define the radiation fields is being investigated, see Principles of Radiation Therapy (OCC-A) and Discussion
- Thyroglobulin, calcitonin, PAX8, and/or thyroid transcription factor (TTF) staining for adenocarcinoma and anaplastic/undifferentiated tumors

# As clinically indicated

- Dental evaluation (DENT-A)
- Nutrition, speech, and swallowing evaluation/therapy (NUTR-A)
- Smoking cessation counseling
- Fertility/reproductive counseling, see fertility and reproductive endocrine considerations in the NCCN Guidelines for Adolescent and Young Adult (AYA) Oncology
- Screening for hepatitis B

# Next

- T0 and HPV-positive, treat as oropharyngeal cancer (ORPH-1)
- T0 and EBV-positive or EBER-positive, treat as nasopharyngeal cancer (NASO-1)
- Primary found, treat as appropriate (NCCN Guidelines Index)
- Primary not found, Workup and Treatment (OCC-2)
    - Strongly consider referral to a high-volume, multidisciplinary cancer center
