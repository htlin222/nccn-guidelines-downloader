# N1 without extranodal extension

- RT
    - Target volume determined by tumor size, nodal station, and HPV and EBV status
    - Whether HPV or EBV positive status may help to define the radiation fields is being investigated, see Principles of Radiation Therapy (OCC-A) and Discussion
    - Use treatment for nasopharyngeal (NASO-2) and p16-positive oropharyngeal cancers (ORPHPV-3 and ORPHPV-4) to guide management of EBV-positive and HPV-positive occult primary tumors
- or Observe

# N2, N3 without extranodal extension

- RT
    - Target volume determined by tumor size, nodal station, and HPV and EBV status
    - Whether HPV or EBV positive status may help to define the radiation fields is being investigated, see Principles of Radiation Therapy (OCC-A) and Discussion
    - Use treatment for nasopharyngeal (NASO-2) and p16-positive oropharyngeal cancers (ORPHPV-3 and ORPHPV-4) to guide management of EBV-positive and HPV-positive occult primary tumors
- or Consider systemic therapy/RT (category 2B)
    - Whether HPV or EBV positive status may help to define the radiation fields is being investigated, see Principles of Radiation Therapy (OCC-A) and Discussion
    - Principles of Systemic Therapy for Non-Nasopharyngeal Cancers (SYST-A)
