# Oncology team interventions

- Collaborate with palliative care specialist/team
    - A patient with one or more positive indicators may benefit from a care plan developed by an interprofessional specialty palliative care team
    - Oncologists should integrate palliative care into general oncology care
    - Consider early consultation/collaboration with a specialty palliative care/hospice team, to improve quality of life and survival
- Collaborate with other health care professionals treating the patient
- Evaluate and treat undiagnosed physical and mental health issues
- Consider additional referrals
    - Mental/behavioral health
    - Psychosocial, spiritual services
    - Other specialists
    - Substance use disorders
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Adult Cancer Pain
- Mobilize community support
    - Religious/spiritual
    - School
    - Community agencies
- Introduce advance care planning conversations
- Expedite referral to hospice services when appropriate

# Reassessment outcomes

- Patient satisfied with response to cancer therapy
- Adequate symptom management
- Reduction of patient/family/caregiver distress, if needed
- Acceptable sense of control
- Decreased caregiver burden
- Strengthened relationships
- Optimized quality of life
- Personal growth and enhanced meaning
- Advance care planning in progress

# Ongoing reassessment, if ongoing needs

- Assess for communication barriers and address as needed
    - Interpretation services
    - Improved literacy of materials
    - Palliative care
- Promote adaptive coping
- Re-evaluate intervention options and intensify as possible
- See NCCN Guidelines for Distress Management
