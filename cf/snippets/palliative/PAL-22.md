# Interventions

- Assess patient's desire to have insomnia and sedation treated, and provide cognitive behavioral interventions
- Educate patient/family/caregivers on patient's condition and treatment options
- Evaluate type/severity of sleep-wake disturbance
    - Include daytime impairments
    - Include co-sleeper observations
- Explore worries and anxiety regarding cancer-related stressors
- Provide sleep hygiene education
- Refer to cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I)
    - CBT-I is preferred over pharmacologic interventions as first-line therapy
    - Reference: Edinger J, et al. J Clin Sleep Med 2021;17:255-262
    - Sleep restriction
    - Stimulus control
- Assess for and treat contributing factors
    - Pain
    - Depression
    - Anxiety
    - Delirium
    - Nausea
    - Medication side effects or withdrawal syndromes
        - eg, corticosteroids, opioids, anticonvulsants, caffeine, hormones, herbals, barbiturates, benzodiazepines, alcohol, TCAs
    - Primary sleep disorders such as obstructive sleep apnea, restless legs syndrome, and periodic limb movement disorder
        - Continuous positive airway pressure (CPAP) / bilevel positive airway pressure (BiPAP)
        - Obtain sleep study, if prognosis allows
- Consider consult to physiatrist for tailored exercise therapy program

# Pharmacologic therapy for refractory sleep/wake disturbance

- Consider addition of pharmacologic therapies for refractory sleep/wake disturbance
- Insomnia
    - Alpha adrenergic blockers
    - Antipsychotics
    - Melatonin-receptor agonists
    - Sedative/hypnotics
    - Antidepressants
- Daytime sedation
    - CNS stimulant/wakefulness-promoting agents
- See Palliative Care Drug Appendix (PAL-A) for specific recommendations for medical management of symptoms

# Reassessment outcomes

- Adequate management of symptoms
- Reduction of patient/family/caregiver distress

# Next

- Outcomes met, continue to treat and monitor symptoms and quality of life
- If ongoing needs
    - Re-evaluate contributing etiologies
    - Change insomnia or antisedation therapy
    - Re-evaluate palliative care interventions and intensify as possible
    - Consult or refer to specialized palliative care services or hospice
- Ongoing reassessment
