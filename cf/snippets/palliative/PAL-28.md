- NCCN Palliative Care v3.2026, PAL-28, p37
- Applies to preparing patients/families/caregivers for end of life and transition to hospice care
- Estimated life expectancy years, months to year, weeks to months, or days to weeks (dying patient)
- All recommendations are category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Interventions

- Assess prognostic awareness and patient/family/caregiver understanding of the expected course of disease
- Provide clear, consistent discussion with the patient/family/caregiver about prognosis and anticipated care needs on an ongoing basis
- Facilitate advance care planning (PAL-29)
    - Assess for decision-making capacity and need for a surrogate decision maker
    - Elicit values and preferences with respect to quality of life
- Introduce, educate, and assess need for specialized palliative care or eligibility and readiness for hospice care

# Interventions for the dying patient, days to weeks

- Educate patient/family/caregiver on dying process
- Address potential need for transitions in care, while ensuring continued involvement of primary care physician and primary oncology team
- Refer to hospice care agencies
- Provide information and additional referrals, as necessary, for
    - Psychosocial assessment
        - Legacy work
        - Grief counseling
    - Spiritual screening
        - Spiritual support if needed
    - Funeral/memorial service planning
- Respect goals and needs of the patient/family/caregiver regarding the dying process
- Inform other involved health care professionals of the patient's death

# Outcomes

- Reduction of patient/family/caregiver distress
- Acceptable sense of control
- Increased family and caregiver resilience
- Strengthened relationships
- Decreased caregiver burden
- Optimized quality of life
- Personal growth and enhanced meaning

# Next

- If ongoing needs, re-evaluate palliative care interventions and intensify as possible
- Ongoing reassessment
