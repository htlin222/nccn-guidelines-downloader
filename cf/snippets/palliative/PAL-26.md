# Source

- NCCN Palliative Care v3.2026, PAL-26, p35
- Applies to social support/resource management at estimated life expectancy of years, months to year, or weeks to months
- Days to weeks (dying patient), go to interventions (PAL-27) instead
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

    - See NCCN Guidelines for Distress Management (DIS-A)
- Need for medical interpreters/translators ___ (interpreter_need)
    - Obtain as needed, and they must not be related to the patient

# Interventions

- Mobilize family and community financial resources
- Ensure support and education to caregiver(s) and family members
    - Counseling
    - Child life services
    - Art and music therapy, if available
    - Support groups
    - Physical medicine and rehabilitation training for transfers and repositioning
    - Psychosocial, spiritual support
- Respond to caregiver-specific burdens and stresses
- Periodically assess and monitor family/caregiver for risk factors associated with complicated grief
- Consider respite care for families and caregivers in crisis

# Reassessment outcomes

- Adequate management
- Optimized quality of life
- Decreased caregiver burden
- Reduction of patient/family/caregiver distress
- Acceptable sense of control
- Ongoing re-evaluation and communication between the patient, caregiver(s), family members, and health care team
