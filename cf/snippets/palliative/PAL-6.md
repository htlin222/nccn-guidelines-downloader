# Consider consultation with specialty palliative care team

- Address patient/family/caregiver concerns about course of disease and decision-making
- Advanced cancer
    - Advanced solid tumors and hematologic malignancies
- Communication needs
    - Language
    - Literacy
    - Culture
    - Cognitive impairment
- Complexity of care needs
    - Physical needs
    - Psychosocial needs
    - Spiritual needs
    - Existential needs
- Complexities related to social determinants of health
    - Refer to the NCCN Distress Thermometer and Problem List, which includes social determinants of health
- Concerns about decision-making capacity
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Older Adult Oncology (OAO-1)
- Evidence of worsening prognosis, including but not limited to:
    - Poor performance status (eg, ECOG ≥3 or KPS ≤50)
    - Cachexia
    - Persistent delirium
- Experiences of palliative care emergency
    - See Discussion for examples
- Frailty
- Frequent emergency department visits or hospital admissions
- High burden of palliative care needs, including distress related to cancer diagnosis and cancer therapy
- High level of psychological distress based on standardized distress screening
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Distress Management (DIS-A)
- High risk of poor pain management, or pain that remains resistant to conventional interventions
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Adult Cancer Pain (PAIN-K, PAIN-L, PAIN-M)
- Limited cancer treatment options due to:
    - Limited access to cancer treatment
    - Disease refractory to cancer treatment
    - Multiple and/or severe comorbid conditions
    - Progressive functional decline or persistently poor performance status
- Need for intensive care unit (ICU)-level care
    - Especially involving multi-organ system failure or prolonged mechanical support
- Need for invasive procedures (eg, palliative stenting or venting gastrostomy)
- Patient/family requests consultation
- Poor prognostic awareness and/or need for advance care planning/clarification of goals of care
- Expressions of distress, including requests for hastened death
- Substance use disorder
- Uncontrolled symptoms

# Next

- Any of the above present, consider consultation with specialty palliative care team
