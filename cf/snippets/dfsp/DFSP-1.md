# Preliminary workup

- History and physical (H&P)
- Biopsy (DFSP-A)
    - Punch, incisional, or core biopsy is strongly recommended, for sufficient tissue sampling and accurate pathologic assessment
        - Preferably include the deeper subcutaneous layer
        - This tumor is frequently misdiagnosed due to inadequate tissue sampling or superficial biopsy
    - Wide undermining is discouraged, due to the difficulty of interpreting subsequent re-excisions pathologically
    - Rebiopsy if the biopsy is indeterminate, or if clinical suspicion remains
    - Hematoxylin and eosin (H&E)
    - Immunopanel (eg, CD34, factor XIIIa)

- DFSP confirmed

# Additional workup once DFSP is confirmed

- Complete skin exam
- Strongly consider multidisciplinary consultation at a center with specialized expertise
    - Especially for large or recurrent dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans (DFSP)
    - Decisions about diagnosis and resection may be complex
- Consider preoperative MRI with contrast, for treatment planning, if extensive subcutaneous extension is suspected
