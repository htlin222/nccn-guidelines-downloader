# Referral

- Consider referral to pulmonary medicine for symptom management

# First-line treatment

- Observe
- Or a trial of a somatostatin analog for symptom control
    - Only if chronic cough/dyspnea is not responsive to inhalers or conventional treatment
    - Octreotide acetate LAR 20–30 mg IM every 4 weeks
    - Or lanreotide 120 mg SC every 4 weeks
    - Higher doses have been shown to be safe
    - For breakthrough symptoms, octreotide 100–250 mcg SC TID can be considered
    - If injection site-related complications occur, consider switching to another SSA
    - Supporting evidence: Almquist, J Clin Oncol 2019; Al-Toubah, Chest 2020

# Surveillance

- Chest CT (without contrast) every 12–24 mo
- Or as clinically indicated
