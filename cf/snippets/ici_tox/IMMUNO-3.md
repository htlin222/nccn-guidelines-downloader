# CARDIO

- Myocarditis
    - Chest pain
    - Dyspnea
    - Fatigue
    - Palpitations (arrhythmia: heart block or ventricular ectopic beats)
    - Syncope
    - Generalized weakness
    - May occur in conjunction with myositis and/or myasthenia gravis; these entities must be ruled out

# DERM

- Bullous dermatitis
    - Inflammation of the skin
    - Presence of bullae, which are filled with fluid
    - The most common immune-related bullous dermatitis is bullous pemphigoid
- Maculopapular rash (morbilliform rash)
    - Macules (flat)
    - Papules (elevated)
- Pruritus
    - Itching sensation, with or without rash
    - May be intense or widespread
    - Intermittent
    - Skin changes from scratching (eg, edema, excoriations, lichenification, oozing/crusts)
    - Limiting instrumental activities of daily living (iADLs)
- Stevens-Johnson syndrome (SJS) and toxic epidermal necrolysis (TEN)
    - Separation of the dermis involving <10% body surface area (BSA), SJS
    - Separation of the dermis involving 10%-30% BSA, overlapping SJS/TEN
    - Separation of the dermis involving >30% BSA, TEN
- Lichen planus
    - Violaceous (dark red/purple) papules and plaques without scale over the trunk and extremities
    - Significant pruritus
    - Erosions and striae (white lines intersecting) in the oral and vulvar mucosa
- Psoriasis and psoriasiform disease
    - Thick red scaly plaques
    - Accentuated on extensor surfaces, scalp, umbilicus, postauricular surfaces
- Oral mucosa inflammation
    - Irritated gums and/or oropharynx
    - Red/white lesions and/or ulcers
    - Lichen planus
    - Mucositis
- Dry mouth (Sicca syndrome)
    - Dry mouth, may cause difficulty with speaking, eating, swallowing, and/or staying asleep
    - Oral sensitivity
    - Dysarthria
    - Dysphagia
    - Dysgeusia
    - Dental caries/erosion with prolonged salivary hypofunction
    - Dry eye, lack of lubrication
- Oral dysesthesia
    - Pain most often described as "burning" in the absence of, or disproportionate to, skin changes
    - Oral sensitivity
    - Dysgeusia, phantogeusia, or other altered sensation with normal clinical findings

# ENDO

- Hyperglycemia-related diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA)
    - Excessive thirst
    - Frequent urination
    - General weakness
    - Vomiting
    - Confusion
    - Abdominal pain
    - Dry skin
    - Dry mouth
    - Increased heart rate
    - Fruity odor on the breath
- Hypothyroidism
    - Fatigue
    - Lethargy
    - Sensation of being cold
    - Possible constipation
    - Bradycardia
    - Patient may be asymptomatic or exhibit minimal symptoms
- Thyrotoxicosis due to thyroiditis
    - Most patients have minimal, if any, symptoms
    - If symptoms do arise, may include (uncommonly) tachycardia, tremor, anxiety
    - If symptoms do arise, may also include enlarged and tender thyroid gland (rarely)
- Hypophysitis
    - Acute onset headache
    - Photophobia
    - Nausea/emesis
    - Fatigue
    - Muscle weakness
    - May have low blood pressure
- Primary adrenal insufficiency
    - High ACTH with low morning cortisol
    - Abnormal cosyntropin stimulation test
    - A rare diagnosis, not usually associated with checkpoint immunotherapy
- Central hypothyroidism
    - Symptoms of overt hypothyroidism (fatigue, bradycardia, lethargy, sensation of being cold, possible constipation)
    - Plus symptoms of central adrenal insufficiency (nausea/emesis, not feeling well, generalized malaise)
