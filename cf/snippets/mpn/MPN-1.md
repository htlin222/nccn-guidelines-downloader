# History and physical

- History and physical (H&P)
    - Spleen size by palpation
    - Evaluation of thrombotic/hemorrhagic events
    - Evaluation of cardiovascular risk factors
- Documentation of transfusion/medication history
- Assessment of symptom burden using MPN Symptom Assessment Form Total Symptom Score (MPN-SAF TSS; MPN-E)

# Blood tests

- Complete blood count (CBC) with differential
    - Examination of blood smear
- Comprehensive metabolic panel
    - Uric acid
    - Lactate dehydrogenase (LDH)
    - Liver function tests (LFTs)
- Serum erythropoietin (EPO) level
- Serum iron studies
- Fluorescence in situ hybridization (FISH) or multiplex reverse transcriptase polymerase chain reaction (RT-PCR) (if available) for BCR::ABL1, to exclude the diagnosis of chronic myeloid leukemia (CML)
    - If BCR::ABL1-positive, see NCCN Guidelines for Chronic Myeloid Leukemia
- Human leukocyte antigen (HLA) testing, if considering allogeneic hematopoietic cell transplant (HCT)
    - See MF-1 and MF-2

# Bone marrow

- Bone marrow aspirate with iron stain
- Bone marrow biopsy with trichrome and reticulin stain
- Bone marrow cytogenetics
    - If inaspirable, metaphase karyotype on peripheral blood or second core, or use FISH for MDS- or MPN-associated abnormalities on peripheral blood
- Interpret against WHO and International Consensus Classification (ICC) diagnostic criteria
    - Primary myelofibrosis (PMF), see MPN-A
    - Polycythemia vera (PV) and post-PV MF, see MPN-B
    - Essential thrombocythemia (ET) and post-ET MF, see MPN-C
- If there is evidence of mast cell aggregates in the bone marrow, see NCCN Guidelines for Systemic Mastocytosis for diagnostic workup

# Molecular testing

- Molecular testing (blood or bone marrow) with multigene panel testing
    - To detect driver mutations (JAK2, CALR, MPL)
    - To detect other pathogenic variants, for diagnosis and prognostic risk stratification
    - Prognostic models incorporating other mutations have been proposed to identify patients with myelofibrosis (MF) as well as PV and ET, to better estimate overall survival (OS), MF-free survival (PV and ET), and rates of leukemic transformation
    - Next-generation sequencing (NGS) is useful to establish clonality
    - Somatic mutations with prognostic significance in patients with MF, see MPN-D

# Acquired von Willebrand syndrome testing

- Consider testing for acquired von Willebrand syndrome (VWS), in patients with elevated platelet count undergoing surgical procedures, or those with unexplained bleeding
    - Plasma von Willebrand factor antigen (VWFA) measurement
    - Von Willebrand activity
    - An expanded panel including VWFA, factor VIII activity, and VWS multimers may be useful under certain circumstances
