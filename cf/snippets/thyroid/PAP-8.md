# Disease monitoring

- Physical examination
- Neck ultrasound at 6–12 months
- TSH
- Tg and TgAb measurement
    - In selected patients who may be at higher risk for residual/recurrent disease (eg, N1 patients), obtain a Tg measurement
    - In those same selected patients, consider concomitant diagnostic RAI imaging

# Finding: rising Tg or new TgAb

- Interpretation of rising or new TgAb is assay dependent
    - Best performed as a radioimmunoassay
    - Use a consistent assay for interpretation of trends
- Consider additional imaging
    - Neck CT or MRI with contrast
    - Chest or abdomen CT
- Consider FDG-PET
- Consider RAI imaging

# Finding: abnormal imaging and/or rising Tg

- Consider biopsy of suspicious areas on imaging with Tg washout
- Follow-up imaging of known iodine-avid disease with imaging (CT or MRI)

# Finding: NED

- Physical examination
- TSH
    - Principles of TSH suppression (THYR-A)
- Tg and TgAb measurement annually if stable
- Neck ultrasound every 1–3 years for 5–8 years, and then discontinue
- See NCCN Guidelines for Survivorship
