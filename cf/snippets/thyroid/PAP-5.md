# Source

- NCCN Thyroid Carcinoma v2.2026, PAP-5, p19
- Papillary carcinoma, RAI being considered based on clinicopathologic features
- Applies to clinicopathologic findings prompting consideration for RAI (PAP-4)
- Applies only without gross residual disease and without known distant metastasis
- Decision is made 6–12 weeks postthyroidectomy
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- ___ (weeks_postop) weeks postthyroidectomy
- Pathology: ___ (pathology)
- Postoperative Tg: ___ (tg)
- No gross residual disease
- No known distant metastasis
- Decide: RAI therapy indicated based on pathology, or not indicated based on pathology

# If RAI therapy indicated based on pathology

- Consider pretreatment neck imaging if postoperative Tg is higher than expected
    - A false-negative pretreatment scan is possible, and should not prevent the use of RAI if otherwise indicated
- Resect clinically significant structural disease if possible
- RAI therapy for remnant ablation or adjuvant therapy
    - Principles of Radiation and RAI Therapy (THYR-C)
    - Pre-ablation diagnostic scans in this setting are commonly done at NCCN Member Institutions, but the Panel recommends selective use, based on:
        - Pathology
        - Postoperative Tg
        - Intraoperative findings
        - Available imaging studies
    - Dosimetry studies are considered in patients at high risk of having RAI-avid distant metastasis
    - Empiric RAI doses may exceed maximum tolerable activity levels in patients with decreased glomerular filtration rate (GFR)
    - Patients on dialysis require special handling

# Next

- RAI therapy indicated based on pathology, give RAI therapy as above, then go to Disease Monitoring and Management (PAP-7)
- RAI therapy not indicated based on pathology, go to Disease Monitoring and Management (PAP-7)
- Levothyroxine to appropriate TSH target (THYR-A)
