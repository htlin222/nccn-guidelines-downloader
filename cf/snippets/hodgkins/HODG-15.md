# Source

- NCCN Hodgkin Lymphoma (Age >=18 years) v2.2026, HODG-15, p33
- Applies to relapsed or refractory classic Hodgkin lymphoma (CHL) in a patient who is not a candidate for HDT/ASCR
- There are no data to support a superior outcome with any of the treatment modalities
- Individualized treatment is recommended
- All recommendations are category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- ___ (age) yo, classic Hodgkin lymphoma, relapsed or refractory
- Not a candidate for HDT/ASCR
- Prior therapy ___ (prior_therapy)
- Prior CPI exposure ___ (prior_cpi)
- Prior BV exposure ___ (prior_bv)
- Interval from CPI- or BV-containing regimen to progression ___ (progression_interval)
- Received abbreviated chemotherapy, 3-4 cycles, without RT ___ (abbreviated_chemo_no_rt)
- Deauville score at restaging ___ (deauville)
    - FDG-PET 5-Point Scale (Deauville Criteria) (HODG-A, 2 of 2)

# Before second-line therapy

- Repeat FDG-PET/CT or diagnostic CT
    - Principles of FDG-PET/CT (HODG-A)
- Clinical trial, if available
- Refer to or consult a center with expertise

# Second-line therapy

- No prior CPI, or progression after >=3 months of a CPI-containing regimen
    - CPI- or BV-containing regimen x 4 cycles
        - Principles of Systemic Therapy for Relapsed or Refractory Disease: CHL (HODG-B 5 of 8)
        - If CPI- or BV-containing regimen contraindicated, an alternative second-line therapy option should be utilized (HODG-B 5 of 8)
    - Restage with FDG-PET/CT
        - Principles of FDG-PET/CT (HODG-A)
- Prior CPI or BV, and progression after <3 months of a CPI- or BV-containing regimen
    - CPI-containing regimen x 4 cycles
        - Principles of Systemic Therapy for Relapsed or Refractory Disease: CHL (HODG-B 5 of 8)
        - If CPI- or BV-containing regimen contraindicated, an alternative second-line therapy option should be utilized (HODG-B 5 of 8)
    - Restage with FDG-PET/CT
        - Principles of FDG-PET/CT (HODG-A)
- Patients who received abbreviated chemotherapy, 3-4 cycles, without RT
    - Second-line systemic therapy, CPI-based therapy preferred if no prior CPI exposure
        - Principles of Systemic Therapy for Relapsed or Refractory Disease: CHL (HODG-B 5 of 8)
    - Plus ISRT
        - Principles of Radiation Therapy (HODG-C)
        - Timing of RT may vary
    - Restage with FDG-PET/CT
        - Principles of FDG-PET/CT (HODG-A)

# Additional therapy after CPI- or BV-containing regimen

- Deauville 1-4, or Deauville 5 with clinical benefit
    - Continue CPI- or BV-containing regimen (HODG-B 5 of 8) as long as deriving clinical benefit, for a CPI-containing regimen, or until progression or unacceptable toxicity
    - Repeat imaging no more than every 3 months unless there is concern for progression
    - With or without ISRT (HODG-C)
- Deauville 5 without clinical benefit, or new symptomatic disease
    - Subsequent therapy, with or without ISRT (HODG-C)
        - Subsequent systemic therapy options include second-line therapy options that were not previously used (HODG-B 5 of 8)
        - Choice depends on prior therapies, prior toxicities, and comorbidities

# Additional therapy after CPI-containing regimen

- Deauville 1-4
    - Continue CPI-containing regimen (HODG-B 5 of 8) as long as deriving clinical benefit
    - Repeat imaging no more than every 3 months unless there is concern for progression
    - With or without ISRT (HODG-C)
- Deauville 5 without clinical benefit, or new symptomatic disease
    - Subsequent therapy, with or without ISRT (HODG-C)
        - Subsequent systemic therapy options include second-line therapy options that were not previously used (HODG-B 5 of 8)
        - Choice depends on prior therapies, prior toxicities, and comorbidities

# Next

- Deauville 1-3 after second-line systemic therapy plus ISRT, go to follow-up (HODG-12)
- Deauville 4-5 after second-line systemic therapy plus ISRT
- Continuing a CPI- or BV-containing regimen, repeat imaging no more than every 3 months unless there is concern for progression
