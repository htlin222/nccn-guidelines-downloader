# Considerations in patients who are older

- CHL in patients who are older is associated with poorer disease outcomes
- The following are more frequent in this population
    - B symptoms
    - Poor performance status
    - Mixed cellularity histologic subtype
    - EBV+ disease
    - Medical comorbidities
- Standard chemotherapy regimens in patients who are older are associated with
    - Dose reductions
    - Treatment toxicity
    - Treatment-related mortality
- There are limited prospective data evaluating alternatives to standard therapies for patients >60 years

# Choosing first-line therapy

- Clinical trial is recommended when available
- Selection of standard versus alternate first-line therapy for a patient >60 years should be based on clinical judgment
    - Goal is to minimize toxicity while maximizing efficacy
- Consider the regimens listed in Principles of Systemic Therapy (HODG-B 2 of 8) in patients >60 years or those unfit for intensive therapy, to lessen/minimize toxicity
    - These regimens have not been proven to overcome the poorer disease outcomes observed in patients >60 years
- Radiation therapy alone is an option when systemic therapy is not considered feasible or safe
    - ISRT alone
    - Extended-field radiation therapy (EFRT) alone
