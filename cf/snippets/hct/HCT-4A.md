# Source

- NCCN Hematopoietic Cell Transplantation v3.2026, HCT-4A, p13
- Hematopoietic cell mobilization regimens, for autologous donors and for allogeneic donors
- An FDA-approved biosimilar is an appropriate substitute for any recommended systemic biologic therapy in the NCCN Guidelines
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Autologous donors

- Filgrastim, with or without plerixafor
    - Filgrastim 10 mcg/kg weight SC for 4-5 days
        - Continued daily until collection goal is met
        - Tbo-filgrastim or an FDA-approved biosimilar is an appropriate substitute for filgrastim
    - Plerixafor 0.24 mg/kg actual body weight SC (max 40 mg/day) on the day before apheresis
        - Generally administered 11 hours prior to stem cell collection
- Filgrastim plus cyclophosphamide, with or without plerixafor
    - Cyclophosphamide 1500-3000 mg/m2 IV
    - Filgrastim 10 mcg/kg SC daily, starting 24 hours after cyclophosphamide and continuing until collection goal is met
        - Tbo-filgrastim or an FDA-approved biosimilar is an appropriate substitute for filgrastim
    - Begin apheresis at least 4-5 days after cyclophosphamide administration
        - Consider checking circulating CD34+ cells and initiating apheresis based on institutional guidelines
    - Plerixafor 0.24 mg/kg actual body weight SC (max 40 mg/day) on the day before apheresis
        - Generally administered 11 hours prior to stem cell collection
- Sargramostim plus cyclophosphamide, with or without plerixafor
    - Cyclophosphamide 1500-3000 mg/m2 IV
    - Sargramostim 250 mcg/m2/day SC, IV over 24 hours or SC once daily, starting 24 hours after cyclophosphamide and continuing until collection goal is met
    - Begin apheresis at least 4-5 days after cyclophosphamide administration
        - Consider checking circulating CD34+ cells and initiating apheresis based on institutional guidelines
    - Plerixafor 0.24 mg/kg actual body weight SC (max 40 mg/day) on the day before apheresis
        - Generally administered 11 hours prior to stem cell collection
- Pegfilgrastim plus plerixafor
    - Pegfilgrastim 6 mg SC on Day 1
    - Upfront plerixafor 0.24 mg/kg actual body weight SC (max 40 mg/day) on Day 3, followed by apheresis on Day 4
- Filgrastim plus motixafortide
    - Filgrastim 10 mcg/kg SC daily for 4 days prior to the first dose of motixafortide
        - Tbo-filgrastim or an FDA-approved biosimilar is an appropriate substitute for filgrastim
    - Motixafortide 1.25 mg/kg actual body weight SC 10-14 hours prior to initiation of apheresis

# Allogeneic donors

- Filgrastim
    - 10 mcg/kg donor weight SC, or split twice daily
    - Daily for 4-5 days
    - Collect on Day 4 or 5
    - Tbo-filgrastim or an FDA-approved biosimilar is an appropriate substitute for filgrastim
