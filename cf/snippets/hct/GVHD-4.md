# First-line therapy, choose one

- Clinical trial
- Systemic corticosteroids 0.5-1 mg/kg/day methylprednisolone (or prednisone dose equivalent)
    - Initial dose may vary depending on organs involved, GVHD severity, patient comorbidities, and overlapping syndromes
- Sirolimus + prednisone

# Add to first-line therapy as indicated

- Topical steroids as clinically indicated
    - Topical steroids (eg, triamcinolone, clobetasol)
    - Topical estrogen for vulvovaginal GVHD
    - Topical tacrolimus
    - Dexamethasone oral rinse for oral GVHD
    - Medium- to high-potency formulations are recommended, except on the face or intertriginous areas where low-potency hydrocortisone can be used
- Inhaled steroid + azithromycin + montelukast for lung involvement (eg, FAM [fluticasone, azithromycin, and montelukast])
    - Acceptable inhaled steroids include budesonide or fluticasone
    - Azithromycin only for treatment of bronchiolitis obliterans syndrome (BOS), not for prophylaxis, due to a suggestion of an increased risk of leukemic relapse or secondary neoplasms in recent clinical trials
    - Patients with progression/worsening of lung chronic GVHD following 2-3 lines of therapy may be evaluated for lung transplant

# Response assessment

- Assess response by GVHD Steroid Response Definitions/Criteria (GVHD-D)

# Next

- Response, additional therapy
    - Continue or consider restarting original immunosuppressive agent
    - Taper steroids as clinically feasible
        - If response, taper systemic steroids to mitigate long-term steroid side effects and risk of infection, as clinically feasible
- No response (steroid-refractory disease), additional therapy
    - Clinical trial
        - Enrollment in well-designed clinical trials should be encouraged since no standard, effective therapy for steroid-refractory GVHD has been identified
        - Selection of therapy for steroid-refractory GVHD should be based on physician experience, agent's toxicity profile, the effect of prior treatment, drug interactions, convenience/accessibility, and patient tolerability
    - Addition of systemic agent to corticosteroids with steroid taper as clinically feasible
        - See Suggested Systemic Agents for Steroid-Refractory GVHD (GVHD-E)
