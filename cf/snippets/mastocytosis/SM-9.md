# Source

- NCCN Systemic Mastocytosis v3.2026, SM-9, p14
- Applies to mast cell leukemia (MCL), with or without an associated hematologic neoplasm (AHN)
- Diagnostic criteria for the variants of systemic mastocytosis: SM-D
- Adverse prognostic variables and risk stratification: SM-I
- All recommendations are category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- ___ (age) yo, mast cell leukemia
- Associated hematologic neoplasm: ___ (ahn)
- Platelet count ___ (platelets) x 109/L
- SM-related organ damage: ___ (organ_damage)
    - Chronic MCL has no organ damage; treatment should still be considered given the poor prognosis of MCL
- Serum tryptase ___ (tryptase)

# Baseline management for all patients

- Referral to specialized centers with expertise in mastocytosis is strongly recommended
- Counsel patients regarding signs and symptoms of disease
    - Counsel about the signs/symptoms and potential triggers of mast cell activation (SM-J)
    - Taylor F, et al. Leuk Res 2021;108:106606
- Avoid known triggers of mast cell activation
- Carry injectable epinephrine (2 auto injectors) to manage anaphylaxis
    - Anti-mediator drug therapy approaches for mast cell activation symptoms: SM-K
- Multidisciplinary collaboration with subspecialists is recommended (SM-L)
    - Anesthesia for procedures/surgery
    - High-risk obstetrics for pregnancy

# Treatment

- Preferred
    - Clinical trial
    - Avapritinib, if platelets are 50 x 109/L or higher
        - Not recommended for patients with platelet counts below 50 x 109/L
        - Refer to the package insert for the full prescribing information, dose modifications, and monitoring for adverse reactions
    - Midostaurin
        - Refer to the package insert for the full prescribing information, dose modifications, and monitoring for adverse reactions
- Other recommended
    - Cladribine
    - AML-based therapy, with consideration of cladribine or midostaurin in the regimen

# Response assessment

- Adequate response
    - Organ damage assessment and response criteria: SM-G
    - Clinical benefit may not reach the threshold of the clinical trial response criteria
- Inadequate response, or intolerance/no response, or loss of response
    - Organ damage assessment and response criteria: SM-G
    - Return or progression of SM-related organ damage
    - Symptomatic or progressive hepatomegaly or splenomegaly
    - Progressive disease-related symptoms
    - Intolerance to drug therapy
- Progression of AHN, or transformation to acute myeloid leukemia (AML)

# Next

- Adequate response
    - Continue treatment
    - and/or consider evaluation for allogeneic HCT
- Inadequate response, or intolerance/no response, or loss of response
    - Re-stage
        - Perform as clinically indicated, if supported by increased symptoms and signs of progression
            - Bone marrow aspirate and biopsy
            - Serum tryptase level
            - Additional staging studies
    - Consider subsequent-line therapy
    - Consider allogeneic HCT
- Progression of AHN, or transformation to AML
    - AHN-directed therapy, including multiagent chemotherapy and/or consideration of allogeneic HCT with concurrent management of MCL
    - See NCCN Guidelines relevant to AHN, or NCCN Guidelines for Acute Myeloid Leukemia
