# Surgical assessment

- Discussion of surgical treatment with patient, and determination of whether patient is amenable to surgery
- Resection criteria (HCC-F)
    - CTP Class A or B
        - CTP Class B only in highly selected patients with CTP Class B liver function, with limited resection
    - No portal hypertension
    - Suitable tumor location
    - Adequate liver reserve
    - Suitable liver remnant
- Transplant criteria (HCC-F)
    - United Network for Organ Sharing (UNOS) criteria
        - AFP level 1000 ng/mL or lower, and patient has a tumor 2-5 cm in diameter, or 2-3 tumors 1-3 cm in diameter
        - No macrovascular involvement
        - No extrahepatic disease
    - Extended criteria
        - Extended criteria and downstaging protocols are available through UNOS (https://www.hrsa.gov/sites/default/files/hrsa/optn/optn_policies.pdf)
- In patients being considered for surgery, patients with CTP Class A, or highly selected patients with CTP Class B liver function, who fit UNOS criteria or extended criteria (www.unos.org) and are resectable, could be considered for resection or transplant
    - These patients should be evaluated by a multidisciplinary team for individualized decision-making

# Treatment, meets resection with or without transplant criteria

- Resection (preferred) (HCC-F)
    - Principles of Pathology (HCC-D)
- Transplant (preferred), if transplant criteria met (HCC-F)
    - Refer to liver transplant center (Mazzaferro V, et al. N Engl J Med 1996;334:693-700)
    - Bridge therapy as indicated
        - Many transplant centers consider bridging therapy for transplant candidates

# Treatment, meets transplant criteria only

- Refer to liver transplant center (Mazzaferro V, et al. N Engl J Med 1996;334:693-700)
- Bridge therapy as indicated
    - Many transplant centers consider bridging therapy for transplant candidates
- Locoregional therapy (HCC-G)
    - Ablation (preferred)
        - In well-selected patients with small, properly located tumors, ablation should be considered as definitive treatment in the context of a multidisciplinary review
    - Arterially directed therapies
    - Radiation therapy (RT) (HCC-H)
- Transplant

# Surveillance

- Imaging every 3-6 mo for 2 y, then every 6 mo (HCC-A)
    - Multiphasic abdomen MRI or multiphase CT scans for liver assessment, CT chest, and CT/MRI pelvis
- AFP every 3-6 mo for 2 y, then every 6 mo (HCC-A)
- Surveillance imaging and AFP should continue for at least 5 years; thereafter screening is dependent on HCC risk factors
- Refer to a hepatologist for a discussion of antiviral therapy for carriers of hepatitis, if not previously done
