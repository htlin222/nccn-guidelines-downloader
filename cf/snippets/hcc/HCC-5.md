# Source

- NCCN Hepatocellular Carcinoma v1.2026, HCC-5, p11
- Applies to liver-confined disease that is unresectable and deemed ineligible for transplant
- Principles of Imaging: HCC-A
- Principles of Mixed HCC-CCA: HCC-C
- Principles of Pathology: HCC-D
- Consider biopsy if imaging is not consistent, or to confirm the imaging diagnosis if it does not meet AASLD or LI-RADS 5 criteria; see Principles of Core Needle Biopsy (HCC-B)

# Treatment options

- Order does not indicate preference
    - The choice of treatment modality may depend on extent/location of disease, hepatic reserve, and institutional capabilities

- Locoregional therapy, see Principles of Locoregional Therapy (HCC-G)

    - Arterially directed therapies

- RT, see Principles of Radiation Therapy (HCC-H)
- Clinical trial
- Systemic therapy, see Principles of Systemic Therapy (HCC-I)
- Best supportive care, see NCCN Guidelines for Palliative Care

# Response assessment

- Assess for response
- Reconsider resection, transplant, or locoregional therapy
    - Reconsider resection with Principles of Pathology (HCC-D) in mind
- Subsequent-line systemic therapy if progression on or after systemic therapy (HCC-I)
- Systemic therapy if progression on or after locoregional therapy (HCC-I)

# Surveillance

- Imaging every 3-6 mo for 2 y, then every 6 mo
    - Multiphasic abdomen MRI or multiphase CT scans for liver assessment, CT chest, and CT/MRI pelvis
    - Principles of Imaging: HCC-A
- AFP every 3-6 mo for 2 y, then every 6 mo
    - Principles of Imaging: HCC-A
- Surveillance imaging and AFP should continue for at least 5 years; thereafter screening is dependent on HCC risk factors
- Consider early imaging per local protocol, for locoregional therapy
