- NCCN Squamous Cell Skin Cancer v2.2026, SCC-11, p21
- Applies to follow-up of treated disease, either local disease or regional/S-ITM disease
- Risk group per risk factors for local recurrence, metastases, or death (SCC-2)
- Identification and management of patients at high risk for multiple primary CSCCs (SCC-B)
- All recommendations are category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# History and physical

- Complete skin exam
- Regional lymph node exam
- Frequency of follow-up should be adjusted based on risk
- Follow-up with a dermatologist is strongly recommended if any of the following criteria are met
    - Past or imminent solid organ, marrow, or stem cell transplant
    - One or more cutaneous melanomas in the past 5 years
    - Four or more non-melanoma skin cancers in the past 5 years
- Local disease, low risk: every 3–12 mo for 2 y, then every 6–12 mo for 3 y, then annually for life
- Local disease, high risk: every 3–6 mo for 2 y, then every 6–12 mo for 3 y, then annually for life
- Local disease, very high risk: every 3–6 mo for 2 y, then every 6 mo for 3 y, then every 6–12 mo for life
- Regional/S-ITM disease: every 2–3 mo for 1 y, then every 2–4 mo for 1 y, then every 4–6 mo for 3 y, then every 6–12 mo for life

# Imaging

- Consider imaging if clinical exam is insufficient for following disease
- Consider imaging if there is appreciable risk of subclinical local or nodal recurrence
- Imaging modality and targeted area should be at the discretion of the treating team, based on the suspected extent of disease (ie, local, regional, metastatic)
- Histologic confirmation is sufficient to diagnose local recurrence
- MRI with and without contrast can be considered to assess extent of local disease
- For nodal or distant metastases, histologic analysis and/or other imaging modalities can be used for confirmation and to gauge extent of disease
- Regional/S-ITM disease: surveillance imaging of regional nodal basin, and to evaluate for distant metastatic disease
    - Ideally based on multidisciplinary board recommendation, or as clinically indicated

# Patient education

- Sun protection
- Self examination of skin
- Regional/S-ITM disease: self examination of skin and lymph nodes
