# Source

- NCCN Squamous Cell Skin Cancer v2.2026, SCC-9, p19
- Applies to tissue sampling confirmed satellitosis / in-transit metastasis (S-ITM)
- Primary treatment follows Principles of Treatment (SCC-C)
- Systemic therapy options follow Principles of Systemic Therapy
- All recommendations are category 2A unless otherwise indicated

- ___ (age) yo, S-ITM at ___ (site)

- Limb only disease ___ (limb_only)

# Treatment planning

- Imaging of the anatomic region capturing S-ITM to determine geographic footprint
    - ie, CT with contrast
- Rule out distant disease, as clinically indicated
    - Chest/abdomen/pelvis CT with contrast
    - or FDG-PET/CT

# Operable disease

- Consider neoadjuvant systemic therapy, followed by resection
    - Systemic therapy per Principles of Systemic Therapy
    - Cemiplimab-rwlc, in one study of 79 patients with CSCC, showed a 51% complete histologic response in the neoadjuvant setting
    - Therefore it may be considered in patients who are considered borderline resectable, unresectable, or for whom surgery may carry a high morbidity
- or upfront resection, with consideration of adjuvant therapy

# Unresectable or incompletely resected disease

- Cemiplimab-rwlc may be considered in patients who are considered borderline resectable, unresectable, or for whom surgery may carry a high morbidity
- Systemic therapy, per Principles of Systemic Therapy
    - With or without RT
    - RT per Principles of Radiation Therapy (SCC-D)
    - Determination of the appropriateness of RT should be performed together with a radiation oncologist
- or regional therapy options
    - Isolated limb infusion/perfusion (ILI/ILP) with Melphalan-based regimens (Useful in Certain Circumstances)
        - Primarily used for patients with limb only disease with progression on, or contraindications to, standard therapies
        - Should only be done at centers with experience with ILI/ILP
