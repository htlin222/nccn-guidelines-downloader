# Source

- NCCN Squamous Cell Skin Cancer v2.2026, SCC-3, p10
- Applies to field cancerization / confluent epidermal dysplasia
- Field cancerization defined as UV induced confluent dysplasia, clinically manifested as diffuse actinic keratoses and superficial (in situ) SCC
- Treatment options here follow Principles of Systemic Therapy
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- ___ (age) yo, field cancerization / confluent epidermal dysplasia at ___ (site)
- Diffuse actinic keratoses
- Superficial (in situ) SCC
- Actinic cheilitis ___ (actinic_cheilitis)
- Prior oral retinoids ___ (prior_oral_retinoid)
- Actinic keratoses with an atypical clinical appearance biopsied for histologic evaluation
- Actinic keratoses that do not respond to appropriate therapy biopsied for histologic evaluation
- Actinic keratoses treated at first development

# Prevention

- Daily sunscreens
- Nicotinamide
    - Oral Nicotinamide may be effective in reducing the development of CSCCs

# Accepted treatment modalities - topical

- Calcipotriene/Fluorouracil (preferred)
    - The longest duration of prophylaxis against SCC has been demonstrated with topical Fluorouracil plus Calcipotriol
- Fluorouracil (preferred)
- Imiquimod
- Tirbanibulin
- Diclofenac (category 2B)

# Accepted treatment modalities - destructive

- Ablative laser vermilionectomy or electrosurgical destruction
    - May be of value in the treatment of extensive actinic cheilitis
- Ablative skin resurfacing (eg, laser, dermabrasion)
- Chemical peels (Trichloroacetic acid)
- Cryotherapy
- Curettage and electrodesiccation (C&E)

# Other modalities that may be considered

- Capecitabine
    - For severe refractory disease that has progressed on oral retinoids
- Photodynamic therapy (PDT) (eg, topical Aminolevulinic acid [ALA], Porfimer sodium)
- Systemic retinoids (eg, Acitretin, Isotretinoin)
    - A therapeutic option used to reduce the development of actinic keratoses
    - Side effects may be significant, especially in patients of childbearing potential
    - Therapeutic benefits are limited to the duration of the regimen
    - Topical retinoids were shown not to reduce development of actinic keratosis
