# Source

- NCCN Occult Primary v2.2026, OCC-11, p18
- Applies to a histologic diagnosis of squamous cell carcinoma
- Additional workup is chosen by clinical presentation, that is by the site of nodal disease
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- Histologic diagnosis: squamous cell carcinoma
- Clinical presentation, site of nodes: ___ (node_site)
- Complete skin exam performed, with focus on the cutaneous areas
- p16 IHC: ___ (p16)
- Human papillomavirus (HPV) in situ hybridization (ISH): ___ (hpv_ish)
- Epstein-Barr virus (EBV) ISH: ___ (ebv_ish)
    - Positive results can help localize the primary site

# Imaging conditions that apply throughout

- CT should be performed with contrast
- MRI should be performed with and without IV contrast
    - Unless contraindicated
- FDG-PET/CT is an alternative in patients with a contraindication to contrast enhancement
- Symptom-directed endoscopy can be considered for individual patients, based on clinical findings and IHC markers

# Head and neck nodes

- Head and neck workup, see NCCN Guidelines for Head and Neck Cancers

# Supraclavicular nodes

- Neck CT with contrast
- Chest CT with contrast
- Abdomen CT with contrast
- Pelvis CT with contrast
- Endoscopy as indicated

# Axillary nodes

- Chest CT

# Inguinal nodes

- Abdomen CT with contrast
- Pelvis CT with contrast
- Careful perineal and lower extremity exam, including:
    - Reproductive organs and genitalia
    - Anus
- Gynecologic oncologist consult
- Anoscopy
- Cystoscopy, if clinically indicated

# Bone

- Bone scan, if only chest/abdomen/pelvis CT was previously done
- Diagnostic imaging studies for painful lesions
- Diagnostic imaging studies for bone scan-positive lesions with potential for fracture in weight-bearing areas
