# Workup and grading, all grades

- Consider diagnostic testing
    - EEG
    - MRI of brain
    - Lumbar puncture
- Grade the event per CART-6

# Management, all grades

- Refer to the prescribing information for product-specific dosing and management, because risks associated with each agent vary widely
- Aspiration precautions
- Withhold oral intake
    - Assess swallow
- Avoid all medications with CNS sedation
- Consider neurology consultation if refractory to dexamethasone
- Neuroimaging every 2-3 days if persistent ICANS

# Grade 1

- Continue treatment if blinatumomab, mosunetuzumab-axgb, or glofitamab-gxbm
- Otherwise hold treatment, for all other bispecific agents
    - For rechallenge after ICANS, see ENGAGE-8
- Oral or IV dexamethasone 10 mg x 1 dose
    - If unresolved or progressing, increase to every 6 hours
- Consider levetiracetam 750 mg every 12 hours
    - Depending on seizure risk associated with agent used

# Grade 2

- Continue treatment if blinatumomab
- Otherwise hold treatment, for all other bispecific agents
    - For rechallenge after ICANS, see ENGAGE-8
- IV dexamethasone 10 mg every 6 hours
- Consider levetiracetam 750 mg every 12 hours
    - Depending on seizure risk associated with agent used

# Grade 3

- Hold treatment, for all bispecific agents
    - For rechallenge after ICANS, see ENGAGE-8
- ICU care
- IV methylprednisolone 1000 mg x 1 dose
    - If refractory, every 12 hours x 3 days
- Levetiracetam 750 mg every 12 hours
    - Depending on seizure risk associated with agent used

# Grade 4

- Permanently discontinue treatment, for all bispecific agents
    - For rechallenge after ICANS, see ENGAGE-8
- ICU care
- IV methylprednisolone 1000 mg every 12 hours
- Levetiracetam 750 mg every 12 hours
    - Depending on seizure risk associated with agent used
