# ICE assessment tool

- Orientation, 4 points
    - Year
    - Month
    - City
    - Hospital
- Naming, 3 points
    - Ability to name 3 objects (eg, point to clock, pen, button)
- Following commands, 1 point
    - Ability to follow simple commands (eg, "Show me 2 fingers" or "Close your eyes and stick out your tongue")
- Writing, 1 point
    - Ability to write a standard sentence (eg, "Our national bird is the bald eagle")
- Attention, 1 point
    - Ability to count backwards from 100 by 10

# ICE scoring

- 7-9, grade 1
- 3-6, grade 2
- 0-2, grade 3
- 0 due to patient unarousable and unable to perform ICE assessment, grade 4

# ASTCT ICANS consensus grading for adults

- Grade is determined by the most severe event, not attributable to any other cause
    - Domains scored: ICE score, level of consciousness, seizure, motor findings, raised ICP/cerebral edema
    - Example: a patient with an ICE score of 3 who has a generalized seizure is classified as grade 3 ICANS
- ICE score
    - Grade 1: 7-9
    - Grade 2: 3-6
    - Grade 3: 0-2
    - Grade 4: 0, patient is unarousable and unable to perform ICE
    - An ICE score of 0 may be classified as grade 3 ICANS if awake with global aphasia, and as grade 4 ICANS if unarousable
- Depressed level of consciousness
    - Should be attributable to no other cause (eg, no sedating medication)
    - Grade 1: awakens spontaneously
    - Grade 2: awakens to voice
    - Grade 3: awakens only to tactile stimulus
    - Grade 4: unarousable, or requires vigorous or repetitive tactile stimuli to arouse; stupor or coma
- Seizure
    - Grade 1: N/A
    - Grade 2: N/A
    - Grade 3: any clinical seizure, focal or generalized, that resolves rapidly
    - Grade 3: nonconvulsive seizures on EEG that resolve with intervention
    - Grade 4: life-threatening prolonged seizure (>5 min)
    - Grade 4: repetitive clinical or electrical seizures without return to baseline in between
- Motor findings
    - Grade 1: N/A
    - Grade 2: N/A
    - Grade 3: N/A
    - Grade 4: deep focal motor weakness such as hemiparesis or paraparesis
- Elevated ICP/cerebral edema
    - Grade 1: N/A
    - Grade 2: N/A
    - Grade 3: focal/local edema on neuroimaging
    - Grade 4: diffuse cerebral edema on neuroimaging
    - Grade 4: decerebrate or decorticate posturing
    - Grade 4: cranial nerve VI palsy
    - Grade 4: papilledema
    - Grade 4: Cushing's triad
    - Intracranial hemorrhage, with or without associated edema, is not considered a neurotoxicity feature and is excluded from ICANS grading; it may be graded according to CTCAE v5.0

# Signs and symptoms outside the grading scale

- May occur and could be attributable to IEC engaging therapies; not included in this grading scale, but careful attention and directed therapy may be warranted
    - Headache
    - Tremor
    - Myoclonus
    - Asterixis
    - Hallucinations
