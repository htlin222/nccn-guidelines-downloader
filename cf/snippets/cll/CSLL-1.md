# Essential

- Hematopathology review of peripheral blood smear and all slides
    - With at least one paraffin block representative of the tumor, if the diagnosis was made on a lymph node or bone marrow biopsy
- Flow cytometry of blood is adequate for the diagnosis of CLL/SLL
- Absolute monoclonal B lymphocyte count (CSLL-1A)

# CLL diagnosis

- Requires the presence of monoclonal B lymphocytes ≥5 x 10^9/L in peripheral blood based on flow cytometry (CSLL-1A)
- Adequate immunophenotyping to establish diagnosis by flow cytometry (CSLL-1A)
    - kappa/lambda
    - CD19
    - CD20
    - CD5
    - CD23
    - CD10
    - CD200
- If flow cytometry is used to establish diagnosis, also include fluorescence in situ hybridization (FISH) for t(11;14), to exclude mantle cell lymphoma (MCL)

# SLL diagnosis

- Requires presence of lymphadenopathy and/or splenomegaly with monoclonal B lymphocytes ≤5 x 10^9/L in peripheral blood
- Confirm by histopathology evaluation of lymph node biopsy, or evaluation of bone marrow or peripheral blood for monoclonal B cells of diagnostic phenotype
- Excisional or incisional biopsy, if lymph node is accessible
    - Fine-needle aspiration (FNA) alone is inadequate for the diagnosis
    - When a lymph node is not easily accessible for excisional or incisional biopsy, a combination of core needle biopsy in conjunction with appropriate ancillary techniques, such as FNA, may be sufficient for diagnosis
        - Multiple core needle biopsies may be required
- Bone marrow aspirate with biopsy can be pursued if peripheral blood and lymph node biopsy material are nondiagnostic
- Adequate immunophenotyping to establish diagnosis by immunohistochemistry (IHC) (CSLL-1A)
    - CD3
    - CD5
    - CD10
    - CD20
    - CD23
    - cyclin D1
    - LEF1
    - SOX11

# Informative for prognostic and/or therapy determination (CSLL-1A)

- FISH to detect
    - +12
    - del(11q)
    - del(13q)
    - del(17p)
- TP53 sequencing
- CpG-stimulated metaphase karyotype for complex karyotype (CK)
- Molecular analysis to detect IGHV mutation status
- Beta-2 microglobulin

# Monoclonal B-cell lymphocytosis (MBL)

- Absolute monoclonal B-lymphocyte count <5 x 10^9/L
- All palpable lymph nodes <1.5 cm
- No anemia
- No thrombocytopenia
- No palpable organomegaly
- No constitutional symptoms
