# TP53 included in a multigene panel

- When TP53 is included as part of a multigene panel, an individual does not need to meet these testing criteria
    - Provided testing criteria on other testing criteria pages are met

# Sarcoma subtype and germline TP53 yield

- In contrast to other types of sarcoma, germline TP53 P/LP variants are rare in those with
    - Ewing sarcoma
    - Gastrointestinal stromal tumor (GIST)
    - Desmoid tumor
    - Angiosarcoma

# P/LP TP53 variant found on tumor-only genomic testing

- Careful evaluation of the personal and family history of the individual, to determine the yield of germline sequencing
    - Somatic TP53 P/LP variants are common in many tumor types in the absence of a germline P/LP variant
    - Mandelker D, et al. Ann Oncol 2019;30:1221-1231
- For testing in the pediatric setting, see Frebourg T, et al. Eur J Hum Genet 2020;28:1379-1386
