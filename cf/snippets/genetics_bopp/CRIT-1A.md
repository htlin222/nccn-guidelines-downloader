# Founder variant testing in Ashkenazi Jewish ancestry

- Testing for three founder P/LP variants of BRCA1/2 may be offered as early as age 18-25 years
    - To individuals who have one grandparent identified as of Ashkenazi Jewish ancestry
    - Irrespective of cancer history in the family
    - As part of longitudinal studies
- For those without access to longitudinal research studies, testing may be provided if there is access to
    - Pre-test education
    - Post-test counseling
    - Additional genetic testing if indicated
    - High-risk management
- Testing should not be offered outside of a medical framework or clinical trial
