    - Gorlin syndrome (PTCH1)
    - Xeroderma pigmentosa

- Colon Cancer

    - RHBDF2
    - Bloom syndrome (BS)/BLM, RECQL3
    - Fanconi anemia (FA)/FANCD1, BRCA2, PALB2
- Gastric Cancer
    - Refers to Genetic/Familial: Colorectal, Endometrial, and Gastric Guidelines
- Gastrointestinal Stromal Tumors (GIST)

    - KIT
    - PDGFRA
    - SDHB
    - NF1
