# Major criteria

- Breast cancer
- Endometrial cancer (epithelial)
- Follicular thyroid cancer
- Three or more GI hamartomas or ganglioneuromas
    - Multiple polyp types are often seen in patients with PHTS
    - Less commonly these may include adenomas, hyperplastic polyps, and other histologies
- Lhermitte-Duclos disease (adult)
- Macrocephaly (megalocephaly)
    - Defined as at or above the 97th percentile
    - 58 cm in an adult female
    - 60 cm in an adult male
    - Reference standard: Roche AF, et al. Pediatrics 1987;79:706-712
- Macular pigmentation of glans penis
- Mucocutaneous lesions
    - Trichilemmoma, three or more, at least 1 biopsy-proven
    - Three or more palmoplantar keratotic pits and/or acral hyperkeratotic papules
    - Three or more mucocutaneous neuromas
    - Oral papillomas, particularly on tongue and gingiva, three or more or 1 biopsy-proven or dermatologist diagnosed
    - The literature available on mucocutaneous lesions is not adequate to accurately specify the number or extent required to be a major criterion for CS/PHTS, so clinical judgment should be used

# Minor criteria

- Autism spectrum disorder
- Colon cancer
- Three or more esophageal glycogenic acanthoses
- Three or more lipomas
- Intellectual disability, ie, IQ at or below 75
- Renal cell carcinoma
- Testicular lipomatosis
- Papillary or follicular variant of papillary thyroid cancer
- Thyroid structural lesions (eg, adenoma, nodule[s], goiter)
- Single gastrointestinal (GI) hamartoma or ganglioneuroma
- Vascular anomalies, including multiple intracranial developmental venous anomalies
- Insufficient evidence exists in the literature to include the following as diagnostic criteria
    - Fibrocystic disease of the breast
    - Fibromas
    - Uterine fibroids

# Not part of the testing criteria

- Melanoma is also associated with PTEN but is not included in the testing criteria

# Revised clinical diagnostic criteria, operational diagnosis in an individual

- Either of the following is sufficient
    - Three or more major criteria, but one must include macrocephaly, Lhermitte-Duclos disease, or GI hamartomas
    - Two major and three minor criteria

# Revised clinical diagnostic criteria, operational diagnosis in a family

- Applies where one individual meets revised PTEN hamartoma tumor syndrome clinical diagnostic criteria, or has a PTEN P/LP variant
- Any of the following is sufficient
    - Any two major criteria, with or without minor criteria
    - One major and two minor criteria
    - Three minor criteria
