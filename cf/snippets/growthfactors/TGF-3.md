# Definitions used in several studies for CIT

- Thrombocytopenia, platelets <100,000/mcL
    - For at least 3 to 4 weeks following last chemotherapy administration
    - And/or following delays in chemotherapy initiation related to thrombocytopenia

# Before starting a TPO-RA for CIT

- Confirm the purpose of a TPO-RA for CIT
    - To maintain dose schedule and intensity of chemotherapy
    - Only when such benefit is thought to outweigh potential risks
- Participation in clinical trials is strongly encouraged whenever possible
- Other TPO-RA agonists, including avatrombopag, have been evaluated in studies
    - Insufficient data from these studies to recommend these agents
    - Additional prospective studies are needed to determine safety, efficacy, and benefits of TPO-RA agonists
- Insufficient data are available to support routine use of TPO-RAs for CIT in pediatric patients

# Romiplostim dosing for CIT

- Weekly dosing beginning at 2-4 mcg/kg
- Increase no more than 1-2 mcg/kg per week
- Target platelet count 100,000-150,000/mcL
- Maximum dose 10 mcg/kg weekly per prescribing information

# Caution

- It is uncertain whether use of TPO-RAs for CIT increases the risk of venous thromboembolism (VTE) in patients with cancer
    - Caution is warranted

# Prolonged thrombocytopenia following hematopoietic cell transplantation

- Several reports have separately examined use of TPO-RA in this setting
    - Including patients with secondary failure of platelet recovery
- Clinical trial participation is encouraged whenever possible for such patients
- Eltrombopag has been evaluated with efficacy in patients with prolonged thrombocytopenia post-allogeneic transplant and poor graft function
