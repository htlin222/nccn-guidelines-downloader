# Hemoglobin concentration to prompt an evaluation of anemia

- Hb ≤11 g/dL, or
- Hb ≥2 g/dL below baseline
    - Consider gender in the evaluation, since women have a lower baseline Hb than men
    - See Discussion for more details

# Initial studies

- CBC with indices
- Blood smear morphology
    - MDS, see NCCN Guidelines for Myelodysplastic Syndromes
    - Myeloid malignancies or ALL, treat underlying disease per NCCN Guidelines for Treatment by Cancer Type

# Evaluate anemia for possible cause as indicated

- First check
    - Reticulocyte count
        - Correct the reticulocyte count for the degree of anemia, see Discussion
    - Mean corpuscular volume (MCV)
- Then consider
    - Hemorrhage
        - Consider upper and lower endoscopic evaluation
    - Hemolysis
        - Direct antiglobulin test
        - DIC panel
        - Haptoglobin
        - Indirect bilirubin
        - Lactate dehydrogenase (LDH)
    - Nutritional
        - Iron
        - Total iron-binding capacity
        - Ferritin
        - B12
        - Folate
        - In general, the lower the level of ferritin, the higher the probability of true iron deficiency anemia
        - In the cancer setting, be aware of a chronic inflammatory state, which may falsely elevate the serum ferritin
        - If serum iron studies are not performed while the patient is fasting, or if the patient has taken a recent oral iron tablet, serum iron levels may be falsely elevated, and thus also falsely elevate the percent transferrin saturation (TSAT)
        - Fasting is preferred when testing for serum iron and total iron-binding capacity
    - Inherited
        - Prior history
        - Family history
    - Renal dysfunction
        - Glomerular filtration rate (GFR) <60 mL/min/1.73 m2
    - Cancer treatment or other drug-induced myelosuppression/anemia
    - Hormone dysfunction
        - Hypogonadism
        - Adrenal dysfunction
        - Hyper/hypothyroidism
    - Anemia of chronic inflammation
        - C-reactive protein (CRP)
        - Erythrocyte sedimentation rate (ESR)
    - Secondary myeloid disorders, including clonal cytopenia of undetermined significance (CCUS)
- Evaluation of Iron Deficiency (ANEM-4)

# Prophylaxis to decrease the incidence of anemia

- Trilaciclib may be used as a prophylactic option to decrease the incidence of anemia and red blood cell (RBC) transfusions (category 2B)
    - Administered before platinum/etoposide ± immune checkpoint inhibitor-containing regimens for ES-SCLC
    - Or before a topotecan-containing regimen for ES-SCLC
