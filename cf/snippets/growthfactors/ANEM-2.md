# Source

- NCCN Hematopoietic Growth Factors v3.2026, ANEM-2, p32
- Applies to anemia in patients with cancer, initial transfusion decision in the acute setting
- The AABB has made recommendations regarding appropriate indications for RBC transfusion; see Discussion for details
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- Hb ___ (hb)
- Recent treatment ___ (recent_treatment)
- ___ (symptom_status)
- Progressive decline in Hb with recent intensive chemotherapy or radiation
- Comorbidities
    - Cardiac disease
    - Chronic pulmonary disease
    - Cerebral vascular disease
- Symptoms (physiologic)
    - Sustained tachycardia
    - Tachypnea
    - Chest pain
    - Dyspnea on exertion
    - Lightheadedness
    - Syncope
    - Severe fatigue preventing work and usual activity
        - Fatigue (FACT-F) and Anemia (FACT-An) subscales of the Functional Assessment of Cancer Therapy (FACT), and the Brief Fatigue Inventory (BFI), are examples of standardized measures for assessing patient-reported fatigue

# Asymptomatic without significant comorbidities

- Observe
    - Degree of severity of comorbidities in combination with the degree of severity of anemia should be taken into consideration when initiating RBC transfusion

# High risk, or asymptomatic with comorbidities

- High risk, ie, progressive decline in Hb with recent intensive chemotherapy or radiation
- Asymptomatic with comorbidities: cardiac disease, chronic pulmonary disease, cerebral vascular disease
    - Degree of severity of comorbidities in combination with the degree of severity of anemia should be taken into consideration when initiating RBC transfusion
- Consider red blood cell (RBC) transfusion per AABB Guidelines
    - The AABB has made recommendations regarding appropriate indications for RBC transfusion; see Discussion for details

# Symptomatic (physiologic)

- RBC transfusion per AABB Guidelines
    - The AABB has made recommendations regarding appropriate indications for RBC transfusion; see Discussion for details
