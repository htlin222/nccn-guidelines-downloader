# Source

- NCCN Hematopoietic Growth Factors v3.2026, TGF-1, p24
- Use of thrombopoietin receptor agonists (TPO-RA) in patients with cancer
- Applies to suspected chemotherapy-induced thrombocytopenia (CIT)
- All recommendations category 2A unless stated otherwise

# Assessment

- Platelet count ___ (platelet_count)
- Current regimen ___ (regimen)
- Other cytopenias ___ (other_cytopenias)
- Blood smear ___ (smear)
- Suspected cause ___ (suspected_cause)

# First check

- Complete blood count (CBC) with differential
    - Including evaluation for other cytopenias
- Blood smear morphology
    - Including evaluation for platelet clumping

# Then consider other potential etiologies

- Nutritional deficiencies
- Medications and supplements suppressing platelet production
- Infection, including viral reactivation
- Disseminated intravascular coagulation (DIC)
- Immune thrombocytopenia
- Heparin-induced thrombocytopenia (HIT), or other medication-induced thrombocytopenia
- Radiation- or chemotherapy-induced myelosuppression
- Bone marrow involvement by underlying malignancy
- Hematologic malignancy, including therapy-related myeloid neoplasia
- Hemolytic anemia
- Dilutional thrombocytopenia secondary to blood loss
- Antiphospholipid syndrome (APS)
- Hypersplenism
- Paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria (PNH)
- Thrombotic microangiopathies (TMAs)
    - Drug-associated TMA
    - Thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura (TTP)

# Management

- Evaluate for other potential causes of thrombocytopenia as indicated
- Treat underlying cause(s) as indicated

# If chemotherapy-induced thrombocytopenia (CIT), consider

- Platelet transfusion per Association for the Advancement of Blood and Biotherapies (AABB) guidelines
- Chemotherapy dose reduction, or change in treatment regimen
- Clinical trial of TPO-RA
- Romiplostim
