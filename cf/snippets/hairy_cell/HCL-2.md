# Indications for treatment

- Evaluate for indications for treatment (Grever MR, et al. Blood 2017;129:553-560)
    - Systemic symptoms
        - Unexplained weight loss, more than 10% within prior 6 months
        - Excessive fatigue
    - Hemoglobin <11 g/dL
    - Platelets <100,000/mcL
    - Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) <1000/mcL
    - Symptomatic organomegaly
    - Progressive lymphocytosis or lymphadenopathy

# Initial treatment

- No indication present
    - Observe until indication for treatment
- Indication present
    - Initial Therapy (HCL-A)
    - Supportive Care for Patients with HCL (HCL-C)

# Response to therapy

- Full hematologic recovery consistent with complete response, by HCL Response Criteria (HCL-B)
    - Observe
    - Relapse at 2 years or later (Grever MR, et al. Blood 2017;129:553-560)
        - Re-evaluate for indications for treatment
    - Relapse at <2 years (Grever MR, et al. Blood 2017;129:553-560)
        - Relapsed/Refractory Therapy (HCL-A)
- Incomplete hematologic recovery, by HCL Response Criteria (HCL-B)
    - Relapsed/Refractory Therapy (HCL-A), if indications for treatment are present after initial therapy
- Progression, by HCL Response Criteria (HCL-B)
    - Progressive Disease After Relapsed/Refractory Therapy (HCL-A)

# Next

- No indication for treatment, observe until indication for treatment
- Indication present, go to Initial Therapy (HCL-A)
- Relapse at 2 years or later, re-evaluate for indications for treatment
- Relapse at <2 years, go to Relapsed/Refractory Therapy (HCL-A)
- Incomplete hematologic recovery with indications for treatment present after initial therapy, go to Relapsed/Refractory Therapy (HCL-A)
- Progression, go to Progressive Disease After Relapsed/Refractory Therapy (HCL-A)
- Supportive care alongside initial and relapsed/refractory therapy, see HCL-C
