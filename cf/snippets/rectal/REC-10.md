# Low-risk polyps removed by polypectomy

- Physical exam and proctoscopy every 3–6 mo for the first 2 y
- Colonoscopy at 1 y after polypectomy
    - Counsel for family history; if LS, FAP, or attenuated FAP is suspected, see the NCCN Guidelines for Genetic/Familial High-Risk Assessment: Colorectal, Endometrial, and Gastric

# Transanal local excision only

- Proctoscopy every 3–6 mo for the first 2 y, then every 6 mo for a total of 5 y
    - With EUS, or MRI with contrast
- Colonoscopy at 1 y after surgery
    - Counsel for family history; if LS, FAP, or attenuated FAP is suspected, see the NCCN Guidelines for Genetic/Familial High-Risk Assessment: Colorectal, Endometrial, and Gastric
    - If advanced adenoma, repeat in 1 y
    - If no advanced adenoma, repeat in 3 y, then every 5 y
        - Advanced adenoma means villous polyp, polyp >1 cm, or high-grade dysplasia
        - Interval per Kahi CJ, et al. Gastroenterology 2016;150:758-768

# Stage I with full surgical staging

- Colonoscopy at 1 y after surgery
    - Counsel for family history; if LS, FAP, or attenuated FAP is suspected, see the NCCN Guidelines for Genetic/Familial High-Risk Assessment: Colorectal, Endometrial, and Gastric
    - If advanced adenoma, repeat in 1 y
    - If no advanced adenoma, repeat in 3 y, then every 5 y
        - Advanced adenoma means villous polyp, polyp >1 cm, or high-grade dysplasia
        - Interval per Kahi CJ, et al. Gastroenterology 2016;150:758-768

# Stage II–IV

- ctDNA is not recommended for surveillance
- History and physical examination every 3–6 mo for 2 y, then every 6 mo for a total of 5 y
- CEA every 3–6 mo for 2 y, then every 6 mo for a total of 5 y
    - Only if the patient is a potential candidate for resection of isolated metastasis
- Chest/abdomen/pelvis (C/A/P) CT
    - Stage II, III: every 6–12 mo for a total of 5 y (category 2B for frequency <12 mo)
    - Stage IV: every 3–6 mo x 2 y (category 2B for frequency <6 mo), then every 6–12 mo for a total of 5 y
- Colonoscopy in 1 y after surgery
    - Counsel for family history; if LS, FAP, or attenuated FAP is suspected, see the NCCN Guidelines for Genetic/Familial High-Risk Assessment: Colorectal, Endometrial, and Gastric
    - Except if no complete preoperative colonoscopy, then colonoscopy in 3–6 mo
    - In stage IV disease managed nonoperatively with complete clinical response, initiate colonoscopy surveillance from first documentation of complete response
    - If advanced adenoma, repeat in 1 y
    - If no advanced adenoma, repeat in 3 y, then every 5 y
        - Advanced adenoma means villous polyp, polyp >1 cm, or high-grade dysplasia
        - Interval per Kahi CJ, et al. Gastroenterology 2016;150:758-768
- FDG-PET/CT is not recommended
- Principles of Survivorship (REC-G)
