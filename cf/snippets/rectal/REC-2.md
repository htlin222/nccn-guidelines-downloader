# Clinical presentation

- Counsel for family history, for all patients with rectal cancer
    - For suspected LS, FAP, and attenuated FAP, see the NCCN Guidelines for Genetic/Familial High-Risk Assessment: Colorectal, Endometrial, and Gastric
- For melanoma histology, see the NCCN Guidelines for Melanoma: Cutaneous

# Pathology and biomarkers

- Biopsy
- MMR/MSI testing
    - Principles of Pathologic Review (REC-B)
- Testing for somatic PI3K pathway alterations, for stage II-III
    - Somatic PI3K pathway alterations include mutations in PIK3CA exon 9 and 20
    - Other PIK3CA, PIK3R1, and PTEN mutations
    - Deep deletions of PTEN
    - Aspirin should not be initiated until after recovery from surgery
    - For patients receiving adjuvant therapy and aspirin, aspirin can be given concurrently with adjuvant chemotherapy
- Pathology review

# Endoscopy

- Colonoscopy
- Consider proctoscopy
    - Principles of Surgery and Locoregional Therapies (REC-C)

# Imaging

- Chest CT and abdominal CT or MRI
    - Principles of Imaging (REC-A)
- Pelvis MRI with or without contrast
    - Principles of Imaging (REC-A)
- Endorectal ultrasound, if MRI is contraindicated or inconclusive, or for superficial lesions
    - Principles of Imaging (REC-A)
- FDG-PET/CT is not indicated
    - Principles of Imaging (REC-A)

# Laboratory

- CBC
- Chemistry profile
- CEA

# Supportive and multidisciplinary care

- Enterostomal therapist as indicated for preoperative marking of site, teaching
- Multidisciplinary team evaluation, including formal surgical evaluation
- Fertility risk discussion/counseling in appropriate patients
