# History, examination, and CEA

- History and physical examination
    - Every 3–6 months for 2 years
    - Then every 6 months for a total of 5 years
- CEA
    - Every 3–6 months for 2 years
    - Then every 6 months for a total of 5 years

# Local surveillance of the rectum

- Digital rectal examination (DRE)
    - Every 3–4 months for 2 years
    - Then every 6 months for a total of 5 years
- Proctoscopy or flexible sigmoidoscopy
    - Every 3–4 months for 2 years
    - Then every 6 months for a total of 5 years
- MRI rectum
    - Every 6 months for up to 3 years

# Systemic imaging

- CT chest/abdomen
    - Every 6–12 months for a total of 5 years
- CT pelvis
    - To be included once no longer doing MRI rectum

# Colonoscopy

- Colonoscopy at 1 year following completion of therapy
    - If advanced adenoma, repeat in 1 year
    - If no advanced adenoma, repeat in 3 years, then every 5 years
