# Risk status and initiation of surveillance

- History of solid organ transplant
    - Initiate at age ≥30 y, or within 2 y of the transplantation
- No history of solid organ transplant
    - Initiate at age ≥40 y

# Surveillance modality and schedule

- Colonoscopy
    - Patient should undergo cystic fibrosis-specific intensive bowel preparation
    - Alternative screening tests could be considered, but data on their efficacy in cystic fibrosis are limited

# Evaluation of surveillance findings

- Normal
    - Repeat colonoscopy in 5 y
- Adenoma(s) or SSP/SSL(s)
    - Repeat colonoscopy in 3 y

# Next

- Normal colonoscopy, repeat colonoscopy in 5 y
- Adenoma(s) or SSP/SSL(s) at colonoscopy, repeat colonoscopy in 3 y
