# Paris subtype of the lesion

- Paris subtype 0–1p lesions
- Paris subtype 0–1s lesions
- Paris subtype 0–IIa lesions
- Paris subtype 0–IIb lesions
- Paris subtype 0–IIc lesions
- Paris subtype 0–III lesions
    - For the 0–IIa, 0–IIb, 0–IIc and 0–III lesions, the Panel recommends consideration of referral to a center of expertise for management of these lesions

# Histology

- Histology may include
    - Adenoma
    - SSP/SSL
    - Hyperplastic polyp
    - TSA

# Referral and lesion marking

- Consider a referral to a center of expertise for large polyp management
- Recommend endoscopic tattoo placement for future lesion identification
    - For sessile polyps ≥20 mm size
    - For laterally spreading lesions (LSLs) ≥20 mm size

# High-risk features suggestive of submucosal invasion

- NICE classification type 3
- Kudo classification type V (VN and VI)
- Non-lifting sign

# Thermal ablation of the resection margin

- Systematic thermal ablation of the resection margin should be performed after complete resection of large colorectal polyps, to minimize recurrence
- Systematic thermal ablation of the resection margin should be performed after complete resection of piecemeal colorectal polyps, to minimize recurrence
- Use either
    - APC
    - Snare tip soft coagulation (STSC)
- Gauci JL, et al. Gut 2025;74:752-760

# Follow-up interval

- Consider follow-up in <3 years when confidence in complete en bloc resection is low
- Perry NJ, et al. Gastrointestinal Endoscopy;103:794-798
