# Reporting of small polyps on CTC

- The American College of Radiology has recommended that reporting of polyps ≤5 mm in size is not necessary
- If polyp(s) of this size are reported, the decision should be individualized
    - Refer for colonoscopy with polypectomy, or
    - Surveillance CTC
