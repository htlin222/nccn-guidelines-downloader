- Prevention of Pneumocystis jirovecii (Pneumocystis carinii) infection
- Categories of infection risk are based on several factors, including underlying malignancy, whether disease is in remission, duration of neutropenia, prior exposure to chemotherapy, CMV serostatus, and intensity of IST

# High risk for Pneumocystis jirovecii

- Allogeneic HCT (category 1)
    - Prophylaxis for at least 6 months and while receiving IST
- Cellular immunotherapies (ie, CAR T-cell, TCR, TIL therapy)
    - Prophylaxis for at least 6 months and while receiving IST
- ALL (category 1)
    - Prophylaxis throughout anti-leukemic therapy
- Alemtuzumab
    - Prophylaxis for a minimum of 2 mo
    - CD4 count >200 cells/mcL can be used as an adjunct in decision-making
- Select immune and targeted treatments (INF-A)
    - Prophylaxis at least through active treatment
- Recipients of prolonged corticosteroids
    - Prophylaxis at least through active treatment
    - Risk of PJP is related to the daily dose and duration of corticosteroid therapy
    - Prophylaxis can be considered at the prednisone equivalent of 20 mg daily or more for 4 weeks or longer
- Receiving temozolomide with or without radiation therapy
    - Prophylaxis at least through active treatment
    - Continue prophylaxis until recovery from lymphocytopenia

# Consider prophylaxis (category 2B)

- Recipients of purine analog therapy and other T-cell-depleting agents
    - Prophylaxis for 6 to 12 mo
    - CD4 count >200 cells/mcL can be used as an adjunct in decision-making
- Autologous HCT
    - Prophylaxis 3 to 6 months after transplant

# Antipneumocystis prophylaxis

- See Antibacterial Agents (FEV-A) for dosing, spectrum, and specific comments/cautions
- Trimethoprim/sulfamethoxazole (TMP/SMX), preferred (category 1)
    - When appropriately dosed, may have activity against other pathogens including Nocardia, Toxoplasma, and Listeria
- If TMP/SMX intolerant, consider TMP/SMX desensitization
- If TMP/SMX intolerant, atovaquone, dapsone, or pentamidine (aerosolized or IV)
    - The list of agents is alphabetical and does not reflect preference
    - Atovaquone may have activity against Toxoplasma
    - For patients receiving dapsone, assessing G6PD levels prior to initiating therapy is recommended
