# Inactivated, subunit, or toxoid vaccines

- May be given as a combined vaccine
- Vaccination may be postponed for patients receiving >20 mg of prednisone
- Number of doses depends on which vaccine formulation is used
- Tdap, 6–12 months after HCT, 3 doses
    - DTaP (diphtheria, tetanus, and acellular pertussis) is not approved for use in ages >7 years
    - Can be used in adult HCT recipients despite the age restriction, given the better response
    - FDA/ACIP-approved 3-dose series for ages ≥7 years is Tdap/Td (tetanus-diphtheria)/Td
- Haemophilus influenzae type b (Hib), 6–12 months after HCT, 3 doses
- Pneumococcal vaccination, 3–6 months after HCT, 3–4 doses
- Hepatitis A (Hep A), 6–12 months after HCT, 2 doses
    - Strongly consider if clinically indicated
    - May consider Hep A and B combined vaccine if immunization for both is needed
- Hepatitis B (Hep B), 6–12 months after HCT, 2–3 doses
    - Strongly consider if clinically indicated
    - May consider Hep A and B combined vaccine if immunization for both is needed
- Meningococcal conjugate vaccine (ACWY), 6–12 months after HCT, 2 doses
    - Meningococcal B vaccine should be considered for patients at high risk
        - Chronic GVHD
        - Asplenia
        - Complement deficiency
        - Receiving a complement C5 inhibitor (eg, eculizumab, ravulizumab)
- Influenza (injectable), 3–6 months after HCT, 1 dose, annually
    - Consider high-dose vaccine or a re-dose
- Inactivated polio vaccine, 6–12 months after HCT, 3 doses
- Recombinant zoster vaccine, 2 doses
    - 50–70 days after autologous HCT
    - May be considered after allogeneic HCT
- HPV vaccine, >6 to 12 months after HCT, 3 doses
    - For patients ≤26 years
    - Consider up to age 45
- COVID-19, 6 months after HCT, ≥1 dose
    - May consider early vaccination at 3 months during community outbreaks and high disease activity
    - See INF-7 for additional information

# Live vaccines

- Refer to the 2-1-8 rule as proposed by Carpenter and Englund
- Measles/mumps/rubella (MMR), ≥24 months after HCT, 1–2 doses
    - May vaccinate earlier when risk:benefit ratio suggests
- Varicella vaccine, ≥24 months after HCT, 2 doses
    - Only if no GVHD or ongoing immunosuppression
    - Only if patient was seronegative for varicella pretransplant
