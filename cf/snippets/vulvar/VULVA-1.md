# Workup

- History and physical (H&P)
- Complete blood count (CBC)
- Biopsy, pathologic review
    - Principles of Pathology (VULVA-A)
- Liver function tests / renal function studies
- Imaging as needed for delineating extent of tumor or for treatment planning
    - Principles of Imaging (VULVA-B)
- Examination under anesthesia (EUA) cystoscopy or proctoscopy as indicated
- Smoking cessation and counseling intervention if indicated
    - NCCN Guidelines for Smoking Cessation
- Consider cervical human papillomavirus (HPV) and cytology testing
- Consider human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) testing
    - Especially in younger patients suspected of having SCC of the vulva or other HPV-related disease
    - Patients with vulvar cancer and HIV should be referred to an HIV specialist
    - Patients with vulvar cancer and HIV should be treated for vulvar cancer as per these guidelines
    - Modifications to cancer treatment should not be made solely on the basis of HIV status
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Cancer in People with HIV
- For patients with vulvar cancer who are older, also see the NCCN Guidelines for Older Adult Oncology
- Assess for distress
    - Refer to the NCCN Distress Thermometer and Problem List, which includes social determinants of health
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Distress Management (DIS-A)
