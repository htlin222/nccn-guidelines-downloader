# Source

- NCCN Vulvar and Vulvovaginal Melanoma v2.2026, VM-2, p38
- Applies to mucosal vulvovaginal melanoma, defined as lesions that occur on the vulva vestibule inside Hart's line
- Cutaneous vulvar melanoma is defined as lesions that occur on the vulva vestibule outside Hart's line
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Workup

- H&P
- Biopsy, pathologic review
- Imaging
    - See Principles of Imaging (ME-D) in the NCCN Guidelines for Melanoma: Cutaneous
- EUA cystoscopy or proctoscopy as indicated
- Consider somatic mutational testing for vulvar melanoma and mucosal vulvovaginal melanoma as clinically indicated (ie, BRAF, KIT)
    - See Principles of Molecular Testing (ME-C) in the NCCN Guidelines for Melanoma: Cutaneous
- Full skin assessment
- Assess for distress
    - Refer to the NCCN Distress Thermometer and Problem List, which includes social determinants of health
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Distress Management (DIS-A)

# Primary treatment

- Resectable
    - Wide local excision with clear margins, at least 1-cm margin if feasible
        - For invasive melanoma, recommend at least 1-cm margins, if feasible, with cautionary measures to avoid disfigurement
        - See Principles of Surgery (VULVA-C)
    - Consider SLNB
        - See Principles of Sentinel Lymph Node Biopsy (SLNB) (ME-F) in the NCCN Guidelines for Melanoma: Cutaneous
- Unresectable, or residual disease
    - Systemic therapy
        - See Systemic Therapy for Metastatic or Unresectable Disease (MELSYS-1) in the NCCN Guidelines for Melanoma: Cutaneous
    - With or without RT
        - See Principles of Radiation Therapy (VM-A)

# Adjuvant treatment

- Observation
- With or without RT, brachytherapy preferred for vaginal lesions
    - See Principles of Radiation Therapy (VM-A)
- With or without EBRT
    - See Principles of Radiation Therapy (VM-A)
- With or without systemic therapy
    - See Systemic Therapy for Metastatic or Unresectable Disease (MELSYS-1) in the NCCN Guidelines for Melanoma: Cutaneous
