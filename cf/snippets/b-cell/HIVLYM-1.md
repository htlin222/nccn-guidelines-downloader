# Essential: immunophenotyping

- Adequate immunophenotyping to establish diagnosis and subclassification
    - eg, DLBCL
    - eg, BL
    - eg, plasmablastic lymphoma
    - eg, PEL
- IHC panel
    - CD45
    - CD20
    - CD3
    - CD10
    - BCL2
    - BCL6
    - Ki-67
    - CD138
    - Kappa/lambda
    - HHV8 latency-associated nuclear antigen (LANA)
        - HHV8 can also be detected by polymerase chain reaction (PCR)
    - CD30 for PEL
- With or without flow cytometry, with peripheral blood and/or biopsy specimen
    - Kappa/lambda
    - CD45
    - CD3
    - CD5
    - CD19
    - CD10
    - CD20

# Essential: genetic and viral testing

- FISH for MYC
- FISH for BCL2 rearrangements, if MYC positive
- FISH for BCL6 rearrangements, if MYC positive
- EBER-ISH
