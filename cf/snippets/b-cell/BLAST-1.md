# Source

- NCCN B-Cell Lymphomas v4.2026, BLAST-1, p106
- Lymphoblastic lymphoma: additional diagnostic testing and workup
- The LL category comprises two diseases
    - T-cell LL (T-LBL), 90%, which corresponds to T-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia (T-ALL), with presentations in extramedullary sites
    - B-cell LL (B-LL), 10%, which corresponds to B-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia (B-ALL), with presentations in extramedullary sites
    - See Cytogenetic and Molecular Prognostic Risk Stratification for B-ALL in the NCCN Guidelines for Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia
- This disease is complex and curable; it is preferred that treatment occur at centers with expertise in the management of the disease
- Special Considerations for Adolescent and Young Adult (AYA) Patients with B-Cell Lymphomas (NHODG-B 4 of 5)
- All recommendations are category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Additional diagnostic testing, essential

- Adequate immunophenotyping to establish diagnosis
    - Typical immunophenotype, B-LL: sIg-, CD10+/-, CD19+, CD20-/+, TdT+, CD34+, CD79a+
    - Typical immunophenotype, T-LL: sIg-, CD10-, CD19/20-, CD3-/+, CD4/8+/+, CD1a+/-, TdT+, CD2+, CD7+, cytoplasmic CD3+, sCD3-/+
- IHC panel
    - CD45, CD19, CD20, CD79a, CD3, CD2, CD5, CD7, TdT, CD1a, CD10, cyclin D1, myeloperoxidase, lysozyme, CD34, CD4, CD8
- With or without flow cytometry, with peripheral blood and/or biopsy specimen
    - kappa/lambda, CD45, CD3, CD5, CD4, CD7, CD8, CD19, CD20, CD10, TdT, CD13, CD33, CD34, CD1a, cytoplasmic CD3, CD22, myeloperoxidase
- FISH: MYC
- FISH: t(9;22)
- FISH: t(8;14) and variants
- Or PCR for BCR::ABL1

# Additional diagnostic testing, useful under certain circumstances

- Additional immunohistochemical studies to establish lymphoma subtype
    - IHC panel: CD22, CD4, CD8, cyclin D1
- Molecular analysis to detect Ig gene rearrangements
- FISH: Ph-like

# Workup, essential

- Physical exam
    - Attention to node-bearing areas, including Waldeyer's ring
    - Attention to size of liver and spleen
- Performance status
- B symptoms
- CBC with differential
- LDH
- Comprehensive metabolic panel
- Uric acid, phosphate
- C/A/P CT with contrast of diagnostic quality
- Lumbar puncture
- Flow cytometry of cerebrospinal fluid
- Bilateral or unilateral bone marrow biopsy, with or without aspirate, with flow and cytogenetics
- Hepatitis B testing
    - Indicated because of the risk of reactivation with immunotherapy + chemotherapy
    - HBsAg and core antibody for a patient with no risk factors
    - For patients with risk factors or previous history of hepatitis B, add e-antigen (NHODG-B)
    - If positive, check viral load and consider consult with gastroenterologist
- Echocardiogram or MUGA scan, if an anthracycline or anthracenedione-based regimen is indicated
- Pregnancy testing in patients of childbearing age, if therapy planned

# Workup, useful in selected cases

- Beta-2-microglobulin
- Brain MRI with and without contrast
- PET/CT scan
    - Initiation of therapy should not be delayed in order to obtain a PET/CT scan
- Discuss fertility preservation
    - Options include sperm banking, IVF, or ovarian tissue or oocyte cryopreservation
