# Source

- NCCN Breast Cancer v6.2026, PHYLL-1, p108
- Special considerations: phyllodes tumor
- Applies when there is clinical suspicion of phyllodes
    - Palpable mass
    - Rapid growth
    - Large size (>3 cm)
    - Imaging with ultrasound suggestive of fibroadenoma except for size and/or history of growth
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Workup

- History and physical exam
- Ultrasound
- Mammogram for patients 30 y and older
- Assess for distress
    - NCCN Distress Thermometer and Problem List, which includes social determinants of health (DIS-A)
- Core needle biopsy
    - FNA or core biopsy may not distinguish a fibroadenoma from a phyllodes tumor in some cases
    - Sensitivity of core biopsy for the diagnosis of phyllodes tumor is greater than that of FNA biopsy
    - Neither core biopsy nor FNA biopsy can always differentiate phyllodes tumors from fibroadenomas
    - In cases with clinical suspicion for phyllodes tumor, excision of the lesion may be needed for definitive pathologic classification

# Core biopsy results

- Indeterminate or suspicious for a benign phyllodes
    - Excisional biopsy
        - Includes complete mass removal, but without the intent of obtaining surgical margins
    - Genetic counseling and testing if patient is at risk for hereditary cancer syndromes, particularly breast, ovarian, and pancreatic cancer
- Suspicious for borderline/malignant phyllodes
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Soft Tissue Sarcoma (PHYLLSARC-1)
    - Genetic counseling and testing if patient is at risk for hereditary cancer syndromes, particularly breast, ovarian, and pancreatic cancer
- Invasive or in situ cancer
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Noninvasive Breast Cancer for DCIS (DCIS-1), or Invasive Breast Cancer (BINV-1)

# Excisional biopsy results

- Fibroadenoma or fibroepithelial lesion
    - Observe
- Benign phyllodes
    - Clinical follow-up for 3 y, no RT
        - Adjuvant RT is not indicated for positive margins
    - Genetic counseling and testing if patient is at risk for hereditary cancer syndromes, particularly breast, ovarian, and pancreatic cancer
    - The Panel endorses the College of American Pathologists Protocol for standardized pathology reporting for all phyllodes tumors
- Phyllodes, borderline or malignant
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Soft Tissue Sarcoma (PHYLLSARC-1)
    - Genetic counseling and testing if patient is at risk for hereditary cancer syndromes, particularly breast, ovarian, and pancreatic cancer
    - The Panel endorses the College of American Pathologists Protocol for standardized pathology reporting for all phyllodes tumors
