# Source

- NCCN Small Bowel Adenocarcinoma v2.2026, SBA-6, p12
- Applies to resected small bowel adenocarcinoma, location duodenum or jejunum/ileum, pMMR/MSS
- Pathologic stage per Principles of Pathologic Review (SBA-B)
- Systemic therapy regimens: Principles of Systemic Therapy (SBA-D 4 of 12)
- Enrollment in a clinical trial is encouraged
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# High-risk features in stage II

- Close or positive resection margins
- <5 lymph nodes examined if duodenal location
- <8 lymph nodes examined if jejunal/ileal primary tumor location
- Tumor perforation
- Further consideration may be made for administering chemotherapy in stage II disease with
    - Lymphovascular or perineural invasion
    - Poorly differentiated histology
    - Based on data extrapolated from colorectal cancer studies

# Adjuvant treatment by pathologic stage

- T1–2, N0, M0
    - Observation
- T3, N0, M0, no high-risk features
    - Observation, or
    - Consider fluorouracil/leucovorin or capecitabine
- T3, N0, M0 with high-risk features, or T4, N0, M0
    - Observation, or
    - FOLFOX or CAPEOX (3–6 mo)
        - No patients with SBA were included in the IDEA pooled analysis of adjuvant colon cancer trials
        - In the absence of direct data in SBA, the noninferior 3-year disease-free survival of 3 months versus 6 months of CAPEOX in colon cancer may be extrapolated
    - Fluorouracil/leucovorin or capecitabine (6 mo), or
    - For duodenal cancers with a positive margin, consider sequential chemo/RT with capecitabine or infusional fluorouracil (SBA-E)
- T any, N1–2
    - FOLFOX or CAPEOX (3–6 mo)
        - No patients with SBA were included in the IDEA pooled analysis of adjuvant colon cancer trials
        - In the absence of direct data in SBA, the noninferior 3-year disease-free survival of 3 months versus 6 months of CAPEOX in colon cancer may be extrapolated
    - Fluorouracil/leucovorin or capecitabine (6 mo), or
    - For duodenal cancers with a positive margin, consider sequential chemo/RT with capecitabine or infusional fluorouracil (SBA-E)

# Surveillance

- No studies have been performed to assess ideal surveillance intervals for SBA; colorectal cancer surveillance data is currently accepted as appropriate for SBA
- History and physical examination every 3–6 mo for 2 y, then every 6 mo for a total of 5 y
- CEA and CA 19-9 every 3–6 mo for 2 y, then every 6 mo for a total of 5 y
- Chest/abdomen/pelvis CT every 6–12 mo for 2 y, then every 12 mo for y 3–5
- FDG-PET/CT is not indicated
- Routine capsule endoscopy is not indicated
- Principles of Survivorship (SBA-F)
