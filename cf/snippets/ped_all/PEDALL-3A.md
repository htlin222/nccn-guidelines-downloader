# Special populations

- Down syndrome, see Special Considerations for Patients with Down Syndrome and Infants (PEDALL-E)
- AYA patients, 15–21 years of age
    - Several retrospective studies have shown substantially improved event-free survival (EFS) when treated on a pediatric protocol, compared to same-aged patients treated on adult ALL regimens

# Systemic therapy

- Principles of Systemic Therapy (PEDALL-G)
- BCR::ABL1-like ALL
    - Tyrosine kinase inhibitors (TKIs) may be considered, see Principles of Systemic Therapy (PEDALL-G)
- Blinatumomab, incorporated into frontline therapy as a postremission approach
    - Based on data from AALL1731, as well as the ECOG1910 study in adults
    - May cause severe, life-threatening, or fatal adverse events, including cytokine release syndrome (CRS) and neurologic toxicities
    - Experience in the use of the drug, as well as resources to monitor the patient closely, are essential
    - Follow the instructions for blinatumomab product preparation (including admixing) and administration strictly, to minimize medication errors including underdosing and overdosing
    - For details, see the blinatumomab prescribing information (https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cder/daf/index.cfm)
    - For patients with an expected very favorable outcome, weigh the benefit of blinatumomab against potential toxicities, including increased infection risk
