# Imaging for major neurologic signs or symptoms

- For patients with major neurologic signs or symptoms at diagnosis, appropriate imaging studies should be performed
    - To detect meningeal disease
    - To detect chloromas
    - To detect central nervous system (CNS) bleeding
- See Evaluation and Treatment of Extramedullary Involvement (PEDALL-D)

# Lumbar puncture and intrathecal chemotherapy

- Timing of LP should be consistent with the chosen treatment regimen
- Pediatric-inspired regimens typically include LP and prophylactic IT chemotherapy at the time of diagnostic workup
- The Panel recommends that LP be done concurrently with initial IT therapy

# Additional evaluation

- Additional testing may be warranted according to clinical symptoms and clinician discretion

# Germline predisposition

- Genes for pathologic germline variants are often somatically mutated in ALL
    - Particularly PAX5, ETV6, and IKZF1
    - These have been shown to confer predisposition to developing B-ALL
- See also the NCCN Guidelines for Genetic/Familial High-Risk Assessment: Breast, Ovarian, Pancreatic, and Prostate

# Fertility preservation

- Fertility preservation is an option for certain patients
- Options
    - Sperm cryopreservation
    - Oocyte cryopreservation
    - Harvesting of ovarian or testicular tissue for cryopreservation
    - Embryo cryopreservation
- Referral to a fertility preservation/reproductive health program should be considered for certain patients

# BCR::ABL1-like classification

- BCR::ABL1-like ALL is classified using
    - LDA
    - FISH
    - RT-PCR
    - NGS
