# Disease extent

- Clinically localized prostate cancer (Any T, N0, M0 or Any T, NX, MX)
- Regional prostate cancer (Any T, N1, M0)
- Metastatic prostate cancer (Any T, Any N, M1)

# Workup, clinically localized prostate cancer

- Perform physical exam
- Perform digital rectal examination (DRE) to confirm clinical stage
- Perform and/or collect prostate-specific antigen (PSA)
- Review diagnostic prostate biopsies
- Estimate life expectancy
    - Principles of Life Expectancy Estimation (PROS-A)
- Inquire about known high-risk germline mutations and family history
    - Perform somatic and/or germline testing as appropriate
    - Principles of Genetics and Molecular/Biomarker Analysis (PROS-C)
- Assess quality-of-life measures
    - Principles of Quality of Life and Shared Decision-Making (PROS-D)

# Workup, regional or metastatic prostate cancer

- Perform physical examination
- Perform bone and soft tissue imaging for staging
    - Principles of Imaging (PROS-E)
- Consider DRE to confirm clinical stage
- Perform and/or collect PSA and calculate PSA doubling time (PSADT)
- Estimate life expectancy
    - Principles of Life Expectancy Estimation (PROS-A)
- Inquire about known high-risk germline mutations and family history
    - Perform somatic and/or germline testing as appropriate
    - Principles of Genetics and Molecular/Biomarker Analysis (PROS-C)
- Assess quality-of-life measures
    - Principles of Quality of Life and Shared Decision-Making (PROS-D)
