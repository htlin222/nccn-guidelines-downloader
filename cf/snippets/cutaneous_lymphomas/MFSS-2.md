# Source

- NCCN Mycosis Fungoides/Sezary Syndrome v2.2026, MFSS-2, p20
- Workup of mycosis fungoides / Sezary syndrome
- All recommendations are category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- ___ (bsa) % body surface area involved
- Type of skin lesion: ___ (lesion_type)
- Skin classification: ___ (skin_class)
- Peripheral adenopathy on palpation: ___ (adenopathy)
- LDH ___ (ldh)

# Essential: history and complete physical examination

- Complete skin examination
    - Assessment of % body surface area (BSA)
        - Palm plus all 5 digits is about 1% BSA
    - Type of skin lesion: patch/plaque, tumor, or erythroderma
- Palpation of peripheral LN regions
- Palpation for organomegaly/masses

# Essential: laboratory studies

- CBC with differential and determination of absolute lymphocyte count
- Flow cytometry to assess and quantitate an expanded T-cell population with aberrant phenotype (MFSS-3)
    - Optional for T1; see Discussion for when Sezary flow cytometric study is appropriate in T1
    - Recommended for any patient with T2-4 skin classification
    - Recommended for any suspected extracutaneous disease including adenopathy
- Clonal TRB and TRG gene rearrangement in peripheral blood lymphocytes, if blood involvement suspected
    - Principles of Biomarker Testing in Cutaneous Lymphomas (PCLYM-B)
- Comprehensive metabolic panel
- LDH
- Sezary syndrome (B2) is as defined on MFSS-3

# Essential: imaging studies

- Integrated whole body FDG-PET/CT (preferred), or C/A/P CT with contrast
    - Indicated for T2b or higher, or LCT, or FMF
    - Indicated with palpable adenopathy or abnormal laboratory studies
    - Consider for T2a, that is patch disease with 10% BSA or more
    - Consider if otherwise clinically indicated: new significant adenopathy on clinical exam, abnormal laboratory results concerning of lymphoma, or accelerated skin disease
    - Patients with cutaneous lymphomas have extranodal disease, which may be inadequately imaged by CT; PET scan may be preferred in these instances

# Essential: distress

- Assess for distress
    - Refer to the NCCN Distress Thermometer and Problem List, which includes social determinants of health (DIS-A)

# Useful in certain circumstances

- Bone marrow biopsy in patients with unexplained hematologic abnormality
- Biopsy of enlarged LN or suspected extracutaneous sites
    - Excisional or adequate core needle biopsy is preferred
    - A fine-needle aspiration (FNA) biopsy alone is insufficient for the initial diagnosis of lymphoma
    - Rebiopsy if pathologic findings are nondiagnostic and/or discordant with the clinical presentation
- Rebiopsy skin if suspicious of LCT or FMF not previously confirmed pathologically, or if aggressive clinical behavior
- Neck CT with contrast if whole body FDG-PET/CT not done
- Pregnancy testing in patients of childbearing potential if contemplating treatments that are contraindicated in pregnancy
    - Many skin-directed and systemic therapies are contraindicated or of unknown safety in pregnancy; refer to full prescribing information for individual drugs
- Discuss fertility preservation
    - Options include sperm banking, IVF, or ovarian tissue or oocyte cryopreservation
