# Source

- NCCN Cutaneous Lymphomas v2.2026, PCTLD-4, p51
- Primary cutaneous CD30+ T-cell lymphoproliferative disorders: primary cutaneous ALCL (PC-ALCL)
- Excludes systemic ALCL
- Regression of lesions may occur in up to 44% of cases
- Therapy references (PCTLD-A)
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

- ___ (age) yo with PC-ALCL

    - N1 requires biopsy-proven lymphoma in LN (see CUTB-A for N definition)
- Prior treatment: ___ (prior_treatment)

# Primary treatment - solitary or grouped lesions

- ISRT (PCLYM-A)
- Surgical excision
    - Small lesions may be excised with minimal non-disfiguring surgery
    - With or without ISRT (PCLYM-A)

# Primary treatment - multifocal lesions

- Preferred
    - Brentuximab vedotin, with supportive care (PCLYM-C)
- Other recommended, each with or without skin-directed therapies (MFSS-A)
    - Methotrexate (≤50 mg weekly)
    - Systemic retinoids
        - Limited data from case reports (eg, bexarotene)
    - Pralatrexate
    - Observation, if asymptomatic
    - Interferon (category 3)
        - Peginterferon or ropeginterferon alfa-2b-njft may be substituted for other interferon preparations, if peginterferon alfa-2a is unavailable

# Primary treatment - PC-ALCL with regional node (N1)

- Preferred
    - Brentuximab vedotin, with supportive care (PCLYM-C), with or without ISRT (PCLYM-A)
    - ISRT (PCLYM-A) in selected cases
        - ISRT to include LN(s), with or without primary skin lesions
- Other recommended
    - Brentuximab vedotin + CHP (cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin, and prednisone)
    - Methotrexate, with or without ISRT (PCLYM-A)
    - Pralatrexate, with or without ISRT (PCLYM-A)
    - CHOP or CHOEP, with or without ISRT (PCLYM-A), in selected cases
- ISRT given here is to include LN(s), with or without primary skin lesions

# Follow-up

- MF can develop over time; continue to conduct thorough skin exam during follow-up
- Response: observe for recurrence
    - Patients with a clinical benefit and/or those with disease responding to primary treatment should be considered for maintenance or tapering of regimens to optimize response duration
- Partial response: treat with other primary treatment options not received before, to improve response before moving onto treatment for refractory disease
- Relapsed disease often responds well to the same treatment
- Patients with disease relapse or persistent disease after initial primary treatment may be candidates for clinical trials

# Relapsed/refractory disease

- Disease confined to skin: retreat with initial treatment
    - Then observe for recurrence
- Multifocal lesions or extracutaneous involvement: treat as multifocal lesions above
- After multifocal or regional node disease fails to respond or is refractory
    - Clinical trial
    - Retreat with same regimen, unless refractory or intolerant
    - Alternative regimen not used for primary treatment
    - Systemic Therapies for Large-Cell Transformation (LCT) (Table 6 - Suggested Regimens for MF with LCT)
