# Primary treatment - limited lesions, asymptomatic

- Observation (preferred)
- or Topical steroids

# Primary treatment - limited lesions, symptomatic

- Topical steroids
- or Phototherapy
    - NB-UVB is generally preferred over PUVA
- or Observation

# Primary treatment - extensive lesions

- Observation (preferred for asymptomatic)
- or Methotrexate
    - 10-35 mg weekly (Kempf W, et al. Blood 2011;118:4024-4035)
- or Phototherapy
    - NB-UVB is generally preferred over PUVA
- or Systemic retinoids
    - Limited data from case reports (eg, bexarotene)
- or Topical steroids
- or Topical mechlorethamine (nitrogen mustard)

# Follow-up

- Life-long follow-up is warranted due to high risk for second lymphoid malignancies
    - Continue to conduct thorough skin exam during follow-up
- Response
    - Continue current management
    - or Observe for recurrence
    - Consider maintenance or tapering of regimens to optimize response duration for patients with a clinical benefit and/or disease responding to primary treatment
    - Relapsed disease often responds well to the same treatment
    - PR should be treated with other primary treatment options not received before, to improve response before moving onto treatment for refractory disease
    - Patients with disease relapse or persistent disease after initial primary treatment may be candidates for clinical trials
- No response/refractory disease
    - Go to relapsed/refractory disease options below

# Relapsed/refractory disease

- Clinical trial
- or Observation
- or Retreat with primary treatment, or treat with alternative regimen not used for primary treatment
- or Alternative regimen not used for primary treatment
- or Other regimens

# Refractory disease after relapsed/refractory treatment

- Clinical trial
- or Brentuximab vedotin

# Next

- Response, continue current management or observe for recurrence
- No response/refractory disease, use the relapsed/refractory disease options above
- Refractory after relapsed/refractory treatment, clinical trial or brentuximab vedotin
