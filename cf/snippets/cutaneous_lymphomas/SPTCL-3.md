# First-line therapy

- Consider single agents, with or without prednisone
    - Cyclosporine, at least 5 mg/kg/day
        - Oral cyclosporine is typically initiated at 3-5 mg/kg/day in divided doses
        - Higher dosage may be necessary to achieve disease control
        - Dose adjustment is based on response and tolerance
        - If disease responds to first-line therapy, consider slow tapering as tolerated or cyclosporine maintenance
        - Dose given ___ (cyclosporine_dose)
    - Methotrexate, PO or SC or IM
    - Bexarotene, PO
- or Local therapy
    - ISRT
        - Consider ISRT for single lesion or limited disease, with or without symptoms or HLH
    - or Intralesional steroids

# Response assessment and additional therapy

- CR or PR
    - Observation
    - or Maintenance therapy with single agents
        - Cyclosporine
        - Methotrexate
        - Bexarotene
        - Disease achieving a clinical benefit and/or responding to first-line therapy should be considered for maintenance or tapering of regimens, to optimize response duration
- Inadequate response, or relapse
    - Retreat with same regimen
    - or Alternate regimen not used in first-line therapy
    - or Local therapy, if not previously used for first-line therapy
- Relapse or refractory disease after multiple previous therapies
    - Consider pralatrexate or romidepsin, with or without prednisone
    - or Alternate regimens, see NCCN Guidelines for T-Cell Lymphomas, Initial Palliative-Intent Therapy PTCL-NOS [PTCL-B 2 of 8]
    - or Clinical trial
