# Initial evaluation

- CT of chest with contrast
- CT or MRI of abdomen/pelvis with contrast
- Consider FDG-PET/CT
- Midline laparoscopy with nodule/mass biopsies
- Image-guided core biopsy, if bulky disease or visceral organ metastasis
- Tumor Ki-67 index
- Soluble mesothelin-related peptide (SMRP), optional
- Assess for distress
    - Refer to the NCCN Distress Thermometer and Problem List, which includes social determinants of health
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Distress Management (DIS-A)
