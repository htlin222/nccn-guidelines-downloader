- Two arms on this page: resected localized disease, and resected metastatic disease
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

- No high-grade peritoneal deposit
- ___ (disease_extent) disease
- Date of surgery ___ (surgery_date), now ___ (mo_since_surgery) mo from date of surgery
- CEA ___ (cea) / CA 19-9 ___ (ca19_9) / CA-125 ___ (ca125)
- Preoperative colonoscopy ___ (prior_colonoscopy)
- Surveillance may not be needed if all of the following hold
    - LAMN, completely resected
    - Confined to the appendix
    - Without high-risk features
        - eg, extra-appendiceal cellular mucin
        - eg, presence of signet ring cells

# Resected localized disease

- History and physical examination
    - Every 6–12 mo for 2 y
    - Then annually for up to 10 y
- CEA, CA 19-9, CA-125
    - Every 6–12 mo for 2 y
    - Then annually for up to 10 y
    - It may not be necessary to measure all tumor markers at all surveillance timepoints, as guided by prior marker elevations for the specific patient
- A/P CT or MRI
    - Every 6–12 mo from date of surgery for 2 years
    - Then every 12–24 mo for up to 10 y
- FDG-PET/CT is not indicated
- Colonoscopy, if no preoperative colonoscopy was done
- Principles of Survivorship (APP-E)

# Resected metastatic disease

- History and physical examination
    - Every 6 mo for 2 y
    - Then annually for up to 10 y
- CEA, CA 19-9, CA-125
    - Every 6–12 mo for 2 y
    - Then annually for up to 10 y
    - It may not be necessary to measure all tumor markers at all surveillance timepoints, as guided by prior marker elevations for the specific patient
- C/A/P CT or MRI
    - Every 6 mo from date of surgery for 2 years
    - Then every 12 mo for up to 10 y
- FDG-PET/CT is not indicated
- Colonoscopy, if no preoperative colonoscopy was done
- Principles of Survivorship (APP-E)
