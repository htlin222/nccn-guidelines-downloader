# Workup for imaging suggesting appendiceal neoplasm or cancer

- CT abdomen/pelvis (A/P) with oral and IV contrast
- FDG-PET/CT is not indicated
    - Only considered in rare circumstances where CT findings are indeterminate, or when CT with contrast is not possible
    - See Principles of Imaging (APP-A)
- Complete blood count (CBC)
- Comprehensive metabolic panel (CMP)
- Carcinoembryonic antigen (CEA)
- CA 19-9
- CA-125
- Consider colonoscopy
    - Colonoscopy is needed for a confirmed appendiceal neoplasm to rule out other primary lesions
    - Could be done concurrently with the other diagnostic studies, but should not delay treatment

# Findings and diagnostic workup/primary treatment

- Appendix-only disease, no metastases suspected
    - Appendectomy
        - See Principles of Surgery and CRS/IPCT (APP-C) for circumstances where a right hemicolectomy may be recommended
- Peritoneal disease only
    - Diagnostic laparoscopy
        - Should be performed by a surgeon with experience in this disease process
        - If metastatic disease suspected, assess extent of disease (Peritoneal Cancer Index [PCI] score)
        - If metastatic disease suspected, obtain biopsies and/or appendectomy, if feasible
    - Image-guided biopsy is preferred for lesions that are safely accessible percutaneously when a non-surgical approach is preferred to confirm the diagnosis (eg, due to comorbidities or extraperitoneal disease)
        - If inaccessible, surgical biopsy may be needed for diagnostic confirmation
- Possible extraperitoneal disease
    - Obtain image-guided biopsy

# Workup for confirmed appendiceal neoplasm or cancer appropriate for resection, or incidental finding already resected

- CT chest/abdomen/pelvis (C/A/P) with oral and IV contrast
- FDG-PET/CT is not indicated
    - Only considered in rare circumstances where CT findings are indeterminate, or when CT with contrast is not possible
    - See Principles of Imaging (APP-A)
- Complete blood count (CBC)
- Comprehensive metabolic panel (CMP)
- Carcinoembryonic antigen (CEA)
- CA 19-9
- CA-125
- Colonoscopy
- Operative note review
    - Thorough review of operative notes is encouraged upon confirmation of appendiceal neoplasm in order to rule out more aggressive disease (eg, perforation, ascites present, signs of mucin)
- Fertility risk discussion/counseling in appropriate patients
