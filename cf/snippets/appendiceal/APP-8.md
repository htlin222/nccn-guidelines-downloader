# Surveillance: resected localized disease

- History and physical examination
    - Every 3–6 mo for 2 y
    - Then every 6 mo for a total of 5 y
- CEA, CA 19-9, CA-125
    - Every 3–6 mo for 2 y
    - Then 6–12 mo for a total of 5 y
- C/A/P CT
    - Every 6–12 mo from date of surgery for a total of 5 y
- FDG-PET/CT is not indicated
- Colonoscopy, if no preoperative colonoscopy was done
- Principles of Survivorship (APP-E)

# Surveillance: resected metastatic disease, including resected LAMN/HAMN primary with high-grade metastatic disease

- History and physical examination
    - Every 3–6 mo for 2 y
    - Then every 6 mo for a total of 5 y
- CEA, CA 19-9, CA-125
    - Every 3–6 mo for 2 y
    - Then every 6 mo for a total of 5 y
- C/A/P CT
    - Every 3–6 mo for 2 years
    - Then every 6–12 mo for a total of 5 y
- FDG-PET/CT is not indicated
- Colonoscopy, if no preoperative colonoscopy was done
- Principles of Survivorship (APP-E)
