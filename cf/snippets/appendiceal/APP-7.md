# Source

- NCCN Appendiceal Neoplasms and Cancers v2.2026, APP-7, p12
- Applies to metastatic AA, GCA, or UC-NOS that is peritoneal-only
- Also applies to high grade peritoneal metastases from LAMN/HAMN
- Peritoneal-only appendix cancer requires multidisciplinary team evaluation, including formal surgical evaluation
- Systemic therapy regimens: APP-D 2 of 11
- CRS and IPCT: Principles of Surgery and CRS/IPCT (APP-C)
- All recommendations are category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Initial evaluation

- Diagnostic laparoscopy
- Biomarker testing to identify actionable mutations
    - Biomarker testing is recommended, although there may be a higher rate of assay failure if specimen has low cellularity
    - See Principles of Pathology and Molecular Review (APP-B)

# Initial treatment

- Preferred: neoadjuvant systemic therapy up to 6 months (APP-D 2 of 11)
- or Consider CRS with or without IPCT in select instances
    - Select instances including low disease burden and/or well-differentiated adenocarcinoma
    - Principles of Surgery and CRS/IPCT (APP-C)

# After neoadjuvant systemic therapy: stable disease or response

- CRS with or without IPCT (APP-C)
    - Multidisciplinary discussion to determine appropriateness for CRS/IPCT
    - Then consider adjuvant therapy
        - Preferred: FOLFOX or CAPEOX
        - or Other options include capecitabine or fluorouracil/leucovorin
        - This recommendation has been extrapolated from colon cancer; duration of perioperative therapy should be limited to no more than 6 months
    - Then surveillance (APP-8)
- or Continue systemic therapy (APP-D 2 of 11)

# After neoadjuvant systemic therapy: progressive disease or inadequate response

- Second-line systemic therapy (APP-D 2 of 11)

# After second-line systemic therapy: stable disease or response

- Consider adjuvant therapy
    - Preferred: FOLFOX or CAPEOX
    - or Other options include capecitabine or fluorouracil/leucovorin
    - This recommendation has been extrapolated from colon cancer; duration of perioperative therapy should be limited to no more than 6 months
- Then surveillance (APP-8)

# After second-line systemic therapy: progressive disease or inadequate response

- Consider clinical trial
- or Continue systemic therapy (APP-D 2 of 11)
- or Best supportive care

# Next

- Stable disease or response after systemic therapy, CRS with or without IPCT then consider adjuvant therapy
- Progressive disease or inadequate response on first-line therapy, go to second-line systemic therapy (APP-D 2 of 11)
- After adjuvant therapy, go to surveillance (APP-8)
- Progressive disease or inadequate response after second-line therapy, consider clinical trial, continue systemic therapy (APP-D 2 of 11), or best supportive care
