# Additional evaluation

- Applies to both presentations, diagnosed after hysterectomy and diagnosed by biopsy or myomectomy
- Expert pathologic review, and consider molecular testing (UTSARC-A)
- Imaging (UTSARC-B)
    - For incidental finding of uterine sarcoma after TH/BSO or fragmented specimen, recommend imaging and consider additional surgical resection on an individual basis
- Consider ER/PR testing for LMS, ESS, and adenosarcoma

# Primary treatment, diagnosed after TH or supracervical hysterectomy (SCH) with or without BSO

- Tumor initially fragmented, or residual cervix
    - Consider re-exploration/re-resection
- Residual tube/ovary
    - Consider completion salpingo-oophorectomy in low-grade ESS or adenosarcoma (ER-positive tumor)

# Primary treatment, diagnosed by biopsy or myomectomy

- Disease limited to uterus
    - TH with en bloc resection, with or without BSO
        - For incidental finding of uterine sarcoma after TH/BSO or fragmented specimen, recommend imaging and consider additional surgical resection on an individual basis
        - Oophorectomy is individualized for patients of reproductive age
        - Favor BSO if ER/PR positive
        - Morcellation should be avoided
    - Additional surgical resection for intraoperative discovery of extrauterine disease is individualized
- Known or suspected extrauterine disease
    - Consider surgical resection based on
        - Symptoms
        - Extent of disease
        - Resectability
    - If surgical resection
        - TH with en bloc resection, with or without BSO
            - Oophorectomy is individualized for patients of reproductive age
            - Favor BSO if ER/PR positive
            - Morcellation should be avoided
        - and Resection of metastatic focus
    - If no surgical resection
        - Systemic therapy (UTSARC-C)
        - and/or Palliative EBRT, with or without brachytherapy (UN-A)
- Not suitable for primary surgery
    - Disease is not amenable to resection, or patient is not suitable for surgery based on comorbidities
    - Systemic therapy (UTSARC-C)
    - and/or Palliative EBRT, with or without brachytherapy (UN-A)
