# Surveillance

- Physical exam, including pelvis
    - Every 3–6 mo for 2–3 y
    - Then every 6–12 mo for up to year 5
    - Then annually
- CA-125
    - Only if initially elevated, or serous histology
- Imaging
    - As indicated based on symptoms, or on examination findings suspicious for recurrence
    - Principles of Imaging (ENDO-B)
- Clinical evaluation and management of potential long-term and late effects of treatment
    - Patient education on symptoms of potential recurrence
    - Patient education on lifestyle, obesity, and exercise
    - Patient education on sexual health
        - Vaginal dilator use
        - Lubricants and moisturizers
        - Local estrogen and hormone therapy for menopause
    - Smoking cessation
    - Nutrition counseling
    - Also see Principles of Gynecologic Survivorship (UN-B), NCCN Guidelines for Survivorship, and NCCN Guidelines for Smoking Cessation

# Locoregional recurrence

- Defined as negative for distant metastases on radiologic imaging
    - Principles of Imaging (ENDO-B)
- Therapy for Locoregional Relapse (ENDO-10)

# Isolated metastases

- Consider resection, and/or EBRT/SBRT
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy for Uterine Neoplasms (UN-A)
- or Ablative therapy
    - Consider ablation for 1–5 metastatic lesions, if the primary cancer has been controlled
- Consider systemic therapy
    - Systemic Therapy for Endometrial Carcinoma (ENDO-D)
- On further recurrence, treat as disseminated metastases

# Disseminated metastases

- Applies when not amenable to local treatment, or on further recurrence
- Systemic therapy
    - Systemic Therapy for Endometrial Carcinoma (ENDO-D)
- ± palliative EBRT
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy for Uterine Neoplasms (UN-A)
- If progression, best supportive care (NCCN Guidelines for Palliative Care)
