# Stage IA, G1, G2

- Observation preferred
- or Consider vaginal brachytherapy if lymphovascular space invasion (LVSI) and/or age ≥60 y
    - Vaginal brachytherapy is strongly suggested if two risk factors are present

# Stage IA, G3

- Vaginal brachytherapy preferred
- or Consider observation if no myoinvasion
- or Consider EBRT if either age ≥70 y or LVSI (category 2B)
    - Initiate EBRT as soon as the vaginal cuff is healed, preferably no later than 12 weeks after surgery

# Stage IB, G1

- Vaginal brachytherapy preferred
- or Consider observation if age <60 y and no LVSI

# Stage IB, G2

- Vaginal brachytherapy preferred
- or Consider EBRT if ≥60 y and/or LVSI
    - Initiate EBRT as soon as the vaginal cuff is healed, preferably no later than 12 weeks after surgery
- or Consider observation if age <60 y and no LVSI

# Stage IB, G3

- RT (EBRT and/or vaginal brachytherapy) ± systemic therapy (category 2B for systemic therapy)
    - Initiate EBRT as soon as the vaginal cuff is healed, preferably no later than 12 weeks after surgery
