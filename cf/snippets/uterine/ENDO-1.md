# Suitable for primary surgery

- Total hysterectomy and bilateral salpingo-oophorectomy (TH/BSO)
    - Principles of Pathology and Molecular Analysis (ENDO-A)
- Surgical staging
    - Minimally invasive surgery (MIS) is the preferred approach when technically feasible
    - The degree of surgical staging to assess disease status depends on preoperative and intraoperative findings
    - Multidisciplinary expertise is recommended
    - Principles of Evaluation and Surgical Staging (ENDO-C)
- Ovarian preservation may be safe in select patients, all of the following
    - Premenopausal
    - Stage IA G1 endometrioid cancer
    - Normal-appearing ovaries
    - No family history of ovarian cancer or Lynch syndrome
    - Preferred: absence of p53 abnormality, mismatch repair (MMR) deficiency, lymphovascular space invasion (LVSI), and positive cytology
    - Salpingectomy is recommended

# Not suitable for primary surgery

- Applies when disease is not amenable to resection, or patient is not suitable for surgery based on comorbidities
- External beam radiation therapy (EBRT) and/or brachytherapy (preferred)
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy for Uterine Neoplasms (UN-A)
- or Consider hormone therapy in select patients
    - Including levonorgestrel intrauterine device (IUD)
    - Systemic Therapy for Endometrial Carcinoma (ENDO-D)
