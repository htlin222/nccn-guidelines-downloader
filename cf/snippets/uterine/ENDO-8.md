# Criteria for considering fertility-sparing options, all criteria must be met

- Grade 1 endometrioid adenocarcinoma (preferred)
    - On hysteroscopy and dilation and curettage (D&C)
    - Confirmed by expert pathology review
- Disease limited to the endometrium
    - On MRI (preferred) or transvaginal ultrasound
    - Principles of Imaging (ENDO-B)
- Absence of suspicious or metastatic disease on imaging
- No contraindications to medical therapy or pregnancy
- Patient has undergone counseling that the fertility-sparing option is not standard of care for the treatment of endometrial carcinoma
- Consultation with a fertility expert
- Recommend molecular evaluation of tumor and evaluation for inherited cancer risk (UN-1)
- Ensure negative pregnancy test

# Primary treatment

- Continuous progestin-based therapy
    - Levonorgestrel IUD (preferred)
    - Megestrol
    - Medroxyprogesterone
- Consider hysteroscopic tumor evaluation and resection
- Consider dual-progestin therapy [(Megestrol or Medroxyprogesterone) + Levonorgestrel IUD]
- Weight management and lifestyle modification counseling
    - See Healthy Lifestyles (HL-1) and Nutrition and Weight Management (SNWM-1) in the NCCN Guidelines for Survivorship

# Monitoring

- Endometrial evaluation every 3–6 mo
    - Either hysteroscopy, D&C, or endometrial biopsy
- Complete response
    - Encourage conception, with continued surveillance and endometrial sampling every 6–12 mo
    - Consider maintenance progestin-based therapy if patient is not actively trying to conceive
- Endometrial cancer present at 6 mo
    - Assessed per Principles of Imaging (ENDO-B)
    - Consider dual-progestin therapy [(Megestrol or Medroxyprogesterone) + Levonorgestrel IUD] if not previously added
- Endometrial cancer present at 12 mo
    - Assessed per Principles of Imaging (ENDO-B)

# TH/BSO with staging, when indicated

- After childbearing complete, or progression of disease on endometrial sampling (ENDO-1)
- Endometrial cancer present at 12 mo, preferred by 12 months (ENDO-1)
- MIS is the preferred approach when technically feasible
    - Principles of Evaluation and Surgical Staging (ENDO-C)
- Multidisciplinary expertise is recommended
    - Principles of Evaluation and Surgical Staging (ENDO-C)
- The degree of surgical staging to assess disease status depends on intraoperative findings
- Ovarian preservation may be considered in select patients
    - Premenopausal
    - Stage IA G1 endometrioid cancer
    - Normal-appearing ovaries
    - No family history of ovarian cancer or Lynch syndrome
    - Preferred: absence of p53 abnormality, MMR deficiency, LVSI, and positive cytology
    - Salpingectomy is recommended

# Next

- Complete response, continue monitoring and encourage conception
- Endometrial cancer present at 6 mo, consider dual-progestin therapy if not previously added, continue monitoring
- Endometrial cancer present at 12 mo, TH/BSO with staging, preferred by 12 months (ENDO-1)
- Childbearing complete, or progression of disease on endometrial sampling, TH/BSO with staging (ENDO-1)
- Surveillance (ENDO-9)
