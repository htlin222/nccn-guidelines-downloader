# Therapy for relapse, no prior RT

- Surgical exploration + resection ± IORT
    - Consider preoperative EBRT (UN-A) ± systemic therapy (UTSARC-C)
        - The use of preoperative EBRT would preclude postoperative EBRT
        - For low-grade ESS or adenosarcoma without SO, the first choice of systemic therapy is anti-estrogen hormone therapy (UTSARC-C)
    - For residual disease, consider EBRT ± brachytherapy (UN-A) ± systemic therapy (UTSARC-C)
        - The use of preoperative EBRT would preclude postoperative EBRT
        - For low-grade ESS or adenosarcoma without SO, the first choice of systemic therapy is anti-estrogen hormone therapy (UTSARC-C)
- or EBRT (UN-A) ± brachytherapy (UN-A) ± systemic therapy (UTSARC-C)
    - For low-grade ESS or adenosarcoma without SO, the first choice of systemic therapy is anti-estrogen hormone therapy (UTSARC-C)

# Therapy for relapse, prior RT

- Surgical exploration + resection ± IORT ± systemic therapy (UTSARC-C)
    - For low-grade ESS or adenosarcoma without SO, the first choice of systemic therapy is anti-estrogen hormone therapy (UTSARC-C)
- or Systemic therapy (UTSARC-C)
    - For low-grade ESS or adenosarcoma without SO, the first choice of systemic therapy is anti-estrogen hormone therapy (UTSARC-C)
- or Selected reirradiation with EBRT (UN-A) and/or brachytherapy (UN-A)
