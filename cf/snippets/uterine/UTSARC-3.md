# Source

- NCCN Uterine Sarcoma v3.2026, UTSARC-3, p47
- Applies to additional therapy following TH with or without BSO, set by pathologic findings and histologic grade
- Histologies covered: high-grade ESS, UUS, LMS, other sarcomas such as PEComa
- Histologic grade per Principles of Pathology and Molecular Analysis (UTSARC-A 2 of 8)
- Systemic therapy options: Systemic Therapy (UTSARC-C)
- Radiation details: Principles of Radiation Therapy for Uterine Neoplasms (UN-A)
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Additional therapy by stage

- Stage I
    - Observe
- Stage II, III
    - Consider observation, only if completely resected with negative margins
    - or Consider systemic therapy (UTSARC-C)
    - and/or Consider EBRT (UN-A)
- Stage IVA
    - Systemic therapy (UTSARC-C)
    - and/or EBRT (UN-A)
- Stage IVB
    - Systemic therapy (UTSARC-C)
    - with or without palliative EBRT (UN-A)
