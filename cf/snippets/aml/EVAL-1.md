# History and laboratory studies

- History and physical (H&P)
- Complete blood count (CBC)
    - Platelets
    - Differential
- Comprehensive metabolic panel (CMP)
- Uric acid
- Lactate dehydrogenase (LDH)
- B12 evaluation
- Folic acid evaluation
- Prothrombin time (PT)
- Partial thromboplastin time (PTT)
- Fibrinogen

# Bone marrow and genetic studies

- Bone marrow (BM) core biopsy and aspirate analyses (AML-A)
    - Immunophenotyping by immunohistochemistry (IHC) stains
    - Immunophenotyping by flow cytometry
    - Analysis of chromosomal structural variations by cytogenetics
    - Fluorescence in situ hybridization (FISH)
    - Next generation sequencing (NGS)
- Molecular analyses for lesions that allow risk stratification as per ELN 2022 (AML-A)
- Comprehensive pathology report
    - Diagnosis of acute myeloid leukemia (AML) within the context of the diagnostic system used
    - Blast count
    - Cellularity
    - Morphologic dysplasia
    - Mutation status, if available
- Consider additional molecular and genetic testing for heritable hematologic malignancy predisposition, in a subset of patients
    - See MDS-D and MDS-E in the NCCN Guidelines for Myelodysplastic Syndromes

# Transplant planning

- Early referral to transplant center, and/or human leukocyte antigen (HLA) typing
    - For patients with potential hematopoietic cell transplantation (HCT) in the future
    - Except for patients with a major contraindication to HCT

# Imaging and CNS evaluation

- Brain CT without contrast, if central nervous system (CNS) hemorrhage suspected (AML-B)
- Brain MRI with and without contrast, if leukemic meningitis suspected (AML-B)
- Consider FDG-PET/CT in individuals with extramedullary disease
- Lumbar puncture (LP), if symptomatic (AML-B)

# Organ function and supportive care

- Evaluate myocardial function, by echocardiogram or multigated acquisition (MUGA) scan
    - In patients with a history or symptoms of cardiac disease
    - In patients with prior or planned exposure to cardiotoxic drugs
    - In patients with prior or planned radiation therapy (RT) to thorax
- Counsel patients on infertility risk, with consideration of fertility preservation as appropriate
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Adolescent and Young Adult (AYA) Oncology
- Consider early integration of palliative care
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Palliative Care
