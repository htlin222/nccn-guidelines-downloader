# Source

- NCCN Smoking Cessation v1.2026, SC-2, p13
- Applies to individuals who smoked within the last 30 days
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- Smoked or used tobacco or nicotine-containing products within the last 30 days
- Products currently used: ___ (product_type)
- Amount per day: ___ (daily_amount)
- Time to first use after waking: ___ (time_to_first_use)
- Longest period gone without smoking or nicotine use: ___ (longest_abstinence)
- Last quit attempt: ___ (last_quit_attempt)
- Tobacco or nicotine use in household and other shared spaces: ___ (household_use)
- Readiness to set a quit date in the next 30 days: ___ (quit_readiness)

# Tobacco use, assess and document in the patient health record

- How much per day do you currently smoke or use tobacco or nicotine-containing products
    - Cigarettes
    - Pipes
    - Cigars
    - Hookah
    - Cigarillos
    - Non-combustible nicotine-containing products
    - Smokeless tobacco
    - Nicotine pouches
- What is the typical amount
- How soon do you smoke or use tobacco or nicotine-containing products after you wake up (ie, within 30 minutes)
    - Time to first cigarette is used to select the strength of the nicotine gum and lozenge (2 mg, 4 mg)

# History of quit attempts, assess and document in the patient health record

- What is the longest period you have gone without smoking or using nicotine-containing products
- Which strategies were helpful
- Which were least helpful and why
    - Side effects
    - Cost
    - Continued cravings
    - Did not work
    - Inadequate behavioral support
- When was your last quit attempt
- Did you use anything to help you quit in the past, if so, what
    - Medications (eg, varenicline, bupropion, NRT)
        - Document medication name, strength, dose, and duration of use during previous quit attempts
        - If medication was discontinued early during prior quit attempt(s), longer duration of use may improve efficacy
    - Support group
    - Behavioral treatment
    - Quitlines, websites, smart phone apps, or other media
    - Non-combustible nicotine-containing products (SC-A)
    - Nicotine pouches
    - Other

# Household and other shared spaces

- Tobacco or nicotine-containing product use of individuals within household and other shared spaces
    - Smoking by other household members is an important predictor for not remaining abstinent, and exposes the patient to secondhand smoke
    - To benefit the patient, individuals in the household and other shared spaces should be encouraged to abstain from smoking
    - Resources may be provided

# Advise patients to quit

- Brief advice of about 3 minutes by health care professionals is associated with a small but important increase in smoking abstinence rates
- Review risks of smoking and benefits of quitting (SC-B)
- Provide patient education resources (SC-C)
- Assess patient readiness to set a quit date in the next 30 days
    - Making an immediate quit attempt is recommended
    - Smoking reduction with pharmacotherapy and counseling may be considered, with a goal of achieving and maintaining abstinence from smoking at a future quit date (ie, 1-3 mo)
