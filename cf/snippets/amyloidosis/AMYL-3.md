# Source

- NCCN Systemic Light Chain Amyloidosis v2.2026, AMYL-3, p7
- Applies to localized light chain amyloidosis (AL), confirmed by MS
- Entered when monoclonal protein or isotypic light chain predominance is present, and/or there is clinical suspicion for localized AL
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Initial diagnostic workup

- Screening for monoclonal protein
    - SPEP and SIFE
    - UPEP and UIFE, with 24-h urine collection
    - Serum FLC assay
    - Must include immunofixation studies in both urine and serum for greatest sensitivity
    - Electrophoresis alone without immunofixation, or testing in serum alone, has low sensitivity
- ECG
- Echocardiogram
- NT-proBNP
- TnT
- Liver function test (LFT)
- Renal function
- Urine analysis
- Organ evaluation and imaging as clinically indicated (AMYL-1)

# If suspicion of other organ involvement

- Bone marrow and tissue biopsy, with congo red staining and liquid chromatography-MS (LC-MS)
    - Tissue biopsy may include fat pad
    - Immunohistochemistry or immunofluorescence can be considered if MS is not available
- Other organ biopsy as clinically indicated (AMYL-2)

# Next

- No systemic disease, long-term follow-up
    - Surgical approaches may be appropriate for symptomatic or cosmetic reasons (eg, skin lesions)
- Systemic disease confirmed, manage as systemic disease (AMYL-B)
- Monoclonal protein or isotypic light chain predominance absent, and no suspicion of other organ involvement
    - Low risk of progression, follow as clinically indicated
- Lymph node involvement
    - Long-term follow-up
    - Consider evaluation for low grade B-cell lymphoma (MZL/MALT/WM/LPL)
        - See NCCN Guidelines for B-Cell Lymphomas
        - See NCCN Guidelines for Waldenstrom Macroglobulinemia/Lymphoplasmacytic Lymphoma
