# Clinical findings workup

- CMR imaging
    - Use transthoracic echocardiogram with global longitudinal strain imaging in patients where CMR is not feasible/optimal
- Serum FLC assay
- SPEP and SIFE
- UPEP and UIFE with 24-h urine collection
    - Screening for monoclonal protein must include immunofixation studies in both urine and serum for greatest sensitivity
    - Electrophoresis alone without immunofixation, or testing in serum alone, has low sensitivity
- Echocardiogram
- ECG
- NT-proBNP, TnT
- Other investigations as clinically indicated (AMYL-1)

# CMR suspicious for cardiac amyloidosis

- Monoclonal protein or elevated FLC with abnormal ratio present
    - Bone marrow and tissue (may include fat pad) biopsy
    - Consider other organ biopsy
    - Congo red staining and LC-MS
        - Immunohistochemistry or immunofluorescence can be considered if MS is not available
    - If positive, treat according to results
    - If negative, proceed with cardiac biopsy
- No monoclonal protein or elevated FLC with abnormal ratio present
    - Cardiac scintigraphy with either pyrophosphate scintigraphy (PYP), 123I-MIBG, or 99mTC-DPD
        - Grade 0-1: tissue diagnosis needed; cardiac or non-cardiac biopsy according to presentation
        - Grade 2-3: proceed with treating as amyloid transthyretin (ATTR); biopsy not needed

# CMR not suspicious for cardiac amyloidosis

- Other systemic signs of amyloidosis present
    - Monoclonal protein or elevated FLC with abnormal ratio present
        - Bone marrow and fat pad biopsy
        - Consider other organ biopsy
            - Previous tissue biopsy within the last 1-2 years can be stained with Congo red
        - Congo red staining and LC-MS (AMYL-1)
    - No monoclonal protein or elevated FLC with abnormal ratio present
        - Look for other causes of cardiac disease
- Systemic signs of amyloidosis NOT present
    - Possible MGUS, SMM, or MM diagnosis, see NCCN Guidelines for Multiple Myeloma

# Next

- Positive biopsy, treat according to results
- Negative biopsy after CMR suspicious with monoclonal protein present, proceed with cardiac biopsy
- Cardiac scintigraphy grade 2-3, treat as ATTR without biopsy
- No monoclonal protein and CMR not suspicious, look for other causes of cardiac disease
- Systemic signs absent, evaluate for MGUS, SMM, or MM
