- Employment and Return to Work: Evaluation/Assessment, Treatment of Contributing Factors
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- ___ (diagnosis) survivor

# Evaluation of work-related concerns

- Discuss survivor's concerns, needs, goals, and desires related to work
- Assess abilities required for job
    - Cognitive tasks
    - Long periods of standing
    - Use of hands
- Assess barriers
    - Assess practical concerns regarding employment
        - Transportation
        - Caregiving responsibilities
        - Health insurance coverage
        - Financial toxicity
    - Assess job flexibility, workplace accommodations, supportive work environment, and paid sick leave

# Assess treatable contributing symptoms

- Fatigue (SFAT-1)
- Pain/neuropathy (SPAIN-1)
- Musculoskeletal/neurologic issues
    - Joint/extremity mobility
    - Deconditioning/loss of muscle mass
    - Sensory neuropathy
- Cognitive dysfunction (SCF-1)
- Anxiety, depression, distress (SANXDE-1)
- Vision/hearing changes

# Assess comorbid conditions

- Organ dysfunction
    - Organ dysfunction resulting from cancer or cancer treatment that may most impact work includes cardiac (SCARDIO-1), pulmonary, and GI
- Hematologic dysfunction/infection risk
    - The majority of solid tumor survivors do not have an increased infection risk
    - Infection risk should be assessed in post-transplant survivors
- Alcohol/substance use

# Treatment of contributing factors

- Treat contributing symptoms/comorbidities with appropriate pharmacologic interventions and/or referrals as needed
- Treat contributing symptoms
    - Fatigue (SFAT-1)
    - Pain/neuropathy (SPAIN-1)
    - Cognitive dysfunction (SCF-1)
    - Anxiety, depression, distress (SANXDE-1)
    - Musculoskeletal/neurologic issues
    - Vision/hearing
- Treat comorbidities
