- Definition of Survivorship
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- ___ (diagnosis)
- Considered a cancer survivor from diagnosis, through the balance of life
    - Adapted with permission from the National Coalition for Cancer Survivorship, as shown in the National Cancer Institute's Office of Cancer Survivorship Definitions web page
- Survivor status ___ (survivor_status)
    - Includes survivors living with cancer and those free of cancer
- Not all individuals with a history of cancer identify with the term "survivor"
    - These guidelines are meant to be inclusive and use the term "survivor" to describe anyone with a history of cancer
- Care phase ___ (care_phase)
    - Applicable to survivors across the continuum of care, including those on prolonged therapy, those with chronic cancers (eg, metastatic disease), and long-term survivors

# What these guidelines focus on

- The vast and persistent impact both the diagnosis and treatment of cancer have on the adult survivor
    - Physical and mental states
    - Health behaviors
    - Professional and personal identity
    - Sexuality
    - Financial standing
- Appropriate to counsel on these impacts early in the treatment trajectory and at regular intervals thereafter

# Survivors living with metastatic disease

- The Panel recognizes the growing population of individuals living with metastatic disease
    - Many aspects of these guidelines pertain to this population of survivors
    - This group is included in the definition of survivorship
    - These guidelines are meant to be applied when helpful to meet the individuals' needs
- Recommended to review the NCCN Guidelines for Survivorship in conjunction with the cancer-specific guidelines for individuals with metastatic disease
    - As more evidence is established for this population, more specific survivorship guidelines for individuals living with metastatic cancers may be developed
