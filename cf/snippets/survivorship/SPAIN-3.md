# Assessment

- Screen for cancer pain or cancer treatment-related pain at regular intervals
- Quantify pain intensity and characterize quality: ___ (pain_description)
    - See Pain Intensity Rating (PAIN-A) in the NCCN Guidelines for Adult Cancer Pain
- Severe uncontrolled pain is a medical emergency and should be addressed promptly
    - Rule out oncologic emergency
- Comprehensive pain assessment (see PAIN-B in the NCCN Guidelines for Adult Cancer Pain), in order to identify:
    - Etiology: ___ (etiology)
    - Pathophysiology
    - Patient goals for comfort and function: ___ (patient_goals)
    - Specific cancer pain syndrome: ___ (pain_syndrome)

# Next

- No pain, rescreen at each subsequent visit
- Pain unrelated to cancer, refer to primary care or other specialists specializing in treatment of a particular painful condition
    - Referral to PCP for non-cancer treatment-related workup and pain management (ie, rheumatoid arthritis)
    - Consider the possibility of pain due to cancer recurrence
- Anticipated painful events and procedures, see Procedure-Related Pain and Anxiety (PAIN-J) in the NCCN Guidelines for Adult Cancer Pain
- Cancer pain syndromes, go to treatment
    - Neuropathic pain (SPAIN-4)
    - Chronic pain syndromes (amputation, neck dissection, mastectomy, thoracotomy) (SPAIN-5)
    - Myalgias, arthralgias (SPAIN-6)
    - Skeletal pain (SPAIN-7)
    - Myofascial pain (SPAIN-8)
    - GI/urinary/pelvic pain (SPAIN-9)
    - Post-radiation pain (SPAIN-10)
    - Lymphedema (SLYMPH-1)
