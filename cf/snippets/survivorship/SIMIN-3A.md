# Footnote d, immunocompromised

- Individuals who are immunocompromised have an increased risk for adverse outcomes from infectious diseases
    - Includes certain inherited immunodeficiencies (eg, chronic granulomatous disease)
    - Includes acquired immunodeficiency from disease or treatment (eg, hematopoietic malignancy, RT, immunosuppressive medications, HIV infection)

# Footnote g, contraindications

- Vaccines Contraindicated or To Use With Caution in Actively Immunocompromised Survivors and Vaccines Contraindicated or To Use With Caution in Close Contacts or Household Members of Immunocompromised Survivors (SIMIN-A)

# Footnote m, dosing

- For dosing and schedule, see General Principles of Vaccines in Cancer Survivors (SIMIN-B)

# Footnote o, timing after therapy

- Inactivated or purified antigens or bacterial components should be administered beginning
    - At least 3 months after cytotoxic chemotherapy or RT
    - At least 6 months after HCT
        - A dose of IIV can be given as early as 4 months after HCT, but a second dose should be considered in this situation

# Footnote p, special-circumstance vaccines

- These vaccines should be considered if there are unique circumstances
    - Functional or anatomic asplenia
    - Survivor's lifestyle, upcoming travel, or local epidemic or risks that merit their use
    - Consult with an infectious disease or travel medicine specialist
    - Vaccination precautions for survivors who had cellular therapy can be found on SIMIN-B

# Footnote u, meningococcus
