# Source

- Footnotes for Sexual Health Survivorship, continued from SSH-3 (cisgender male with concerns/issues regarding sexual health)
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- Total morning testosterone ___ (morning_testosterone) ng/dL, if evaluating for testosterone therapy

# Applicability to gender diverse survivors

- Sexual function and management of hormone-related symptoms are important aspects of quality of life for all cancer survivors
- The recommendations here are mainly based on data in cisgender survivors
    - Should be followed for gender diverse survivors as applicable, with the involvement of the appropriate health care specialists

# Cardiovascular risk screening

- There is less evidence in individuals assigned female at birth compared to those assigned male at birth
    - But there are reports of an association between CVD and sexual dysfunction in all individuals
- All cancer survivors with sexual dysfunction should be screened for cardiovascular risk factors (SCVD-1)

# Referral to a sexual health specialist

- Sexual health specialists may come from a range of backgrounds, including
    - Primary care
    - Gynecology
    - Urology
    - Oncology
    - Psychology
    - Rehabilitation medicine
- Whenever possible, refer survivor to an appropriate local resource for further sexual health evaluation

# Referral to a sex therapist

- Referral to a sex therapist certified by the AASECT (https://www.aasect.org)

# PDE5 inhibitor dosing

- Dosing should be titrated to optimal effect

# Testosterone therapy criteria

- If total morning testosterone ___ (morning_testosterone) <300 ng/dL
    - Repeat second morning total testosterone and free testosterone, LH, and prolactin
    - Then testosterone therapy may be indicated
- Should be used with caution in individuals with breast cancer
- Contraindicated in metastatic or active prostate cancer
- Should not be prescribed to those who are currently trying to conceive
- Long-term impact of exogenous testosterone on spermatogenesis should be discussed with patients who are interested in future fertility
