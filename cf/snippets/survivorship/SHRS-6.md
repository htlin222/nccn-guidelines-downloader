- Gynecomastia

# Vasomotor symptoms treatment

- Modification to ADT (NCCN Guidelines for Prostate Cancer)
- Pharmacologic treatments
    - Hormonal therapy in appropriate candidates, with referral to appropriate specialist for dosing and management
        - Testosterone should be used with caution in individuals with carcinoma of the breast
        - Testosterone is contraindicated in metastatic or active prostate cancer
        - Medroxyprogesterone
            - Contraindicated in individuals assigned male at birth who have breast cancer and are also receiving ADT
        - Estrogen (eg, diethylstilbestrol)
            - Contraindicated in individuals assigned male at birth who have breast cancer and are also receiving ADT
    - Non-hormonal therapies
        - Venlafaxine
        - Gabapentin
        - Non-hormonal pharmacologic treatments and dosing: SHRS-A
- Non-pharmacologic treatments
    - Acupuncture
    - Exercise/physical activity (SPA-1)
    - Lifestyle modifications (HL-1)
        - Drinking alcohol may cause hot flashes; individual responses to alcohol vary
        - If alcohol is a trigger, consider limiting intake
    - CBT
    - Weight loss if survivor has obesity or overweight (SNWM-1)
    - Herbal supplements
        - Routine use not recommended; may interfere with hormonal cancer treatments (SSUP-1)
        - Discuss use with survivor before starting
        - Dietary supplements are not evaluated by the FDA for purity, safety, or effectiveness before marketing
        - Potential harms exist, including toxicities and drug interactions (Discussion)
- If no response after 2 or 3 lines of therapy, consider referral to specialists as appropriate

# Gynecomastia treatment

- Prophylactic radiation
    - Must be delivered prior to development of breast tissues
- Tamoxifen
- Reduction mammoplasty
