# Assessment

- Screen every patient for fatigue as a vital sign, at regular intervals
    - Recommended screening question: "How would you rate your fatigue on a scale of 0-10 over the past 7 days?"
    - Severity: 0-10 scale (0 = No fatigue; 10 = Worst fatigue you can imagine), or None, mild, moderate, severe
- Fatigue score: ___ (fatigue_score)

# None to mild (0-3)

- Ongoing re-evaluation
- Education and counseling on general strategies for management of fatigue with an emphasis on continued surveillance
