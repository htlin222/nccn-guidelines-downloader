# Source

- NCCN Survivorship v3.2026, SPAIN-8, p120
- Cancer pain syndrome: myofascial pain
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- Muscle cramps or spasms present
    - Check electrolytes, calcium and magnesium levels, and hydration status: ___ (cramp_workup)

# Nonpharmacologic treatment

- Physical activity
- Range-of-motion exercises
- Strengthening exercises
- Soft tissue/myofascial release massage
- Ultrasonic stimulation
    - Type of heat treatment that can penetrate directly to the bone; use with caution
    - Not recommended for patients with multiple myeloma or bone metastases
- Acupuncture or acupressure

# Pharmacologic treatment

- Topical ointments (ketamine) and patches (ie, lidocaine, capsaicin)
- NSAIDs
- Anticonvulsant drugs
- SNRIs
- Acetaminophen
- COX-2 inhibitors

# Referral

- Consider referral to
    - Pain management services
    - Interventional specialist
    - Physical therapy
    - Physical medicine and rehabilitation
    - Palliative care
        - For services such as trigger point injections
