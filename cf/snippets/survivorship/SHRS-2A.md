# Hormonal therapies

- Relatively contraindicated in survivors of hormonally mediated cancers
- Use with caution in those with increased genetic cancer risk
- Androgens (eg, testosterone)
    - Use with caution in individuals with carcinoma of the breast
    - Contraindicated in individuals with metastatic or active prostate cancer
- Medroxyprogesterone acetate (a progestin)
    - Individuals assigned male at birth who have breast cancer may also receive ADT, and these medications are contraindicated
- Estrogen (eg, diethylstilbestrol)
    - Individuals assigned male at birth who have breast cancer may also receive ADT, and these medications are contraindicated

# Herbal supplements

- Survivors often use herbal supplements for vasomotor symptom management
- Some supplements may interfere with hormonal cancer treatments
- Routine use of supplements is not recommended (SSUP-1)
- Providers should encourage survivors to discuss such therapies prior to use
