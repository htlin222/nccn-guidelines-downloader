# Footnotes a-b, highly processed foods

- Highly (sometimes referred to as "ultra") processed foods are industrial formulations typically with 5 or more, and usually many, ingredients
    - Examples: soft drinks, sweet or savory packaged snacks, reconstituted meat products (eg, sausage, chicken nuggets), prepared frozen dishes
    - Besides salt, sugar, oils, and fats, ingredients include food substances not commonly used in culinary preparations
        - Hydrolyzed protein
        - Modified starches
        - Hydrogenated or interesterified oils
    - Additives whose purpose is to imitate sensorial qualities of unprocessed or minimally processed foods and their culinary preparations, or to disguise undesirable qualities of the final product
- Consumption of highly processed foods is associated with an increased risk of cancer

# Footnote c, alcohol

- Data on the effect of alcohol consumption on cancer recurrence is mixed
- If alcohol is consumed, lower amounts are associated with lower risk
