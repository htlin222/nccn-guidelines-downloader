- NCCN Survivorship v3.2026, SFAT-4, p103
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- Treat contributing factors
    - Medications/side effects
    - Pain (SPAIN-1)
    - Emotional distress (SANXDE-1)
        - Also see NCCN Guidelines for Distress Management
    - Anemia
        - Treat iron, B12, folate deficiency, if present
        - Consider referral/further evaluation for anemia or cytopenias
    - Sleep disturbance (SSD-1)
    - Nutritional deficit/imbalance
    - Comorbidities
