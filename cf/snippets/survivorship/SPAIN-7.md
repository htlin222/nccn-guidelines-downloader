# Vertebral compression, general measures

- Bisphosphonates or other antiresorptive medications if appropriate
- NSAIDs
- Muscle relaxants
- Consider vertebral augmentation (ie, vertebroplasty, kyphoplasty)
- Acetaminophen
- COX-2 inhibitors
- Consider referral to pain management services, interventional specialist, physical therapy, physical medicine and rehabilitation, orthopedic services, and/or palliative care

# Acute vertebral compression

- Opioids (SPAIN-2)
    - Initiating opioids in cancer survivors should be carefully considered if other interventions are unsuccessful
- Bracing (ie, thoracolumbar sacral orthosis [TLSO], Jewett brace)
- Limited bed rest
- Weight-bearing exercises when pain improves
- Physical therapy

# Chronic vertebral compression

- Weight-bearing exercises
- Physical therapy, thoracic and lumbar stabilization exercises
- Consider medial branch blocks and radiofrequency ablation for post-compression arthritic pain

# Avascular necrosis

- Physical therapy, based on weight-bearing and range-of-motion restrictions
- Opioids (SPAIN-2)
- Muscle relaxants if myofascial component
- Core decompression
- Joint replacement as clinically indicated
- Nerve ablation evaluation and bracing for patients who are not joint replacement candidates

# Osteonecrosis of the jaw

- Referral to oral surgeon
- Anti-convulsants
- SNRIs
- Opioids (SPAIN-2)
