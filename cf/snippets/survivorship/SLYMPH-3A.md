# Footnote a, PREVENT trial evidence for screening

- In the PREVENT trial, postoperative breast cancer survivors underwent up to 36 months of surveillance for lymphedema
- Those with subclinical lymphedema were treated with a compression intervention
    - After which only 16% of participants experienced progression to clinical lymphedema
- Dietrich MS, et al. Lymphat Res Biol 2023;21:304-313

# Footnote b, objective measurement tools

- Tools for objective measurements may include
    - Perometry
    - 3D camera
    - Tape measure/girth measure converted to volumetric measures
    - Bioimpedance spectroscopy

# Footnote c, certified lymphedema therapists

- Certified lymphedema therapists can be identified using resources provided by
    - Lymphology Association of North America (LANA; https://www.clt-lana.org/therapists)
    - National Lymphedema Network (https://lymphnet.org)
- NCCN recommends attention to evidence-based practice and specialized training for lymphedema management

# Footnote d, baseline measurement

- Preoperative baseline measurement is recommended
- Misdiagnosis will occur up to 50% of the time in absence of baseline measurements
- If baseline measurements are not available
    - Emphasis should be on incorporating clinical exam and symptoms alongside objective measurements
    - Use the unaffected limb as a reference

# Footnote f, compression garments

- Compression garments should be prescribed
- Optimally, they should be fitted and measured by a certified lymphedema therapist

# Footnote g, resistance training without a certified therapist

- If a certified therapist is not available, survivors with lymphedema can perform resistance training with a professional trainer who has knowledge of cancer-related physical activity principles
- Weights should be slowly progressed as tolerated
- Lymphedema should be evaluated periodically

# Footnote h, aerobic exercise

- Aerobic exercise or other forms of physical activity as tolerated
- See Principles of Physical Activity for Survivors with or At Risk for Lymphedema (SLYMPH-B)

# Footnote i, referral if no certified therapist

- If a certified lymphedema therapist is not available, consider referral to appropriate provider for treatment

# Footnote j, trained personnel

- Trained personnel can include
    - Physical and occupational therapists
    - Certified exercise professionals
    - Physical medicine and rehabilitation clinicians
- Specialized training in working with survivors is available for both physical therapists and exercise professionals
    - ACSM (https://acsm.org/certification/professional-resources/profinder/)
    - APTA Oncology section (https://oncologypt.org)
