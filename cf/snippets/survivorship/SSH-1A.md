# Validated tools to assess sexual concerns in cancer survivors

- Brief Symptom Checklist (Brief Sexual Symptom Checklist for Women [SSH-A])
- Sexual Health Inventory for Men (SHIM) (SSH-B)
- Arizona Sexual Experience Scale
- Female Sexual Functioning Index (FSFI), including a breast-specific adaptation of the FSFI
    - Bartula I, et al. Breast Cancer Res Treat 2015;152:477-488
- PROMIS Sexual Function and Satisfaction Measure (SexFS)

# Referral

- Sexual health specialists may come from a range of backgrounds
    - Primary care
    - Gynecology
    - Urology
    - Oncology
    - Psychology
    - Rehabilitation medicine
- Whenever possible, refer survivor to an appropriate local resource for further sexual health evaluation
- Referral to a sex therapist certified by the American Association of Sexuality Educators, Counselors and Therapists (AASECT)
