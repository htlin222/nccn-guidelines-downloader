# A — Awareness and assessment

- Awareness of risks and presentation of heart disease
- Assessment of CVD and cardiovascular risk
    - Consider use of a CVD risk assessment tool
        - ASCVD Risk Estimator Plus (American College of Cardiology)
        - PREVENT Risk Calculator (American Heart Association)
- Aspirin use as appropriate
    - Indicated for secondary prevention
    - Clinician-survivor discussion required for primary prevention with careful weighing of benefits and risks

# B — Blood pressure

- Blood pressure monitoring/management
    - Clinician-survivor discussion regarding the use of hypertension treatment and blood pressure goals

# C — Cholesterol and cessation

- Cholesterol assessment/management
    - Clinician-survivor discussion regarding the use of statin therapy for primary prevention and lipid profile goals
- Cigarette/tobacco cessation (NCCN Guidelines for Smoking Cessation)

# D — Diet, dose, and diabetes

- Diet and weight management (SNWM-1)
- Dose (cumulative) of anthracyclines and/or radiation to heart
- Diabetes mellitus prevention/treatment

# E — Exercise and echocardiogram

- Exercise (SPA-1)
- Echocardiogram (ECHO) and/or electrocardiogram (ECG) based on individual risk
    - Consider use of a CVD risk assessment tool
