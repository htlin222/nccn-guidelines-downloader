# Source

- NCCN Survivorship v3.2026, SNWM-3, p57
- Nutrition and Weight Management: Assessment and Interventions
- Coordination with PCPs and other involved providers is recommended
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- Evaluate weight status based on BMI criteria
    - BMI ___ (bmi) kg/m2
    - Underweight: BMI <18.5 kg/m2
    - Healthy weight: BMI 18.5-24.9 kg/m2
    - Overweight: BMI 25-29.9 kg/m2
    - Obese: BMI ≥30 kg/m2
- Evaluate involuntary weight change
    - Weight change ___ (weight_change_pct) % within 3 months
    - Consider workup for disease recurrence in the setting of cachexia or significant involuntary weight loss/gain >5% within 3 months

# Clinical evaluation

- Assess current dietary and physical activity habits and ask about
    - Daily food intake and eating habits
    - Physical activity habits
    - Willingness to address weight (if necessary) and past strategies used to change
        - For additional resources, see ASCO information on Obesity and Cancer
    - Barriers to nutrition and weight management
        - Access to healthful, nutrient-dense foods
        - Financial and socioeconomic issues
        - Time
        - Appetite and changes in eating patterns

# Interventions

- Assess treatment effects and medical issues
    - Effects of treatment
        - Gastrointestinal (GI) dysmotility
        - Swallowing issues/dysphagia
        - Dysgeusia/change in taste
        - Oropharyngeal anatomic changes
        - Bowel dysfunction
        - Digestive enzyme insufficiency
        - GI tract reconstruction/anastomoses
    - Comorbidities
        - CVD
        - Diabetes
        - Renal disease
        - Liver disease
        - Mood disorders (eg, anxiety and depression)
        - Thyroid dysfunction
        - GI disease
    - Medication use
    - Dental health
    - Supplement use
    - Psychosocial distress and fear of recurrence
- Discuss General Principles of Nutrition (SNWM-1)
- Discuss General Principles of Weight Management (SNWM-2)
- Discuss General Principles of Physical Activity (SPA-1)
