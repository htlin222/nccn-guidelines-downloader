# Recommended for all cancer survivors

- Inactivated influenza vaccine (IIV) or recombinant influenza vaccine (RIV), recommended annually (SIMIN-C)
- Tetanus, diphtheria, pertussis vaccine
- Recombinant zoster vaccine (RZV) in all survivors ≥50 years
- HPV vaccine if previously unvaccinated
    - Recommended for adults through age 26 years
    - Shared decision-making for ages 27-45 years
    - Vaccination not recommended for persons >45 years
- Pneumococcal vaccine in all survivors ≥50 years (SIMIN-B)
- Hepatitis B (HepB) vaccine for all adults <60 years
- COVID-19 vaccines
    - Recommendations regarding COVID-19 vaccines are continually changing
    - For management of concurrent COVID-19 and cancer, see NCCN Guidelines for Prevention and Treatment of Cancer-Related Infections
- Respiratory syncytial virus (RSV) vaccine in all survivors ≥75 years

# Recommended if some special circumstance or risk factor is present

- Consider if unique circumstances such as functional or anatomic asplenia, survivor's lifestyle, upcoming travel, or local epidemic or risks that merit their use
    - Consult with an infectious disease or travel medicine specialist
    - Vaccination precautions for survivors who had cellular therapy (SIMIN-B)
- Hepatitis A (HepA) vaccine
- HepB vaccine indicated for adults ≥60 years with certain risk factors
- Haemophilus influenzae type b (Hib) vaccine
- Meningococcus vaccine, recommended in patients at high risk or those with functional or anatomic asplenia
- Pneumococcal vaccine in immunocompromised survivors ≥19 years
    - Immunocompromised includes certain inherited immunodeficiencies (eg, chronic granulomatous disease) and acquired immunodeficiency from disease or treatment (eg, hematopoietic malignancy, RT, immunosuppressive medications, HIV infection)
- Consider RZV vaccine in immunocompromised survivors ≥19 years
- Other vaccines as indicated for anticipated travel or specific exposure risk
    - Consider referral to travel medicine
- RSV vaccine in immunocompromised survivors ≥50 years
