- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- Focal neurologic deficits
- High risk or known metastatic disease/brain primary
- Onset, temporality ___ (onset_temporality)
- Age ___ (age) (a risk factor for developing cognitive deficiency)
- Trajectory over time ___ (trajectory)
- Cancer treatment history ___ (treatment_history)
- Prescription medications/OTC medications and supplements ___ (medications)
- Education attainment ___ (education)
- Caregiver assessment of cognitive function ___ (caregiver_assessment)
- Nature of impairments per patient ___ (impairment_description)
    - Difficulty paying attention
    - Difficulty multitasking
    - Frequently leaves tasks incomplete
    - Difficulty finding words
    - Difficulty remembering things
    - Needs more prompts, like notes or reminders, than before
    - Takes longer to think through problems, or thinking seems slower
    - Impact on functional performance
    - Impact on job performance
- Assessment of medical history that may impact cognitive function

# Contributing factors

- Medications/side effects
- Emotional distress
    - Depression/anxiety (SANXDE-1 and NCCN Guidelines for Distress Management)
- Symptom burden
    - Pain (SPAIN-1)
    - Fatigue (SFAT-1)
    - Sleep disturbance (SSD-1)
- Comorbidities
- Use of alcohol and other agents that alter cognition
- New-onset vitamin deficiencies and endocrinopathies (eg, thyroid-stimulating hormone [TSH], B1, B12, D)

# Specialized evaluation

- Neuroimaging
