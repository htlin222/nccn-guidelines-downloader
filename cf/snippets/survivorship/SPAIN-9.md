# For GI pain (abdominal pain/cramping)

- Adequate hydration
- Consider referral to gastroenterologist
- Bowel regimen

# For chronic pelvic pain

- Consider referral to specialist in pelvic floor pain such as urologist, gynecologist, or physical medicine and rehabilitation
- Consider physical therapy for pelvic floor exercises
- Adequate hydration
- Bowel regimen
- Dorsal column stimulation for chronic cystitis and chronic pelvic pain
- Multidisciplinary treatment for chronic pelvic pain is preferred if available

# For dyspareunia

- See SSH-2
- Consider referral to gynecologist or sexual health specialist

# For refractory GI/urinary/pelvic pain

- Consider referral to pain management services, interventional specialist, physical therapy, physical medicine and rehabilitation, and/or palliative care
