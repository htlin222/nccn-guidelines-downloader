# Exercise warm-up

- All exercise should be preceded by a light-intensity aerobic warm-up

# Exercise intensity definitions

- Light physical activity: no noticeable change in breathing pattern
- Moderate exercise: can talk, but not sing
- Vigorous exercise: can say a few words without stopping to catch a breath
    - See Examples of Physical Activity (SPA-B)

# Evidence for structured exercise in colon cancer

- In patients receiving adjuvant therapy for colon cancer, health education and a structured exercise program resulted in longer disease-free and overall survival compared with health education alone
    - Structured exercise program included behavior-support sessions and supervised physical activity sessions aimed at increasing cardiovascular exercise
