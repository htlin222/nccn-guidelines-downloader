# Nonpharmacologic treatment

- Physical activity (category 1 for AI-induced arthralgia)
- Heat (ie, paraffin wax, hot pack)
- Cold pack
- Aquatic therapy
- Ultrasonic stimulation
    - Type of heat treatment that can penetrate directly to the bone, use with caution
    - Not recommended for patients with multiple myeloma or bone metastases
- Massage
- Acupuncture (category 1 for AI-induced arthralgia)
- Yoga

# Pharmacologic treatment

- Consider switching to an alternative AI or tamoxifen for AI-induced arthralgia
- SNRIs (category 1 for AI-induced arthralgia)
- TCAs
- Anticonvulsant drugs (ie, gabapentin, pregabalin)
- Acetaminophen
- COX-2 inhibitors
- Nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs)
- Muscle relaxants

# Referral

- Consider referral to pain management services, interventional specialist, physical therapy, physical medicine and rehabilitation, orthopedic services, and/or palliative care
