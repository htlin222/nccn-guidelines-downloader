# Source

- NCCN Survivorship v3.2026, SANXDE-2, p80
- Screening questions to be asked at regular intervals, especially when there is a change in clinical status or treatment, or patient presents with multiple somatic symptoms
    - If the NCCN Distress Thermometer is used as a primary screening tool, these questions would follow for those survivors with an elevated level of distress
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- In the past 2 weeks, on more days than not, has the patient

# Nervous/anxious

- Had worries or fears related to your cancer?
- Felt nervous, or worried about other things?
- Had trouble controlling your worry?

# Sad/depressed

- Had less interest or enjoyment in activities than usual?
- Felt sad or depressed?

# Trauma screening

- Had nightmares or thoughts about your cancer, your treatment, or other effects of treatment when you did not want to?
- Tried hard not to think about events or effects related to your cancer, or went out of your way to avoid situations that reminded you of those events?

# If "Yes" to any of the above, additional screening for impact of mood on quality of life

- Had difficulty functioning or withdrawn from daily activities because of these feelings or problems?
- Had trouble sleeping (eg, staying asleep, falling asleep, getting too much sleep)?
    - If isolated problem with sleep in the absence of other symptoms, see (SSD-1)
- Had difficulty concentrating?
    - If isolated problem with concentration in the absence of other symptoms, see (SCF-1)
- A positive response to any of the questions should result in further assessment

# Next

- Nervous/anxious and impact on quality of life, go to Screening for anxiety (SANXDE-3) or Screening for post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) (SANXDE-4)
- Sad/depressed and impact on quality of life, or mixed depressed/anxious and impact on quality of life, go to Screening for depression (SANXDE-5)
- Trauma (one or both symptoms screened "yes") and impact on quality of life, go to Trauma Screening (SANXDE-D)
- No significant impact of mood on quality of life, go to Rescreen at next visit
