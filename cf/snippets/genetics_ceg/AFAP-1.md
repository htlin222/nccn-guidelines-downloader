# Classical FAP phenotype

- Germline APC PV
- Presence of >=100 cumulative adenomas, sufficient for clinical suspicion of FAP
    - Or fewer polyps at younger ages, especially in a family known to have FAP
    - A clinical diagnosis of classical FAP is suspected when >=100 polyps are present at a young age
    - Identification of a germline APC PV confirms the diagnosis of FAP
    - Individuals with >=100 polyps occurring at older ages (>=35 to 40 y) may be found to have AFAP instead
- Autosomal dominant inheritance
    - There is a 30% spontaneous new PV rate, so family history may be negative
    - Especially noteworthy if onset age <50 y
- Multigene panel testing (MGPT) is recommended, to differentiate APC from MAP and other adenomatous polyposis syndromes and idiopathic adenomatous polyposis (IAP)
    - CRC/polyposis gene list (HRS-A)
    - Surveillance recommendations (GENE-1)

# Possible additional findings in classical FAP

- CHRPE
- Osteomas, supernumerary teeth, odontomas
- Desmoids, epidermoid cysts
- Duodenal and other small bowel adenomas
- Gastric fundic gland polyps (FGP)

# Increased cancer risk in classical FAP

- CRC
- Medulloblastoma
- Papillary carcinoma of the thyroid, especially cribriform morular variant
- Hepatoblastoma
- Gastric cancer
- Duodenal/periampullary cancer
- Cancer Risks (FAP-A)

# Attenuated FAP (AFAP) phenotype

- Germline APC PV
- Presence of 10 to <100 cumulative adenomas, average of 30 polyps
    - There is currently no consensus on what constitutes a clinical diagnosis of AFAP
    - AFAP is considered when 10 to <100 adenomas are present, and is confirmed when an APC PV is identified
- Autosomal dominant inheritance
    - There is a 30% spontaneous new PV rate, so family history may be negative
    - Especially noteworthy if onset age <50 y
- Frequent right-sided distribution of polyps
- Adenomas and cancers at age older than classical FAP, mean age of cancer diagnosis >50 y
- Upper GI findings, thyroid and duodenal/periampullary cancer risks are similar to classical FAP
- Other extraintestinal manifestations such as CHRPE are unusual
- Desmoid tumors are associated with 3' mutations in the APC gene
- Multigene panel testing (MGPT) is recommended, to differentiate APC from MAP and other adenomatous polyposis syndromes and idiopathic adenomatous polyposis (IAP)
    - CRC/polyposis gene list (HRS-A)
    - Surveillance recommendations (GENE-1)
