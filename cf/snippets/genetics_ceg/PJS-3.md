# Breast (female), lifetime risk 32%-54%

- Mammogram and breast MRI annually, initiate ~30 y
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Genetic/Familial High-Risk Assessment: Breast, Ovarian, Pancreatic, and Prostate (BRCA-A) for further breast screening recommendations regarding mammogram and breast MRI screening
    - High-quality breast MRI limitations
        - Need for a dedicated breast coil
        - Ability to perform biopsy under MRI guidance
        - Experienced radiologists in breast MRI
        - Regional availability
    - Breast MRI is performed preferably days 7-15 of menstrual cycle for premenopausal patients
    - The appropriateness of imaging modalities and scheduling is still under study
- Clinical breast exam every 6-12 mo, initiate ~30 y

# Colon, lifetime risk 39%

- High-quality colonoscopy every 2-3 y, initiate ~18 y
    - Shorter intervals may be indicated based on polyp size, number, and pathology

# Stomach, lifetime risk 29%

- Upper endoscopy every 2-3 y, initiate ~18 y
    - Shorter intervals may be indicated based on polyp size, number, and pathology

# Small intestine, lifetime risk 13%

- Small bowel visualization (VCE or CT/MR enterography) every 2-3 y, initiate ~18 y
    - Shorter intervals may be indicated based on polyp size, number, and pathology

# Pancreas, lifetime risk 11%-36%

- Annual imaging of the pancreas with either EUS or MRI/MRCP, initiate ~30-35 y
    - Both ideally performed at center of expertise
    - Based on clinical judgment, early initiation age may be considered, such as 10 y younger than the earliest age of onset in the family
    - Also see NCCN Guidelines for Genetic/Familial High-Risk Assessment: Breast, Ovarian, Pancreatic, and Prostate

# Cervix, lifetime risk at least 10%

- Typically minimal deviation adenocarcinoma, formerly known as cervical adenoma malignum
- Annual pelvic examination and Pap smear, initiate ~18-20 y
- Consider total hysterectomy (including uterus and cervix) once completed with childbearing

# Uterus, lifetime risk 9%-10%

- Annual pelvic examination with endometrial biopsy if abnormal bleeding, initiate ~18-20 y
- The risk of uterine cancer with STK11 may encompass endocervical adenocarcinomas as well as minimal deviation adenocarcinoma of the cervix

# Ovary (SCTAT), lifetime risk at least 20%

- Annual pelvic examination with annual pelvic ultrasound, initiate ~18-20 y

# Lung, lifetime risk 7%-17%

- Provide education about symptoms and smoking cessation
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Smoking Cessation
- No other specific recommendations have been made

# Testes (Sertoli cell tumors), lifetime risk 9%

- Annual testicular exam and observation for feminizing changes
