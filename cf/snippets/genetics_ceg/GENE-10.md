# MLH3 biallelic - colon cancer and colorectal phenotype

- Estimated absolute risk: insufficient data to define
- Management
    - Begin high-quality colonoscopy at age 25 to 30 y
    - Repeat every 2 to 3 y if negative
    - If polyps are found, colonoscopy every 1 to 2 y
        - Consideration of surgery if the polyp burden becomes unmanageable
    - Surgical evaluation if appropriate
- Strength of evidence: limited
- Colorectal phenotype
    - 30 to more than 100 polyps
    - Adenomas

# MLH3 biallelic - endometrial cancer

- No evidence of increased risk

# MLH3 biallelic - gastric cancer

- No evidence of increased risk

# MLH3 biallelic - other cancers and other risks

- Unknown or insufficient evidence
- Breast and brain tumors were noted in the 5 families reported
- MLH3 heterozygote cancer risks are unclear
