# Tylosis with esophageal cancer (TOC)

- Gene RHBDF2, autosomal dominant
- Estimated average age of presentation 45 years
- Cumulative risk for diagnosis through age 80 y: >95% lifetime
- Esophageal screening
    - Upper GI endoscopy every 1-2 y
    - Starting at age 20-30 y
    - Random biopsies from upper esophagus
    - Random biopsies from mid esophagus
    - Random biopsies from lower esophagus
    - Targeted biopsies of any abnormal appearing mucosa
- Other clinical features to look for
    - Non-epidermolytic palmoplantar keratoderma appearing in infancy/early childhood
        - Presents as hyperkeratosis of palms and soles with well-demarcated borders
    - Oral leukoplakia in some patients

# Fanconi anemia (FA)

- Autosomal recessive
- Genes, most common
    - FANCA (64%)
    - FANCC (12%)
    - FANCG (8%)
- Genes, rare
    - FANCD1 (BRCA2) (2%)
    - FANCJ (BRIP1) (2%)
    - FANCN (PALB2) (0.7%)
    - FANCO (RAD51C) (0.1%)
    - FANCR (RAD51) (<0.1%)
    - FANCS (BRCA1) (0.1%)
- Estimated average age of presentation: not reported
- Cumulative risk for diagnosis through age 80 y
    - Individuals with FA have a significantly increased risk (500-700 fold) of SCC of the esophagus compared to the general population
    - Reliable, esophagus-specific lifetime risk estimates applicable across FA genotypes have not been identified
- Esophageal screening
    - Upper endoscopy annually
    - Starting at age 18-20 y, or 10 y following bone marrow transplant
    - Biopsies from upper esophagus
    - Biopsies from mid esophagus
    - Biopsies from lower esophagus
    - Targeted biopsies of any abnormal appearing mucosa
- Other clinical features to look for
    - Progressive bone marrow failure
    - Congenital anomalies (skeletal/radial ray, renal, cardiac, skin), including short stature, microcephaly, developmental delays, limb malformations
    - Increased chromosome fragility with DNA crosslinking agents
    - Elevated risks for other squamous cancers (head/neck, anogenital)
    - Elevated risk for acute myeloid leukemia (500-fold increased risk)
    - Elevated risk for additional solid tumors (liver, brain)
