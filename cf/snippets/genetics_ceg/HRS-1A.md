# Assessment and counseling around testing

- Obtain a comprehensive assessment for hereditary colorectal/endometrial/esophageal/gastric cancers (EVAL-B)
- Genetic counseling/patient education is highly recommended
    - When genetic testing is offered
    - After results are disclosed
- Involve someone with expertise and experience in cancer genetics early, for patients who potentially meet criteria for an inherited syndrome
    - Genetic counselor
    - Medical geneticist
    - Oncologist
    - Gastroenterologist
    - Surgeon
    - Oncology nurse
    - Other health professional with expertise and experience in cancer genetics
- Principles of Cancer Risk Assessment and Counseling (EVAL-A)
- NCCN Guidelines for Genetic/Familial High-Risk Assessment: Breast, Ovarian, Pancreatic, and Prostate

# A P/LP variant found on tumor somatic testing

- Somatic P/LP variants in several genes with germline implications are common
    - TP53
    - STK11
    - PTEN
    - APC
- Such a variant will rarely be indicative of a need for germline testing
    - Unless clinical/family history features suggest the possibility of a germline P/LP variant

# Testing to aid surgical decision-making and medical management

- Planning extent of colon resection
- Planning type and timing of risk-reducing surgeries
- See the relevant NCCN Treatment Guidelines for further details

# LS-related cancers

- Colorectal
- Endometrial
- Gastric
- Ovarian
- Pancreatic
- Urothelial
- Brain, usually glioblastoma
- Biliary tract
- Small intestine
- Sebaceous adenomas, sebaceous carcinomas, and keratoacanthomas as seen in Muir-Torre syndrome

# What counts as a mismatch repair deficient (dMMR) tumor

- Any tumor that is microsatellite instability-high (MSI-H) by polymerase chain reaction (PCR) or next-generation sequencing (NGS)
- Or any tumor with abnormal/dMMR protein expression on immunohistochemistry (IHC)
    - Without concurrent MLH1 promoter hypermethylation
    - Without concurrent BRAF V600E mutation

# What counts as a personal or family history of polyps

- Based on cumulative lifetime history in the proband or a single family member of
    - Adenomas
    - Hamartomas
    - Serrated polyps/lesions

# What genetic evaluation implies

- Review of the criteria specific for the condition
- May or may not include additional evaluation by a genetics expert
- May or may not include germline testing
