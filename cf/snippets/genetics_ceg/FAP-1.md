# Source

- NCCN Genetic/Familial High-Risk Assessment: Colorectal, Endometrial, and Gastric v1.2026, FAP-1, p86
- Applies to a personal history of classical FAP
- Cancer risks for FAP: FAP-A
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

- Extent and severity of polyposis: ___ (polyposis_severity)
- Rectal involvement: ___ (rectal_involvement)
- APC genetic testing result: ___ (apc_result)
    - APC genetic testing is recommended in a proband to confirm a diagnosis of FAP
    - Confirming the PV also allows PV-specific testing in other family members
- Location of the PV in the APC gene: ___ (apc_pv_location)
    - Knowing the location can be helpful for predicting severity of polyposis, rectal involvement, and desmoid tumors
- Family history of early cancer or severe genotype: ___ (family_history)

# Treatment

- Proctocolectomy or colectomy
    - Surgical options for treating the colon and rectum in patients with FAP: FAP-F
    - Timing of proctocolectomy in patients <18 y of age is not established, since colon cancer is rare before age 18
    - In patients <18 y without severe polyposis and without family history of early cancer or severe genotype, the timing of proctocolectomy can be individualized
        - An annual colonoscopy is recommended until time of surgery
