# Source

- NCCN Testicular Cancer v2.2026, SEM-6, p13
- Applies to pure seminoma with recurrence after prior first-line chemotherapy
- It is preferred that patients with recurrent seminoma be treated at centers with expertise in the management of this disease

# Second-line therapy

- Clinical trial
- Chemotherapy (TEST-F)
    - Conventional-dose second-line chemotherapy
        - VeIP for 4 cycles
            - VeIP = Vinblastine/Ifosfamide/Cisplatin
        - TIP for 4 cycles
            - TIP = Paclitaxel/Ifosfamide/Cisplatin
    - High-dose chemotherapy
- Includes best supportive care and palliative care, see NCCN Guidelines for Palliative Care

# Fertility

- Recommend sperm banking if clinically indicated
    - Patients should wait at least 6 months post-chemotherapy to conceive a child, or up to 2 years under extreme caution
    - While more data are needed in this space, the Panel believes these recommendations are currently well supported

# Response assessment after treatment

- CT with contrast, or MRI with and without contrast, of C/A/P and any other sites of disease is recommended
- FDG-PET/CT from skull base to mid thigh may also be considered
    - In assessing treatment response and residual masses following chemotherapy in patients with seminoma
