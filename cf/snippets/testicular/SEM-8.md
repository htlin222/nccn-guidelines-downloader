# Source

- NCCN Testicular Cancer, Pure Seminoma, v2.2026, SEM-8, p15
- Applies to recurrent pure seminoma after prior second-line chemotherapy
- It is preferred that patients with recurrent seminoma be treated at centers with expertise in the management of this disease
- Third-line therapy includes best supportive care and palliative care
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Palliative Care
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Response assessment after treatment

- CT with contrast of C/A/P and any other sites of disease, or
- MRI with and without contrast of C/A/P and any other sites of disease
- FDG-PET/CT from skull base to mid thigh may also be considered
    - For assessing treatment response and residual masses following chemotherapy in patients with seminoma

# Third-line therapy after prior first- and second-line conventional-dose chemotherapy

- Clinical trial, or
- High-dose chemotherapy (preferred), or
    - Third-Line Systemic Therapy Regimens for Metastatic Germ Cell Tumors (TEST-G)
- Conventional-dose third-line chemotherapy, or
    - Third-Line Systemic Therapy Regimens for Metastatic Germ Cell Tumors (TEST-G)
- Molecular testing for biomarker-driven therapy if progression, or
    - Should include both NGS and IHC testing
    - Molecular testing is to assess for sensitivity to targeted therapies (TEST-G)
    - Pan-cancer, tumor-agnostic treatments can be considered for patients with actionable mutations
- RT in select patients, or
    - Principles of Radiotherapy for Pure Testicular Seminoma (TEST-C)
- Consider surgical treatment if resectable solitary site
