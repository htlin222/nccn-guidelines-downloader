- Applies to pure seminoma, clinical stage IIC and stage III
- Primary treatment, split by risk classification for advanced disease (TEST-D)
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

- Beta-hCG ___ (bhcg) IU/L
    - If serum beta-hCG >1000 IU/L, consider the possibility of a nonseminoma
        - Re-review the surgical specimen with pathology
        - Consider discussion with a high-volume center
    - Beta-hCG alone should not be used to stage or risk stratify patients with pure seminoma
- LDH ___ (ldh)
    - Use of LDH to risk stratify metastatic seminoma is controversial
- Estimated GFR ___ (gfr)
    - Relevant to bleomycin eligibility

# Risk classification

- Risk Classification for Advanced Disease (TEST-D)
- Good risk
    - All stage IIC and stage III seminomas, except stage III with non-pulmonary visceral metastases
- Intermediate risk
    - Stage III with non-pulmonary visceral metastases (eg, bone, liver, brain)
    - Based on metastases to organs other than the lungs (stage IIIC)
- Stage IIIB does not apply to pure seminomas

# Before starting treatment

- Discuss sperm banking prior to chemotherapy or radiation treatment

# Primary treatment

- First-line chemotherapy regimens for germ cell tumors (TEST-E)
- Good risk
    - BEP for 3 cycles (category 1), or
    - EP for 4 cycles (category 1)
    - Consider intensification of therapy for patients with good risk and an LDH >2.5 times the upper limit of normal (ULN)
- Intermediate risk
    - BEP for 4 cycles (category 1, preferred), or
    - VIP for 4 cycles
- Consider a bleomycin-free regimen in patients at increased risk for bleomycin toxicity
    - Reduced GFR
    - Older age
- BEP = Bleomycin/Etoposide/Cisplatin
- EP = Etoposide/Cisplatin
- VIP = Etoposide/Ifosfamide/Cisplatin
