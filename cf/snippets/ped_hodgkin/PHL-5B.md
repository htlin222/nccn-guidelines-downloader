# Systemic therapy

- Regimens are based off of studies with pediatric data
- Give per Principles of Systemic Therapy (PHL-E)
- CPIs should be used with caution in those with
    - Active or previously documented autoimmune disease
    - And/or current use of immunosuppressive agents
- Nivolumab is dosed per kg in patients <18 years of age
    - See Principles of Systemic Therapy (PHL-E)
- BrECADD regimen
    - Brentuximab vedotin
    - Etoposide
    - Cyclophosphamide
    - Doxorubicin
    - Dacarbazine
    - Dexamethasone
- One footnoted regimen on this page has only been studied in patients 12 years of age and older with stage III-IV disease

# Radiation therapy

- Give per Principles of Radiation Therapy (PHL-F)
- ISRT can safely replace IFRT (PHL-F)
- Decide on radiation per Principles of Criteria for Response-Adapted Radiation Therapy (PHL-A)

# Imaging for response assessment

- FDG-PET/CT or FDG-PET/MRI
- Contrast-enhanced diagnostic CT or MRI of original sites of disease
    - Only if those sites are not included with the FDG-PET
    - And only if the treatment approach includes need for assessment of anatomic sites of disease for evaluation of response
- See Principles of Imaging (PHL-C)
- After a regimen that includes a CPI
    - False-positive findings may be observed on FDG-PET/CT or FDG-PET/MRI, given the potential for delayed response or pseudo-progression
    - Clinical judgment is often necessary in such settings
    - Repeat imaging at short intervals, per LYRIC recommendations, may be considered prior to obtaining a biopsy
    - Cheson BD, et al. Blood 2016;128:2489-2496

# Supportive care

- PJP prophylaxis is recommended for all Bv-containing regimens
- For growth factor recommendations, see Select Principles of Supportive Care (PHL-G)
