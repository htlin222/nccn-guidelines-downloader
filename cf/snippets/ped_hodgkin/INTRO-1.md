# Scope of these guidelines

- The panel considers pediatric to include any patient aged ≤18 years
- The panel definition may be applicable to AYA patients aged ≤39 years
- These guidelines are therefore intended to include AYA patients
- These guidelines may apply to patients treated in adult oncology settings
- For general oncologic care of AYA patients, see the NCCN Guidelines for Adolescent and Young Adult (AYA) Oncology

# Trials and consultation
