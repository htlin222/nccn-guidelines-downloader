# Biopsy and pathology

- Core needle biopsy may be adequate if it is diagnostic
- Fine-needle aspiration (FNA) is not an appropriate diagnostic test
- See Principles of Pathology (PHL-B)
    - For typical immunophenotype, see Principles of Pathology (PHL-B)

# Screening for autoimmune disorders

- CPIs should be used with caution in those with active or previously documented autoimmune disease
- CPIs should be used with caution in those with current use of immunosuppressive agents

# Imaging and staging

- Diagnostic imaging should be done prior to initiating chemotherapy
    - Done to allow for staging and risk stratification
- Consultation with radiation oncologist is strongly recommended
    - When considering treatment options
    - When considering adequacy of imaging for potential future radiation therapy (RT)
- See Principles of Imaging (PHL-C)
- See Principles of Staging (PHL-D)

# FDG-PET interpretation

- FDG-PET positive at sites inconsistent with the usual presentation of Hodgkin lymphoma (HL)
    - Additional clinical evaluation may be required for staging
- Unusual disease presentation, ie, HIV
    - Additional clinical evaluation may be required for staging
- See Principles of Staging (PHL-D)
- FDG-PET negative for anatomic lesions of concern
    - Biopsy should be considered

# Fertility

- Fertility preservation is an option for some patients
- Refer to fertility clinic for further discussion when able, prior to initiation of chemotherapy
- Close A, et al. Pediatr Blood Cancer 2023;70:e30407

# Pulmonary function before bleomycin

- In general, FEV1/FVC >60% by PFT is acceptable for use of bleomycin
    - FEV1 is forced expiratory volume in the first second
    - FVC is forced vital capacity
    - Unless due to large mediastinal mass from HL
- For children who are unable to cooperate for PFTs, the criteria are:
    - No evidence of dyspnea at rest
    - No exercise intolerance
    - A pulse oximetry reading of >92% on room air

# Bone marrow involvement

- Homogeneous pattern of marrow uptake on FDG-PET/CT
    - Thought to be secondary to cytokine release
    - In most instances bone marrow involvement is not assumed
- Multifocal (≥3) skeletal FDG-PET lesions without cortical destruction on CT
    - Marrow involvement may be assumed
    - A bone marrow biopsy is not needed to confirm involvement
    - Purz S, et al. J Clin Oncol 2011;29:3523-3528

# Psychosocial and social determinants of health

- Refer to the NCCN Distress Thermometer and Problem List, which includes social determinants of health
- See NCCN Guidelines for Distress Management (DIS-A)

# Classification

- Classic Hodgkin lymphoma (CHL) includes:
    - Nodular sclerosis
    - Mixed cellularity
    - Lymphocyte-depleted subtypes
    - Lymphocyte-rich subtypes
- If grey-zone, see NCCN Guidelines for B-Cell Lymphomas
- Nodular lymphocyte-predominant Hodgkin lymphoma (NLPHL)
    - Per WHO 2022, NLPHL remains under the family of HL
    - In the ICC 2022 update, the term NLPHL was replaced with new terminology, nodular lymphocyte-predominant B-cell lymphoma (NLPBL)
    - Alaggio R, et al. Leukemia 2022;36:1720-1748; Campo E, et al. Blood 2022;140:1229-1253
    - Recommendations for management of NLPHL remain in the pediatric HL guidelines, as treatment regimens are typically guided by HL experience in clinical practice
