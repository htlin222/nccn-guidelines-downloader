# Risk stratification is in transition

- Pediatric HL risk stratification is evolving from traditional pediatric risk group definitions to a stage-based system aligned with adult staging
- See NCCN Guidelines for Hodgkin Lymphoma (Adult)
- Be aware that historical pediatric risk classifications and contemporary stage-based approaches may overlap during this transition

# What counts as an E-lesion

- An E-lesion is a contiguous infiltration of a lymph node mass into extralymphatic structures or organs
    - For example, lung
    - For example, bone
- Pleural involvement should be considered an E-lesion
- Pericardial involvement should be considered an E-lesion
- A pleural effusion alone is not considered an E-lesion
- A pericardial effusion alone is not considered an E-lesion

# What is stage IV instead of an E-lesion

- Disease that extends beyond the lymphatic system without adjacent lymphatic involvement is considered stage IV
- Liver involvement is always considered stage IV disease
- Bone marrow involvement is always considered stage IV disease
- CNS disease is considered extra-axial

# Radiation therapy

- Involved-site RT (ISRT) can safely replace involved-field RT (IFRT)
    - See PHL-F
