# Preferred primary treatment

- Ongoing clinical trial
- or Nivolumab-AVD x 6 cycles (category 1) (S1826)
    - Checkpoint inhibitors (CPIs) should be used with caution in those with active or previously documented autoimmune disease and/or current use of immunosuppressive agents
    - This regimen has only been studied in patients >=12 years of age with stage III-IV disease
    - Nivolumab is dosed per kg in patients <18 years of age, see Principles of Systemic Therapy (PHL-E)
    - Response assessment
        - Adequate response, see Follow-up (PHL-8)
        - Inadequate response
            - Consider residual-site RT (RSRT) to sites of inadequate response
            - or see Therapy for relapsed or refractory disease (PHL-9)
            - False-positive findings may be observed on FDG-PET/CT or FDG-PET/MRI after treatment with a regimen that includes a CPI, given their potential for delayed response or pseudo-progression
            - Clinical judgment is often necessary in such settings
            - Repeat imaging at short intervals (per LYRIC recommendations) may be considered prior to obtaining a biopsy, see Principles of Imaging (PHL-C)

# Other recommended primary treatment

- Bv-AVE-PC x 2 cycles (category 1) (AHOD1331)
    - Not for stage IIIA
    - PJP prophylaxis is recommended for all Bv-containing regimens
    - Response assessment
        - Adequate response, rapidly responding lesion (RRL)
            - Bv-AVE-PC x 3 cycles
            - ISRT to sites of large mediastinal adenopathy (LMA), see Principles of Radiation Therapy (PHL-F)
        - Inadequate response, slow responding lesion (SRL)
            - Bv-AVE-PC x 3 cycles
            - ISRT to sites of LMA and SRL, and boost to sites of inadequate response at EOST
- or OEPA x 2 cycles (category 1) (EuroNet-PHL-C1)
    - Not for stage IIIA, other than stage IIIAE
    - Response assessment
        - Adequate response, COPDAC x 4 cycles
        - Inadequate response, COPDAC x 4 cycles
- or AEPA x 2 cycles (HLHR13)
    - Not for stage IIIA
    - PJP prophylaxis is recommended for all Bv-containing regimens
    - Response assessment
        - Adequate response, CAPDAC x 4 cycles
        - Inadequate response, CAPDAC x 4 cycles

# Additional treatment after COPDAC or CAPDAC

- Useful in certain circumstances, see Principles of Radiation Therapy (PHL-F)
    - ISRT to all sites, and boost to sites of inadequate response at end of chemotherapy
    - or RNRT to sites of inadequate response
- End of therapy assessment
    - See Follow-up (PHL-8)
    - or if concern for persistent disease, see Therapy for relapsed or refractory disease (PHL-9)

# Useful in certain circumstances (PHL-5A)

- Bv-AVD x 6 cycles (S1826), if unable to receive or tolerate a checkpoint inhibitor (CPI)
    - PJP prophylaxis is recommended for all Bv-containing regimens
    - This regimen has only been studied in patients >=12 years of age with stage III-IV disease
    - Response assessment
        - Adequate response, see Follow-up (PHL-8)
        - Inadequate response
            - Consider RSRT to sites of inadequate response, see Principles of Radiation Therapy (PHL-F)
            - or see Therapy for relapsed or refractory disease (PHL-9)
- or ABVE-PC x 2 cycles (category 2B) (AHOD0031)
    - Stage IIIA only
    - Response assessment
        - Adequate response, rapid early response (RER)
            - ABVE-PC x 2 cycles
            - End of therapy assessment
                - See Follow-up (PHL-8)
                - or if less than CR, ISRT to all sites; ISRT can safely replace IFRT (PHL-F)
        - Inadequate response, slow early response (SER)
            - ABVE-PC x 2 cycles
            - ISRT; ISRT can safely replace IFRT (PHL-F)
            - End of therapy assessment
                - See Follow-up (PHL-8)
                - or if concern for persistent disease, see Therapy for relapsed or refractory disease (PHL-9)
- or Consider BrECADD or additional adult regimens if aged >18 y
    - BrECADD regimen: brentuximab vedotin, etoposide, cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin, dacarbazine, and dexamethasone
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Hodgkin Lymphoma (Adult)

# What a response assessment consists of

- FDG-PET/CT or FDG-PET/MRI
- and contrast-enhanced diagnostic CT or MRI of original sites of disease, if not included with FDG-PET and the treatment approach includes need for assessment of anatomic sites of disease for evaluation of response
- See Principles of Imaging (PHL-C)
- Response criteria, see Principles of Criteria for Response-Adapted Radiation Therapy (PHL-A)
