# Source

- NCCN Gastric Cancer v3.2026, GAST-10, p21
- Applies to peritoneal only metastatic disease, including positive cytology, as the only disease
- Peritoneal carcinoma as only disease follows Chicago Consensus Working Group. Ann Surg Oncol 2020;27:1768-1773
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- ___ (age) yo
- Peritoneal only metastatic disease, no other site of disease
- Peritoneal cytology ___ (cytology)
- Extraperitoneal disease present ___ (extraperitoneal_disease)
- Systemic therapy given so far ___ (months_of_systemic_therapy) months
- Peritoneal Cancer Index (PCI) at restaging ___ (pci)
- Disease status at restaging ___ (disease_status)
- Cytoreduction predicted at multidisciplinary consultation ___ (cytoreduction_predicted)

# Workup, if not done previously

- H&P
- Staging with CT chest/abdomen/pelvis
    - With oral contrast
    - With IV contrast
- Consider diagnostic laparoscopy
- FDG-PET/CT as clinically indicated
- Pathology review
    - Principles of Pathologic Review and Biomarker Testing (GAST-B)

# Treatment

- Systemic therapy for a minimum of 3 months
    - Principles of Systemic Therapy (GAST-F 4 of 9)

# Response assessment: restaging

- CT scan
- Diagnostic laparoscopy with washings
    - Document the Peritoneal Cancer Index (PCI)
    - With or without biopsy
- Consider FDG-PET/CT
- Consider EGD

# Additional treatment: low PCI, 10 or less

- Criteria, all of the following
    - Low PCI, 10 or less
    - Stable or improved disease
    - No metastatic progression
    - No extraperitoneal disease
- Multidisciplinary consultation
    - Principles of Multidisciplinary Team Approach (GAST-E)
- Complete cytoreduction predicted
    - Clinical trial
    - or Gastrectomy with cytoreductive surgery, and intraperitoneal chemotherapy (IC)/hyperthermic intraperitoneal chemotherapy (HIPEC)
        - Principles of Surgery (GAST-C 3 of 5)
    - or Continue systemic therapy (GAST-F)
- Incomplete cytoreduction predicted
    - Clinical trial
    - or Continue systemic therapy (GAST-F)

# Additional treatment: high PCI, greater than 10

- Criteria, any of the following
    - High PCI, greater than 10
    - Progression of disease
    - Extraperitoneal metastases
- Systemic therapy (GAST-F)
- or Clinical trial
- or Best supportive care
    - Principles of Palliative Care/Best Supportive Care (GAST-J)

# Next

- Complete cytoreduction predicted, gastrectomy with cytoreductive surgery and IC/HIPEC (GAST-C 3 of 5)
- Incomplete cytoreduction predicted, or high PCI, continue systemic therapy (GAST-F)
- Best supportive care (GAST-J)
