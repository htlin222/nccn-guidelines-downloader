# Source

- NCCN Antiemesis v2.2026, PEDAE-1, p41
- Applies to pediatric parenteral anticancer agents, high and moderate emetic risk levels
- Every mg/m2 or g/m2 threshold on this page refers to mg/m2/dose or g/m2/dose, except where otherwise noted
- Frequency of emesis is the proportion of patients who experience emesis in the absence of effective antiemetic prophylaxis
- For medications not listed in the pediatric tables, refer to adult data when no pediatric-specific information is available
- Table framework is based on the clinical experience of Panel members and on published emetogenicity classifications
- All recommendations category 2A unless stated otherwise

# High emetic risk, more than 90% frequency of emesis

- Busulfan
- Carboplatin ≥175 mg/m2
- Carmustine >250 mg/m2
- Cisplatin ≥12 mg/m2
- Cyclophosphamide ≥1200 mg/m2
- Cyclophosphamide + anthracycline (eg, Doxorubicin)
- Cyclophosphamide + Etoposide
- Cytarabine ≥3000 mg/m2/day
- Cytarabine ≥150 mg/m2 + Daunorubicin
- Cytarabine ≥300 mg/m2 + Etoposide
- Cytarabine ≥90 mg/m2 + Methotrexate ≥150 mg/m2
