# High emetic risk

- High emetic risk is >90% frequency of emesis
    - Frequency of emesis is the proportion of patients who experience emesis in the absence of effective antiemetic prophylaxis
- AC combination
    - Defined as any chemotherapy regimen that contains an anthracycline and cyclophosphamide
- Busulfan
    - Should be considered highly emetogenic chemotherapy (HEC) when used in the hematopoietic cell transplant (HCT) setting
- Carboplatin AUC ≥4
- Carmustine >250 mg/m2
- Cisplatin
- Cyclophosphamide >1500 mg/m2
- Dacarbazine
- Datopotamab deruxtecan-dlnk
- Doxorubicin ≥60 mg/m2
- Epirubicin >90 mg/m2
- Fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki
- Ifosfamide ≥2 g/m2 per dose
- Mechlorethamine
- Melphalan
    - Should be considered highly emetogenic chemotherapy (HEC) when used in the hematopoietic cell transplant (HCT) setting
- Streptozocin
- Thiotepa
    - Should be considered highly emetogenic chemotherapy (HEC) when used in the hematopoietic cell transplant (HCT) setting
- Zolbetuximab-clzb
    - Risk of emesis and nausea is highest with the initial infusion
    - Risk has been noted in those patients with an intact stomach
    - Adjusting or pausing the infusion may be needed
    - Use of acid-suppressing prophylaxis may be needed
    - Based upon patient tolerability, providers may consider a de-escalation of emetic prophylaxis with subsequent infusions

# Moderate emetic risk

- Moderate emetic risk is >30%–90% frequency of emesis
    - Frequency of emesis is the proportion of patients who experience emesis in the absence of effective antiemetic prophylaxis
- Aldesleukin >12–15 million IU/m2 or 600,000 IU/kg
    - The dose of aldesleukin is administered 3 to 24 hours after lifileucel
- Bendamustine
- Carboplatin AUC <4
    - May be highly emetogenic in certain patients
- Carmustine ≤250 mg/m2
    - May be highly emetogenic in certain patients
- Clofarabine
- Cyclophosphamide ≤1500 mg/m2
    - May be highly emetogenic in certain patients
- Cytarabine >200 mg/m2
- Dactinomycin
    - May be highly emetogenic in certain patients
- Daunorubicin
    - May be highly emetogenic in certain patients
- Dinutuximab
- Doxorubicin <60 mg/m2
    - May be highly emetogenic in certain patients
- Dual-drug liposomal encapsulation of cytarabine and daunorubicin
- Epirubicin ≤90 mg/m2
    - May be highly emetogenic in certain patients
- Idarubicin
    - May be highly emetogenic in certain patients
- Ifosfamide <2 g/m2 per dose
    - May be highly emetogenic in certain patients
- Irinotecan
    - May be highly emetogenic in certain patients
- Irinotecan (liposomal)
- Lurbinectedin
- Methotrexate ≥250 mg/m2
    - May be highly emetogenic in certain patients
- Mirvetuximab soravtansine-gynx
- Naxitamab-gqgk
- Oxaliplatin
    - May be highly emetogenic in certain patients
- Romidepsin
- Sacituzumab govitecan-hziy
- Temozolomide
- Trabectedin
    - May be highly emetogenic in certain patients
