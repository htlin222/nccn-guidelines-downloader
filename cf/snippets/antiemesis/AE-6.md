# Day 1 - treatment option A

- Olanzapine 2.5-10 mg PO once daily (bedtime)
    - Data suggest a 2.5-mg dose may be efficacious, especially when used as part of a 4-drug regimen
    - Olanzapine 2.5-5 mg may be less effective than 10 mg when used as part of a 3-drug regimen
    - Consider lower doses, especially for patients who are older or who are at high risk for oversedation
- Neurokinin-1 receptor antagonist (NK1 RA), choose one
    - Aprepitant 125 mg PO once
    - Aprepitant injectable emulsion 130 mg IV once
        - Unique formulation of aprepitant, NOT interchangeable with the IV formulation of fosaprepitant
    - Fosaprepitant 150 mg IV once
    - Fosnetupitant 235 mg / Palonosetron 0.25 mg IV once, available as fixed combination product only
    - Netupitant 300 mg / Palonosetron 0.5 mg PO once, available as fixed combination product only
    - Rolapitant 180 mg PO once
        - Extended half-life, should not be administered at less than 2-week intervals
- 5-HT3 RA, choose one
    - No further 5-HT3 RA is required if netupitant/palonosetron or fosnetupitant/palonosetron fixed combination product is used
    - When used in combination with an NK1 RA, there is no preferred 5-HT3 RA (AE-A)
    - Granisetron 10 mg subcutaneous (SQ) once
        - Granisetron extended-release injection is a unique formulation using a polymer-based drug delivery system, intended for SQ administration, NOT interchangeable with the IV formulation
        - Extended half-life, should not be administered at less than 1-week intervals
    - Granisetron 2 mg PO once
    - Granisetron 0.01 mg/kg (max 1 mg) IV once
    - Granisetron 3.1 mg/24-h transdermal patch, applied 24-48 h prior to first dose of anticancer therapy
    - Ondansetron 16-24 mg PO once
    - Ondansetron 8-16 mg IV once
    - Palonosetron 0.25 mg IV once
- Dexamethasone 12 mg PO/IV once
    - Doses may be individualized; higher doses may be considered, especially when an NK1 RA is not given concomitantly
    - For patients receiving MEC or HEC, especially those with few identifiable CINV risk factors or who are intolerant to corticosteroids, lower doses given for shorter durations, limiting administration to Day 1, or even elimination of dexamethasone on Day 1 and/or subsequent days may be acceptable and may not compromise nausea or antiemesis control
    - If dexamethasone is eliminated on Day 1 or subsequent days, consider an antiemetic combination containing a 5-HT3 RA, NK1 RA, and olanzapine on Day 1, and olanzapine for delayed CINV
    - Avoid corticosteroid premedications with cellular therapies
    - Consider a dexamethasone-sparing approach with ICI therapy as well (AE-B)

# Day 1 - treatment option B

- Olanzapine 2.5-10 mg PO once daily (bedtime)
    - Consider lower doses, especially for patients who are older or who are at high risk for oversedation
- Palonosetron 0.25 mg IV once
- Dexamethasone 12 mg PO/IV once
    - Doses may be individualized; higher doses may be considered, especially when an NK1 RA is not given concomitantly
    - Avoid corticosteroid premedications with cellular therapies
    - Consider a dexamethasone-sparing approach with ICI therapy as well (AE-B)
- If not used previously, consider escalating to a 4-drug regimen (option A) if emesis occurred during a previous cycle of anticancer therapy with a 3-drug regimen
    - Olanzapine-containing regimen B or E, or NK1 RA-containing regimen C or F
    - Olanzapine-containing regimens may be useful for patients with severe nausea (AE-C)

# Days 2, 3, and 4 - treatment option A, preferred

- Olanzapine 2.5-10 mg PO daily (bedtime) on Days 2, 3, and 4
    - Consider lower doses, especially for patients who are older or who are at high risk for oversedation
- Aprepitant 80 mg PO daily on Days 2 and 3, if Aprepitant PO is used on Day 1
- With or without dexamethasone 8 mg PO/IV daily on Days 2, 3, and 4
    - Elimination of dexamethasone on subsequent days may be acceptable and may not compromise nausea or antiemesis control
    - Avoid corticosteroid premedications with cellular therapies
    - Consider a dexamethasone-sparing approach with ICI therapy as well (AE-B)
