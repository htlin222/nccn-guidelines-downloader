# Prevention is key

- Use optimal antiemetic therapy during every cycle of treatment
- Avoid strong smells that may precipitate symptoms

# Behavioral therapy

- Relaxation/systematic desensitization
    - Hypnosis
    - Relaxation exercises
        - Guided imagery
        - PMR
        - Biofeedback
        - Music therapy
- Cognitive distraction
- Yoga, if approved by physician

# Acupuncture/acupressure

- Acupuncture/acupressure

# Consider anxiolytic therapy

- Lorazepam 0.025 to 0.05 mg/kg/dose PO
    - Especially for patients where anxiety may be contributing to breakthrough CINV
    - Begin on the night before treatment
    - Then repeat the next day
    - And/or continue PO/IV every 6 hours before anticancer therapy begins
    - Max 2 mg/dose
- or Olanzapine 0.05 to 0.2 mg/kg, 2.5 to 10 mg, PO once at bedtime prior to first dose of anticancer therapy
    - Pediatric randomized controlled trials evaluating olanzapine excluded patients under 5 years of age
    - Safety has been established among pediatric patients with mental health conditions
    - Caution is warranted in younger children
