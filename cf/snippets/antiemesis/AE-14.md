# Emetic risk levels on this page

- Moderate emetic risk, 30%-90% frequency of emesis
- Low emetic risk, 10%-30% frequency of emesis
- Minimal emetic risk, <10% frequency of emesis

# Radiopharmaceutical agents listed on this page

- I-131 MIBG (Iodine-131 Metaiodobenzylguanidine)
- Lutetium Lu-177 dotatate
    - Nausea associated with therapy derives largely from the amino acid infusion accompanying treatment
    - Variation in emetogenicity is reported between various compounded and commercially available amino acid products
    - Give antiemetics 30 minutes prior to the start of the amino acid infusion
    - Glucocorticoids can induce down-regulation of subtype 2 somatostatin receptors (SST2)
    - The manufacturer recommends avoiding repeated administration of high doses of glucocorticoids during treatment
    - The Panel suggests avoiding glucocorticoids as antiemetic prophylaxis, as other agents are available options
        - 5-HT3 RAs
        - NK1 RAs
- Lutetium Lu-177 vipivotide tetraxetan
- Radium-223 dichloride
- Sodium iodide I-131
- Strontium-89
- Yttrium-90 ibritumomab tiuxetan
- Yttrium-90 microspheres
