# Prevention is key

- Use optimal antiemetic therapy during every cycle of treatment
- Avoid strong smells that may precipitate symptoms

# Behavioral therapy and acupuncture/acupressure

- Relaxation/systematic desensitization
- Hypnosis
- Relaxation exercises
    - Guided imagery
    - Progressive muscle relaxation (PMR)
    - Biofeedback
    - Music therapy
- Cognitive distraction
- Yoga, if approved by physician
- Acupuncture/acupressure

# Consider anxiolytic therapy

- Check Pharmacologic Considerations for Antiemetic Prescribing (AE-B)
- Lorazepam 0.5–1 mg PO
    - Begin on the night before treatment
    - Then repeat the next day, 1–2 hours before anticancer therapy begins
- or Olanzapine 2.5–5 mg PO
    - Once at bedtime prior to the first dose of anticancer therapy
