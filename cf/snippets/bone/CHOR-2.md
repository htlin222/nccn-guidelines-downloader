# Source

- NCCN Bone Cancer v1.2027, Chordoma, CHOR-2, p12
- Applies to chordoma at presentation, by site: sacrococcygeal, mobile spine, or skull base/clival
- Surgical management: Principles of Bone Cancer Management (BONE-A)
- RT details: Principles of Radiation Therapy (BONE-C)
- All recommendations are category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Sacrococcygeal and mobile spine

- Primary treatment, if resectable
    - Wide excision (BONE-A)
    - With or without adjuvant RT (BONE-C)
        - RT may be given preoperatively, intraoperatively, and/or postoperatively
- Primary treatment, if unresectable
    - Consider definitive RT (BONE-C)
- Adjuvant treatment
    - Consider adjuvant RT (BONE-C) for positive surgical margins
    - Consider adjuvant RT (BONE-C) for large extracompartmental tumors

# Skull base/clival

- Primary treatment, if resectable
    - Intralesional excision
        - Maximal safe excision
        - Maximal tumor removal is recommended when appropriate
    - With or without adjuvant RT (BONE-C)
        - RT may be given preoperatively, intraoperatively, and/or postoperatively
        - Intraoperative RT may not be an option for skull base/clival chordoma
- Primary treatment, if unresectable
    - Consider definitive RT (BONE-C)
- Follow-up contrast-enhanced MRI of primary site, to assess adequacy of excision
- Adjuvant treatment
    - Consider adjuvant RT (BONE-C) for positive surgical margins
    - Consider adjuvant RT (BONE-C) for large extracompartmental tumors
    - Consider re-excision (BONE-A) if necessary
