# Source

- NCCN Bone Cancer v1.2027, Chondrosarcoma, CHON-2, p8
- Applies to atypical cartilaginous tumors
    - Defined as low-grade intracompartmental appendicular tumors
    - WHO Classification of Tumours, Soft tissue and bone tumours, 2020
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

- Tumor is appendicular

# Primary treatment

- Intralesional excision, with or without surgical adjuvant
    - Wide excision optional in expendable bones
- or Observation
- There are no known standard chemotherapy options for conventional chondrosarcoma grades 1-3
    - Ivosidenib is an option for susceptible IDH1 mutations
    - Systemic Therapy Agents (BONE-B)

# Surveillance

- Physical exam
- X-rays of primary site and/or cross-sectional imaging
    - Every 6-12 mo for 2 y, then annually, as clinically indicated
    - CT with contrast
    - or MRI with and without contrast

# Workup for local recurrence

- Imaging of primary site as clinically indicated
    - X-ray
    - MRI with and without contrast
    - CT with contrast
- Chest imaging
- Bone scan (optional)
- Biopsy to confirm diagnosis

# Next

- Local recurrence, do the recurrence workup above
- If there is malignant transformation, treat as described on CHON-3
