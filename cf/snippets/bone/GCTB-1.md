# Workup

- History and physical examination
- Imaging of primary site as clinically indicated
    - x-ray
    - MRI with and without contrast
    - CT, optional
- Chest imaging
- Bone scan (optional)
- Biopsy to confirm diagnosis
    - Consider brown tumor of hyperparathyroidism as a differential diagnosis
    - Biopsy per Principles of Bone Cancer Management (BONE-A)
- If there is malignant transformation, treat as described for osteosarcoma (OSTEO-1)
