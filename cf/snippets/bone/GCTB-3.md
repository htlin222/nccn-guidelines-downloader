- Applies after primary treatment of giant cell tumor of bone (GCTB-2)
- All recommendations are category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Surveillance

- Physical exam
- Imaging of surgical site, as clinically indicated
    - X-ray
    - Contrast-enhanced CT and/or MRI
- Chest imaging
    - Every 6-12 mo for 4 y
    - Then annually thereafter, as clinically indicated

# Local recurrence, resectable

- Consider chest imaging
- Consider denosumab prior to surgery
    - Risk of local recurrence is increased when denosumab is used prior to curettage
    - Denosumab may be beneficial to define peripheral tumor extent when planning wide excision
- Then treat as on GCTB-2

# Local recurrence, resectable with unacceptable morbidity or unresectable axial lesions

- Treat as on GCTB-2

# Metastatic recurrence

- Treat as on GCTB-2

# Next

- No recurrence, continue surveillance above
- Local recurrence, go to GCTB-2
- Metastatic recurrence, go to GCTB-2
