# Workup

- All patients evaluated and treated by a multidisciplinary team with expertise in the management of chordoma
    - Multidisciplinary team, see TEAM-1
- History and physical
- Adequate cross-sectional imaging of primary site
    - eg, MRI
    - eg, CT
- Screening MRI of spinal axis
- C/A/P CT with contrast
- Consider FDG-PET/CT
    - Skull base to midthigh
- Consider bone scan, if FDG-PET/CT is negative

# Histologic subtype

- Conventional, including chondroid
- Poorly differentiated
- Dedifferentiated
