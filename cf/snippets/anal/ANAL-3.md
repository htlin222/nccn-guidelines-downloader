# Follow-up after treatment

- Evaluate in 8–12 wk with exam + DRE
- Complete remission, go to surveillance below
- Persistent disease, see ANAL-4
    - Based on the results of the ACT-II study, it may be appropriate to follow patients who have not achieved a complete clinical response with persistent anal cancer up to 6 months following completion of RT and chemotherapy, as long as there is no evidence of progressive disease during this period of follow-up
    - Persistent disease may continue to regress even at 26 weeks from the start of treatment
- Progressive disease, see ANAL-4

# Surveillance after complete remission

- DRE every 3–6 mo for 5 y
- Inguinal node palpation every 3–6 mo for 5 y
- Anoscopy every 6–12 mo x 3 y
- Chest/abdomen/pelvis (C/A/P) CT with contrast, or chest CT without contrast and abdomen/pelvis MRI with contrast, annually for 3 y (stage II–III)
- Principles of Survivorship (ANAL-D)

# Treatment of recurrence

- Local recurrence
    - Abdominoperineal resection (APR), see Principles of Surgery (ANAL-A)
        - Consider the use of immunotherapy (nivolumab, pembrolizumab, retifanlimab-dlwr, cemiplimab-rwlc, dostarlimab-gxly, tislelizumab-jsgr, toripalimab-tpzi, or penpulimab-kcqx) (category 2B) before proceeding to APR
        - Institutional experience has demonstrated that some patients receive a good response and can avoid surgery
        - Consider muscle flap reconstruction
    - and/or the inguinal node recurrence options below
- Inguinal node recurrence
    - RT if definitive radiation possible (preferred), see Principles of Radiation Therapy (ANAL-C)
    - or Carboplatin/paclitaxel + retifanlimab-dlwr, see Principles of Systemic Therapy (ANAL-B)
        - Alternate checkpoint inhibitor therapy options: cemiplimab-rwlc, dostarlimab-gxly, nivolumab, pembrolizumab, penpulimab-kcqx, tislelizumab-jsgr, or toripalimab-tpzi
        - While there are insufficient data, there is anticipated efficacy for use of these agents with carboplatin/paclitaxel due to drug class effect
    - or Groin dissection, see Principles of Surgery (ANAL-A)
- Metastatic disease, go to First-line Systemic Therapy (ANAL-B 2 of 5)
    - Palliative RT may be considered in symptomatic patients
    - Records of previous RT should be carefully reviewed and considered prior to potential reirradiation of previously irradiated fields, see Principles of Radiation Therapy (ANAL-C)

# Surveillance after treatment of local recurrence

- Inguinal node palpation every 3–6 mo for 5 y
- C/A/P CT with contrast, or chest CT without contrast and abdomen/pelvis MRI with contrast, annually for 3 y
- FDG-PET/CT is not indicated
- Principles of Survivorship (ANAL-D)

# Surveillance after treatment of inguinal node recurrence

- DRE every 3–6 mo for 5 y
- Inguinal node palpation every 3–6 mo for 5 y
- Anoscopy every 6–12 mo x 3 y
- C/A/P CT with contrast, or chest CT without contrast and abdomen/pelvis MRI with contrast, annually for 3 y
- FDG-PET/CT is not indicated
- Principles of Survivorship (ANAL-D)
