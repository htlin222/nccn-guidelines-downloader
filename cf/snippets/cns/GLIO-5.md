# Pathology

- Biomarker testing as recommended in Principles of Brain Tumor Pathology (BRAIN-E)
- Multidisciplinary discussion, or referral to a brain tumor center for consultation
    - Strongly recommended by the Panel for newly diagnosed, recurrent, or progressive glioma of any grade
    - Also strongly recommended whenever an IDH inhibitor is being considered for newly diagnosed oligodendroglioma or astrocytoma
    - Principles of Brain and Spine Tumor Management (BRAIN-D)

# Adjuvant treatment, good PS, KPS 60 or higher

- Consider clinical trial
- or RT with adjuvant temozolomide (preferred)
    - Principles of Radiation Therapy for Brain and Spinal Cord (BRAIN-C)
    - Systemic Therapy Options (GLIO-A)
- or RT with concurrent and adjuvant temozolomide
- or IDH inhibitor (category 2B)
    - Strict histopathologic criteria do not currently exist for definitively diagnosing a WHO grade 2 versus a WHO grade 3 IDH-mutated astrocytoma
    - Some Panel members felt it may be reasonable to treat newly diagnosed grade 3 disease up front with an IDH inhibitor in certain circumstances, including but not limited to when the enhancing portion of the tumor has been resected and all remaining tumor is non-enhancing, probable grade 2 tumor
    - Other Panel members disagreed, because postponing radiation and cytotoxic chemotherapy, which have been shown to improve survival, may undertreat the patient
    - Multidisciplinary discussion or referral before choosing this option (BRAIN-D)

# Adjuvant treatment, poor PS, KPS below 60

- RT, hypofractionated preferred, with or without concurrent and/or adjuvant temozolomide
- or temozolomide (category 2B)
- or IDH inhibitor (category 2B)
    - If the KPS is low because of neurologic deficits from the brain tumor, RT plus temozolomide is the preferred treatment instead
    - If the KPS is low for other reasons, such as medical comorbidities, an IDH inhibitor is reasonable, as it may be tolerated better than radiation and/or cytotoxic chemotherapy
- or palliative/best supportive care

# Follow-up

- Imaging per Principles of Brain and Spine Tumor Imaging (BRAIN-A)
- Within the first 3 months after completion of RT and concomitant temozolomide, recurrence can be indistinguishable from pseudoprogression on neuroimaging
