# Source

- NCCN Adult Glioma v2.2026, GLIO-12, p26
- High-Grade Glioma: Other
- Applies to PXA WHO grade 3
- Applies to high-grade astrocytoma with piloid features (HGAP)
- Applies to H3-mutated high-grade glioma
- The treatment recommendations on this page are general high-grade glioma options
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- Diagnosis ___ (diagnosis)
- MGMT promoter status ___ (mgmt)
- Multigene panel testing (MGPT) ___ (mgpt)

# Pathology

- Tumor MGMT status performed, to possibly expand therapeutic options
- Multigene panel testing (MGPT) performed, to possibly expand therapeutic options
- For recommended biomarker testing, see Principles of Brain Tumor Pathology (BRAIN-E)

# Adjuvant treatment if MGMT promoter methylated or indeterminate

- Consider pyrosequencing if not done, for indeterminate MGMT promoter status
- Select one option
    - Consider clinical trial
    - RT + concurrent temozolomide and adjuvant temozolomide
    - RT and adjuvant temozolomide
- RT per Principles of Radiation Therapy for Brain and Spinal Cord (BRAIN-C)

# Adjuvant treatment if MGMT promoter unmethylated

- Select one option
    - Consider clinical trial
    - RT
    - RT + concurrent temozolomide and adjuvant temozolomide (category 2B)
    - RT and adjuvant temozolomide (category 2B)
- RT per Principles of Radiation Therapy for Brain and Spinal Cord (BRAIN-C)

# Follow-up

- Imaging per Principles of Brain and Spine Tumor Imaging (BRAIN-A)
    - Within the first 3 months after completion of RT and concomitant temozolomide, diagnosis of recurrence can be indistinguishable from pseudoprogression on neuroimaging
