# Evaluation

- Hormone testing, drawn 7:00 AM to 9:00 AM
    - Follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH)
    - Luteinizing hormone (LH)
    - Adrenocorticotropic hormone (ACTH)
    - Cortisol
    - Testosterone
    - Growth hormone (GH)
    - Insulin-like growth factor 1 (IGF-1)
    - Prolactin
    - Thyroid-stimulating hormone (TSH)
    - T3/T4
- Endocrinology evaluation
- Visual field testing
- Sellar imaging
    - MRI-pituitary protocol, annually for 5 years, then every 2 years thereafter

# Functioning adenoma

- Prolactinoma
    - Systemic therapy (SELLAR-A)
    - If unable to tolerate therapy, refer for surgery or RT
        - Fractionated RT is indicated after subtotal resection; consider close monitoring after gross total resection
        - SRS preferred when feasible; fractionated RT or SRS based on geometry, field size, and distance from critical structures like optics
- All others (TSH, ACTH, GH)
    - Transsphenoidal pituitary surgery
        - Endoscopic transsphenoidal surgery preferred
    - If unable to achieve biochemical control, or if signs of tumor growth on imaging, refer for RT or consider systemic therapy (SELLAR-A)
        - Fractionated RT is indicated after subtotal resection; consider close monitoring after gross total resection
        - SRS preferred when feasible; fractionated RT or SRS based on geometry, field size, and distance from critical structures like optics

# Nonfunctioning adenoma with visual impairment from tumor

- Transsphenoidal pituitary surgery
    - Endoscopic transsphenoidal surgery preferred
- If residual tumor that cannot be addressed surgically, consider RT or serial imaging and observation
    - Fractionated RT is indicated after subtotal resection; consider close monitoring after gross total resection
    - SRS preferred when feasible; fractionated RT or SRS based on geometry, field size, and distance from critical structures like optics
- The treatment for surgically refractory or multiply recurrent adenomas is RT (stereotactic radiosurgery [SRS] preferred), or
- Systemic therapy (SELLAR-A, see refractory/multiply recurrent adenomas)

# Nonfunctioning adenoma, asymptomatic

- Monitoring
    - Annual imaging (MRI-pituitary protocol) for 5 years, then every 2 years thereafter
- For refractory or recurrent adenomas, treatment of choice is RT
- Consider surgery if follow-up imaging demonstrates significant progression, or if there is new vision loss
