- Applies to WHO grade 4 IDH1/2-mutant astrocytoma, after pathology
- Adjuvant treatment branches on performance status
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- WHO grade 4 IDH1/2-mutant astrocytoma on pathology
- Mutation ___ (idh)
- KPS ___ (kps)
- Recommended biomarker testing done, see Principles of Brain Tumor Pathology (BRAIN-E)
- Multidisciplinary discussion, or referral to a brain tumor center for consultation
    - Panel strongly recommends this for newly diagnosed, or recurrent, or progressive glioma of any grade
    - Also strongly recommended if treatment with an IDH inhibitor is being considered for newly diagnosed oligodendroglioma or astrocytoma
    - See Principles of Brain and Spine Tumor Management (BRAIN-D)

# Adjuvant treatment, good PS, KPS 60 or higher

- Consider clinical trial
- Or RT with concurrent and adjuvant temozolomide
    - RT per Principles of Radiation Therapy for Brain and Spinal Cord (BRAIN-C)
    - Temozolomide per Systemic Therapy Options (GLIO-A)
- Or RT with adjuvant temozolomide
    - RT per Principles of Radiation Therapy for Brain and Spinal Cord (BRAIN-C)
    - Temozolomide per Systemic Therapy Options (GLIO-A)
    - Treatment of grade 4 disease is extrapolated from interim analyses of data from the CATNON study; final results of CATNON are not yet available
- Or RT with concurrent and adjuvant temozolomide plus alternating electric fields
    - RT per Principles of Radiation Therapy for Brain and Spinal Cord (BRAIN-C)
    - Temozolomide per Systemic Therapy Options (GLIO-A)

# Adjuvant treatment, poor PS, KPS below 60

- RT, hypofractionated preferred, with or without concurrent and/or adjuvant temozolomide
    - RT per Principles of Radiation Therapy for Brain and Spinal Cord (BRAIN-C)
    - Temozolomide per Systemic Therapy Options (GLIO-A)
    - Treatment of grade 4 disease is extrapolated from interim analyses of data from the CATNON study; final results of CATNON are not yet available
- Or temozolomide alone (category 2B)
    - Temozolomide per Systemic Therapy Options (GLIO-A)
- Or palliative/best supportive care

# Follow-up

- Imaging per Principles of Brain and Spine Tumor Imaging (BRAIN-A)
    - Within the first 3 months after completion of RT and concomitant temozolomide, diagnosis of recurrence can be indistinguishable from pseudoprogression on neuroimaging
