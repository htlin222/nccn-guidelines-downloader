# Before starting therapy

- Consider early referral to a stem cell transplant center
    - For autologous stem cell collection
    - At the time of cycle 1
- Review Principles of Systemic Therapy (OV-D)
- Review Management of Drug Reactions (OV-E)

# Primary therapy - preferred

- VPCBAE, up to 6 cycles
    - Vinblastine
    - Cisplatin
    - Cyclophosphamide
    - Bleomycin
    - Doxorubicin
    - Etoposide

# Primary therapy - other recommended

- PAVEP, 4–6 cycles
    - Cisplatin
    - Doxorubicin
    - Etoposide
    - Cyclophosphamide

# Primary therapy - useful in certain circumstances

- BEP, 3–4 cycles
    - Bleomycin
    - Etoposide
    - Cisplatin
- Cisplatin/Etoposide, 4 cycles
- Pembrolizumab

# Recurrence therapy

- Ipilimumab + Nivolumab
- Paclitaxel + Bevacizumab
    - Based on activity in relapsed epithelial ovarian cancer
    - And on the absence of a taxane in the primary therapy regimens
