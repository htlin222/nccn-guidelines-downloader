# Surgery and histologic diagnosis

- Surgery, per Principles of Surgery (OV-B)
- Histologic diagnosis, per Principles of Pathology (OV-C)
- Classify by WHO Histologic Classification (OV-F)
- LCOC are typically diagnosed after surgery
    - For workup, see OV-1
- Individuals with LCOC may benefit from gynecologic oncology pathology confirmation
    - and/or second opinion

# Tumor biomarker testing

- Tumor biomarker testing can be considered, if not previously done
    - It may help guide treatment
    - Due to emerging therapeutics for LCOC, there is value in identifying potential pathways for rare cancers
    - It may be useful for clinical trial recruitment
- There are limited data in these cancers given their infrequency
    - It will be difficult to acquire prospective data
- Individualized treatment may be the best treatment for these rare tumors
