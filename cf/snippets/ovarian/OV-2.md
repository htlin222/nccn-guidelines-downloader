# Evaluation before neoadjuvant therapy

- Evaluation by a gynecologic oncologist (OV-B)
    - Recommended for all patients with suspected ovarian malignancies; published data demonstrate that primary assessment and debulking by a gynecologic oncologist results in a survival advantage
    - Recommended for patients being evaluated for neoadjuvant therapy prior to being considered a poor surgical candidate
    - Recommended for management of occult STICs
    - Recommended for consideration of laparoscopic evaluation to determine feasibility of debulking surgery in select patients
    - Endometrial biopsy as clinically indicated
- and Histologic confirmation, biopsy preferred (OV-C)
    - If biopsy is not feasible, cytopathology from ascites or pleural effusion combined with CA-125:CEA ratio of >25 can be used
- and/or Laparoscopic evaluation to determine feasibility of resection
- Confirmed poor surgical candidate or low likelihood of optimal cytoreduction

# Neoadjuvant therapy

- Neoadjuvant therapy (category 1)
    - See Principles of Systemic Therapy (OV-D) and Management of Drug Reactions (OV-E)
    - Evaluation by a gynecologic oncologist before starting it (OV-B)
    - Does not apply to low malignant potential (LMP) or other noninvasive cancers (LCOC-1)
    - Completion surgery after 3-4 cycles is preferred; however, surgery may be performed after 4-6 cycles based on the clinical judgment of the gynecologic oncologist
- and Genetic risk evaluation and germline and somatic biomarker testing, if not previously done
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Genetic/Familial High-Risk Assessment: Breast, Ovarian, Pancreatic, and Prostate
    - See NCCN Guidelines for Genetic/Familial High-Risk Assessment: Colorectal, Endometrial, and Gastric
    - Germline and somatic BRCA1/2 status informs maintenance therapy
    - In the absence of a BRCA1/2 mutation, HRD status may provide information on the magnitude of benefit of PARPi therapy
    - For PARPi therapy in advanced stage disease, include a measure of HR (OV-C)
    - Principles of Pathology (OV-C)

# Interval debulking surgery

- Interval debulking surgery (IDS) with completion hysterectomy/BSO and cytoreduction (OV-B, OV-C)
    - Consider hyperthermic intraperitoneal chemotherapy (HIPEC)/cisplatin (OV-B)
    - Completion surgery after 3-4 cycles is preferred; surgery may be performed after 4-6 cycles based on the clinical judgment of the gynecologic oncologist
- Adjuvant therapy after IDS (OV-D, OV-E)

# Response evaluation

- Stable disease
    - IDS with completion hysterectomy/BSO and cytoreduction, consider HIPEC, or
    - Continue current therapy, for a total of at least 6 cycles (OV-D, OV-E), or
    - Therapy for Persistent Disease or Recurrence (OV-7)
- Progressive disease
    - Therapy for Persistent Disease or Recurrence (OV-7)
