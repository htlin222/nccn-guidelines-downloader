# Source

- NCCN Epithelial Ovarian Cancer/Fallopian Tube Cancer/Primary Peritoneal Cancer v4.2026, OV-4, p10
- Applies after pathologic staging, to select primary chemotherapy or primary adjuvant therapy
- Systemic therapy principles (OV-D) and management of drug reactions (OV-E)
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- ___ (age) yo
- Histology ___ (histology)
- Grade ___ (grade)
- Pathologic stage ___ (stage)
- Serous cancer categorized as either low-grade or high-grade
    - Grade 2 serous is considered high-grade
- Consider expert pathologic review to confirm histologic diagnosis
    - See WHO Histologic Classification (OV-F)

# Primary chemotherapy / primary adjuvant therapy by stage and histology

- Less common ovarian cancer (LCOC), any stage, including grade 1 endometrioid, go to LCOC-1
    - Less common histologies are carcinosarcoma, clear cell, mucinous, low-grade serous, grade 1 endometrioid, borderline epithelial, malignant sex cord-stromal tumors, and germ cell tumors
    - Consider expert pathologic review to confirm histologic diagnosis (OV-F)
- Grade 2 endometrioid, stage IA or IB
    - Observe, or
    - Intravenous (IV) platinum-based therapy
        - See primary regimens for stage I disease (OV-D, 5 of 12)
- Stage IC, high-grade serous or grade 2 or 3 endometrioid
    - IV platinum-based therapy
        - See primary regimens for stage I disease (OV-D, 5 of 12)
- Stage II, stage III, or stage IV, grade 3 endometrioid/high-grade serous carcinoma
    - Platinum-based chemotherapy
        - See primary regimens for stage II–IV disease (OV-D, 6 of 12)
- Imaging studies as clinically indicated, see Principles of Imaging (OV-A)

# Monitoring while receiving primary chemotherapy

- Every 1–3 cycles: physical exam and consider pelvis exam
- As indicated: interim CBC and chemistry profiles
- CA-125 ___ (ca125) or other tumor markers as clinically indicated prior to each cycle of chemotherapy
- Imaging studies as clinically indicated, see Principles of Imaging (OV-A)

# Supportive care

- Provide symptom management and best supportive care
    - Refer to the Distress Thermometer and Problem List, which includes social determinants of health (DIS-A)
    - For high-grade serous histology, HRT can be considered for symptom management after assessing for individual factors
- Refer for palliative care assessment, if appropriate
- See NCCN Guidelines for Palliative Care
- See NCCN Guidelines for Survivorship
