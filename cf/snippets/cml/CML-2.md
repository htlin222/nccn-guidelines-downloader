# Low-risk score, primary treatment

- Preferred
    - First-generation (1G) TKI
        - Imatinib (category 1)
    - or Second-generation (2G) TKI, alphabetical order
        - Bosutinib (category 1)
        - Dasatinib (category 1)
        - Nilotinib (category 1)
    - or Allosteric TKI
        - Asciminib (category 1)
    - or Clinical trial

# Intermediate- or high-risk score, primary treatment

- Preferred
    - Second-generation (2G) TKI, alphabetical order
        - Bosutinib (category 1)
        - Dasatinib (category 1)
        - Nilotinib (category 1)
    - or Allosteric TKI
        - Asciminib (category 1)
- Other recommended
    - First-generation (1G) TKI
        - Imatinib
- or Clinical trial
