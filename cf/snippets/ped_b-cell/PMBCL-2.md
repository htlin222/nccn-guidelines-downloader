# Source

- NCCN Pediatric Aggressive Mature B-Cell Lymphomas v1.2026, PMBCL-2, p18
- Primary mediastinal large B-cell lymphoma (PMBCL), relapse or refractory disease
- Optimal treatment has not been established; enrollment in a clinical trial is recommended
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Assessment

- ___ (age) yo
- Relapse or refractory PMBCL
    - In the vast majority of patients, relapse occurs within 18 months of diagnosis
    - EOT PET scan can have a fair number of false positives
    - Biopsy is warranted to confirm relapse
- Biopsy obtained and relapse confirmed

# Treatment of relapse or refractory disease

- Selected treatment: ___ (treatment)
- Clinical trial (preferred)
- In the absence of a clinical trial, suggested regimens include:
    - DHAP + rituximab
        - Principles of Systemic Therapy (PBCL-B 9 of 15)
        - An FDA-approved biosimilar is an appropriate substitute for any recommended systemic biologic therapy
    - ICE + rituximab
        - Principles of Systemic Therapy (PBCL-B 9 of 15)

        - Principles of Systemic Therapy (PBCL-B 9 of 15)

    - Brentuximab vedotin + nivolumab
        - Principles of Systemic Therapy (PBCL-B 9 of 15)

# Consolidation / additional therapy, by response

- Complete response (CR)
    - Consider RT, if not previously given, for localized disease
        - Avoidance of RT is strongly preferred in pediatric patients, to reduce the risk of late complications from normal tissue damage
        - There are not enough data on the use of RT in pediatric patients with PMBCL
        - When RT is used, deliver it with advanced RT techniques to minimize dose to OAR (heart, cardiac substructures, lungs, breast, spinal cord)
        - See Principles of Radiation Therapy in the NCCN Guidelines for Pediatric Hodgkin Lymphoma
        - Normal tissue dose constraints are in Principles of Radiation Therapy in the NCCN Guidelines for Hodgkin Lymphoma
    - or Autologous HCT, if eligible, with or without RT
        - RT is often included in high-dose therapy regimens given prior to autologous HCT
        - RT could be an option for local recurrence
        - Allogeneic HCT is not considered an optimal approach
    - or Clinical trial
- Partial response (PR)
    - Consider RT, if not previously given, for localized disease
        - Avoidance of RT is strongly preferred in pediatric patients, to reduce the risk of late complications from normal tissue damage
        - There are not enough data on the use of RT in pediatric patients with PMBCL
        - When RT is used, deliver it with advanced RT techniques to minimize dose to OAR (heart, cardiac substructures, lungs, breast, spinal cord)
        - See Principles of Radiation Therapy in the NCCN Guidelines for Pediatric Hodgkin Lymphoma
        - Normal tissue dose constraints are in Principles of Radiation Therapy in the NCCN Guidelines for Hodgkin Lymphoma
    - or Clinical trial
    - or Brentuximab vedotin + nivolumab, or pembrolizumab
        - Principles of Systemic Therapy (PBCL-B 9 of 15)
    - or Anti-CD19 chimeric antigen receptor (CAR) T-cell therapy, after 2 or more prior chemoimmunotherapy regimens
        - Axicabtagene ciloleucel
        - Lisocabtagene maraleucel
        - Plan for management of cytokine release syndrome (CRS) and neurologic toxicity
- Less than partial response
    - Consider RT, if not previously given, for localized disease
        - Avoidance of RT is strongly preferred in pediatric patients, to reduce the risk of late complications from normal tissue damage
        - There are not enough data on the use of RT in pediatric patients with PMBCL
        - When RT is used, deliver it with advanced RT techniques to minimize dose to OAR (heart, cardiac substructures, lungs, breast, spinal cord)
        - See Principles of Radiation Therapy in the NCCN Guidelines for Pediatric Hodgkin Lymphoma
        - Normal tissue dose constraints are in Principles of Radiation Therapy in the NCCN Guidelines for Hodgkin Lymphoma
    - or Clinical trial
    - or Best supportive care
