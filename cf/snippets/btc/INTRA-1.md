# Source

- NCCN Intrahepatic Cholangiocarcinoma v1.2026, INTRA-1, p16
- Applies to an isolated intrahepatic mass with imaging characteristics consistent with malignancy but not consistent with hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC)
    - Principles of Surgery (INTRA-A)
    - If the presentation is an occult primary, see NCCN Guidelines for Occult Primary
- Refer to the NCCN Distress Thermometer and Problem List, which includes social determinants of health; see NCCN Guidelines for Distress Management (DIS-A)
- All recommendations category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Workup

- Multidisciplinary evaluation
- H&P
- Imaging (Principles of Imaging, BIL-A)
    - Multiphasic abdomen/pelvis CT/MRI with IV contrast (preferred), or contrast-enhanced ultrasound (CEUS)
    - Chest CT, with or without contrast
- Consider CEA
    - A baseline test; should not be done to confirm diagnosis
- Consider CA 19-9
    - A baseline test; should not be done to confirm diagnosis
- LFTs
- Surgical consultation
- Consider esophagogastroduodenoscopy and colonoscopy
- Consider viral hepatitis serologies
    - ASCO guidelines for management of viral hepatitis B virus (HBV) in patients with cancer/receiving chemotherapy: Hwang JP, et al. J Clin Oncol 2020;38:3698-3715
- Consider biopsy
    - Principles of Surgery (INTRA-A)
    - Principles of Mixed HCC-CCA (INTRA-B)
    - Principles of Pathology (INTRA-C)
- Consider alpha-fetoprotein
- Consider referral to a hepatologist

# Primary treatment, resectable

- Resectable (Principles of Surgery, INTRA-A)
- Primary treatment per Principles of Mixed HCC-CCA (INTRA-B)
- Consider staging laparoscopy
    - Laparoscopy may be done in conjunction with surgery if no distant metastases are found
- Resection (Principles of Pathology, INTRA-C) and regional lymphadenectomy (Principles of Surgery, INTRA-A)
- Consider ablation
    - Principles of Arterial/Locoregional Therapy for Intrahepatic Cholangiocarcinoma (INTRA-D)
    - For small single tumors <3 cm

# Primary treatment, unresectable

- Biopsy, if not previously performed
    - Principles of Mixed HCC-CCA (INTRA-B)
    - Principles of Pathology (INTRA-C)
- Biomarker testing (Principles of Biomarker Testing, BIL-B)
    - For patients with dMMR/MSI-H tumors, consider germline testing and/or referral to a genetic counselor
    - For patients with a family history suggestive of BRCA1/2 pathogenic variants (PVs), consider germline testing and/or referral to a genetic counselor
- Options, order does not indicate preference; the choice of treatment modality may depend on extent/location of disease and institutional capabilities
    - Systemic therapy (Principles of Systemic Therapy, BIL-C)
    - Clinical trial
    - Combination of chemotherapy and chemoradiation
        - Principles of Systemic Therapy (BIL-C)
        - Principles of Radiation Therapy (BIL-D)
        - There are limited clinical trial data to define a standard regimen or definitive benefit; participation in clinical trials is encouraged
        - For a list of gemcitabine-based regimens and fluoropyrimidine-based regimens to be used before or after chemoradiation, see Adjuvant Chemotherapy (BIL-C, 1 of 5)
    - Chemoradiation
        - Principles of Systemic Therapy (BIL-C)
        - Principles of Radiation Therapy (BIL-D)
        - There are limited clinical trial data to define a standard regimen or definitive benefit; participation in clinical trials is encouraged
    - Consider locoregional therapy (Principles of Arterial/Locoregional Therapy for Intrahepatic Cholangiocarcinoma, INTRA-D)
        - Arterially directed therapies
            - Hepatic arterial infusion chemotherapy, with or without systemic chemotherapy, may be used in a clinical trial or at experienced centers in carefully selected cases
        - RT (Principles of Radiation Therapy, BIL-D)
    - Consider referral to a transplant center
        - Principles of Surgery (INTRA-A)
        - For small single tumors <3 cm
        - Eligible for transplant exception points if all of: biopsy-proven intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma (iCCA) or mixed HCC-iCCA, presence of cirrhosis, unresectable, received locoregional or systemic therapy, and 6 months from time of diagnosis or last treatment with no new lesions or extrahepatic disease
        - Consider referral to hepatologist
    - Best supportive care, see NCCN Guidelines for Palliative Care

# Primary treatment, metastatic disease

- Biopsy, if not previously performed
    - Principles of Mixed HCC-CCA (INTRA-B)
    - Principles of Pathology (INTRA-C)
- Biomarker testing (Principles of Biomarker Testing, BIL-B)
    - For patients with dMMR/MSI-H tumors, consider germline testing and/or referral to a genetic counselor
    - For patients with a family history suggestive of BRCA1/2 pathogenic variants (PVs), consider germline testing and/or referral to a genetic counselor
- Options, order does not indicate preference; the choice of treatment modality may depend on extent/location of disease and institutional capabilities
    - Systemic therapy (preferred), Principles of Systemic Therapy (BIL-C)
    - Clinical trial (preferred)
    - Consider locoregional therapy (Principles of Arterial/Locoregional Therapy for Intrahepatic Cholangiocarcinoma, INTRA-D)
        - Arterially directed therapies
            - Hepatic arterial infusion chemotherapy, with or without systemic chemotherapy, may be used in a clinical trial or at experienced centers in carefully selected cases
        - RT (Principles of Radiation Therapy, BIL-D)

# Next

- After resection, adjuvant treatment and surveillance (INTRA-2)
- After treatment of unresectable or metastatic disease, assess for response and
    - Reconsider resection or locoregional therapy, or
    - Subsequent-line systemic therapy if progression on or after systemic therapy (BIL-C)
    - Consider re-evaluation for liver transplantation
