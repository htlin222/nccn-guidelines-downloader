# Definition of cancer-related fatigue

- A sense of tiredness or exhaustion
    - Distressing
    - Persistent
    - Subjective
- Tiredness or exhaustion may be physical, emotional, and/or cognitive
    - Physical
    - Emotional
    - Cognitive
- Related to cancer or cancer treatment
- Not proportional to recent activity
- Interferes with usual functioning
