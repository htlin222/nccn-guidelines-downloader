# Source

- NCCN Pediatric Diffuse High-Grade Gliomas v1.2026, PGLIO-2, p9
- Applies to pediatric diffuse high-grade gliomas, EXCEPT diffuse midline glioma, H3 K27-altered, or pontine location
- Diagnoses covered include, in addition to other high-grade glial entities
    - Diffuse hemispheric glioma, H3 G34-mutant
    - Pediatric diffuse high-grade glioma, H3 wild-type and IDH wild-type
    - Infant-type hemispheric glioma
- Pathology per Principles of Neuropathology (PGLIO-B)
- All recommendations are category 2A unless otherwise indicated

# Adjuvant treatment, age 3 years or older

- Clinical trial (preferred)
- Standard brain RT + concurrent temozolomide + adjuvant temozolomide, with or without adjuvant lomustine
    - Standard brain RT, per Principles of Radiation Therapy Management (PGLIO-D)
    - Concurrent temozolomide
    - Adjuvant temozolomide, per Principles of Systemic Therapy (PGLIO-E)
    - Adjuvant lomustine, optional, per Principles of Systemic Therapy (PGLIO-E)
- Standard brain RT alone, per Principles of Radiation Therapy Management (PGLIO-D)
- Standard brain RT, with or without concurrent temozolomide, + adjuvant targeted therapy based on the molecular composition of the tumor
    - Standard brain RT, per Principles of Radiation Therapy Management (PGLIO-D)
    - Concurrent temozolomide, optional, per Principles of Systemic Therapy (PGLIO-E)
    - Adjuvant targeted therapy chosen on the molecular composition of the tumor, per Principles of Systemic Therapy (PGLIO-E)

# Adjuvant treatment, age under 3 years

- Clinical trial (preferred)
- Systemic chemotherapy, to delay need for radiation, per Principles of Systemic Therapy (PGLIO-E)
- Adjuvant targeted therapy based on the molecular composition of the tumor, per Principles of Systemic Therapy (PGLIO-E)
- Standard brain RT, only if other options are not feasible, per Principles of Radiation Therapy Management (PGLIO-D)

# Follow-up

- RT completed ___ (rt_completion_date)
- Brain MRI, per Principles of Neuroimaging (PGLIO-A)
    - 2–6 weeks after RT
    - Then every 2–3 months for 1 year
    - Then every 3–6 months indefinitely
