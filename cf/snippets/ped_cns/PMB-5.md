# Clinical staging

- Brain and spine MRI (PMB-A)
- CSF analysis

# Surgery, for localized brain recurrence

- Consider maximum safe resection (PMB-C)
- Brain MRI to document extent of resection (PMB-A)
    - Postoperative follow-up is ideally between 24-48 hours after surgery

# Treatment for localized brain recurrence

- Systemic therapy (PMB-E)
    - and/or additional focal radiation (PMB-D)
        - Multidisciplinary consult is needed to determine if radiation is recommended
        - Radiation may be considered for palliation or treatment for recurrence
    - or CSI and appropriate boost, if CSI not previously done
- or Clinical trial
- or Consider repeat CSI (PMB-D)
    - Only for patients with at least a 2-year interval since initial CSI
    - Only if initial CSI was <36 Gy
- or Palliative/best supportive care including radiation or resection, if indicated (PMB-D)
    - Consider resection for palliation of symptoms where indicated

# Treatment for disseminated disease

- CSI (PMB-D) and appropriate boost, if CSI not previously done
    - and/or Systemic therapy (PMB-E)
- or Clinical trial
- or Consider repeat CSI (PMB-D)
    - Only for patients with at least a 2-year interval since initial CSI
    - Only if initial CSI was <36 Gy
- or Palliative/best supportive care, including radiation or resection, if indicated (PMB-D)
    - Consider resection for palliation of symptoms where indicated
