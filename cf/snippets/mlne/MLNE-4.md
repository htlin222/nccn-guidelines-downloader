# Presentation patterns

- Chronic phase
    - May present in the BM or PB as an MPN or MDS/MPN, with or without eosinophilia
    - BM may exhibit an atypical mast cell proliferation, often in an interstitial pattern
        - Not the typical aggregates found in SM
- Blast phase, 20% blasts or more
    - May present in the BM or PB as AML, ALL, or mixed phenotype acute leukemias
        - For a phenotype of ALL, the differential diagnosis of JAK2 and ABL1 fusions includes Ph-like ALL
    - EMD represents a blast phase component
    - May also present as an EMD with an "MPN-like" picture in blood and marrow
- Accelerated phase
    - There is no current definition for accelerated phase disease
    - Similar to myeloid neoplasms such as CML, 10%-19% blasts in the BM or PB have been used to define accelerated phase
- EMD
    - May present as extramedullary myeloid sarcoma
    - May present as T-cell or B-cell lymphoblastic lymphoma
    - May present as myeloid/T-cell or B-cell lymphoid mixed phenotype acute leukemias
    - May present alone, or with chronic or blast phase disease involving the BM or PB
    - Lineage involvement of the EMD may be different from the lineage involving the BM or PB
- Clinical presentation partly reflects the fusion partner gene for the TK
    - Best exemplified by the diverse phenotypes in FGFR1-rearranged diseases

# Step 1 Disease extent

- Does disease involvement include BM, PB, or EMD, or all?
- If clinically suspected, imaging to identify EMD

# Step 2 Disease stage

- Is chronic phase or blast phase disease present in the BM or PB?
- Staging of BM or PB to determine if chronic vs. blast phase
    - For a phenotype of ALL, the differential diagnosis of JAK2 and ABL1 fusions includes Ph-like ALL
- If present, EMD represents a blast phase component

# Step 3 Disease lineage(s)

- What are the disease lineage(s) of the BM or PB and EMD components?
- Immunophenotyping to determine lineage in BM or PB and/or EMD
    - Lineage involvement of the EMD may be different from the lineage involving the BM or PB
