Margins — stage I / II invasive
Positive = ink on tumour: any invasive cancer or DCIS touching the ink. Generally requires further surgery — re-excision to a negative margin, or mastectomy
Margins — DCIS
≥2 mm with whole breast RT; at least 2 mm with excision alone. DCIS with microinvasion (≤1 mm) follows the DCIS rule
Radiation by pathologic nodal status
BINV-2 after BCS, BINV-3 after mastectomy
Then triage systemic therapy
Histology, HR and HER2 status (BINV-4). RT typically follows systemic therapy
Details that decide the operation
Before an oncoplastic conservation
Assess the likely cosmetic outcome first; warn about positive margins and a possible second operation. Consider a staged reconstruction when margin status is unclear (lobular, multifocal or multicentric)
Reconstruction branches on radiation
No RT history and none needed → implant, autologous or combination. RT history or need, or either unknown → the dedicated BINV-H pathways. Inflammatory cancer → delayed. Delayed flaps for contour defects wait ≥6 months after RT
What the pathology report must state
Gross and microscopic margin status. Distance, orientation and tumour type at the closest margin. Quantification of disease approaching the inked margin
