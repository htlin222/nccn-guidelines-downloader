Rules that are easy to lose
Round to the nearest millimetre — with one exception
1.0–1.4 mm rounds up to 2 mm, so it is not called microinvasive (T1mi). The T1a line states the same rule the other way round: round anything >1.0–1.9 mm to 2 mm
What does not qualify as T4
Invasion of the dermis alone; invasion of or adherence to pectoralis muscle without invasion of chest wall structures
Suffixes and one deletion
(sn) and (f) mark confirmation by sentinel node biopsy or needle biopsy. LCIS is benign and was removed from TNM in AJCC 8th edition
