Numbers that run alongside
1–100per cent ER staining that counts as ER-positive. ER-low-positive is 1–10%, where efficacy data are limited. PR is positive at 1–100% nuclear staining, negative below 1%
3–5years Adjuvant bisphosphonate to consider in postmenopausal (natural or induced), high-risk node-negative or node-positive patients
1–2years DEXA interval on an aromatase inhibitor, by risk
Baseline BMD if age >65, family history, or long-term steroids
≥12months Amenorrhoea, the usual clinical threshold for menopause — but insufficient on its own after chemotherapy or on tamoxifen. Natural menopause falls between 42 and 58
All eight are from NCCN Invasive Breast Cancer v6.2026 — BINV-6, BINV-7, BINV-8, BINV-K and BINV-O. A recurrence score only means something once the patient has been established as a chemotherapy candidate.
