Three more that do not fit a card. Bone-modifying agents need expected survival ≥3 months, adequate renal function and a dental examination first, and zoledronic acid is given every 12 weeks. Sacituzumab govitecan requires prior endocrine therapy, a CDK4/6 inhibitor and ≥2 lines of chemotherapy (one containing a taxane, at least one in the metastatic setting). Albumin-bound paclitaxel substituted out of medical necessity must not exceed 125 mg/m² weekly.
