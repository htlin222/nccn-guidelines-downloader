Reading the report — where the boundaries sit
1–100per cent ER IHC staining of 1%–100% is ER-positive and qualifies for endocrine therapy — BINV-6
1–10per cent ER-low positive. Limited data, heterogeneous; often behaves closer to ER-negative — weigh endocrine therapy individually
≤10per cent HER2 IHC 0+ = faint, partial membrane staining in ≤10% — reported apart from 0/absent, on primary or metastatic samples
>90per cent “Pure” for favorable histology — judged on the surgical excision specimen, not core biopsy alone
Deciding on chemotherapy
26recurrence score Postmenopausal pT1b–T3, pN1mi or pN1: one cut point. ≥26 chemotherapy then endocrine, <26 endocrine — both category 1
≤15 / 16–25 / ≥26recurrence score Premenopausal pT1b–T3, pN0: three bands, not one. NCCN is unsure whether the benefit below 26 comes from chemotherapy-induced ovarian suppression
≥4positive nodes pN2/pN3 (≥4 nodes >2 mm): little data on gene expression assays here — decide on clinical factors
≤0.5centimetres pT1a with pN0 — the one HR−/HER2− cell with no adjuvant therapy. High-risk features (young, high grade) may still prompt chemotherapy — category 2B
All eight are from the guideline, not from trials: BINV-A, BINV-6, BINV-7, BINV-8, BINV-10, BINV-11 and BINV-N of NCCN Invasive Breast Cancer v6.2026. The ER-low positive footnote appears on the HR+/HER2+ page (BINV-5) word for word as well — it is not specific to HER2-negative disease.
