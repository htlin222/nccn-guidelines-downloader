Read BINV-28 as “metastatic patients need no follow-up”
All four BINV-19 pathways and both axillary BINV-20 pathways open with “consider appropriate systemic therapy to best response if indicated”, and only then surgery
Systemic → surgery → radiation. CALOR showed systemic therapy after recurrence improves disease-free survival, especially in ER-negative disease
BINV-18 requires “biopsy of at least first recurrence of disease”, and re-testing of ER/PR and HER2, with a further biopsy considered at progression
False negatives happen and primary and metastasis can disagree. Starting from the primary's receptors is the exception, for when a biopsy cannot safely be obtained
Every premenopausal patient is told about the effect of chemotherapy on fertility and asked about future pregnancy; those who may want it are referred before chemotherapy and/or endocrine therapy
Refer late and the window for oocyte retrieval is gone
BINV-C: “hormone-based birth control is discouraged regardless of the HR status of the patient's cancer”
Non-hormonal IUD, barrier methods, or sterilisation of the patient or partner where no pregnancy is wanted
The opposite: an individualised survivorship plan, a lifelong relationship with primary care, preventive screening set by prognosis, and routine screening and referral for a long list of symptoms
At every progression all options are laid out, including supportive care alone and hospice. Stopping treatment is a discussion to open, not a failure of one
Common practice NCCN v6.2026
Every “repeat” carries a qualifier: repeat BCS is for selected patients with limited data, the accuracy of repeat SLNB is unproven, and repeat RT is “if feasible” and must account for the prior dose. All are may be considered; none is a should.
