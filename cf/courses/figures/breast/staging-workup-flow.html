Re-evaluate ER/PR and HER2 · multigene panel testing, somatic and germline, to identify candidates for targeted therapy · tissue and plasma ctDNA each have limitations, so a negative result on one can be followed by the other · genetic counselling if at risk · distress
Full systemic imaging
Chest CT ± contrast · abdomen ± pelvis CT or MRI with contrast · brain MRI if suspicious CNS symptoms · spine MRI if back pain or cord compression · bone scan or sodium fluoride PET/CT (category 2B). Useful in selected cases: FDG-PET/CT (consider FES-PET/CT for ER-positive and lobular disease); radiographs of symptomatic and weight-bearing bones
