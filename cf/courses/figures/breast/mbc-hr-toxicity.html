What to check, and how often — HR-positive, HER2-negative metastatic disease
Agent or setting What to monitor When / how often What NCCN says to do Source
Every patient on treatment Symptoms, examination, performance status, weight, LFTs, CBC Endocrine ± CDK4/6i: every 1–3 months. Chemotherapy or targeted therapy: prior to each cycle Reassess in patients with new or worsening signs or symptoms, regardless of the interval BINV-R 3
CT chest/abdomen/pelvis with contrast; bone scan Endocrine ± CDK4/6i: every 2–6 months. Chemo or targeted: CT every 2–4 cycles, bone scan every 4–6 Keep to the same method over time. With long-term stable disease the frequency can be reduced BINV-R 2
BINV-R 3
Tumour markers, PET/CT, brain MRI As clinically indicated Markers can rise in responding disease — an isolated increase “should rarely be used to declare progression” BINV-R 1
Endocrine + CDK4/6 inhibitor Treatment-emergent toxicity Particularly early in initiation of these therapies “Management of toxicity is essential.” If frail or with extensive comorbidity, consider single-agent AI BINV-P 1
Aromatase inhibitor Bone mineral density, if at risk (age >65, family history, chronic steroids) Baseline assessment recommended Footnoted on both the entry page and the endocrine page, so it holds on any line BINV-21
BINV-P 3
Bone metastases — denosumab, zoledronic acid, pamidronate Dental status; renal function; expected survival Dental exam with preventive dentistry before the first dose; optimal zoledronic acid schedule every 12 weeks Give (category 1) if bone metastasis is present, survival ≥3 months and renal function adequate — all with calcium and vitamin D BINV-21
Fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan (HER2-low) Interstitial lung disease / pneumonitis Regular monitoring is recommended With a history of ILD/pneumonitis there are no data on managing safety or toxicity BINV-Q 2
Cytotoxic therapy Cardiac function; peripheral neuropathy; DPYD Regular cardiac monitoring for current or prior anthracycline or HER2-targeted therapy On a taxane, consider cryotherapy of hands and feet. For DPYD testing see NCCN Guidelines for Colon Cancer BINV-Q 1
Every cell is NCCN wording from the page named in the last column. The intervals in the first block are the guideline’s own table, and it says of itself that the optimal frequency is uncertain and taken from trial practice. Alpelisib, capivasertib, inavolisib and everolimus carry no row — BINV-Q gives their dosing and biomarker eligibility but no monitoring, so there is nothing to quote.
