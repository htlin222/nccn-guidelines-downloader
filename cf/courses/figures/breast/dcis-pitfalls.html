Read the DCIS report with the invasive Nottingham grade
DCIS-1: it “should not be performed for preoperative (biopsy-determined) pure DCIS unless there is some clinical-radiographic-pathologic suggestion of invasion or axillary metastasis”
The three SLNB exceptions are all about it being impossible later, not about staging
The same page writes it into the treatment itself: “Total mastectomy with sentinel lymph node biopsy”
Omission fits a narrow group: >50 years, non-palpable, low-grade, ER-positive, normal axilla. If invasion turns up later, that node is gone
It is category 2B, and DCIS-1 says it may be considered only when all four RTOG 9804 conditions hold
And what is described is omitting radiation with endocrine therapy, not doing nothing. The condition most often overlooked is screen-detected
BINV-F does not endorse it: with excision alone, IBTR is much higher regardless of margin width, even in prospectively defined low-risk groups
A wide margin does not buy back a course of radiation
DCIS-2: “Since a survival advantage has not been demonstrated, individual consideration of risks and benefits is important”
What it lowers is ipsilateral and contralateral breast events. It is also only for ER-positive DCIS
ST-4 keeps the two apart: invasive cancer uses Nottingham (three components, 1–3 points each), DCIS uses nuclear grade
Grade III is one of the DCIS-1 recurrence risk factors, so misreading it feeds into the decision about omitting radiation
