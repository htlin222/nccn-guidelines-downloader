Two populations carry a written caveat rather than a number: in pT1a–b, N0 the benefit of HER2-targeted therapy is uncertain — “a population not studied in randomized trials” — and in HR-positive tumours approaching T1mic (<1 mm) its absolute benefit is negligible, with endocrine therapy still a reasonable systemic option.
