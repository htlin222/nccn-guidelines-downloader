BINV-A: report the staining pattern per the current CAP protocol — 0/absent or 0+/with membrane staining
The distinction “is currently clinically relevant since patients with metastatic disease may be eligible for treatment targeting non-amplified levels of HER2 expression”
The same footnote sits on BINV-5, BINV-6, BINV-7 and BINV-8: 1%–10% is heterogeneous and the data are limited
Eligible for endocrine therapy is not the same as deriving the same benefit — weigh it individually
BINV-A: re-review the pathology and consider repeat testing or consultation
Those are the BINV-11 favorable histologies, and HER2-negative is one of that page's own conditions — the two pages surface the conflict for you
BINV-A lists five situations in which retesting on a subsequent surgical or other specimen may be considered
Most often missed: a scant invasive component, and a later specimen containing a higher-grade, morphologically distinct carcinoma
BINV-18: biopsy at least at first recurrence and reassess ER/PR and HER2; consider repeating at progression
ER/PR can be falsely negative, and primary and metastatic sites can disagree. Add multigene panel testing, somatic and germline
BINV-N: only 21-gene is Yes in the predictive column; the other assays read Not determined
