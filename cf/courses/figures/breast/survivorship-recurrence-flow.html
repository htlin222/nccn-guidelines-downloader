A multidisciplinary approach is especially important here. Repeat SLNB may be considered but its accuracy is unproven; after prior ALND or mastectomy, repeat SLNB with lymphoscintigraphy may be considered rather than ALND.
Repeat BCS ± APBI/PBI may be considered in selected patients who decline mastectomy and meet consensus criteria — data are limited. Any repeat RT must account for the prior dose and the late normal-tissue toxicity of the sum.
