Treat the two CDK4/6 sentences as one sentence
Read “± pertuzumab” as “add pertuzumab”
Escalate on residual DCIS alone
Make T-DXd the default for residual HER2-positive disease
Close the case on “PMRT + RNI is not routinely indicated”
Judge response on mammography
The HR+/HER2− pCR box says consider ribociclib; the residual box says consider abemaciclib or ribociclib
Both point to BINV-K for eligibility, but they do not offer the same drugs
“Complete (up to) 1 year of HER2-targeted therapy with trastuzumab (category 1) ± pertuzumab”
Only node positive at initial staging turns it into pertuzumab + trastuzumab (category 1). The criterion is initial staging, not ypN
“Recommendations do not apply to residual DCIS (ypTis)”
DCIS alone is not residual invasive disease — the footnote sits on T-DM1 and on capecitabine
T-DXd (category 1) for those with high risk of recurrence, or T-DM1 (category 1)
T-DXd carries a population qualifier and T-DM1 does not; both are category 1
The same box carries NSABP B-51: follow-up limited, long-term non-inferiority not shown for this subgroup
Weigh undertreatment against the established long-term survival benefit of PMRT + RNI — young or long life expectancy, ER-positive, residual disease in the breast, multiple risk factors
“MRI is more accurate than mammography for assessing tumor response to preoperative therapy”
And use the studies that were abnormal at initial staging, not whichever one is at hand
Common practice NCCN v6.2026
Also: the inoperable list has four entries, not just inflammatory breast cancer; no response is a reason to consider further systemic therapy and/or preoperative radiation, not to finish the planned course; and chemotherapy and endocrine therapy are given sequentially, endocrine after chemotherapy.
