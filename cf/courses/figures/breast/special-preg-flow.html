Entry　Confirmed breast cancer during pregnancy, no distant metastases on staging.　Staging if indicated: chest x-ray with abdominal shielding · abdominal ultrasound for liver metastases · non-contrast spine MRI if indicated for bone metastases.　CT, nuclear imaging (bone scan or PET/CT) and contrast-enhanced MRI are contraindicated during pregnancy.
