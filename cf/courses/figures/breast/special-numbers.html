Special presentations — inflammatory breast cancer and phyllodes tumour
≤6months Erythema, edema and peau d'orange arising within 6 months and covering
≥1/3 of the breast, plus pathologic confirmation of invasive cancer
notrequired Dermal lymphatic involvement may be seen but is not required
for the diagnosis of inflammatory breast cancer
1year Complete up to one year of HER2-targeted therapy after surgery
for HER2-positive inflammatory breast cancer — category 1
3years Clinical follow-up for benign phyllodes, with no radiation.
Suspect phyllodes at >3 cm; add mammography from age 30
Special populations — pregnancy and males
2absolutes Chemotherapy must not be given in the first trimester;
radiation is given in no trimester
35weeks Avoid chemotherapy beyond 35 weeks, so that maternal
bone marrow can recover before delivery
5–10years Adjuvant tamoxifen in males. If tamoxifen is contraindicated,
a GnRH analog with an AI — single-agent AI is not recommended
2years Bone mineral density in males on an adjuvant GnRH analog:
baseline, then every 2 years
All from NCCN Invasive Breast Cancer v6.2026 — IBC-1, IBC-2, PHYLL-1, PREG-1, PREG-2 and BINV-J. Not shown: an interval of 12–16 weeks between treatment modalities is generally considered acceptable in pregnancy, and the preferred schedules are doxorubicin/cyclophosphamide every 3 weeks and weekly paclitaxel.
