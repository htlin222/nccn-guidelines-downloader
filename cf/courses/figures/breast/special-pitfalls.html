Reconstruction may only be delayed. Chest wall radiation plus comprehensive regional nodal irradiation, covering any undissected axilla at risk
IBC-1: “Dermal lymphatic involvement may be seen but is not required for the diagnosis”
The diagnosis is clinical plus pathological — onset within 6 months over ≥1/3 of the breast, with invasive cancer confirmed
Both PAGET-2 pathways read “full-thickness skin biopsy of involved NAC”
Negative breast imaging with a positive NAC leads to “consider breast MRI and tissue sampling, if indicated”; a biopsy site that does not heal is re-biopsied
PHYLL-1, one sentence: “Adjuvant RT is not indicated for positive margins”
The single most counter-intuitive line on the page — and the one a surgeon most wants to override
PREG-2 bans six classes: anti-HER2, CDK4/6 inhibitors, ADCs, endocrine therapy, PARP inhibitors, immune checkpoint inhibitors
Supportive care changes too: avoid NK1 inhibitors, and avoid dexamethasone and betamethasone where possible
BINV-J: single-agent adjuvant AI “has been associated with inferior outcomes compared to tamoxifen alone … and is not recommended”
In advanced disease it is “preferred that … a GnRH analog should be given concurrently” with an AI. This is the only male-specific drug rule on the page
