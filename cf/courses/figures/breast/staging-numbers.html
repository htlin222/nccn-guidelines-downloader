The rulers
1 · 5 · 10 · 20 · 50millimetres The whole T ruler, five marks. T1mi ≤1 · T1a >1–5 · T1b >5–10 · T1c >10–20 · T2 >20–50 · T3 >50 mm
0.2 and 2.0millimetres ITC ≤0.2 mm, recorded pN0(i+) and still pN0; micrometastasis >0.2 to ≤2.0 mm, about 200 cells; >2.0 mm is a truly positive node
1–3 / 4–9 / ≥10positive nodes pN1a · pN2a · pN3a, with at least one deposit >2.0 mm
3–5 / 6–7 / 8–9nottingham points G1 · G2 · G3. Tubule formation, nuclear pleomorphism and calibrated mitotic count, 1–3 points each. DCIS uses nuclear grade instead
The rules that catch people out
1.0–1.4mm rounds up to 2 The one exception to rounding — rounding down would make it T1mi. But the T1a bracket on the same page reads “round any measurement >1.0–1.9 mm to 2 mm”. The source contradicts itself; do not memorise this as settled
4months Imaging after surgery may change the stage only if it is done within 4 months of diagnosis, with no disease progression in between, and the patient had no preoperative therapy
>50years, postmenopausal Omitting SLNB may be considered: cT1N0 with a negative axillary ultrasound, HR+/HER2−, grade 1–2, and willing to accept whole breast radiation and endocrine therapy
<11oncotype dx score T1N0M0 or T2N0M0, HER2-negative, ER-positive → pathological prognostic stage IA. Genomic information is not required to assign a pathological prognostic stage
From the AJCC tables reproduced in NCCN Invasive Breast Cancer v6.2026 (ST-1 to ST-4, ST-11) and from BINV-E. Two more rules worth keeping next to these: M0 includes M0(i+) and pM0 is not a valid category, so every M0 is clinical; and LCIS is a benign entity that was removed from TNM staging in the AJCC 8th edition.
