What happens when — and what each step decides for the next one
Step 1Diagnosis and workup Confirm HER2 and HR. Stage. Genetic counselling if indicated. Baseline LVEF before any anti-HER2 agent
Step 2Decide: operate first, or treat first ≥cT2 or ≥cN1 → preoperative systemic therapy. Smaller, node-negative tumours may go straight to surgery
Step 3 — if treated firstPreoperative systemic therapy Chemotherapy + trastuzumab ± pertuzumab. Clip the tumour bed and the sampled node before starting
Step 4Surgery — and the pathology report This is the branch point. pCR and residual disease lead to different adjuvant therapy
Step 5Adjuvant systemic therapy pCR → complete one year of anti-HER2.
Residual → ado-trastuzumab emtansine, category 1.
If HR-positive, endocrine therapy as well
Step 6Radiation Usually after chemotherapy. Anti-HER2 therapy may run concurrently
Step 7Surveillance History and exam 1–4× a year for 5 years, then annually. Mammography every 12 months
Running alongside, from step 3 onwards
LVEF every 3 months while on anti-HER2

One year total of anti-HER2 therapy
Preoperative and adjuvant portions count towards the same year
Endocrine therapy if HR-positive
Sequentially after chemotherapy, not concurrently. Can run at the same time as radiation and anti-HER2
Never anthracycline concurrently with trastuzumab
Concurrent trastuzumab + pertuzumab with an anthracycline is to be avoided
Taxane substitution caps at 125 mg/m²
Only when medically necessary, and the two versions do not carry the same category
Scalp cooling and cryotherapy
Offered to reduce alopecia and peripheral neuropathy during taxane-based therapy
