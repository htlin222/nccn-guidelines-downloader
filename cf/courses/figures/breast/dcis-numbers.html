All from DCIS-1, DCIS-2 and BINV-F of NCCN Invasive Breast Cancer v6.2026. Two more that belong next to these: the six factors that raise local recurrence risk — larger DCIS, a palpable mass, grade III, margins ≤2 mm, ER-negative, age <50 — and the narrow group in whom SLNB may be omitted at mastectomy: >50 years, non-palpable, low-grade, ER-positive DCIS with a normal axilla.
