Red line — what changes
Inflammatory
breast cancer
Multidisciplinary exam + medical photography. Chest CT, abdomen ± pelvis CT or MRI, bone scan or FDG-PET/CT. Breast MRI optional
Preoperative systemic therapy first. Total mastectomy — skin- and nipple-sparing contraindicated · level I/II ALND · chest wall + comprehensive RNI · delayed reconstruction only
A pertuzumab-containing regimen may be given preoperatively if HER2-positive. Finish the cytotoxic course, then endocrine therapy if ER/PR-positive. Up to 1 year of anti-HER2 therapy (category 1)
Diagnosis is clinical + pathological: onset ≤6 months, erythema and oedema over ≥1/3 of the breast. Dermal lymphatic involvement is not required
Paget
disease
Clinical breast exam · bilateral diagnostic mammogram, ultrasound as necessary. Full-thickness skin biopsy of the involved NAC, plus core biopsy of any breast lesion
Central lumpectomy including the NAC ± SLNB, with whole breast RT — or total mastectomy including the NAC ± SLNB, with or without reconstruction
Nothing specific to Paget. If DCIS or invasive cancer is found, adjuvant systemic therapy follows the DCIS or invasive breast cancer guideline
The skin biopsy must be full thickness. Negative imaging with a positive NAC still means consider breast MRI. Mastectomy is always an option
Phyllodes
tumour
H&P · ultrasound · mammogram if ≥30 y. Core needle biopsy — neither core biopsy nor FNA can always separate phyllodes from fibroadenoma
Excisional biopsy: complete removal, not aimed at obtaining margins. Benign → clinical follow-up for 3 years, no RT. Borderline or malignant → sarcoma guideline
None in this guideline. Borderline and malignant phyllodes are managed under NCCN Soft Tissue Sarcoma (PHYLLSARC-1)
Adjuvant RT is not indicated for positive margins. Suspicion is clinical: palpable mass, rapid growth, size >3 cm
Follows the general pathway Situation-specific rule NAC = nipple–areolar complex ALND = axillary lymph node dissection
Recurrent inflammatory breast cancer is treated as recurrent or stage IV disease.
Breast cancer during pregnancy and breast cancer in males are the other two special situations — a separate figure with the same four columns.
