“Trastuzumab (category 1) (± pertuzumab for pT2–T3)”
One sentence doing two jobs: pertuzumab is optional, and only in pT2–T3. A pT1c, pN0 tumour is not inside the bracket
“Pertuzumab + trastuzumab (category 1, preferred)” or “trastuzumab (category 1)”
The second is also category 1 — a parallel route, not a lesser one
Benefit is uncertain — “a population not studied in randomized trials”
The decision must balance against the known toxicities. Say the uncertainty out loud rather than deciding for the patient
“If node positive at initial staging, pertuzumab + trastuzumab (category 1)”
pCR clears the nodes; the stratification still uses the pre-treatment stage
“Recommendations do not apply to residual DCIS (ypTis)”
