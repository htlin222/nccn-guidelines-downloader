When to stop 200 mg every 21 days, or 400 mg every 6 weeks Continue until progression or unacceptable toxicity, or up to 24 months. After progression on a PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitor there are no data for another one BINV-Q 14

Sacituzumab govitecan Myelosuppression 10 mg/kg IV on days 1 and 8, every 21 days Every row listing this drug carries the same footnote: see NCCN Guidelines for Hematopoietic Growth Factors BINV-Q 3
Fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan (HER2 IHC 1+ or 2+/ISH−) Interstitial lung disease / pneumonitis Regular monitoring is recommended With a history of ILD/pneumonitis there are no data on managing safety or toxicity BINV-Q 3
Cytotoxic therapy Peripheral neuropathy on a taxane; alopecia Not stated Consider cryotherapy of hands and feet; glove constriction may be considered. Consider scalp cooling BINV-Q 1
DPYD before capecitabine; cardiac function after an anthracycline Regular cardiac monitoring for current or prior anthracycline use For DPYD see NCCN Guidelines for Colon Cancer; for cardiac risk see NCCN Guidelines for Survivorship BINV-Q 1
Bone metastases — denosumab, zoledronic acid, pamidronate Dental status; renal function; expected survival Dental exam with preventive dentistry before the first dose; optimal zoledronic acid schedule every 12 weeks Give (category 1) if bone metastasis is present, survival ≥3 months and renal function adequate — all with calcium and vitamin D BINV-21
Disease assessment Symptoms, examination, performance status, weight, LFTs, CBC; imaging Prior to each cycle; CT chest/abdomen/pelvis every 2–4 cycles; bone scan every 4–6 Reassess on new or worsening signs or symptoms regardless of the interval from previous studies BINV-R 3
Every cell is NCCN wording from the page named in the last column; “not stated” means the concern is raised without an interval. Olaparib, talazoparib and eribulin appear on these pages with dosing only — no agent-specific monitoring is written for them, so none is invented here.
