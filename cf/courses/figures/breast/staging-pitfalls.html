BINV-1: “Routine systemic staging is not indicated for non-metastatic (M0) cancer in the absence of systemic symptoms”
Additional imaging only for signs or symptoms of metastasis, or clinically high risk. A false positive drags a curable patient through a chain of biopsies
ST-3: that table is only for regions where biomarker tests are not routinely available. US registries must use the clinical and pathological prognostic stage groups
The two tables often give different stages for the same TNM
ST-9 excludes it: “does not apply to patients treated with systemic or radiation prior to surgical resection”
Use the yc or yp prefix; a pathological complete response (ypT0ypN0cM0) is not assigned an anatomic stage group at all
BINV-B: “False-positive findings on breast MRI are common. Surgical decisions should not be based solely on the MRI findings”
Biopsy the suspicious area separately. Preoperative MRI has not been shown to improve re-excision rate, local recurrence or survival
ST-1 denies both: invasion of the dermis alone does not qualify as T4, and neither does invasion of or adherence to pectoralis muscle without invasion of chest wall structures
One T category too high lifts the whole treatment intensity
ST-2 puts the pN0 boundary at ITC — pN0 is no metastasis or ITC only, and >0.2 mm is already pN1mi
It carries through to the ST-6 and ST-9 stage tables, and changes which adjuvant therapy cell the patient lands in
