Trastuzumab ± pertuzumab Left ventricular ejection fraction Before and during treatment. Optimal frequency is not known; the FDA label says every 3 months Regular cardiac monitoring is recommended — see NCCN Guidelines for Survivorship: Cardiovascular Disease Risk Assessment BINV-M 1
Overlap with an anthracycline At regimen selection Significant cardiac toxicity: concurrent trastuzumab and pertuzumab with an anthracycline should be avoided BINV-M 4
