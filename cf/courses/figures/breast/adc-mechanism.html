Three parts, four steps — and the part that explains HER2-low
Picks the target: HER2, or TROP-2 for sacituzumab
Holds the payload until the antibody is inside. Cleavable linkers are what allow the bystander effect
The cytotoxic drug. Its potency, and how many copies ride on each antibody, set the therapeutic window
Why this matters clinically
A cleavable linker plus a membrane-permeable payload kills the neighbouring cell too — even if that cell carries little or no target. That is the mechanistic reason a HER2-directed ADC can work in HER2-low disease, where trastuzumab alone does not.
The antibody binds its antigen. Expression level decides how much drug arrives
The whole complex is drawn into the cell inside a vesicle
Free payload is now loose in the cytoplasm and kills that cell
Membrane-permeable payload crosses into neighbouring cells that express little or no target
