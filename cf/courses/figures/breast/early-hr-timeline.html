What happens when — and what each step decides for the next one
Step 1Diagnosis and staging ER staining 1%–100% is ER-positive; 1%–10% is ER-low-positive, with limited efficacy data. And menopausal status — it decides which of the three pages you read
Step 2Surgery — then read pT and pN pT1a ≤0.5 cm, pT1b 0.6–1.0 cm, pT1c >1 cm. pN1mi >0.2 to ≤2.0 mm, pN1 1–3 nodes, pN2/pN3 ≥4 ipsilateral nodes >2 mm. pT1a with pN0 is the one box that is only “consider endocrine therapy” (category 2B)
Step 3Candidate for chemotherapy? → then the assay The question comes before the assay, not after. If a candidate and pN0: strongly consider the 21-gene RT-PCR assay (category 1). Premenopausal pN1: only “consider a gene expression assay to assess prognosis”. pN2/pN3: no assay, decide on clinical factors
Step 4 — if the score or the stage says soChemotherapy Chemotherapy followed by endocrine therapy. Postmenopausal RS ≥26 (category 1). Premenopausal RS 16–25, and RS ≥26 (category 1). pN2/pN3 (category 1) on clinical factors alone
Step 5Endocrine therapy — 5 years, then extend or stop Premenopausal: tamoxifen for 5 y (category 1) ± ovarian suppression or ablation, or an aromatase inhibitor for 5 y + ovarian suppression (category 1). Postmenopausal: an aromatase inhibitor for 5 y (category 1). Then extended therapy — or none
Step 6 — from the first aromatase inhibitorBone health Baseline BMD if at risk (age >65, family history, chronic steroids), then DEXA every 1–2 years. A bisphosphonate or denosumab is acceptable to maintain it. Separately: adjuvant bisphosphonate 3–5 years for postmenopausal (natural or induced) high-risk patients
Step 7Surveillance History and exam 1–4× a year for 5 years, then annually. Mammography every 12 months. No labs or imaging for metastasis screening in the absence of signs or symptoms

Menopausal status is not settled once
Chemotherapy-induced amenorrhoea is not menopause; FSH and estradiol must be repeated serially, and status cannot be determined during ovarian suppression
Interactions with tamoxifen
Fluoxetine and paroxetine reduce formation of endoxifen and 4-OH tamoxifen — use with caution. CYP2D6 genotyping is not recommended
Adherence is part of follow-up
Assess and encourage adherence. On tamoxifen: age-appropriate gynaecologic screening; routine annual pelvic ultrasound is not recommended
